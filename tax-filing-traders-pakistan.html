Tax Filing for Traders in Pakistan
Complete 2026 Guide

📅 May 31, 2026 ⏱ 9 min read ✍️ Kamboh Associates

Traders — including shopkeepers, wholesalers, distributors, and retail merchants — form the backbone of Pakistan's economy. FBR has introduced multiple tax schemes specifically designed for traders, recognizing that traditional income-based taxation is complex for cash-heavy trading businesses.

Two main options for traders: (1) The Tajir Dost Scheme (FBR's simplified fixed monthly tax for small traders and shopkeepers), or (2) the Normal Tax Regime based on actual business income. Choosing the right one depends on your business size.

Option 1: FBR Tajir Dost Scheme (Fixed Tax)

The Tajir Dost scheme allows small traders and shopkeepers to pay a fixed monthly tax based on their business category and location. Benefits:

  • No need to maintain complex accounts
  • Simple flat monthly payment via FBR app
  • Protection from tax audits under this scheme
  • Eligible for active taxpayer status and ATL listing

Option 2: Normal Tax Regime

Larger traders and those with turnover exceeding Rs 10 million typically file under the normal tax regime, declaring actual business income:

  • Gross sales/turnover declared
  • Cost of goods sold deducted
  • Gross profit calculated
  • Business expenses deducted to arrive at net profit
  • Tax computed at normal slab rates on net profit

Minimum Turnover Tax (Section 113)

Important: Even if your trading business shows a loss, FBR applies minimum turnover tax at 1.25% of gross turnover under Section 113. This cannot be avoided even with legitimate losses — it's the minimum tax floor for businesses.

Deductible Expenses for Traders

  • Cost of goods sold: Purchase price of goods for resale
  • Shop rent: Monthly rental for business premises
  • Employee salaries: Sales staff, cashiers, delivery workers
  • Electricity and utilities: Shop running costs
  • Transportation: Delivery vehicle fuel and maintenance
  • Business insurance: Stock, fire, liability coverage
  • Bank charges: Transaction fees, letter of credit charges
  • Depreciation: On shop fixtures, display equipment, vehicles

Withholding Tax on Trading Transactions

Traders encounter multiple withholding tax points:

  • At import stage: Advance income tax deducted on imports (adjustable)
  • Cash withdrawals: 0.6% (non-filer) / 0% (filer) on bank cash withdrawals above Rs 50,000
  • Purchases from manufacturer: WHT under Section 153
  • Utility bills: Advance tax on electricity bills above Rs 20,000

How to File as a Trader

  1. Register on FBR IRIS and obtain your NTN
  2. Choose your scheme: Tajir Dost (fixed) or normal regime
  3. For normal regime: compile sales records, purchase invoices, and expense receipts
  4. Prepare a simple Profit & Loss statement for the tax year
  5. Log in to IRIS → Declaration → Income Tax Return (IT-2 for individuals)
  6. Fill business income section with gross sales minus cost of goods = gross profit
  7. Deduct all documented business expenses
  8. Enter all withholding tax credits
  9. Pay balance and submit before September 30

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FBR trader fixed tax scheme in Pakistan?
FBR introduced a simplified fixed tax scheme for small traders and shopkeepers. Under this scheme, traders pay a fixed monthly tax based on their business location and type, instead of maintaining complex accounts.
Do traders need to maintain accounts for tax purposes?
Small traders under the fixed tax scheme do not need formal accounts. However, traders with annual turnover exceeding Rs 10 million or those under the normal tax regime must maintain proper books of accounts.
What is minimum turnover tax for traders?
Traders with business turnover are subject to minimum turnover tax at 1.25% of gross turnover under Section 113 of ITO 2001, even if they show a book loss.
