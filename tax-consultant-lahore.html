What documents do I need for tax filing?

For salaried individuals: CNIC, salary slips or Form 16 from employer, bank statements. For business owners: CNIC, bank statements, sales records, expense receipts. For freelancers: CNIC, bank statements showing foreign remittances, platform payment records. All documents can be sent via WhatsApp.

When is the income tax return deadline?

The income tax return deadline for Pakistan residents is September 30 each year for that tax year, unless FBR announces an extension. Late filing attracts a penalty of Rs. 1,000 per month. File before the deadline with Kamboh Associates — same-day service available.

Step-by-Step Process to Become a Tax Filer

  1. Get your NTN — Register at iris.fbr.gov.pk or message Kamboh Associates at 0328-4675162
  2. Gather your documents — CNIC, salary slips, bank statements, property documents
  3. Login to IRIS — File your income tax return and wealth statement online
  4. Pay any tax due — Via 1-Bill or bank CPR before the deadline
  5. Check ATL status — Verify on atl.fbr.gov.pk within 30 days of filing
