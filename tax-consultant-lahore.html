Kamboh Associates is a Lahore tax practice based in Awan Town, working with individuals and businesses across the city since 2008. Different parts of Lahore have very different tax files, though, so instead of one generic page we've written a separate guide for each area, focused on the questions we actually get from there.

How Lahore's tax files differ from area to area

A household in DHA usually has property transfers, several cars and sometimes foreign assets to reconcile. A shopkeeper in Anarkali or Shah Alam Market has a cash-heavy trading business and sales tax questions. A salaried family in Wapda Town or Johar Town mostly needs its employer's deductions checked and its advance taxes claimed. Factory owners around Kot Lakhpat and Sundar deal with withholding, sales tax and EOBI. Overseas families in Bahria and Askari need residency and property advice.

Each area guide below takes the issue we see most from that locality and explains it properly — so pick the one closest to you, or the one whose topic fits your situation, wherever you live.

What we do for Lahore clients

  • Income tax returns for salaried people, pensioners, freelancers, landlords and businesses
  • Wealth statements that reconcile with property, vehicles and bank data
  • NTN registration and getting back on the Active Taxpayer List
  • Replies to FBR notices (Sections 114, 111, 122, 176 and others)
  • Sales tax registration with FBR or the Punjab Revenue Authority, and monthly returns
  • AOP and company setup, corporate returns and withholding statements

How it works

  1. WhatsApp us on 0328-4675162 with a line about your situation.
  2. We tell you what's needed and the fixed fee, before any work starts.
  3. You send documents — CNIC, salary certificate, bank statement, property papers, the notice.
  4. We prepare the return, send it to you to review, and file after you approve.
  5. You receive the FBR acknowledgement on WhatsApp.

The individual return deadline is normally 30 September each year. Filing in July or August leaves time to fix documents; filing in the last week usually doesn't.
