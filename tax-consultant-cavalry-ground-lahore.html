Services in Cavalry Ground

Tax help for Cavalry Ground residents

Cavalry Ground is a residential area within Lahore Cantonment, with a busy commercial market and many serving and retired armed forces families, professionals and shop owners. Common questions involve pensions, rental income from family property, and returns for small businesses in the market.

Pensions and commuted pension

Pension received by a retired employee is declared in the return; the treatment of pension and commuted pension depends on the current provisions of the Income Tax Ordinance, so check them for your tax year. Bank profit on savings is declared with the bank's withholding certificate as a credit.

Shops in Cavalry Ground market

Shop owners declare sales, purchases, rent, staff and utilities, with stock at 30 June. Tax collected on commercial electricity bills is credited when the bills are kept. If you rent out a shop instead, the rent is property income.

Property in Lahore Cantt

Property in the cantonment is administered by the cantonment board, but federal tax on buying (Section 236K) and selling (Section 236C) applies as elsewhere, at lower rates for filers. Our Lahore Cantt guide lists block-level guides for the wider area.

Filing status for Tax Year 2026

The Tax Year 2026 return deadline for individuals and AOPs was extended to 31 October 2026 and has now passed. A late return can still be filed on IRIS. Under Section 182 the late-filing penalty is 0.1% of the tax payable per day, up to 50%, with a minimum of Rs 40,000 (Rs 5,000 where at least 75% of income is salary below Rs 5 million). Joining the Active Taxpayers List after the due date also needs the Section 182A surcharge: Rs 25,000 for individuals, Rs 50,000 for AOPs and Rs 100,000 for companies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a pension taxable?

Pension is declared in the return, and its tax treatment depends on the current Income Tax Ordinance provisions for your tax year. Keep the pension statement.

Is tax on my shop's electricity bill adjustable?

Tax collected on commercial electricity bills is generally claimed as a credit in the return when the bills are kept.

Does federal tax apply to cantonment property?

Yes. Advance tax on buying and selling property applies in cantonments as elsewhere, with lower rates for filers.

Can I file without visiting an office?

Yes. Returns are filed on IRIS, and documents can be shared on WhatsApp or email.
