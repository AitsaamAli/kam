## Overlap risk — pehle se plan ki hui differentiation

Yeh sections ek doosre ke bohat qareeb hain, is liye har cluster ka angle pehle se fix hai:

- **C vs G** — C property/income-head ki baat karta hai, G business *structure* ki. C mein kabhi bhi "AOP kaise bane" nahi likha jayega.
- **D vs F** — D *ghalti ke baad* ka rasta hai (correction), F *normal case* ka process/timeline hai. D mein timeline sirf correction ki, F mein sirf normal filing ki.
- **E vs H** — E "kaun sa bundle chahiye" (life stage), H "us bundle ki cost kyun itni hai" (pricing logic). E mein fee sirf ek line, H mein fee poora topic.
- **H04 (non-filer cost) vs live ATL/filer pages** — H04 sirf *transaction-level* cost par rahega, ATL mechanics par nahi, warna existing filer/ATL group se overlap ho jayega.
- Har part ke baad `validate.js` sab purane batch files ke sath ek sath chalega (`node validate.js batch-C1.js batch-C2.js ...`), warna cross-batch overlap pakda nahi jata.
