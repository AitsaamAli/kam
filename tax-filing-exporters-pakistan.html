Tax Filing for Exporters in Pakistan
Complete 2026 Guide

📅 May 31, 2026 ⏱ 9 min read ✍️ Kamboh Associates

Pakistan's exporters — textile manufacturers, garment producers, rice traders, leather goods makers, IT services exporters — operate under a special income tax regime designed to encourage foreign exchange earnings. Understanding these rules is essential for tax compliance and maximizing your net export income.

Key advantage: Export proceeds are subject to a 1% final withholding tax under Section 154 of the Income Tax Ordinance 2001. This 1% (deducted by your bank when foreign remittances are credited) is your full and final income tax on export income — no additional tax is payable on those earnings.

The 1% Final Tax on Export Proceeds

When foreign currency is received in your Pakistani bank account as export proceeds, your bank automatically deducts 1% income tax before crediting the balance. This works as follows:

  • Your buyer pays USD/EUR/GBP into your Pakistan bank account
  • Bank converts to PKR at current exchange rate
  • Bank deducts 1% of the PKR amount as income tax (Section 154)
  • Bank credits the remaining 99% to your account
  • This 1% is the complete income tax on those export earnings

DTRE Scheme — Duty-Free Import of Inputs

The Duty and Tax Remission for Exporters (DTRE) scheme allows exporters to import raw materials, components, and packing materials without paying customs duty, sales tax, and income tax at import stage — on the condition that the finished products are exported within a set timeframe.

DTRE is particularly beneficial for:

  • Textile and garment exporters (yarn, fabric inputs)
  • Leather goods manufacturers (raw hides, accessories)
  • Sports goods producers (raw materials)
  • Engineering goods exporters (components)

Income from Both Export and Local Sales

Many businesses export some goods and sell locally too. The tax treatment differs:

  • Export income: 1% final tax on export proceeds only
  • Local sales income: Normal business income tax at slab rates
  • You must maintain separate records for export and local sales
  • File under "Mixed Income" and declare each source separately

IT/Services Exporters

Software houses, freelancers, and IT service exporters receive special treatment. Under current rules, IT services exported (income remitted to Pakistan through banking channels) are taxed at 0.25% as a final tax — even lower than the general 1% export rate.

Rebates and Incentives for Exporters

  • Export rebate on income tax: Exporters get a reduction in their advance tax obligations
  • Sales tax zero-rating: Export supplies are zero-rated for sales tax
  • Income from export proceeds in FX: Protected from currency risk through hedging (bank schemes)
  • Long-term export finance: SBP-subsidized financing for exporters

Annual Filing Requirements

Even with a final tax regime, exporters must file an annual income tax return to:

  • Maintain Active Taxpayer List (ATL) status
  • Declare their total export proceeds
  • Reconcile with bank-deducted WHT certificates
  • Declare local business income (if any) and pay tax
  • File the mandatory Wealth Statement

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the income tax rate for exporters in Pakistan?
Exporters pay 1% income tax on export proceeds as a final tax under Section 154. This 1% is deducted by banks when foreign remittances are credited and serves as the full and final income tax liability on those exports.
What is the DTRE scheme for Pakistani exporters?
DTRE (Duty and Tax Remission for Exporters) allows exporters to import raw materials and inputs duty-free, on the condition that the finished goods are exported. It significantly reduces the cost of export production.
Do exporters need to file an annual income tax return?
Yes. Even though the 1% tax on exports is final, exporters must still file an annual income tax return with FBR to maintain active taxpayer status and remain on the Active Taxpayers List (ATL).
