Filing status for Tax Year 2026

The Tax Year 2026 return deadline for individuals and AOPs was extended to 31 October 2026 and has now passed. A late return can still be filed on IRIS. Under Section 182 the late-filing penalty is 0.1% of the tax payable per day, up to 50%, with a minimum of Rs 40,000 (Rs 5,000 where at least 75% of income is salary below Rs 5 million). Joining the Active Taxpayers List after the due date also needs the Section 182A surcharge: Rs 25,000 for individuals, Rs 50,000 for AOPs and Rs 100,000 for companies.

Frequently Asked Questions

My clothing shop's sales jump before Eid. Is that a problem for FBR?

No, if your records show it. A month-by-month sales record makes seasonal spikes easy to explain.

I paid pagri for my shop in Karim Block. Do I declare it?

Yes. The pagri right is an asset at the amount you paid, and it belongs in your wealth statement with its source of funds.

Our family house is in my father's name. Who pays the tax on sale?

Your father, as the seller. He pays 236C advance tax at transfer, and the gain is calculated in his return.

Are medicines subject to sales tax?

Registered medicines are largely exempt or specially treated under sales tax law. Cosmetics, toiletries and many other pharmacy products are generally taxable goods.
