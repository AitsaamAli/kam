DHA Bahria Town Lahore Property Tax 2026 — 236C & 236K Complete Guide | Filer vs Non-Filer

July 17, 2026Aitsaam AliAuthorBudget 2026-27

DHA Lahore aur Bahria Town mein property deals aksar crore rupay ki hoti hain, is liye har deal par advance tax bhi lakhon mein banta hai. Registrar ya housing authority ye tax sale ya transfer ke waqt hi kaat leti hai: seller se section 236C ke tehat aur buyer se section 236K ke tehat. Aap Active Taxpayers List (ATL) mein hain ya nahi, isi se rate bilkul badal jata hai. ATL se bahar seller ka 236C rate 2.75% ki jagah 11.5% ho jata hai, aur ATL se bahar buyer ka 236K rate 1.25% ki jagah 10.5% se 18.5% tak. Is guide mein ITO 2001 (amended upto 30 June 2026) ke rates, deal-size ke hisaab se misaalein, capital gains, rent aur deal se pehle ki tayyari hai.

Short answer

Buyer (236K): ATL par 1.25% of fair market value; ATL se bahar 10.5% (Rs 5 crore tak), 14.5% (Rs 5–10 crore), 18.5% (Rs 10 crore se upar). Seller (236C): ATL par 2.75%; ATL se bahar 11.5%. Rs 3 crore ki deal mein dono taraf ATL par hon to Rs 12 lakh, dono ATL se bahar hon to Rs 66 lakh. Source: ITO 2001, First Schedule Part IV Division X aur XVIII, Tenth Schedule.

Section 114C — Bari Deals Par Pabandi Ka Qanoon

Finance Act 2025 ne section 114C aur Fifteenth Schedule shamil kiye. In ke mutabiq "ineligible person" ki aisi property transfer register, record ya attest nahi ki jayegi jis ki fair market value Rs 100 million (Rs 10 crore) se zyada ho.

  • Eligible person kaun hai: jis ne pichle tax year ki return file ki ho aur wealth statement mein deal ke liye kaafi resources dikhaye hon, ya jis ne FBR portal par "sources of investment and expenditure statement" file ki ho (section 114C(4)(a)). Individual ke case mein immediate family (parents, spouse, dependent children) bhi shamil hai.
  • Sufficient resources: deal value ka 130% cash aur cash-equivalent assets (section 114C(4)(e)).
  • Kin par lagu nahi: non-resident persons aur public companies (section 114C(2)).
  • Kab se lagu: section 114C(5) ke mutabiq ye pabandiyan us tareekh se lagu hongi jo Federal Government Gazette notification se tay kare. Bari deal se pehle FBR par current notification check karein.

Property Wealth Statement — DHA / Bahria Owners Ke Liye

Agar aap ke paas DHA ya Bahria mein property hai, to return ke saath wealth statement mein usay declare karna zaroori hai:

  • Har property alag likhein: address, plot/house number aur jis saal li gayi.
  • Khareed ke paise ka source: wealth reconciliation mein dikhayein ke property kis aamdani ya asset se li gayi.
  • Bechi hui property: jis saal bechi, us saal wealth statement se nikaalein aur sale proceeds dikhayein.
  • Rental income: kiraye par di hui property ka rent "Income from Property" ke head mein declare karein (section 15).

Agar property ka paisa declared aamdani se match na kare, to FBR section 111 (unexplained income or assets) ke tehat sawal kar sakta hai. 236C/236K ka data registrar se FBR tak jata hai, is liye return aur wealth statement deal ke mutabiq honi chahiye.

DHA / Bahria Mein Rental Property — Tax Guide

Kiraye se aamdani section 15 ke tehat "Income from Property" ke head mein taxable hai. Rent ki alag tax table (First Schedule Division VIA) Finance Act 2021 ne khatam kar di thi. Ab individual ka rent baqi aamdani ke saath return mein jata hai aur normal individual rates par tax banta hai.

  • Kam rent: agar rent fair market rent se kam ho, to section 15(4) ke tehat FBR fair market rent maan sakta hai.
  • Amenities aur services: rent ke saath utilities ya services ka charge ho, to woh hissa "Income from Other Sources" mein jata hai (section 15(3A)).
  • Tenant company ho: company ya prescribed tenant rent dete waqt section 155 ke tehat tax kaat sakta hai. Ye tax return mein adjust hota hai.

Rent ke saath doosri aamdani bhi ho, to sab ko mila kar tax banta hai. Is liye hum har client ka hisaab return mein uski poori aamdani dekh kar banate hain.

FAQs — DHA Bahria Town Property Tax Lahore

DHA Lahore mein plot khareedne par kitna advance tax lagega?
Buyer par section 236K fair market value ka 1.25% lagta hai agar woh ATL par ho. Rs 2.5 crore ki value par ye Rs 3,12,500 banta hai. ATL se bahar buyer par 10.5% (Rs 26,25,000) lagega. Stamp duty, CVT aur society transfer fee alag hain.
Bahria Town ki property bech raha hoon — tax kab aur kaise katega?
Transfer ke waqt registrar ya authority section 236C ke tehat gross consideration ka 2.75% (ATL par) ya 11.5% (ATL se bahar) collect karti hai. Rs 2 crore par ye Rs 5,50,000 ya Rs 23,00,000 banta hai. Ye tax return mein adjustable hai. Lekin agar property usi tax year mein khareedi aur bechi gayi, to ye minimum tax hai.
Kya DHA ya Bahria ke transfer par bhi 236C/236K lagta hai?
Haan. Section 236C aur 236K ki Explanation ke mutabiq jo bhi transfer register, record ya attest karta hai, us mein housing authority aur housing society bhi shamil hain. Is liye society ke transfer par bhi ye advance tax collect hota hai.
DHA property par capital gains tax kitna hai?
1 July 2024 ya baad mein khareedi property par ATL shakhs ke liye 15%, holding period jitna bhi ho. 30 June 2024 tak khareedi property par rate holding period aur property type se tay hota hai: open plot 6 saal baad, constructed property 4 saal baad, aur flat 2 saal baad 0% (First Schedule Part I Division VIII).
Overseas Pakistani ke liye kya alag hai?
Agar non-resident individual (POC, NICOP ya CNIC) ne property FCVA ya NRVA account ke zariye khareedi ho, to section 236K(2) ke proviso ke tehat collect hua 236K final discharge hai. Aisi property bechne par 236C, section 37 ke capital gains ki jagah final tax hai. Section 114C ki pabandiyan non-residents par lagu nahi (section 114C(2)).
