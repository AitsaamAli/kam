Property Wealth Statement — DHA / Bahria Owners Ke Liye

DHA ya Bahria mein property owner ho — wealth statement mein declare karna compulsory hai. Annual return mein property ka DC value (ya market value, whichever higher) include karo:

  • Residential property: Purchase price ya DC value mein se jo zyada — wealth statement mein
  • Commercial property: Market value declare karo — FBR verification possible
  • Plot vs constructed: Alag treatment — constructed property ki value higher hoti hai
  • Rental income: DHA/Bahria mein rented property hai toh rental income bhi declare karo — Section 15 tax

Property wealth statement mein declare nahi ki — FBR Section 111 unexplained income notice. DHA aur Bahria registrations FBR ke paas jaati hain. Agar property hai aur return mein show nahi — risk hai.

FAQs — DHA Bahria Town Property Tax Lahore

DHA Lahore mein plot khareedne par total kitna kharch hoga?
DHA Phase 5 mein 1 kanal plot Rs 2.5 crore par: 236K filer Rs 3,12,500 + Stamp Duty Rs 7,50,000 + Registration Fee Rs 2,50,000 + DHA transfer approximately Rs 2–3 lakh = total Rs 15–16 lakh additional charges. Non-filer ho to 236K Rs 6,25,000 — Rs 3,12,500 extra sirf ek tax mein.
Bahria Town ki property bech raha hoon — paisa kaisay milega aur tax kab dena hoga?
Sale deed registration ke waqt registrar 236C tax kaatta hai aur aapko baaki raqam milti hai. Agar filer ho: Rs 2 crore property mein se registrar Rs 5.5 lakh kaatta hai, baaki aapko. Non-filer: Rs 11 lakh kaat ta — Rs 5.5 lakh zyada nuksan. Ye refund nahi hota (non-filer ke liye final tax hai).
Kya DHA transfer letter par bhi 236C/236K lagta hai?
DHA transfer letter (non-registered dealing) technically registered sale nahi — lekin FBR aur DHA dono ab aise deals track karte hain. Risky hai: agar aage registered hui sale deed — registered price par tax lagega. FBR DHA se data share bhi leta hai. Proper registered sale karo — filer ho aur low rate enjoy karo.
DHA property par capital gains tax bhi lagta hai?
Capital Gains Tax (CGT) property holding period ke mutabiq lagtl hai — 4 years se zyada hold karo toh zero CGT. 1 year mein becho: 15% CGT. 1-2 years: 12.5%. 2-3 years: 10%. 3-4 years: 5%. 4+ years: Zero. DHA properties mostly long-term hold hoti hain — isliye CGT kam lagta hai. Annual return mein ye bhi declare karna zaroori hai.
Overseas Pakistani DHA mein property khareed sakta hai?
Haan — Overseas Pakistanis DHA mein property khareed sakte hain. NRP (Non-Resident Pakistani) status pe: foreign remittance se khareedni ho toh tax concessions available hain.
