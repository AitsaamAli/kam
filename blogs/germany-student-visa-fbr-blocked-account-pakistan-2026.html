Germany Student Visa — FBR Documents & Blocked Account Financial Evidence (2026)

28 August 2026Aitsaam Ali11 min read2026 Verified

Har saal hazaron Pakistani students German admission le lete hain, blocked account provider bhi choose kar lete hain, aur phir aakhri stage par atak jate hain — jab bank se 40 lakh rupay se zyada ki outward remittance karani hoti hai aur koi poochta hai ke yeh raqam aayi kahan se hai. Yeh guide us aakhri stage ki hai, jise zyada tar consultants "formality" keh kar chhor dete hain.

TL;DR:

  • 2026 mein Germany student visa ke liye blocked account (Sperrkonto) mein €11,904 chahiye — yani €992 per month, poore saal ke liye.
  • Blocked account ka paisa Pakistan se Germany transfer hota hai, is liye aapke bank ko source of funds ki documentation chahiye — sirf embassy ko nahi.
  • Sponsor ki last 3 saal ki income tax returns + wealth statement is documentation ka core hain. Non-filer sponsor sabse bara risk hai.
  • APS certificate 2026 mein 4–6 mahine ka appointment wait maang raha hai — yeh waqt FBR ka kaam mukammal karne ke liye istemal karein.
  • Property bech kar funds arrange kar rahe hain to filer aur non-filer ke darmiyan Section 236C par 2.75% vs 5.5% ka farq parta hai.

Poori tasveer: Germany mein paisa mulk se bahar nikalna parta hai

Yeh ek line hai jo Germany ke poore financial process ko samjha deti hai, aur jo UK ya Australia par lagoo nahi hoti. UK student visa mein aap apne ya sponsor ke Pakistani account mein maintenance funds 28 din tak rakhte hain aur bank statement embassy ko dikha dete hain. Paisa Pakistan hi mein rehta hai. Germany mein aisa nahi hota — aap ko woh raqam ek German bank ke blocked account mein bhejni parti hai, visa apply karne se pehle.

Is ek farq ki wajah se aapke case mein ek naya stakeholder shamil ho jata hai jo UK case mein hota hi nahi: aapka Pakistani bank. Aur bank ka sawal embassy ke sawal se mukhtalif hota hai. Embassy yeh dekhti hai ke aap financially sustainable hain ya nahi. Bank yeh dekhta hai ke jo raqam mulk se bahar ja rahi hai woh declared, tax-paid income se aayi hai ya nahi.

Practical natija: aap ka case embassy tak pohanchne se pehle bank par ruk sakta hai. Aur agar aapke sponsor ki tax history khali hai, to bank ke paas aapko clear karne ki koi documentary bunyad hi nahi hoti — chahe paisa bilkul jaiz ho.

Yahi wajah hai ke hum Germany-bound families ko admission process ke saath saath, uske baad nahi, FBR side par kaam shuru karne ka mashwara dete hain. Yeh sequencing ka masla hai, mehnat ka nahi.

Blocked account mein kitna paisa — 2026 ke asal figures

Blocked account ki raqam German Federal Foreign Office har saal BAföG rate ke sath link kar ke set karta hai. 2026 ke liye student visa (§16b) par yeh €992 per month hai, yani poore saal ke liye €11,904.

Ek cheez jo bohat se applicants miss kar dete hain: yeh figure sirf regular university students ke liye hai. Agar aap language course visa par ja rahe hain, ya Opportunity Card par, to requirement zyada hai — €1,091 per month, yani €13,092 saalana. Logic yeh hai ke university students ko subsidised hostel, sasti Mensa canteen aur semester transport ticket milti hai; language students aur job seekers ko yeh sahoolat nahi milti.

Yeh raqam permanent nahi hai. Har saal revise hoti hai aur kuch categories mein embassy is se zyada bhi maang sakti hai. Transfer se pehle apne blocked account provider aur German Mission Pakistan ki current requirement dobara confirm karein.

Blocked account ka mechanism yeh hai ke poore saal ka paisa aap ek dafa jama karte hain, aur Germany pohanchne ke baad har mahine sirf ek muqarrara hissa release hota hai. Aap poora amount ek sath nahi nikal sakte — aur yeh system ka maqsad hi hai, taake student ke paas saal bhar mustaqil kharcha maujood rahe.

Sponsor kaun ban sakta hai aur uske kagaz kya honge

Pakistani cases mein sponsor tagreeban hamesha walid, bara bhai, ya kabhi kabhi chacha/mamu hote hain. Sponsor kaun hai, is se zyada ahem yeh hai ke us sponsor ka tax record kya kehta hai.

Farz karein aapke walid ki apni dukan hai aur mahana aamdani theek thaak hai — lekin unhone kabhi return file nahi ki. Aap ke nazdeek unki income real hai. Bank ke nazdeek uska koi record nahi. Aur bank sirf record dekh sakta hai, aapke mohallay ki reputation nahi.

Is liye sponsor ke liye jo cheezein tayyar honi chahiyen:

NTN certificate — bunyadi tax identity. Iske baghair baaqi kuch nahi ban sakta.
Pichhle teen saal ki income tax returns — sirf ek saal ki return kamzor lagti hai. Teen saal ka consistent record ek pattern banata hai.
Wealth statement — sponsor ke assets ka poora naqsha: property, savings, business capital, vehicles.
ATL status — return file kar dena aur Active Taxpayers List par aana do alag cheezein hain. Apna status 9966 par SMS bhej kar check karein, format: ATL <13-digit CNIC>.
Business sponsors ke liye — SECP registration documents aur company ki apni tax return, kyunki us case mein income ka source ek registered entity hai.

Source-of-funds chain — teen documents jo aapas mein match karne chahiyen

Yeh section is poori guide ka sabse ahem hissa hai. Documentation ka maqsad sirf kagaz jama karna nahi — teen numbers ka aapas mein logical rishta banana hai:

DocumentKya batata haiKis se match hona chahiye
Income tax returnSponsor ki saalana declared incomeRemittance amount is income ke muqable plausible lage
Wealth statementSponsor ke total assetsAgar income kam hai lekin assets zyada, to gap yahan explain ho
Bank statementPaisa kis account se ja raha haiYeh account upar waale dono documents se jura hona chahiye

Ek misaal se samjhen. Sponsor ki return mein saalana income 18 lakh rupay hai. Remittance 42 lakh ki ho rahi hai. Yeh apne aap mein galat nahi — ho sakta hai paisa ek plot bech kar aaya ho, ya barson ki saving se. Lekin agar wealth statement mein na woh plot nazar aata hai na woh saving, to teenon documents ek doosre ko support nahi kar rahe. Yahi wo lamha hai jahan file rukti hai.

Aur yeh sirf visa ka masla nahi rehta. Section 111 (unexplained income) ke tehat FBR ke paas ikhtiyar hai ke bari, undeclared transactions par sawal kare. Jo family sirf visa ke liye ek dafa bara transfer karti hai aur uska koi tax record nahi banati, woh apne liye ek alag masla khareed rahi hoti hai — jo aksar do saal baad notice ki shakal mein wapas aata hai, jab bacha Germany mein hota hai aur walid akele notice handle kar rahe hote hain.

APS ka intezar dushman nahi, mauqa hai

APS (Akademische Prüfstelle / Academic Evaluation Centre) certificate Pakistani students ke liye lazmi hai. Iska daftar German Embassy Islamabad ke ehaate mein hai aur application online aps-southasia.de se hoti hai. Fee tagreeban €185 hai aur certificate paanch saal tak qabil-e-istemal rehta hai.

Ab asal baat: 2026 mein APS appointment ka wait tagreeban 4 se 6 mahine chal raha hai, aur interview ke baad processing mein mazeed 8 se 12 hafte lag sakte hain. Zyada tar students is intezar ko zaya waqt samajhte hain.

Isko differently dekhein: yeh chaar se chhe mahine bilkul wohi arsa hai jo sponsor ka NTN banane, teen saal ki pending returns file karne aur wealth statement finalize karne mein lagta hai. Jo student APS ke din se FBR ka kaam parallel shuru karta hai, uske paas visa appointment tak mukammal documentation hoti hai. Jo intezar karta hai, woh do process series mein chalata hai aur ek poora intake miss kar deta hai.

Yaad rahe APS academic verification hai — degree aur transcripts ki asliyat check hoti hai, financial documents ka is se koi taluq nahi. Dono track alag hain, isi liye inhein sath sath chalaya ja sakta hai. Bachelor degree HEC attested aur Matric/FSc IBCC attested honi chahiye, phir MOFA stamping.

Agar property bech kar paisa arrange kar rahe hain

Bohat si families blocked account ka paisa plot ya makan bech kar arrange karti hain. Yahan filer hona ya na hona seedha jeb par asar daalta hai, visa se qata nazar.

Property sale par withholding tax Section 236C ke tehat filer ke liye 2.75% hai aur non-filer ke liye 5.5% — poora double. Ek crore ki property par yeh farq 2.75 lakh aur 5.5 lakh ka hai. Yani sirf is ek transaction par non-filer hone ki qeemat tagreeban 2.75 lakh rupay banti hai. Iske muqable NTN registration Rs. 2,000 hai aur ek saal ki return Rs. 3,500 se 5,000 ke darmiyan.

Isi tarah agar property khareedi ja rahi ho to Section 236K filer ke liye 1.25% aur non-filer ke liye 2.5% hai. Property par capital gains tax holding period ke sath 15% se 0% tak slide karta hai aur chhe saal se zyada holding par exempt ho jata hai — non-filer ke liye CGT rate filer se double hoti hai.

Matlab yeh ke filer banna is family ke liye kharcha nahi, bachat hai. Aur uska side benefit yeh hai ke jo return aap tax bachane ke liye file kar rahe hain, wohi return aapke bete ke source-of-funds documentation ka hissa ban jati hai. Ek kaam, do faide.

Paanch ghaltiyan jo har saal cases kharab karti hain

1. FBR ka kaam aakhir mein rakhna. Admission, APS aur language pehle, tax documentation aakhri mahine mein. Agar teen saal ki returns pending hain to yeh ek hafte ka kaam nahi.

2. Sirf ek saal ki return file karana. Ek akela saal, wo bhi visa se do mahine pehle file kiya gaya, credible pattern nahi banata. Teen saal ka record banayein.

3. Return file kar ke ATL check na karna. Bohat se log yeh farz kar lete hain ke return file hote hi filer ho gaye. ATL par naam aane mein waqt lagta hai aur kabhi kabhi nahi aata.

4. Sirf embassy ke baare mein sochna. Bank aksar embassy se pehle sawal karta hai, aur bank ke sawal ka jawab sirf tax documentation se milta hai.

5. Ghalat category ka figure calculate karna. Student rate par paisa bhej dena jab actually language course ya Chancenkarte par jaana hai — is se account short reh jata hai aur poora transfer dobara karna parta hai.

Germany Student Visa & FBR Documents — Aam Sawalat

Germany student visa 2026 ke liye blocked account mein kitna paisa chahiye?
2026 mein student visa ke liye €11,904 saalana, yani €992 per month. Yeh raqam German Federal Foreign Office BAföG rate ke sath link kar ke har saal update karta hai. Language course aur Opportunity Card ke liye requirement zyada hai — €1,091 per month, yani €13,092 saalana.
Kya German embassy tax return maangti hai?
German embassy tax return ko UKVI ki tarah ek alag mandatory checklist item ke tor par list nahi karti. Lekin practically yeh do jagah zaroori ho jati hai: pehla, blocked account ki outward remittance ke liye aapke Pakistani bank ko source of funds ki documentation chahiye; dusra, embassy ko yeh dekhna hota hai ke sponsor poore degree period ka kharcha utha sakta hai — aur uska sabse mazboot proof sponsor ki income tax return aur wealth statement hai.
Mere walid non-filer hain, kya main phir bhi apply kar sakta hoon?
Apply kar sakte hain, lekin source-of-funds documentation kamzor rahegi aur remittance stage par masla aane ka imkan hai. Behtar rasta yeh hai ke apply karne se kai mahine pehle sponsor ka NTN banwa kar returns file karwa li jayen. NTN Rs. 2,000 hai aur pending return Rs. 3,500 se 5,000 per saal — yeh cost is ke muqable bohat kam hai ke aakhri waqt par transfer ruk jaye aur poora intake nikal jaye.
APS certificate Pakistani students ke liye lazmi hai?
Ji haan. APS certificate Pakistani applicants ke liye zaroori hai, application aps-southasia.de se hoti hai aur daftar German Embassy Islamabad mein hai. Fee tagreeban €185 hai aur certificate paanch saal chalta hai. 2026 mein appointment ka wait 4 se 6 mahine chal raha hai, is liye Germany ka faisla karte hi sabse pehla qadam APS hona chahiye. Requirements badal sakti hain, is liye current status APS South Asia aur German Mission se confirm kar lein.
Blocked account ka paisa property bech kar arrange karun to kya masla hoga?
Masla nahi, bashart-e-ke woh sale properly document ho aur wealth statement mein nazar aaye. Sath hi yaad rahe ke property sale par withholding tax filer ke liye 2.75% aur non-filer ke liye 5.5% hai — yani filer hone se ek crore ki property par tagreeban 2.75 lakh rupay ki bachat hoti hai, jo poori documentation ki cost se kai guna zyada hai.

Sponsor ka NTN, teen saal ki pending returns aur wealth statement — sab kuch remotely, office aane ki zaroorat nahi. APS ke intezar ka waqt zaya na karein.
