Revised Return for Salaried People, Tax Year 2026: The Errors We See Most and How to Correct Them

11 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 10 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 11 November 2026
Salaried returns are not as simple as they look. Most errors sit in other income, tax credits and the wealth statement, and all can be revised.

Salaried people are often told their tax is 'already deducted', so the return does not matter. The deduction covers the salary, but the return also covers other income, credits and a wealth statement, and that is where problems start. This guide lists the errors we correct most often in salaried returns and how to fix each.

TL;DR

A salaried person's Tax Year 2026 return can be revised under Section 114(6) when an omission or wrong statement is discovered, without the Commissioner's approval if filed within 60 days of the original. The most common errors are figures that do not match the employer's certificate, bank profit or rent left out, freelance or side income omitted, adjustable credits not claimed, and wealth statements that do not reconcile with take-home pay and household spending. If the revision increases tax, filing voluntarily with the tax and default surcharge before a notice means no penalty under Section 114(6A).

Matching the Employer's Certificate

Your employer deducts tax under Section 149 and reports it. The return should show gross salary, taxable allowances and the tax deducted exactly as on the certificate. If the certificate itself is wrong, for example a bonus missing or tax under-deducted, ask the employer to correct it before you revise, so the figures match FBR's records.

Other Income Salaried People Forget

  • Profit on bank deposits and savings certificates. Declare it. For individuals, profit on debt up to Rs 5 million is taxed separately under Section 7B, so it is not added to salary; above Rs 5 million it is taxed under the normal rules.
  • Rent from a house, portion or shop.
  • Freelance, consultancy or tutoring income received alongside the job.
  • Capital gains on property, shares or mutual funds sold during the year.
  • Pension or income from a previous employer in the same year.

The Salaried Wealth Statement

For most salaried people the reconciliation is simple: opening wealth plus take-home salary, plus any other income and inflows, less household spending, equals closing wealth. Problems arise when a car, plot or gold is bought but not shown, or when household spending is set unrealistically to make the numbers fit. Correct the assets and set spending to a realistic figure supported by your actual life: rent, school fees, utilities, travel. Our guide to assets people leave out lists what to check.

How to Revise, Step by Step

  1. Check the filing date on your IRIS acknowledgement; within 60 days, no approval is needed.
  2. Collect the employer's certificate, bank certificates, rent records and any other income documents.
  3. Correct income, credits and the wealth statement; prepare the revised reconciliation.
  4. Write short, signed reasons for each change.
  5. Pay any additional tax with default surcharge before filing.
  6. File the revised return and wealth statement on IRIS; keep the acknowledgements.

If Tax Goes Up, or Down

If tax increases, filing the revision voluntarily with the tax and default surcharge before any notice under Section 177 or 122(9) means no penalty is recovered, under Section 114(6A). If tax goes down because credits were missed, a refund can be claimed under Section 170 within three years; see our guide to refunds and missed credits.

If You Have Missed the 60 Days

Revision needs the Commissioner's approval, which is deemed granted if no written order is made within 60 days of your request, or if the revision increases your declared income. For a broader view of post-deadline corrections, see our guide to correcting a rushed filing after the deadline.

Two Employers in One Year

If you changed jobs during Tax Year 2026, each employer deducted tax on the salary it paid, often without knowing about the other. The return must combine both salaries, and the tax on the total may differ from the sum of the two deductions. Collect both certificates; if tax was under-deducted, pay the balance with the revision; if over-deducted, claim the excess.

The closing statement must then add up to Rs 4,290,000: for example, bank balances, a car bought during the year at its cost, and gold. If the car was left out, the closing statement would show less wealth than expected, which is usually "fixed" by inflating spending. Showing the car, and spending at its real level, makes the reconciliation honest and balanced.

Working Spouses

If your spouse has their own income, they file their own return and wealth statement. Their assets are not included in yours unless they are your dependent. Decide clearly who owns what, especially for jointly bought property and gold, and keep both statements consistent.

Allowances and Benefits

Allowances and benefits provided by the employer are generally part of salary and appear on the certificate. Check that the return shows them as the employer reported them; if a benefit was left off the certificate, ask the employer to correct it rather than adjusting the return on your own.

Salaried Revision Checklist

  • ☐ Employer's certificate (or two, if you changed jobs).
  • ☐ Bank certificates for profit and tax deducted.
  • ☐ Rent agreement and rent received, if any.
  • ☐ Freelance or side-income records and remittance statements.
  • ☐ Utility, mobile and vehicle tax evidence for adjustable credits.
  • ☐ Purchase documents for any car, plot or gold bought in the year.
  • ☐ Last year's wealth statement for opening figures.
  • ☐ Realistic household spending figures.

Frequently Asked Questions

My employer deducted all my tax. Do I still need to revise a wrong return?
Yes. The return also covers other income, credits and your wealth statement. An error in any of them can be corrected by a revised return under Section 114(6).
My employer's certificate is wrong. What should I do?
Ask the employer to correct it first, so your revised return matches the figures FBR receives.
Do I need to declare bank profit if tax was deducted?
Yes. Declare the profit and the tax deducted. For individuals, profit on debt up to Rs 5 million is taxed separately under Section 7B; above that it is taxed under the normal rules.
I forgot to show the car I bought. Do I need to revise?
Yes, revise the wealth statement to show it and explain how it was paid for. If the money came from undeclared income, revise the return too.
How long do I have to revise without approval?
Sixty days from the date you filed the original return.
What does a salaried return cost with you?
Our published fee for a salaried return is Rs 3,500; any revision fee is confirmed before we start.
I had two employers this year. What should I check?
Combine both salaries and both tax deductions. The tax on the total may differ from the two deductions added together; pay any shortfall or claim any excess.
Does my working wife's income go in my return?
No. A spouse with their own income files their own return and wealth statement; their assets go in yours only if they are dependent.
How do I set household spending realistically?
Build it from rent, school fees, utilities, food, travel and other regular costs you can support with records.
Should I adjust my return if a benefit is missing from my certificate?
Ask your employer to correct the certificate first, so your return and the employer's statement agree.
Do I need my last year's wealth statement to revise?
Yes. This year's opening figures must match last year's closing statement.
Can I revise after the 60 days if I find more errors?
Yes, with the Commissioner's approval, which is deemed granted if not decided within 60 days of your request, or if the revision increases declared income.
