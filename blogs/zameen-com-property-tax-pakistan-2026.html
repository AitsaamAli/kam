Section 236C aur 236K — Kya Hai Ye Tax?

Section 236C — Property Seller par Withholding Tax. Jab aap property bechte hain, registrar sale deed ke waqt aapki sale amount se ye tax kaat leta hai. Ye tax FBR ko jaata hai. Aap ki liability sirf sale price ka ek percentage hai.

Section 236K — Property Buyer par Withholding Tax. Jab aap property khareedtey hain, registrar aap se ye tax collect karta hai registration ke waqt. Buyer ki jeb se seedha jaata hai.

Dono mein filer aur non-filer ka rate alag hota hai — aur farq significant hai. Ye taxes adjustable hain aapki annual tax return mein — matlab final liability mein se deduct ho jaate hain, ya refund milta hai.

Important: 236C/236K ke tax rates property ki FBR Valuation Table value par apply hote hain — market price par nahi (agar FBR value kam ho tou). Kuch cases mein market value par bhi apply ho sakta hai. Apna case assess karwao pehle — Kamboh Associates free assessment deta hai.

236C/236K Adjustable Hai — Refund Bhi Milta Hai

Ek important baat: 236C aur 236K tax adjustable hain — matlab ye final tax nahi hain. Jab aap annual income tax return file karte hain:

  • 236K (buyer WHT) aapki tax liability mein se deduct hota hai — agar zyada kataa gaya tou refund milta hai
  • 236C (seller WHT) bhi adjustable hai — capital gain tax calculate hokar actual liability tay hoti hai
  • Property pe capital gain agar kam hai (short holding period) — refund possible hai
  • Return file karna zaroori hai refund claim karne ke liye — jo return file nahi karta, refund nahi milta

Property seller ne 236C nahi diya — ab kya hoga?
Agar registrar ne 236C nahi kata tou FBR baad mein directly seller se recover karta hai — notice aa sakta hai. Kuch cases mein property ki registration bhi hold ho sakti hai. Registrar ka kaam hai 236C kaatna — agar galti se nahi kata, seller ki responsibility hai proactively pay karna.
Property gifted ya inherit ki hai — kya tax lagega?
Gift mein 236C seller par lagta hai (gifter par). Inheritance mein generally 236C nahi lagti — inheritance tax Pakistan mein nahi hai. Lekin property register karwate waqt documentation zaruri hai. Complex cases mein Kamboh Associates se consult karo — galat assumption mahnga pad sakta hai.

Builder ki receipt aur allotment letter important hain. Kamboh Associates builder/developer property transactions mein specialized hai — WhatsApp karo case ke details se.
