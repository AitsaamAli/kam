Why Pakistanis in Dubai Need an NTN

A National Tax Number (NTN) is required for any Pakistani citizen who wants active filer status with FBR, whether or not they currently live in Pakistan. For Pakistanis based in Dubai and across the UAE, holding an NTN and filer status matters if you own property, vehicles, or bank accounts in Pakistan, or plan to invest back home. Filer status also significantly reduces withholding tax on property purchases, vehicle registration, and banking transactions.

Can You Register for NTN Without Visiting Pakistan?

Yes. NTN registration through the FBR IRIS portal can be completed entirely online. There is no legal requirement to be physically present in Pakistan, visit an FBR office, or appear in person at any stage. Kamboh Associates handles the entire application remotely on your behalf using documents shared over WhatsApp.

Key Insight: Many overseas Pakistanis assume NTN registration requires a trip back home. It does not — the whole process, from document collection to FBR IRIS submission, can be completed while you remain in Dubai or anywhere else in the UAE.
