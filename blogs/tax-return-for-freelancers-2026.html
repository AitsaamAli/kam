Tax Return for Freelancers Pakistan 2026 — Fiverr Upwork Guide

June 2026 Kamboh Associates 6 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Tax Consultant | NTN • SECP • Sales Tax Specialist

How freelancers file income tax return in Pakistan 2026. Fiverr, Upwork, Toptal income declaration, foreign income — step by step.

TL;DR

Freelancers earning through Fiverr, Upwork, or direct clients must declare gross income and can claim WHT already deducted on remittances as credit against their final liability. Business expenses like internet, equipment, and a home office allocation are deductible against freelance income.

What Freelancers Can Actually Deduct

Freelancers filing as sole proprietors can deduct genuine business expenses against gross income before arriving at taxable profit — internet and phone bills (proportional to business use if also used personally), software subscriptions and tools directly used for client work, a proportional home office allocation if a dedicated space is used for work, professional development courses relevant to the freelance work, and platform fees charged by Fiverr, Upwork, or payment processors. What doesn't qualify: personal living expenses dressed up as business costs, a full home rent claim when only a corner of a room is used for work, or vague "miscellaneous" deductions without receipts. Keep simple digital records — invoices, receipts, bank statements — for every claimed expense, since a freelancer's deduction claims get scrutinized more closely than a salaried employee's return precisely because there's more room for interpretation in what counts as a legitimate business cost. A simple monthly spreadsheet logging each expense with a short note on its business purpose, updated as costs are incurred rather than reconstructed at filing time, is usually enough to satisfy this standard without needing formal bookkeeping software or a dedicated accounting system for what is often still a one-person operation. As freelance income grows and the business starts to look less like a side hustle and more like an established operation — hiring subcontractors, taking on retainer clients, scaling revenue significantly — it's worth revisiting whether continuing as a sole proprietor still makes sense or whether formal incorporation offers better liability protection and more favorable tax planning options at that later stage of growth — model both structures against actual numbers with a consultant before switching, since incorporation brings its own ongoing compliance costs that only make real practical sense once revenue and operational complexity clearly and genuinely justify making that switch.

Freelancer Return Walkthrough on IRIS

  1. Collect platform statements. Download yearly earnings reports from Fiverr, Upwork or your client invoices for 1 July to 30 June.
  2. Match them with bank credits. Each withdrawal through Payoneer, a bank wire or another channel should be traceable to a bank statement entry.
  3. Get the bank's certificates. Ask your bank for the certificate of tax deducted on foreign inward remittances and the proceeds realisation certificate if issued.
  4. Choose the right income head. Export of IT and IT-enabled services has its own treatment, while services for local clients are ordinary business income.
  5. Enter expenses. Internet, equipment, software and platform fees, backed by records.
  6. Claim tax already paid. Withholding on remittances, mobile and internet bills, and vehicle tax.
  7. Complete the wealth statement. Include foreign balances still held in Payoneer or Wise accounts at 30 June.

Payoneer and Wise Balances at Year End

Money sitting in a Payoneer or Wise wallet on 30 June is an asset of yours, just like a bank account. Many freelancers leave it out of the wealth statement because it has not reached Pakistan yet. Declaring it avoids a mismatch when the money arrives in a later year.

PSEB Registration

IT freelancers can register with the Pakistan Software Export Board as individual IT professionals. Registration supports the reduced treatment for IT services exports and is often asked for by banks when processing foreign receipts. It is inexpensive and worth doing once income becomes regular.

Common Freelancer Filing Errors

  • Declaring only the amount withdrawn, not total platform earnings.
  • Forgetting to claim tax deducted on remittances.
  • Showing a new laptop or car in the wealth statement with no income to explain it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I declare money still in my Payoneer account?

Yes. Balances held in Payoneer, Wise or similar wallets on 30 June are your assets and belong in the wealth statement.

Is PSEB registration required for freelancers?

It is not mandatory for everyone, but it supports the reduced tax treatment for IT services exports and helps when banks ask for proof.

Can I claim tax deducted by my bank on foreign remittances?

Yes. Ask the bank for a certificate of tax deducted and claim it in your return.
