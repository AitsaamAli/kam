NPO / Trust Registration Pakistan 2026 — 100% Tax Exemption Kaise Milta Hai

July 23, 2026Aitsaam Ali8 min readNGOs & Charities

NGO, charitable trust, ya welfare society chalana chahte hain aur legally 100% tax exemption chahte hain? Pakistan mein ye Section 100C ke tehat possible hai — lekin sirf tab jab aapki organization sahi tareeqay se register ho aur FBR se non-profit approval hasil kare. Ye guide registration routes, FBR approval process, aur annual compliance requirements — sab explain karta hai.

TL;DR

SECP Section 42, Trust deed, ya Society registration — teeno routes available. FBR se separate "non-profit organization" approval chahiye Section 100C ke 100% tax credit ke liye. Surplus charitable objects par kharch karna zaroori — profit distribute nahi kar sakte.

Sabse credible route: SECP ke Section 42 ke tehat "company limited by guarantee" register karna sab se zyada structured aur donor-trusted route hai — especially agar aap foreign funding ya large-scale operations plan kar rahe hain.

Section 100C — 100% Tax Credit Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

Section 100C ke tehat FBR-approved non-profit organizations ko unki income par 100% tax credit milta hai — matlab effectively income tax zero ho jata hai. Lekin ye credit automatically nahi milta — organization ko ye conditions poori karni hoti hain:

  • Surplus income ko organization ke declared charitable/welfare objects par hi kharch karna
  • Members/directors ko koi profit distribution nahi ho sakti
  • Proper books of account maintain karna aur audit karwana
  • Annual income tax return FBR ko file karna
  • Governance structure transparent aur objects mein clearly defined honi chahiye

FBR Se Non-Profit Approval — Process

Entity Register Karo

SECP Section 42, Trust deed, ya Society registration — jo route best suit kare wo complete karo.

NTN Registration

FBR se organization ka NTN register karo — SECP/Trust certificate ke saath.

Non-Profit Status Application

Commissioner Inland Revenue ko application submit karo — constitution, objects, aur governance documents ke saath.

Review aur Approval

FBR objects aur structure review karta hai ke genuinely non-profit hai. Approval milne par 100C credit eligible ho jata hai.

Annual Compliance Maintain Karo

Har saal return file karo, audited accounts submit karo — tabhi credit continue rahega.

Common mistake: Kai NGOs sirf SECP/Society registration karke sochti hain ke automatically tax-exempt hain — lekin FBR se separate non-profit approval na lena Section 100C credit ko invalid kar sakta hai, jis se pichli sari income par tax liability ban sakti hai.

Aksar Puchhe Gaye Sawal — NPO/Trust Registration

NPO registration ke liye kaunse routes available hain Pakistan mein?
Teen main routes hain: (1) SECP ke Section 42 ke tehat "company limited by guarantee" — non-profit company structure, (2) Trust Act ke tehat trust deed registration, (3) Societies Registration Act 1860 ke tehat society registration. SECP Section 42 route sabse zyada credible aur commonly used hai large NPOs ke liye.
Section 100C ka tax credit kya hai?
Section 100C ke tehat FBR-approved non-profit organizations ko unki income par 100% tax credit milta hai — yani effectively income tax zero ho jata hai, sharte ke NPO apni conditions (surplus ko charitable objects par kharch karna, proper accounts maintain karna, annual return file karna) poori kare.
FBR se NPO approval kaise milti hai?
SECP/Trust/Society registration ke baad, Commissioner Inland Revenue ko application submit ki jati hai "non-profit organization" status ke approval ke liye. Constitution/trust deed, objects, aur governance structure review hote hain. Approval milne ke baad hi Section 100C credit claim ho sakta hai.
NPO ko har saal kya compliance maintain karni hoti hai?
Annual income tax return file karna, audited financial statements maintain karna, surplus income ko organization ke charitable objects par hi kharch karna (members ko profit distribute nahi kar sakte), aur SECP/relevant authority ko annual filing submit karna zaroori hai — in mein se koi bhi miss hone par 100C credit khatam ho sakta hai.
