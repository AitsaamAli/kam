Why Individual Service Pricing Doesn't Fit Most People

A first-time filer doesn't need just an income tax return — they typically need NTN registration first, then the return, then usually a wealth statement the same year. A new small business doesn't need just SECP registration — it needs NTN, likely sales tax registration, and often EOBI registration within the same few months. Pricing each of these as a separate transaction, with a separate WhatsApp conversation and a separate payment, adds friction that doesn't reflect how compliance actually works in practice.

Kamboh Associates bundles the services people genuinely need together into fixed-price packages, both because it's a better experience and because it's priced lower than booking each service individually.

First-Time Filer Starter Package

Designed for anyone who has never filed a tax return before — a new salaried employee, a young professional, or someone who has simply never gotten around to it — this package covers the complete first-time filing journey in one transaction.

What's included: NTN registration, first-year income tax return filing, wealth statement preparation, and Active Taxpayer List (ATL) confirmation once your name appears — everything a first-time filer needs to go from zero tax history to fully compliant, active filer status.

Business Starter Package

For anyone launching a new small business — a shop, a services company, a small trading operation — this package bundles the formation-stage compliance requirements that would otherwise be four or five separate conversations.

What's included: NTN registration for the business, SECP company registration (or guidance on sole-proprietorship/AOP structure if that fits better), sales tax registration if your turnover requires it, and your first business income tax return. Businesses that need EOBI registration for employees can add it to the same package rather than starting a separate process later.

Annual Compliance Package (Existing Filers & Businesses)

For individuals and businesses that are already registered and filing, but want ongoing compliance handled as one relationship rather than repeated one-off engagements each time a deadline approaches.

What's included: Annual income tax return, wealth statement update, sales tax monthly filing (for businesses), and priority WhatsApp access for any FBR notice that comes up during the year — all covered under one annual fee rather than billed per incident.

Overseas Pakistani Compliance Package

Bundled specifically for overseas Pakistanis who need NTN registration alongside wealth statement preparation for Pakistan-based property or assets, since these two needs almost always come together for this client group.

What's included: NTN registration, wealth statement covering Pakistan-based property/assets, and property transfer tax compliance support if a purchase or sale is involved — priced and delivered as one remote engagement rather than three separate ones.

Freelancer Compliance Package

Built specifically for Pakistan's growing freelancer population — people earning through Upwork, Fiverr, or direct foreign clients — who typically need NTN registration, PSEB eligibility review, and correctly structured filing all at once rather than discovering the PSEB angle only after several years of standard-rate filing.

What's included: NTN registration, PSEB eligibility assessment and registration if the freelancer's work qualifies, and the first year's income tax return filed with export-income treatment applied correctly from the start. Freelancers who only need standard filing without PSEB (because their work doesn't qualify as IT export) still benefit from the bundled NTN-plus-return pricing rather than booking each separately.

Common situations: Kamboh Associates builds a custom-priced bundle for these mixed situations rather than forcing a client to book unrelated services separately just because no pre-set package fits.
