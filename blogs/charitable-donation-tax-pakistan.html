Charitable Donation Tax Deduction Pakistan 2026 — Section 61 Guide

June 24, 2026Aitsaam Ali6 min readAll Taxpayers

Approved charities ko diya gaya donation sirf sawab ka kaam nahi — Pakistan mein ye legally tax-deductible bhi hai. Section 61 ke tehat FBR-approved non-profit organizations ko donation dekar aap apni taxable income kam kar saktay hain. Ye guide eligibility, limits, aur documentation cover karta hai.

TL;DR

FBR-approved NPOs ko donation par up to 30% of taxable income deduction milti hai (Section 61). Zakat official channels se alag se deductible hai (Section 60). Official receipt aur bank proof zaroori hai claim ke liye.

Section 61 Deduction Kaise Kaam Karti Hai

AspectDetail
Eligible RecipientFBR-approved Non-Profit Organizations (NPO status)
Deduction LimitUp to 30% of taxable income
Deduction TypeDirect reduction from taxable income (not tax credit)
Payment MethodBanking channel strongly recommended (cash proof mushkil)

Kaunsi Organizations Eligible Hain

Sirf wo organizations jinke paas FBR se official "approved" NPO status hai, unko diya gaya donation deductible hota hai. Ye status verify karna zaroori hai — bohot si genuine-looking charities is approval ke bagair hoti hain, jinko donation dena ache neeyat se hota hai lekin tax deduction nahi milti.

Verify karna zaroori hai: Donation karne se pehle organization se unki FBR approval reference ya certificate mangein. Sahi documentation ke bagair deduction claim FBR audit mein disallow ho sakti hai.

Zakat Ka Separate Treatment

Zakat jo official banking channel (bank ka automatic Zakat deduction) se pay hoti hai, wo Section 60 ke tehat directly tax se deductible hoti hai — ye Section 61 ke charitable donation se alag provision hai. Dono benefits ek dusre ko exclude nahi karte — agar aap ne dono kiye hain toh dono claim ho saktay hain.

Documentation Requirements

  • NPO se official donation receipt — unka NTN aur approval reference sath
  • Bank transfer ya cheque ka proof (cash donations verify karna mushkil hota hai)
  • Return filing ke waqt IRIS ke "Deductions" section mein amount enter karo

Yaad rakhein: 30% limit se zyada donation kiya toh excess amount deduct nahi hogi — carry-forward bhi nahi hoti generally. Apni annual giving planning karte waqt ye limit dhyaan mein rakhein.

Aksar Puchhe Gaye Sawal — Charitable Donation Tax

Charitable donation par tax deduction kaise milti hai Pakistan mein?
Section 61 ke tehat, FBR-approved non-profit organizations ko diye gaye donations par tax deduction milti hai — taxable income mein se donation amount minus ho jata hai, jo up to 30% of taxable income tak allowed hai.
Kya har NGO ko donation deductible hai?
Nahi — sirf FBR se "approved" non-profit organizations (NPO status ke sath) ko diya gaya donation deductible hai. Donation dene se pehle organization ki approval status verify karo, warna deduction disallow ho sakti hai.
Zakat ka tax treatment kya hai?
Zakat jo official banking channels (Zakat Fund deduction) se pay ki jati hai, wo directly tax se deductible hoti hai — Section 60 ke separate provision ke tehat. Zakat aur charitable donation dono alag-alag claim ho saktay hain.
Donation deduction claim karne ke liye kya document chahiye?
NPO ki taraf se diya gaya official donation receipt (organization ka NTN aur approval reference ke sath), aur bank transfer/cheque ka proof — cash donations ke liye documentation zyada mushkil hoti hai verify karna.
