Mid-Year Mein Job Chhori Aur Business Shuru Kiya — Us Saal Ka Return Kaise Banega

September 2026 Kamboh Associates 11 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Tax Consultant | NTN & Compliance Specialist

Job chhor kar apna business shuru karna ek bara faisla hai, aur is faisle ka asar aapke us saal ke tax return par bhi parta hai. Aadhe saal ki salary aik jagah se, aur baqi aadhe saal ki business income doosri jagah se — dono ko ek hi return mein sahi tarah jama karna zaroori hai. Jo log yeh sochte hain ke "job wala hissa to already tax kat gaya, ab bas business wala dikha dena hai," woh aksar galat calculation kar baithte hain. Is guide mein hum poora practical process dekhenge.

TL;DR

Job se business mein switch karne wale saal mein aapki income do alag heads mein aati hai — salary aur business — aur return dono ko combine kar ke total taxable income banata hai. Job ki salary par jo tax kat gaya woh credit hai, dobara tax nahi lagta. Lekin business shuru hone ke sath advance tax ka apna alag calendar shuru ho jata hai, jo salary ke monthly withholding se bilkul mukhtalif hai. Exact rates FBR ke maujooda rate card se ya humse confirm karein.

Ek Saal Mein Do Income Heads Kaise Kaam Karte Hain

Pakistan ka tax return system income ko heads mein taqseem karta hai — salary, business, property, capital gains aur others. Jab aap saal ke darmiyan job chhor kar business shuru karte hain, to aapki income do heads mein bant jati hai: pehle kuch mahine salary head mein, aur baqi mahine business head mein. Return ek hi hota hai — aap do alag return nahi bharte — lekin us ek return ke andar dono heads ki entry alag hoti hai aur har head ka apna hisaab hota hai.

Yeh samajhna zaroori hai kyunke bohat se log yeh sochte hain ke jab tak woh "poora saal job mein nahi the," unka case ghair-mamuli ho jata hai. Haqeeqat mein FBR ka system is scenario ko routinely handle karta hai — bas dono heads ki entries sahi honi chahiyen, aur unka combined total tax slab ke mutabiq lagna chahiye.

Practical misaal: Ek sahab ne July se October tak job ki, phir November mein apna consultancy business shuru kar diya. Unka return July-October ki salary ko "Salary" section mein, aur November-June ki business income ko "Business" section mein dikhayega — dono mil kar unki total taxable income banayenge.

Job Wale Mahinon Ki Salary — Kya Karna Hai

Jitne mahine aap job mein rahe, us arse ki salary "Salary" head mein wohi tarah jayegi jaise poora saal job karne wale ki jati hai. Employer se aakhri salary certificate zaroor len — yeh certificate us pura arse ki gross salary aur us par kaati gayi tax ki tafseel deta hai.

Yeh katouti "advance tax" ki soorat mein already FBR ko jama ho chuki hai — is liye return mein isay "Adjustable Tax" section mein credit ke tor par dikhaya jata hai. Aapko dobara us par tax nahi dena, bas usay sahi jagah likhna hai taake final calculation mein woh minus ho.

Employer se final salary certificate/statement lein jismein exit date tak ki tafseel ho.
Agar kisi provident fund ya gratuity ka payout mila, uska treatment alag section mein hoga (neeche dekhein).
Agar employer ne exit ke waqt leave encashment diya, woh bhi salary income mein shamil hota hai.

Business Shuru Hone Ke Baad — Kya Badal Jata Hai

Job chhorne ke din se business ki income "Business" head mein aati hai, aur yahan calculation ka tareeqa salary se mukhtalif hai. Salary mein tax withholding hoti hai — employer kaat kar jama karwata hai. Business mein aap khud apni income aur allowable expenses ka record rakhte hain, aur net profit par tax lagta hai.

Iska matlab yeh hai ke aapko ab records maintain karne parenge — kitni sale hui, kitna kharcha aaya, kya inventory hai (agar applicable ho), aur bank statement kis tarah business activity ko reflect karta hai. Jitna acha record shuru se rakha jaye, saal ke aakhir mein return banana utna aasan hota hai.

Advance Tax Regime Mein Shift Kab Hota Hai

Yeh woh hissa hai jo naye business owners ko aksar surprise karta hai. Job mein hote hue advance tax ka bojh employer uthata hai — aapko kuch alag se karne ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Lekin jaise hi aap business shuru karte hain, Section 147 ke tehat quarterly advance tax ka calendar aap par bhi lag sakta hai, aapki estimated income ke mutabiq.

Practically, agar aapne saal ke darmiyan business shuru kiya hai, to pehle saal advance tax ka daira thoda kam sakht hota hai kyunke koi "pichla saal ka reference" nahi hota. Lekin yeh mat sochein ke pehla saal poori tarah advance-tax-free hai — jitni jaldi business ki income clear hone lagti hai, utni jaldi advance tax ka assessment shuru ho sakta hai. Exact threshold aur calculation FBR ke current rules ke mutabiq hoti hai, is liye apne specific case ke liye humse ya FBR se confirm karein.

Khabardar: "Mera to sirf pehla saal hai" sochte hue advance tax bilkul ignore na karein. Agar business income tezi se barh rahi hai, to saal ke aakhir mein ek bara tax bill aur us par jurmana dono ka samna ho sakta hai.

NTN Update Aur STRN Ki Zaroorat

Agar aapka NTN pehle se job ki wajah se bana hua hai, to woh khatam nahi hota — bas ab usay business profile ke sath update karna hota hai IRIS mein, taake aapka business activity type aur address record ho jaye. Agar aap kisi aisi service ya product mein hain jahan sales tax lagta hai, to STRN (Sales Tax Registration Number) ki zaroorat bhi assess honi chahiye — yeh income tax NTN se bilkul alag registration hai.

IRIS profile mein business activity/address update karayein.
Assess karein ke aapki services/products sales-tax-able hain ya nahi.
Agar STRN chahiye to woh alag se register karayein — NTN hone se automatically STRN nahi ban jata.

Gratuity Ya Provident Fund Payout Ka Treatment

Job chhorte waqt agar aapko gratuity ya provident fund ka payout mila hai, to yeh ek alag treatment ka topic hai — kuch hissa exempt ho sakta hai, kuch taxable, aur yeh aapke employment ki muddat aur fund ke type par depend karta hai. Yeh raqam aksar naye business mein initial capital ke tor par lagai jati hai, is liye wealth statement mein iska record rakhna zaroori hai taake baad mein "yeh paisa kahan se aaya" ka sawal na bane.

Agar aapka case is tarah ka hai, to gratuity/provident fund ki taxation ko is business-switch return se pehle alag se samajh lena faida-mand hota hai — yeh ek technical area hai jahan galat calculation bohat aam hai.

Personal Aur Business Bank Account Alag Rakhna

Yeh choti si aadat bohat bara farq banati hai. Agar aap apni job ki salary aur naye business ki income ek hi personal account mein mix karte hain, to saal ke aakhir mein yeh alag karna mushkil ho jata hai ke kaunsa paisa kis head se aaya. Business ke liye alag account khulwana — chahe woh sole proprietor ke naam par hi ho — record-keeping ko kaafi aasan bana deta hai aur reconciliation mein waqt bachata hai.

Wealth Statement Mein Transition Kaise Dikhayein

Wealth statement mein saal ke shuru aur aakhir ki position dikhani hoti hai. Job-se-business switch wale saal mein yeh thora zyada dhyan talab hota hai kyunke:

Job se mile final dues (gratuity, leave encashment) ko inflow ke tor par dikhana hoga.
Agar business mein capital lagayi hai, to woh asset/investment ke tor par aani chahiye.
Business ke shuruati kharche (rent, setup, inventory) reconciliation mein reflect hone chahiyen.

Agar yeh sab tally nahi karta — yani aapki assets mein izafa hua lekin declared income us izafe ko justify nahi karti — to system red flag utha sakta hai. Isi liye is transition wale saal ka wealth statement normal saal se zyada ehtiyaat se banana chahiye.

Common Ghaltiyan

Sirf business income dikhana, job ke mahinon ki salary chhor dena: employer ka record already FBR ke paas hai, mismatch turant nazar aata hai.
Advance tax bilkul plan na karna: "pehla saal hai" sochte hue business income tax ke liye set aside na karna.
Personal aur business kharche mix karna: reconciliation todta hai aur record-keeping mushkil banata hai.
Gratuity/provident fund ko galat sheet mein likhna: exempt aur taxable hissa alag hona chahiye.
STRN ki zaroorat ko ignore karna: agar services/products sales-tax-able hain to yeh separate registration miss karna aage chal kar penalty bulata hai.

Frequently Asked Questions

Job aur business ki income ek hi return mein jayegi ya alag?
Ek hi return mein jayegi. Return ek hota hai lekin usme salary aur business income ke liye alag alag sections hote hain — dono mil kar aapki total taxable income banate hain.
Job ki salary par jo tax kat gaya, kya woh business income par dobara lagega?
Nahi. Salary par kaati gayi tax aapka credit hai jo already jama ho chuka hai. Return mein isay adjustable tax ke tor par dikhaya jata hai taake dobara tax na lage.
Pehle hi saal business ke liye advance tax dena parta hai?
Ho sakta hai, aapki income ke level par depend karta hai. Naye business ke pehle saal mein koi "pichla reference" nahi hota, lekin agar income tezi se banti hai to advance tax ka assessment shuru ho sakta hai. Apna specific case confirm karayein.
Mera NTN job ke zamane se pehle se hai — kya naya banwana parega?
Nahi, wohi NTN chalta hai. Bas IRIS profile mein business detail update karani hoti hai. Agar sales tax applicable ho to STRN alag se register karana hoga.
Gratuity ka paisa business mein laga diya — kya masla ban sakta hai?
Masla nahi bante ga agar sahi tarah document ho. Gratuity/provident fund payout ko wealth statement mein inflow ke tor par dikhayein aur business mein lagi hui raqam ko investment/capital ke tor par — is se reconciliation match rahegi.
