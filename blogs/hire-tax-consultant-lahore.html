If you're hiring for anything beyond a basic salaried return — company registration, an FBR notice response, sales tax registration — ask specifically whether the consultant has handled that exact service before, since general tax knowledge doesn't automatically transfer to every specialized filing type. Red Flags Worth Walking Away From

A handful of patterns show up repeatedly in complaints about bad tax-consultant experiences in Lahore, and they're worth recognizing before you're already committed.

Common warning signs: A consultant who pressures you to pay the full fee immediately before doing any work or confirming your specific requirement, rather than confirming scope and price first. Refusal to provide any written confirmation of the service and fee agreed. Communication that goes silent for days once payment is received, with no update on progress. None of these are automatically disqualifying on their own, but more than one appearing together is a strong signal to look elsewhere.

What to Check Changes Depending on What You're Hiring For

The due diligence that matters most shifts depending on the specific service — hiring for a straightforward salaried return carries different stakes than hiring someone to represent you in an FBR audit or handle a company's ongoing corporate compliance.

For a first-time filer: the priority is finding someone who explains the process clearly and doesn't rush you through documentation you don't understand, since this sets the pattern for how you'll approach filing every year afterward. For a business owner registering a company: the priority shifts to whether the consultant genuinely understands SECP's requirements for your specific business type, not just FBR's individual filing process — these are related but distinct areas of expertise, and a consultant strong in one isn't automatically strong in the other. For anyone responding to an FBR notice: the priority becomes speed of response and demonstrated experience with that exact notice type, since a generic response to a specific notice section can make the situation worse rather than better. Kamboh Associates staffs each of these differently based on what the client actually needs, rather than routing every inquiry through the same generic intake process.
