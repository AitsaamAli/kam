Return Reject Ya Invalid Ho Gaya — Wajuhat Aur Dobara Submit Karne Ka Process

September 2026 Kamboh Associates 10 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Tax Consultant | NTN & Compliance Specialist

Return submit karne ke baad yeh maan lena ke kaam khatam ho gaya, hamesha sahi nahi hota. Kabhi baad mein pata chalta hai ke return "invalid" ho gaya, ya kisi wajah se reject ho gaya, aur us ka koi notification bhi shayad foran nazar na aaye. Is guide mein hum dekhenge ke aisa kyun hota hai aur ab kya karna chahiye.

TL;DR

Return reject ya invalid hone ki aam wajuhat mein malformed data, wealth statement ka na jamna, galat tax year, ya koi technical mismatch shamil hota hai. Sab se pehla qadam yeh hai ke IRIS mein return ki status confirm karein — sirf submission ka screenshot kaafi nahi, status "valid" ya "processed" dikhna chahiye. Agar invalid nikle to dobara mukammal filing karni parti hai, purani submission par bharosa nahi karna chahiye. Yeh masla khaas tor par un logon ke sath aata hai jo deadline ke aakhri lamhon mein jaldi mein filing karte hain, is liye waqt se pehle filing karna is se bachne ka sab se asaan tareeqa hai.

Yeh maamla jitni jaldi samajh liya jaye utna asaan rehta hai.

Invalid Return Ka Matlab Kya Hai

Return "submit" ho jana aur return "valid" hona do alag cheezein hain. Submit ka matlab hai ke aapka data IRIS ke server tak pohanch gaya. Valid hone ka matlab hai ke us data ne poori tarah process ho kar aapki qanooni filing ki haisiyat hasil kar li. Kabhi ek return submit to ho jata hai lekin baad mein system ki taraf se ya kisi missing element ki wajah se invalid qarar diya jata hai — is soorat mein qanooni tor par ab yeh mana jata hai ke goya return file hi nahi hua.

Ahem farq: "submitted" ka matlab yeh nahi ke aapka kaam mukammal ho gaya. Hamesha final status "valid" ya "processed" dekhein.

Reject Ya Invalid Hone Ki Aam Wajuhat

Wealth statement sath jama nahi hui — sirf return submit hui, sath wali wealth statement chhoot gayi.
Koi zaroori attachment ya document jo lazmi tha, missing raha.
Galat tax year mein submit ho gaya jab ke woh dusre saal ke liye already exist karta tha.
Koi technical validation IRIS ne baad mein cross-check kar ke fail ki, jo submit ke waqt foran nazar nahi aayi.
Payment ka challan (agar tax payable tha) sahi tarah link nahi hua.

IRIS Mein Status Kaise Check Karein

Apni return history mein jaa kar dekhein ke status kya likha hai. "Submitted" ke sath sath "valid" ya "processed" jaisa lafz hona chahiye. Agar status "pending," "invalid," ya kisi aur ambiguous soorat mein ho, to yeh alarm hai ke maamla mukammal nahi hua. Acknowledgement download kar ke bhi dekhein — agar acknowledgement download nahi ho raha ya us par koi ajeeb marking hai, to yeh bhi ek signal hai.

Agar Deadline Guzar Chuki Ho To

Sab se zyada pareshani us waqt hoti hai jab pata chalta hai ke return invalid tha aur ab deadline bhi guzar chuki hai. Aisi soorat mein ghabrane ke bajaye foran dobara filing ka process shuru karein — der se filing ka koi bojh ban sakta hai, lekin bilkul filing na karna us se kahin zyada mehnga sabit hota hai. Jitni jaldi masla pakda jaye, utni jaldi theek karna asaan hota hai.

Dobara Submit Karne Ka Process

Pehle purani submission ki asal wajah samjhein — kaun sa hissa missing ya galat tha.
Poori filing (return aur wealth statement dono) dobara mukammal taiyar karein, sirf missing hissa nahi.
Sahi tax year select karein — yeh galti bhi aam hai.
Agar payment pending hai to nayi PSID bana kar ada karein aur return se link karein.
Submit karne ke baad foran status check karein ke "valid" dikha raha hai.

Wealth Statement Ki Status Bhi Check Karein

Kai baar return valid ho jata hai lekin wealth statement ka status alag reh jata hai. Dono ka status alag alag check karna zaroori hai, kyunke ek ki success doosre ki success ki zamanat nahi deta. Wealth statement ka status bhi "submitted" se aage "processed" ya "valid" hona chahiye.

Aainda Ke Liye Bachao

Filing ke foran baad sirf submit button dabane par mutmain na hon — kam az kam agle ek do din baad wapas IRIS mein jaa kar status dobara confirm karein. Yeh ek chhoti si aadat aainda kisi bhi surprise se bacha sakti hai, kyunke kabhi kabhi processing mein thora waqt lagta hai aur status foran nahi badalta.

Agar Bank Ya Visa Office Ko Return Ka Sabot Dena Ho

Bohat baar yeh masla us waqt khulta hai jab kisi bank facility, property deal, ya visa application ke liye return ka acknowledgement maanga jata hai — aur us waqt pata chalta hai ke jo return diya gaya tha woh invalid nikla. Aisi soorat mein sirf apni filing check karna kaafi nahi, foran professional se rabta kar ke asal wajah dhoondni chahiye, kyunke deadline ki tarah is jagah bhi waqt ka dabao hota hai aur galat ya adhoori maloomat dena bank ya visa office ke saamne aur zyada mushkil khara kar sakta hai.

Kya Purane Saalon Mein Bhi Aisi Ghalti Ho Sakti Hai

Agar aapko ek saal ka invalid return mila hai, to yeh dekhna faidemand hota hai ke kahin purane saalon mein bhi aisi hi koi ghalti to nahi rahi. Khaas tor par agar aapne kabhi khud filing ki thi bina professional guidance ke, to ek baar apni poori return history dekh kar sab saalon ka status confirm kar lein. Yeh ek chhoti si kawaish aainda kisi bare masle se bacha sakti hai.

Invalid Return Aur Late Filing Mein Farq

Yeh samajhna zaroori hai ke invalid return aur late filing do alag cheezein hain. Late filing ka matlab hai ke return waqt par nahi hua lekin jab hua woh valid tha. Invalid return ka matlab hai ke jo submission hui woh qanooni tor par register hi nahi hui, chahe woh waqt par hi kyun na ki gayi ho. Dono ka treatment mukhtalif hota hai, aur is farq ko samajhna zaroori hai taake sahi rasta apnaya ja sake — kabhi sirf dobara submit karna kaafi hota hai, kabhi is se pehle koi aur qadam bhi uthana parta hai, jaise koi missing document jama karna ya koi technical masla FBR ke sath resolve karna.

Purana Aur Naya Acknowledgement — Farq Pehchanein

Jab aap dobara filing karte hain, to naya acknowledgement milta hai jo purane se alag reference number rakhta hai. Yeh farq is liye ahem hai kyunke agar kabhi dono acknowledgements mil jayein — purana (invalid) aur naya (valid) — to sirf latest wala hi kisi ko dikhana chahiye. Purana acknowledgement dikhana, chahe ghalti se hi kyun na ho, ghalat-fehmi paida kar sakta hai, kyunke woh ab qanooni tor par valid nahi.

Professional Se Filing Karwane Ka Ek Chhupa Faida

Jab filing kisi professional ke zariye hoti hai, to reject ya invalid hone ka khatra numaya kam ho jata hai, kyunke ek tajurbekar consultant IRIS ke common validation points se waqif hota hai aur submit karne se pehle hi unhein check kar leta hai. Yeh sirf sahulat ki baat nahi — deadline ke qareeb agar khud filing invalid ho jaye, to dobara mukammal karna waqt aur ghabrahat dono barhata hai, jab ke professional ke zariye yeh khatra shuru se hi kam rehta hai.

Khandan Ke Kisi Aur Fard Ka Bhi Return Check Ho

Agar aapko apna return invalid milta hai, to yeh mauqa hai ke khandan ke doosre filers (jinki filing shayad usi tareeqe se ya usi waqt hui thi) ka status bhi ek baar check kar lein. Kabhi ek hi ghalti — jaise galat internet connection ya koi mushtarka technical masla — kai logon ki filing ko ek sath mutasir kar deti hai, aur agar ek ka pata chal jaye to baqi ko bhi waqt par theek kiya ja sakta hai.

Yeh silsila khaas tor par un khandaano mein faidemand hai jahan poora ghar ek hi consultant se apna return karwata hai — ek ghalti sab ke liye ek sath check hone ka mauqa bhi ban jati hai, aur poore khandan ka record ek hi tarah durust rehta hai.

Return reject ya invalid hona ghabrane ki baat nahi, lekin nazar-andaz karne ki baat bhi nahi. Jitni jaldi status ka pata chale aur dobara mukammal filing ho jaye, utna hi kam nuqsan hota hai — deadline ke qareeb ho ya door, yeh usool hamesha sahi rehta hai.

Chahe wajah data ki ho ya server ki, chahe deadline aane wali ho ya guzar chuki ho, sahi tareeqa hamesha ek hi hai — status confirm karein, wajah samjhein, aur mukammal filing dobara karein. Adhoori ya jaldi mein ki gayi correction sirf naya masla khada karti hai.

Har case mukhtalif hota hai — kabhi sirf tax year select karna reh gaya, kabhi wealth statement sath jama hi nahi hui. Asal wajah samajhe bina dobara try karna waqt zaya karta hai, is liye pehla qadam hamesha yehi hota hai ke wajah pukhta tor par pehchani jaye.

Agar ek se zyada wajuhat ek sath mil jayein — jaise wealth statement bhi missing thi aur tax year bhi galat tha — to sab ko ek sath address karein, na ke ek theek kar ke doosri ko chhor dein. Adhoora fix sirf agle round ki masla-yaabi ko taal deta hai, khatam nahi karta.

Frequently Asked Questions

Return submit ho gaya tha, ab pata chala invalid hai — kya iska matlab file hi nahi hua?
Qanooni tor par ji haan, agar return invalid qarar diya gaya hai to yeh mana jata hai ke goya woh file hi nahi hua. Zaroori hai ke dobara mukammal filing ki jaye.
Return valid hui ya nahi, kaise pata chale?
IRIS mein apni return history dekhein — sirf "submitted" kaafi nahi, status "valid" ya "processed" dikhna chahiye. Acknowledgement bhi download kar ke confirm karein.
Deadline guzar chuki hai aur return invalid nikla — ab kya karoon?
Foran dobara filing ka process shuru karein. Der se filing ka kuch bojh ban sakta hai, lekin bilkul file na karna us se kahin zyada mehnga sabit hota hai. Jitni jaldi pakda jaye, utna asaan hai.
Return valid ho gaya lekin wealth statement ka status pata nahi — kya dono check karna zaroori hai?
Ji haan. Dono ka status alag alag check karna zaroori hai kyunke ek ki success doosre ki zamanat nahi deti. Wealth statement ka bhi "valid" ya "processed" status dekhein.
Reject hone ki sab se aam wajah kya hai?
Sath wali wealth statement na jamna, koi zaroori attachment missing rehna, ya galat tax year mein submit ho jana sab se aam wajuhat hain. Payment ka challan sahi link na hona bhi ek wajah ho sakti hai.
Submit karne ke baad kitni der mein status confirm hona chahiye?
Aam tor par foran ya kuch waqt mein status update ho jata hai, lekin behtar hai ke ek do din baad wapas jaa kar status dobara confirm karein, taake koi surprise na ho.
