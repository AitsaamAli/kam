For textile exporters: Once your NTN is issued, you can apply for Sales Tax Registration (STRN) to access zero-rating on export supplies and claim input tax refunds — a major benefit for Faisalabad's export-focused units.

Why Faisalabad Traders Need Filer Status

With frequent banking transactions for yarn and fabric trade, non-filer status means double withholding tax on banking instruments and cash withdrawals. Filer status, secured through NTN registration, immediately reduces this burden for active traders.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can I get my NTN in Faisalabad?
Individual NTN registration is typically issued the same day. Business and AOP/partnership NTN registration for textile units usually takes 1-3 business days depending on documentation.
Do textile traders in Faisalabad need a separate sales tax registration?
Yes, in addition to NTN, textile manufacturers and traders dealing in taxable supplies should register for Sales Tax (STRN) with FBR, and may need PRA registration if providing any taxable services.
Can I register NTN for my Faisalabad business without visiting an FBR office?
Yes, the entire NTN registration process is completed online via the IRIS portal. We handle documentation and submission remotely for clients across Faisalabad.
