Bahria Sector F Lahore — Doctors With a Hospital Job and a Private Practice

Updated October 2026

3 min read
Bahria Sector F

Many doctors in and around Bahria divide their time between a salaried hospital post and evening practice in a private clinic. That produces two kinds of income — salary and professional fees — which are taxed differently and are frequently filed as if they were one.

Salary and practice income are separate heads

  • Hospital salary: taxed as salary; the hospital deducts tax monthly and issues an annual certificate.
  • Private practice fees: business (professional) income; you report fees received minus clinic expenses.
  • Fees from hospitals as a visiting consultant: often paid to you as a professional, with tax withheld by the hospital.

Each has its own section in the return. Mixing them — for example, adding clinic fees to salary — can produce the wrong tax and makes your records look odd. Our page on tax filing for doctors covers the profession in more depth.

Consulting at multiple hospitals

Doctors who visit several private hospitals usually receive fees from each, with tax deducted at source. Collect each hospital's withholding certificate. Those deductions are credited against your total liability; missing one means paying tax twice on the same income.

Cash at the clinic

Many patients pay in cash. Keep a daily patient register with fees — even a simple notebook — and deposit regularly. This is what lets you show practice income confidently instead of estimating it at year end.

Doctors often buy equipment in one large payment and expense it all in one year. Major equipment is a capital asset and should be depreciated, not expensed at once. Doing it right avoids a query and spreads the benefit.

Wealth statement for a doctor's household

Two incomes usually fund a house, cars and children's education. Make sure the wealth statement uses both salary and practice profit to explain asset growth. If your spouse is also a doctor, see our two-earner couples page.

Locum, online consultations and teaching

Doctors increasingly earn from sources beyond the clinic: locum shifts, paid online consultations through apps, teaching at medical colleges, and pharmaceutical-sponsored talks. Each of these is income. Online platforms and colleges often deduct tax at source and can issue certificates. Fees from abroad for telemedicine or second opinions may have their own treatment. List every source in July and collect the matching certificate, so nothing is filed twice or left out.

Bahria Sector F — questions we get asked

Do I file my hospital salary and clinic income together?
In the same return, but under separate heads — salary and business/professional income. Each is calculated on its own rules.
Can I claim my ultrasound machine as an expense?
Major equipment is a capital asset and is depreciated over several years rather than expensed in one year.
Private hospitals deduct tax from my consultation fees. How do I claim it?
Collect each hospital's withholding certificate. The deductions are credited against your total tax liability in the return.
Most patients pay in cash. How do I show income?
Keep a daily register of patients and fees and deposit regularly. Your return then shows recorded fees minus documented clinic expenses.
