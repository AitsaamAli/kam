Italy Se Pakistan Paisa Bhejna Aur Property Khareedna — Tax Guide (2026)

28 August 2026Aitsaam Ali12 min read2026 Verified

Italy jaane ka maqsad koi visa nahi hota — maqsad paisa hota hai. Ghar bhejna, behan ki shaadi, bhai ka karobar, aur aakhir mein apna ek tukra zameen ya makan. Yeh guide theek isi hisse ke baare mein hai: paisa Pakistan kaise aata hai, uska record kaise banta hai, aur woh record na hone par kya hota hai jab aap us paise se property khareedne jate hain.

TL;DR:

  • Non-resident ki foreign income aam tor par Pakistan mein chargeable nahi hoti — is liye Italy ki kamai par Pakistan mein dobara tax ka sawal aam tor par nahi uthta.
  • Asal masla tax ka nahi, record ka hai: paisa hamesha qanooni banking channel se bhejein.
  • Section 111 (unexplained income) ke tehat FBR aisi bari transactions par sawal kar sakti hai jinka koi declared source na ho.
  • Property khareedte waqt filer par 1.25% withholding lagti hai aur non-filer par 2.5%. Bechte waqt 2.75% ke muqable 5.5%.
  • Ghar walon ke naam property khareedna aam hai — lekin us se aapka apna record nahi banta, aur baad mein masla ban sakta hai.

Asal maqsad yehi hai

Baqi tamam guides — quota, permit, residency status — us safar ke baare mein hain jo aapko Italy le jata hai. Yeh guide us cheez ke baare mein hai jis ke liye aap gaye thay.

Aur is mein ek cheez qabil-e-ghaur hai. Zyada tar Pakistani workers jo Italy jate hain, woh apna paisa bilkul theek tareeqe se kamate hain aur bilkul theek tareeqe se bhejte hain. Masla be-eemani ka nahi hota. Masla yeh hota hai ke saalon ka paisa aata rehta hai aur uska koi jama shuda record nahi banta — aur phir ek din jab us paise ko zameen ya makan mein badalna hota hai, tab pehli dafa yeh sawal uthta hai ke yeh raqam kahan se aayi.

Kya bheja hua paisa taxable hai?

Yeh sab se zyada poocha jane wala sawal hai, aur is ka jawab aam tor par tasalli-bakhsh hai.

Aam usool yeh hai ke non-resident ki foreign income Pakistan mein chargeable nahi hoti. Aap Italy mein kamate hain, wahan ke qawaid ke mutabiq tax dete hain, aur jo paisa aap ghar bhejte hain use Pakistan mein kamai gayi income ki tarah nahi dekha jata. Sath hi Pakistan aur Italy ke darmiyan double taxation agreement bhi maujood hai — Pakistan ke aise 60 se zyada mulkon ke sath treaties hain — jinka maqsad hi yeh hai ke ek hi income par dono jagah poora tax na lage.

To phir ehtiyat kis baat ki? Ehtiyat tax ki nahi, saboot ki hai. Yeh keh dena ke "yeh paisa main ne Italy mein kama kar bheja tha" kaafi nahi hota — us baat ke peeche kagaz hona chahiye. Aur woh kagaz us waqt banta hai jab aap paisa bhejte hain, us waqt nahi jab aap property khareedte hain.

Channel ka faisla sab se ahem hai

Italy mein Pakistani workers ke paas paisa bhejne ke kai rastay hote hain. Bank transfer, licensed money transfer services, ya woh ghair-rasmi intezam jo har community mein maujood hota hai — koi jaan pehchan ka aadmi jo Pakistan mein aapke ghar paisa pohancha deta hai.

Ghair-rasmi rasta aksar sasta aur tez lagta hai. Uski asal qeemat saalon baad zahir hoti hai.

Yeh farq das saal ke arse mein bohat bara ho jata hai. Jo shakhs har mahine bank se paisa bhejta raha, uske paas das saal ka ek mukammal record hota hai jo khud ba khud yeh keh raha hota hai ke uske paas paisa kahan se aaya. Jo ghair-rasmi tareeqe se bhejta raha, uske paas kuch bhi nahi hota — halanke usne bilkul wohi mehnat ki thi.

Record kaise banta hai

Achhi khabar yeh hai ke record banane ke liye kuch khaas karna nahi parta. Bas teen aadatein kaafi hain:

Hamesha ek hi Pakistani account mein bhejein. Alag alag accounts mein bikhra hua paisa jama karna baad mein mushkil hota hai. Ek account — aam tor par aapka apna — sab kuch aasan kar deta hai.
Bank ki receipts sambhal kar rakhein. Aaj kal yeh app par hi mil jati hain. Ek folder bana lein.
Saalana return file karte rahein. Yehi wo cheez hai jo saal dar saal aapke record ko rasmi shakal deti hai. Return Rs. 3,500 se 5,000 mein Italy se hi ho jati hai.

Teesri aadat sab se ahem hai aur sab se zyada nazar-andaz hoti hai. Bank statement dikhata hai ke paisa aaya. Return aur wealth statement dikhati hai ke aapne usay declare kiya. Dono ka farq bara hai — pehla ek transaction ka saboot hai, dusra aapke poore maali wujood ka.

Property khareedna — yahan sab kuch jama hota hai

Yeh wo lamha hai jiske liye poora safar tha. Aur yehi wo lamha hai jab pichhle saalon ki tamam laparwahiyan ek sath saamne aati hain.

Jab aap Pakistan mein property khareedte hain to teen cheezein hoti hain. Pehla, purchase par withholding tax lagti hai — Section 236K ke tehat filer ke liye 1.25% aur non-filer ke liye 2.5%. Dusra, aapke naam ek naya bara asset aa jata hai jo aapke wealth statement mein nazar aana chahiye. Teesra, agar aapki declared income aur is khareed ke darmiyan bara faasla hai, to us faasle ki wazahat honi chahiye.

Teesri cheez hi asal masla hai. Ek shakhs jiska Pakistan mein koi declared record nahi, aur jo achanak 60 lakh ka plot khareed leta hai, ek aisi soorat paida karta hai jismein sawal uthna qudrati hai. Yeh be-eemani ka ilzam nahi — yeh sirf yeh haqeeqat hai ke kagaz par kuch bhi nahi likha.

Aur ulta bhi utna hi sach hai: ek shakhs jiske paas das saal ki bank remittances hain, saalana returns hain, aur wealth statement mein har saal barhta hua balance nazar aata hai — uske liye 60 lakh ka plot bilkul mantaqi agla qadam lagta hai. Wohi paisa, wohi mehnat, magar bilkul mukhtalif soorat-e-haal.

Section 111 ko samajhna

Section 111 (unexplained income) wo qanooni ikhtiyar hai jiske tehat FBR aisi transactions par sawal kar sakti hai jinka koi declared source na ho. Yeh khaas tor par overseas workers ke context mein ahem hai, kyunki unke case mein bare amounts aana normal hai — aur normal hone ke bawajood, agar record nahi hai to woh explain nahi kiye ja sakte.

Is se bachne ka tareeqa mushkil nahi:

Paisa formal channel se aaye. Bank ka record khud apni jagah ek jawab hai.
Aapki filing chalti rahe. Har saal ek return, chahe income kam ho.
Wealth statement update hoti rahe. Khaas tor par un saalon mein jab koi bara asset khareeda ho.
Property aapke apne naam par ho, agar aap hi uske asal maalik hain.

Yeh chaar cheezein saal mein ek dafa, chand hazar rupay ke kharche mein ho jati hain — aur yehi wo farq hai jo baad mein ek notice aur ek routine kaam ke darmiyan hota hai.

Filer banne ki asal bachat — hisaab ke sath

Ab yeh dekhtay hain ke filer hona kitne ka faida hai. Yeh baat asool ki nahi, hisaab ki hai.

TransactionFilerNon-filer
Property purchase — Section 236K1.25%2.5%
Property sale — Section 236C2.75%5.5%
Capital gains on property15% se 0%, holding period ke sath (6 saal se zyada par exempt)Filer rate ka double

Aik amali misaal. Farz karein aap ne Italy mein aath saal kaam kar ke 80 lakh rupay ka plot khareeda. Filer ke tor par purchase par Section 236K ki withholding 1 lakh banti hai. Non-filer ke tor par 2 lakh. Farq: 1 lakh rupay — sirf khareedne par.

Ab farz karein paanch saal baad aap use 1 crore 20 lakh mein bechte hain. Filer par Section 236C ki withholding 3 lakh 30 hazar banti hai. Non-filer par 6 lakh 60 hazar. Farq: 3 lakh 30 hazar.

Yani sirf in do transactions par filer aur non-filer ka farq tagreeban 4 lakh 30 hazar rupay hai. Aur is poore arse mein saalana return file karte rehne ki cost? Terah saal × Rs. 5,000 = tagreeban 65,000 rupay. Bachat kharche se chhe guna se bhi zyada hai — aur ismein capital gains ka farq shamil hi nahi.

Apna ATL status kabhi bhi check kar sakte hain: 9966 par SMS bhejein, format ATL <13-digit CNIC>. Yeh Italy se bhi apne Pakistani number par ho sakta hai.

Ghar walon ke naam khareedna

Yeh Italy ke Pakistani workers mein bohat aam hai, aur iske peeche wajahat bhi mantaqi hoti hain — aap bahar hain, koi yahan hona chahiye jo kaam dekhe, aur walid ya bhai par bharosa hai.

Lekin do baatein saaf honi chahiyen. Pehla: agar property kisi aur ke naam hai to aapka apna record us se nahi banta. Aapki wealth statement mein woh asset nazar nahi aayega, aur aapki maali position kagaz par wahi rahegi jo pehle thi. Dusra: qanooni maalik wohi hota hai jiska naam kagaz par hai. Ghar ka bharosa apni jagah, lekin virasat, ikhtilaf ya kisi hadsay ki soorat mein kagaz hi chalta hai.

Iska matlab yeh nahi ke aisa karna hi nahi chahiye. Iska matlab yeh hai ke yeh faisla soch samajh kar kiya jaye, mahaz suhulat ki bina par nahi — aur agar aap khud maalik hain to property apne naam par ho, aur agar kisi ko aapki taraf se kaam karna hai to uske liye baaqaida authority ka intezam ho.

Remittance, Property Aur Tax — Aam Sawalat

Italy se Pakistan bheja gaya paisa taxable hai?
Aam usool yeh hai ke non-resident ki foreign income Pakistan mein chargeable nahi hoti, aur qanooni banking channel se aane wali remittance ko Pakistan mein kamai gayi income ki tarah nahi dekha jata. Sath hi Pakistan aur Italy ke darmiyan double taxation agreement bhi maujood hai. Asal ehtiyat tax ki nahi, record ki hai — paisa hamesha formal channel se bhejein aur uski receipts sambhal kar rakhein.
Section 111 kya hai aur mujhe is se kya khatra hai?
Section 111 (unexplained income) wo qanooni ikhtiyar hai jiske tehat FBR aisi bari transactions par sawal kar sakti hai jinka koi declared source na ho. Overseas workers ke liye yeh is liye ahem hai ke unke case mein bari raqmein aana normal hai — lekin agar remittance ghair-rasmi channel se aayi ho aur koi filing bhi na ho, to us paise ka koi documentary sahara nahi hota, chahe woh bilkul jaiz kamai ho.
Property khareedte waqt filer aur non-filer mein kitna farq parta hai?
Purchase par Section 236K ke tehat filer ko 1.25% withholding lagti hai aur non-filer ko 2.5%. Bechte waqt Section 236C ke tehat filer ko 2.75% aur non-filer ko 5.5%. 80 lakh ka plot khareed kar 1 crore 20 lakh mein bechne ki soorat mein sirf in do transactions ka farq tagreeban 4 lakh 30 hazar rupay banta hai — jabke saalana return ki cost Rs. 3,500 se 5,000 hai.
Kya main property apne walid ke naam khareed loon?
Aisa kiya ja sakta hai aur bohat se overseas workers karte hain, lekin do baatein samajh lein. Pehla, agar property kisi aur ke naam hai to aapka apna maali record us se nahi banta — aapki wealth statement mein woh asset nazar nahi aayega. Dusra, qanooni maalik wohi hota hai jiska naam kagaz par hai, aur virasat ya ikhtilaf ki soorat mein kagaz hi chalta hai. Agar aap khud maalik hain to property apne naam par honi chahiye.
Main Italy mein hoon — kya filer banne ke liye Pakistan aana paregi?
Nahi. NTN registration, saalana return, wealth statement aur FBR notice ka jawab — sab kuch remotely ho jata hai, kyunki Pakistan ka poora tax system online hai. Kamboh Associates ke overseas clients ka kaam WhatsApp par hota hai aur unmein se kaafi kabhi daftar aaye hi nahi. Sirf yeh yaad rakhein ke filer banna ek din ka kaam nahi — property ke registry waale din tak intezar na karein.
