Changing, Suspending or Cancelling a PRA Registration: Sections 28-29 and Rules 5-14

21 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 11 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 21 November 2026
A PRA registration does not end when you stop working. It ends when PRA de-registers you.

Registration is the start of a record, not a one-time event. Businesses move, rename, add branches, get sold or close, and each of those has its own rule. This guide puts them in one place, with the timelines the Act and rules set.

Short answer

Change of name, address or other particulars: Form PST-01, decided preferably within thirty days (rule 11). Transfer between PRA offices on application or by PRA (rule 12). Suspension: PRA may temporarily inactivate a registration, must give notice with reasons, may then suspend by removing the name from its list, and may withdraw the suspension within sixty days (section 28). De-registration: apply on Form PST-02 when you stop providing taxable services (rule 5); PRA decides within three months, extendable by up to sixty days (section 29(4)). Both are free (rule 15).

Changing Your Particulars

Short answer: Report changes on Form PST-01 under rule 11; PRA approves or rejects, preferably within thirty days.

Rule 11 applies to "a change in the name, address or other particulars as stated in the registration certificate". You intimate the proposed change on Form PST-01, and PRA "may either approve or reject the request for change preferably within thirty days of the receipt of such intimation". Common changes:

  • A new business address, or a new branch to add
  • A new service to add to the certificate (rule 2(3) lets one certificate list all services)
  • A new bank account, or one closed
  • A new email or mobile; Form PST-01's declaration accepts notices sent to them as legal notice

Transferring to Another PRA Office

Short answer: Shifting to another PRA office's area: apply for transfer with documents (rule 12(2)); PRA can also transfer on its own motion.

Rule 12 covers jurisdiction. If you shift your business activity from one PRA office's area to another, or have another valid reason, you apply for transfer "along with necessary documents, if any", and PRA decides after verification (rule 12(2)). PRA can also transfer a registration on its own motion, with notice to you (rule 12(3)).

Inactivation and Suspension: Section 28

Short answer: Notice first, then suspension by removing the name from the list; PRA may withdraw it within sixty days (section 28).

Rule 6 adds a separate route: for tax fraud, deliberate non-payment or short payment, or evasion, PRA may suspend and, after inquiry and a hearing, cancel. Section 28(7) and rule 9 make the same point: cancellation or suspension does not end your liabilities. Rule 8 says invoices issued during a suspension do not give tax credit, adjustment or refund, which your customers will notice. Check your status any time with the Taxpayer Profile Inquiry.

If a cancellation was based on a misunderstanding of the facts, rule 7 lets PRA revive it, on conditions and from a date it specifies.

De-registration: Section 29 and Rule 5

Short answer: Apply on Form PST-02 when you stop taxable services (rule 5); decided within three months, extendable by sixty days (section 29(4)).

Rule 5: every registered person "who ceases to provide taxable service shall apply" for cancellation on Form PST-02. After any audit or enquiry, PRA de-registers from the date it specifies or the date outstanding tax is deposited, whichever is later. Form PST-02 lists the grounds:

  • Ceased to carry on business
  • The service has become exempt
  • Taxable turnover in the last twelve months has stayed below the threshold (with the value and reasons)
  • Transfer or sale of the business, or merger (with proof)
  • Other, described

Section 29(2) lets a person who believes he no longer meets section 25 apply; under section 29(3), PRA de-registers if satisfied he is not required to be registered and has met his obligations. Section 29(4) sets the timetable: PRA disposes of the application within three months, extendable by up to sixty days for recorded reasons. PRA may also de-register a person or class not required to be registered (section 29(1)). Rule 15 makes de-registration free.

Selling or Merging the Business

Short answer: Selling the business needs the Commissioner's prior permission (rule 14) and notice within one month of the sale (section 20).

Rule 14 requires the Commissioner's prior permission before a registered business is sold or disposed of, and the explanation says sale includes merger. The buyer must obtain a new registration or, if allowed, changes to the existing one (rule 14(2)). Section 20(1) of the Act separately requires the former owner to tell the Commissioner within one month of a sale or transfer as an ongoing concern, and section 20(2) makes the buyer liable for tax on the business's services and liable to registration.

If You Were Registered by Mistake

A wrong compulsory registration is not de-registered; it is cancelled ab initio under rule 13, with no tax liability for that period. See compulsory registration.

Closing a Company or Firm: Who Stays Liable

Three sections of Chapter III matter when a business ends rather than simply de-registers:

  • Deceased owner (s.21): the tax liability of a deceased registered person is the first charge on his estate in the hands of his successors.
  • Bankruptcy (s.22): if the estate in bankruptcy continues the business, the tax liability passes to it and is paid before other creditors' claims.
  • Wound-up company or enterprise (s.23): where tax cannot be recovered from a private company or business enterprise that is wound up, every owner, partner or director during the relevant period is jointly and severally liable.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change my address on PRA registration?
Intimate the change on Form PST-01 under rule 11; PRA approves or rejects it, preferably within thirty days.
How long does PRA de-registration take?
Section 29(4): PRA disposes of the application within three months, extendable by up to sixty days for recorded reasons.
Which form is used to cancel PRA registration?
Form PST-02, under rule 5 of the Registration and De-registration Rules 2012. It is free under rule 15.
What happens if my PRA registration is suspended?
Your name is removed from PRA's published list (s.28(4)); PRA may withdraw the suspension within sixty days if satisfied with your remedial action (s.28(5)).
Can I sell my PRA-registered business?
Only with the Commissioner's prior permission (rule 14). The former owner must also inform the Commissioner within one month of the sale (section 20(1)).
Does de-registration clear my old tax liability?
No. Rule 5 de-registers from the later of the date PRA specifies and the date outstanding tax is deposited, and rule 9 keeps undischarged liability alive.
Can PRA de-register me on its own?
Yes. Section 29(1) lets PRA de-register a registered person, or a class of persons, not required to be registered under the Act.
Can a cancelled PRA registration be restored?
Rule 7 lets PRA revive a registration cancelled on an incorrect understanding of facts, on conditions and from a date it specifies.
Do I need permission to merge my business with another?
Yes. Rule 14 requires the Commissioner's prior permission for a sale, and its explanation says sale includes merger.
Do I still file while a de-registration application is pending?
Your registration continues until PRA de-registers you from the date it specifies (rule 5); obligations of a registered person apply until then.
