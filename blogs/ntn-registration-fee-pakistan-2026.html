Is NTN Registration Actually Free in Pakistan?

Yes, at the government level. FBR does not charge any statutory fee for issuing a National Tax Number to an individual or a business. The Income Tax Ordinance 2001 does not prescribe an NTN issuance fee, and the IRIS portal itself does not ask for any payment during registration. Anyone technically capable of navigating IRIS — creating a login, entering CNIC and personal details, selecting the correct taxpayer category, and submitting — can obtain an NTN at zero government cost.

So when a consultant advertises an "NTN registration fee," that number is entirely the service charge, not a pass-through of a government tax. This matters because it means the fee should reflect the value of getting it done correctly and quickly — not a fixed toll you're paying regardless of who does it.

What Actually Changes the NTN Registration Price

Individual NTN registration for a salaried person or a simple freelancer is Rs. 2,000 in almost every case, because the document and information requirements are minimal. The price moves in a small number of specific situations:

  • Business/company NTN registration requires additional documentation (trade license or SECP incorporation certificate, business bank account, business address proof) and is priced as part of the broader company registration or sole proprietorship registration service, not as a standalone NTN fee.
  • AOP (Association of Persons) NTN registration needs a partnership deed and each partner's CNIC, which adds review time — this is billed as part of the AOP registration service, currently Rs. 8,000 for the AOP tax return package which includes registration.
  • Overseas Pakistanis registering with a NICOP instead of a CNIC sometimes need an additional verification step, but the fee itself stays Rs. 2,000 for a straightforward individual case.
  • Reactivating a lapsed or blocked NTN (common if a previous registration was left inactive for years) can take longer than a fresh registration and may be quoted slightly differently depending on how long it's been dormant.

Outside of these cases, there is no legitimate reason an individual NTN registration should cost significantly more than Rs. 2,000 — if a consultant is quoting several times that for a first-time salaried individual, ask specifically what additional work justifies the difference.

The Catch Behind "Free NTN Registration" Offers

Because NTN issuance genuinely has no government fee, some services advertise "free NTN registration" to attract attention — and then recover the cost elsewhere. The most common patterns to watch for:

  • The registration is free, but you're required to also purchase an annual tax return "package" you didn't ask for, priced well above market rate
  • The category is registered incorrectly (often as a business category when you're actually salaried) so that a future correction becomes a separate paid service
  • No ATL enrollment is done, meaning you technically have an NTN but aren't an active filer — a gap that only surfaces later when you're charged the non-filer withholding rate on a bank or property transaction

A transparently priced Rs. 2,000 registration that includes ATL enrollment and a correct category selection is, in practice, cheaper than a "free" registration that creates a problem you pay to fix a year later.

Documents You Actually Need for NTN Registration

One of the reasons NTN registration is genuinely a same-day service is that the document list is short — there's no need to gather a large file before starting:

  • CNIC (front and back, clear photo) — this is the core identity document IRIS registration is built around
  • Active mobile number registered in your own name, since FBR sends a verification code to it during registration
  • Personal email address for your IRIS login credentials
  • Employer name and address, if you're registering as a salaried individual
  • Nature of business and business address, if you're registering as self-employed or a freelancer

That's the complete list for a standard individual registration — no salary slips, no bank statements, and no property documents are needed at this stage. Those become relevant later, at return-filing time, not at registration.

Common Mistakes That Delay or Complicate NTN Registration

Most NTN registration problems are avoidable and come from a small set of recurring errors:

  • Registering under the wrong category — choosing "business individual" when you're actually salaried (or vice versa) creates a mismatch that has to be corrected before you can file your first return cleanly. This is the single most common issue we see in NTN records inherited from other services.
  • Using a mobile number not registered in your own name — IRIS ties verification to the number's registered owner, and a mismatch here can stall the process.
  • Attempting to register a CNIC that already has an NTN — this typically happens when someone forgot they registered years earlier, or a previous employer registered on their behalf without telling them. Always check first rather than assuming you're starting fresh.
  • Leaving the profile registered but never filing a return — having an NTN without ever becoming an active filer defeats much of the purpose, since you remain exposed to non-filer withholding rates on property and banking transactions despite technically being "registered."

None of these are difficult to avoid with a properly handled registration — which is exactly what the Rs. 2,000 fee is paying for.
