DNFBP Registration for a Sole Proprietor: One-Person Property Offices and Jewellers, Explained

26 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 12 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 26 November 2026
Most property dealers and many jewellers in Pakistan are one-person businesses. FBR has a specific rule for who the compliance officer is when there is no one else.

The typical Pakistani property office is one person with a phone, a register and a network of buyers and sellers. Many jewellers are the owner, a family member and a karigar. FBR's AML rules were written for every size of business, and small owners often assume they are too small to matter or that they cannot meet the rules alone. FBR has addressed both points. This guide explains what registration means for a sole proprietor, who the compliance officer is, and how a one-person business can meet the duties without a compliance department.

Short answer

A sole proprietor who works as a real estate agent, a DPMS taking cash of Rs 2 million or more, or a non-ICAP/ICMAP accountant doing specified services is a DNFBP and must register with FBR under regulation 3. Registration runs through the proprietor's own IRIS account, since a sole proprietorship has no separate legal identity. FBR's FAQs (Q29) say the proprietor must designate an employee as compliance officer, but where there are no employees, FBR's Condition No. 3 (2022) deems the sole proprietor to be the AML/CFT compliance officer under section 7G of the AML Act. The role does not have to be standalone. All other duties apply in full.

Size Does Not Take You Out of Scope

Short answer: Size does not decide it: the activity does. A one-person real estate office, a DPMS taking Rs 2 million cash, or an accountant doing specified services is a DNFBP.

Nothing in FBR's Regulations exempts small businesses. What decides it is the activity:

You are a sole proprietor whoYou are a DNFBP ifSource
Buys, sells or arranges property for clientsYou execute purchases and sales, participate in real estate transactions or carry out professional transactional activity for property transferReg. 2(1)(n), as substituted by S.R.O. 1439(I)/2026
Sells jewellery, bullion, gems or stonesYou engage in a cash transaction with a customer of Rs 2 million or moreReg. 2(1)(fa), inserted by S.R.O. 1439(I)/2026
Practises as an accountant, not an ICAP or ICMAP memberYou carry out the specified activities in the AML Act, such as company formation or managing client moneyReg. 2(1)(a); FBR FAQs Q1–Q3

FBR's FAQs (Q19) make the same point for accountants: there is “no financial limit / threshold / benchmark”; the services decide. A one-person office that does one property deal a month is as much a DNFBP as a firm that does a hundred.

Registering a Business That Is Also You

Short answer: A sole proprietorship is the owner. Registration runs through the owner's own NTN and IRIS account.

A sole proprietorship has no legal identity apart from its owner. The business name on the signboard is a trade name; in FBR's records the taxpayer is the individual. That has three consequences for DNFBP registration:

  1. The IRIS account is yours. FBR's DNFBP portal link opens the IRIS login, and the registration is made through the proprietor's own NTN and IRIS profile, with the business details recorded in it. Our sole proprietor guide covers setting up that profile.
  2. You are senior management. FBR's FAQs (Q27) list “a sole proprietor of the Reporting Entity” first in the meaning of senior management. Regulation 3(2)'s request for criminal-record information on senior management and beneficial owners therefore points at you.
  3. You are the beneficial owner. The natural person who ultimately owns and controls the business is the proprietor.

Who Is the Compliance Officer?

Short answer: Designate an employee if you have one; if you have none, Condition No. 3 (2022) deems you the compliance officer under section 7G.

Regulation 7(1)(a) requires a compliance officer “at the management level”. FBR's FAQs answer the sole-proprietor question directly (Q29):

“Sole Proprietor must designate an employee to be the Compliance Officer. However, if there are no employees, FBR has imposed Condition No 3 (2022) which states that the Sole Proprietor shall be deemed to be AML/CFT compliance officer for the purpose of section 7G of the AML Act, 2010 and FBR's DNFBPs AML/CFT Regulations, 2020. Moreover, the Compliance Officer can carry out other duties not related to AML / CFT compliance. It does not have to be a standalone position.”

Your set-upCompliance officer
Proprietor alone, no employeesYou, by Condition No. 3 (2022)
Proprietor with one or more employeesDesignate an employee; FBR's FAQs (Q27) describe senior management as including an employee with sufficient knowledge and authority on ML/TF risk
Any caseThe role may be combined with other work; it need not be a separate post

Whoever it is, regulation 7(3) expects the officer to have access to all customer records and to be responsible for keeping the policy current and for timely reporting of STRs and CTRs to FMU. FBR's FAQs (Q26) require the officer's contact details to be shared with FBR.

The Duties a One-Person Business Must Still Meet

Short answer: Risk assessment, written policy, CDD, sanctions screening, record-keeping, CTRs and STRs on goAML all still apply.

DutyWhat it looks like in a one-person office
Risk assessment (reg. 4)A written page on your customers, areas, deal types and cash use, updated each year, reflecting FBR's sector risk ratings
Policy and controls (reg. 4(2))A short policy you have approved as senior management, saying what you check and when you refuse
Compliance programme (reg. 7)Officer named; hiring screening if you take on staff; your own training; an independent check of the system
CDD (reg. 8)A CDD form and CNIC copy, stamped “Original seen”, for every buyer and seller; beneficial owner where the customer is a company
Screening (reg. 13)Check names against UN and domestic lists before each deal; see sanctions screening
Records (reg. 6)One file per deal, kept at least five years after the relationship ends; a list of customers you refused
Reports (reg. 14)goAML registration; CTR for cash of Rs 2 million or more; STR on suspicion

Example: A Property Dealer in Johar Town

Imran runs a property office in Johar Town, Lahore, alone, arranging plot and house sales for about three deals a month. He has an NTN as a salaried person from an old job and no business details on IRIS. To register, he updates his IRIS profile with the business, registers as a DNFBP in the real estate agent category with the Lahore Directorate, and, with no employees, records himself as compliance officer under Condition No. 3. He writes a one-page risk assessment (cash-heavy plot deals, some overseas buyers), adopts a short policy, starts a CDD form for every party, registers on goAML, and keeps a box file per year. When an overseas buyer pays Rs 2,500,000 in cash for a token, he files a CTR. Nothing in this requires staff; it requires a routine.

How This Sits With Your Tax Registration

DNFBP registration is an AML registration, separate from income tax. You still file your return as a sole proprietor, and a property office providing services in Punjab may also need PRA registration. Our guide to sole proprietor services and fees sets out the published fees for the tax side.

Training and the Independent Check, for One Person

Regulation 7(1) lists four parts of a compliance programme, and two of them sound as if they need an organisation: an ongoing employee training programme, and an independent audit function to test the system. Regulation 7(2) explains what testing means: an assessment of the adequacy and effectiveness of the policies, controls and procedures, with recommendations. For a sole proprietor that can be scaled without losing the substance:

  • Training: keep a record of what you have studied each year, FBR's sector guidance and red flag indicators, FBR webinars, and any session you attend, and train any employee you take on before they handle customers.
  • Hiring screening: regulation 7(1)(b) requires screening when hiring to ensure integrity and skills; for a small office, a CNIC copy, references and a short record of why the person was hired.
  • Independent check: someone other than you, an outside adviser or a peer, reviews a sample of your deal files against your policy once a year and writes down what they found.

The record is what matters. Rule 4(2) of the Sanctions Rules 2020 protects a business that took all reasonable steps; a dated training note and a one-page review are evidence of exactly that. See our penalties guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a one-person property office need DNFBP registration?
Yes, if it executes, participates in or carries out transactional activity for property sales. FBR's Regulations set no size threshold for real estate agents.
Who is the compliance officer in a sole proprietorship?
FBR's FAQs (Q29) say the proprietor must designate an employee; if there are no employees, Condition No. 3 (2022) deems the sole proprietor to be the AML/CFT compliance officer.
Can the compliance officer do other work?
Yes. FBR's FAQs say the compliance officer can carry out other duties and it does not have to be a standalone position.
Is the DNFBP registration in my name or the shop's name?
A sole proprietorship has no separate legal identity, so registration runs through the proprietor's own NTN and IRIS account, with the business details recorded.
Is a small jeweller a DNFBP?
A jeweller is a DPMS for FBR's purposes when it engages in a cash transaction with a customer of Rs 2 million or more.
Do I still need to file my income tax return?
Yes. DNFBP registration is an AML registration; your tax return and any provincial sales tax registration are separate obligations.
Does a sole proprietor need an independent audit for AML?
Regulation 7(1)(d) requires an independent audit function to test the system. For a one-person business, an annual review of a sample of files by someone other than the proprietor, recorded in writing, serves that purpose.
Do I need to train myself as a one-person DNFBP?
Regulation 7 requires an ongoing training programme. Keeping a dated record of the FBR guidance and sessions you have studied each year shows it in practice.
