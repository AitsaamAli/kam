💼 UK Skilled Worker Visa — NTN, Tax Returns Aur Sponsorship Document Guide 2026

August 25, 2026Aitsaam Ali10 min readUKVI Verified

UK Skilled Worker visa student visa se ek badi tarah mukhtalif hai — yahan applicant ke paas already ek UK employer ka Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS) hota hai, aur zyada tar maintenance funds employer khud cover karta hai. Lekin is simplicity ke bawajood, Pakistan side ka FBR documentation utna hi important reh jata hai — khaas kar un cases mein jahan applicant self-sponsored hai, dependents ke saath ja raha hai, ya UK jaane se pehle Pakistan mein apna business/employment history clean karna chahta hai. Ye guide specifically Skilled Worker route ko student aur family visa categories se separate kar ke cover karti hai.

TL;DR: Agar UK employer maintenance cover karta hai (jo CoS par mention hota hai), personal funds ki zarurat nahi. Self-sponsored cases mein £1,270, 28 consecutive days mein bank mein chahiye. Dependents (spouse/children) ke liye additional £285 (partner) aur £315 (per child) maintenance chahiye — jab tak CoS employer coverage confirm na kare. Pakistan mein FBR history clean rakhna especially un applicants ke liye zaruri hai jo apna business band kar ke ja rahe hain ya property rakhte hain.

Points-Based System Mein Financial Documents Ka Role

UK ka Skilled Worker route ek points-based system (PBS) par chalta hai — job offer, salary threshold, aur English language proficiency mandatory points dete hain, jabke financial requirement inke saath ek separate maintenance criterion hai, na ke points ka hissa. Iska matlab ye hai ke Skilled Worker visa mein financial documentation ka role student visa jaisa "eligibility-defining" nahi — ye ek compliance step hai jo employer sponsorship ke around structure hota hai. Yehi wajah hai ke jahan student visa mein case worker deeply financial history scrutinize karta hai, Skilled Worker visa mein zyada focus CoS ki validity aur genuineness par hota hai — lekin jab bhi applicant self-sponsored ho ya Pakistan-side assets involved hon, FBR documentation ka weight wapas barh jata hai.

Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS) — Kya Hai Aur Maintenance Kaise Determine Karta Hai

Skilled Worker visa ka pura structure Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS) ke around banta hai — ek UK-licensed sponsor employer applicant ko digital CoS issue karta hai jisme job role, salary, aur — sabse important — ye batata hai ke maintenance funds employer cover kar raha hai ya nahi. Agar CoS par ye confirm ho ke employer maintenance cover karega, applicant ko personal bank balance dikhane ki zarurat nahi hoti.

  • Employer-covered maintenance: CoS par explicitly mention hota hai, applicant ke personal funds ki zarurat khatam
  • Self-sponsored / CoS mein coverage na ho: £1,270, 28 consecutive days tak bank mein chahiye
  • Already 12 months se UK mein legally rehte hue apply karna: is scenario mein bhi maintenance requirement waive ho sakta hai

Practically iska matlab ye hai ke Skilled Worker applicants ko sabse pehle apne employer se confirm karwana chahiye ke CoS mein maintenance coverage clause hai ya nahi — kyunke ye seedha decide karta hai ke Pakistan se kitni financial documentation chahiye hogi.

Dependents — Spouse Aur Bachon Ke Liye Extra Requirements

Skilled Worker visa holders apne dependents (spouse aur bachon) ko bhi saath le ja sakte hain, lekin ye ek alag maintenance calculation trigger karta hai — jab tak employer ka CoS coverage dependents ko bhi cover na kare:

  • Partner/Spouse: additional £285 (28 din tak hold)
  • Har bacha: additional £315 per child (28 din tak hold)
  • Dependents ke documents: nikah nama/marriage certificate, bachon ke birth certificates, aur relationship proof

Yahan par bhi wahi principle apply hota hai jo main applicant par hota hai — agar family ka koi member Pakistan mein property ya business rakhta hai jo family ke financial background ko support karta hai, FBR wealth statement us background ko document karti hai. Ye especially un families ke liye relevant hai jahan husband UK ja raha hai lekin family ka joint asset base Pakistan mein already established hai.

Business Owners Jo Skilled Worker Route Se UK Ja Rahe Hain

Ek growing category ye hai — Pakistani professionals jo apna khud ka business ya freelance practice chala rahe hote hain, aur UK mein ek employer se CoS le kar move kar rahe hote hain. Is scenario mein extra caution zaruri hai:

  • Business ko close karna ya continue rakhna: agar business Pakistan mein continue rehti hai (remote ownership), income Pakistan mein taxable rehti hai aur regular filing zaruri hai
  • Employees/partners ko handover: agar business kisi partner ko transfer ho rahi hai, FBR mein ownership change register karwana zaruri hai
  • Pending liabilities clear karna: koi bhi outstanding sales tax, withholding tax, ya audit notice UK jaane se pehle resolve honi chahiye — warna future mein Pakistan wapasi par masla ban sakti hai

Ye group aksar student ya visitor visa applicants se zyada complex FBR history rakhta hai — business registration, sales tax, employee withholding — is liye inke liye ek proper "exit compliance review" karna specifically valuable hai, sirf ek saal ki return file karna kaafi nahi hota.

Common Mistakes — Skilled Worker Applicants

  • CoS coverage confirm na karna: employer se maintenance coverage clarify kiye bina khud se assume kar lena ke funds ki zarurat nahi — aur last moment mein pata chalna ke chahiye the
  • Dependents ka maintenance bhool jana: sirf main applicant ka £1,270 calculate karna, spouse/children ke additional amounts miss karna
  • Business ki pending liabilities ignore karna: UK visa mil jaane ke baad Pakistan mein FBR notices aana shuru hona jo departure se pehle resolve nahi ki gayin
  • Salary certificate aur CoS salary mismatch: agar applicant currently kisi Pakistani company mein kaam kar raha hai, uski declared salary FBR return mein aur CoS par mention "current employment" thoda alag ho toh explain karna zaruri hai

UK Pohanchne Ke Baad — Pakistan Mein Ongoing Obligations

Skilled Worker visa milne ke baad bhi Pakistan ke saath tax relationship immediately khatam nahi hoti — khaas kar pehle saal mein jab residency status transition mein hota hai. 182-din rule ke mutabiq, agar aap us tax year mein Pakistan mein 182 din se kam rahe, non-resident classify hote hain — lekin agar move mid-year hui hai, us saal ka split residency status bhi ho sakta hai jo dono countries mein filing ko thoda complex bana deta hai. Pakistan mein agar property, savings account, ya koi bhi income-generating asset hai, annual non-resident return file karte rehna chahiye — chahe UK income Pakistan mein taxable na ho. UK-Pakistan Double Taxation Agreement (DTA) ke under double tax se bachne ke liye Foreign Tax Credit claim ki ja sakti hai.

ILR Aur Settlement Ke Liye Pakistan Tax History Ki Ahmiyat

Skilled Worker route typically 5 saal ke baad Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) ki taraf leads karta hai. ILR application mein directly Pakistan tax history mandatory nahi — Home Office primarily UK residence continuity, HMRC tax compliance, aur Life in UK test dekhta hai. Lekin practically, do scenarios mein Pakistan FBR history relevant ban jati hai:

  • Additional document requests: agar Home Office kisi applicant ke financial history par additional scrutiny kare (especially jahan applicant ne Pakistan se assets liquidate kiye hon UK settle hone ke liye), FBR records supporting evidence ke tor par kaam aate hain
  • Property aur business continuity: agar settlement ke baad bhi Pakistan mein property ya business assets hain, un ka clean tax record future cross-border transactions (inheritance, sale, transfer) ko significantly asaan banata hai

Is liye best practice yehi hai ke Skilled Worker se ILR tak ke poore journey mein — chahe UK side ka focus HMRC compliance par ho — Pakistan side ki FBR filing bhi parallel maintain ki jaye, especially agar Pakistan mein koi financial footprint baqi hai.

FAQs — UK Skilled Worker Visa Documentation

Agar CoS mein employer maintenance cover kar raha hai, kya phir bhi FBR documents chahiye?
UKVI ke liye technically mandatory nahi agar CoS maintenance fully cover karta hai. Lekin agar aap Pakistan mein business/property rakhte hain ya future mein wapas aana chahte hain, clean FBR record recommended hai — future transactions aur family ke visa applications dono ke liye helpful.
Dependents ke liye kitna extra maintenance chahiye hota hai?
Partner/spouse ke liye additional £285 aur har bacha ke liye £315, 28 consecutive days tak bank mein — jab tak CoS employer coverage in dependents ko bhi cover na kare.
Main apna business Pakistan mein continue rakh sakta hun UK jaane ke baad?
Haan, lekin business ki income Pakistan mein taxable rehti hai aur regular FBR filing zaruri hoti hai — chahe aap UK mein resident ho jayein. Non-resident status ka matlab sirf UK-source income Pakistan mein taxable nahi hoti, Pakistan-source business income phir bhi declare karni hoti hai.
Self-sponsored Skilled Worker visa ka matlab kya hai?
Self-sponsored ka matlab hai ke CoS ke bawajood maintenance funds coverage clause nahi hai, ya applicant ko khud apne funds show karne hote hain — is case mein £1,270 ka standard maintenance requirement apply hota hai jaisa doosri visa categories mein hota hai.
UK jaane se pehle Pakistan mein business band karna zaruri hai?
Zaruri nahi, lekin agar business continue nahi karni, formal closure aur FBR ke saath deregistration process complete karna chahiye — warna pending returns aur notices accumulate hote rehte hain jo baad mein complications create karte hain.
Section 8 tax clearance certificate Skilled Worker visa applicants ke liye kyun recommended hai?
Skilled Worker route generally long-term settlement (aur eventually ILR) ki taraf leads karta hai. Section 8 certificate ek clean exit record provide karta hai jo future property transactions ya Pakistan wapasi ke waqt useful hota hai — visa ke liye mandatory nahi lekin practically valuable.
