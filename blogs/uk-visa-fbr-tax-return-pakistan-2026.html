🇬🇧 UK Visa Ke Liye Pakistan FBR Tax Return aur NTN — UKVI Documents Complete Guide 2026

July 15, 2026Aitsaam Ali9 min readUKVI Verified

UK mein 1.6 million+ Pakistanis hain — aur har saal hazaaron naye log UK Skilled Worker, student ya family visa ke liye apply karte hain. UKVI (UK Visas and Immigration) ka process financial adequacy par heavy focus karta hai. Pakistan mein FBR tax returns, NTN aur wealth statement ye sab documentation ka core hissa hain. Ye guide sirf UK ke liye hai — UKVI requirements, maintenance funds, aur departure checklist.

UKVI Aur Pakistan FBR — Kya Connection Hai

UK Home Office ka UKVI system require karta hai ke applicant prove kare ke unke paas enough maintenance funds hain — aur ye funds genuine hain (sham funds nahi). Pakistan mein income ka sabse verifiable proof FBR ke saath filing hai:

  • Skilled Worker Visa: Agar employer maintenance cover karta hai — personal funds ki zarurat nahi. Self-sponsored cases mein £1,270 (28 consecutive days mein) required hain.
  • Student Visa: London ke liye £1,334/month, baaki UK £1,023/month — 9 months ke liye + tuition. Parents/sponsor ki FBR returns + bank statements strongest proof.
  • Family Visa (Spouse): UK mein sponsor ki income threshold £29,000/year (2024 se increased). Pakistan mein applicant ki FBR returns additional financial history.
  • Visitor Visa: Strong ties to Pakistan prove karna hota hai — property, business, job — sab FBR returns mein declared hota hai.

UKVI sham funds dekhta hai: Agar bank mein suddenly paise aaye aur FBR history match na kare — UKVI suspicious ho sakta hai. Tax returns ye prove karte hain ke funds aap ki genuine kamai se hain — not borrowed ya staged.

UK Jane Ke Baad Pakistan Tax Status

  • Non-resident: Pakistan mein 182 din se kam raho us saal toh non-resident — UK income Pakistan mein taxable nahi
  • UK-Pakistan Double Taxation Agreement (DTA): Available hai — income dono jagah double taxable nahi hogi
  • Pakistan mein property: Rental income 15% flat rate par taxable Pakistan mein
  • UK mein: HMRC ko worldwide income declare karna hogi — Pakistan income bhi (FTC claim possible)
  • Return file karo: Pakistan mein non-resident return file karna recommended — property transactions ke liye future mein zaruri

NHS Surcharge: UK visa application mein NHS Immigration Health Surcharge bhi deni hoti hai — £1,035/year per adult (2026). Ye alag payment hai visa fee ke علاوه. Pakistan se UK ja rahe ho toh ye plan karo — total UK visa cost student ke liye £490 (visa) + NHS surcharge significant hoti hai.

FAQs — UK Visa FBR

UK Skilled Worker visa ke liye FBR return zaruri hai?
Directly UKVI nahi maangta — lekin financial background, source of funds aur genuine employment history ke liye FBR returns helpful hain. Agar CoS (Certificate of Sponsorship) hai toh maintenance generally employer cover karta hai — lekin FBR returns Pakistan ties prove karne ke liye additional benefit deti hain.
UK student visa ke liye parents ki returns chahiye?
Haan. UK student visa maintenance (£1,334/month London, £1,023/month baaki UK, 9 months ke liye) prove karne ke liye parents ya sponsor ki bank statements + FBR tax returns combination best proof hai. Returns income legitimacy establish karti hain — sirf bank statement ke bina sham funds ka concern ho sakta hai.
Visitor visa ke liye Pakistan mein kya prove karna hota hai?
UK visitor visa mein "genuine visitor" prove karna hota hai — strong ties to Pakistan: property (FBR wealth statement mein), job (salary certificate + employer letter), family. FBR returns aur wealth statement property aur financial ties ka strongest documentary evidence hain.
UK jane ke baad Pakistan mein tax dena hoga?
Non-resident hone ke baad UK income Pakistan mein taxable nahi. Pakistan mein property se rental income 15% flat par taxable hai. UK-Pakistan DTA ke under double taxation se bacha ja sakta hai. UK mein HMRC ko Pakistan income bhi declare karni hogi — FTC (Foreign Tax Credit) claim karo.
UK BHC (British High Commission) Islamabad kahan hai?
British High Commission Islamabad: Diplomatic Enclave, Ramna 5, Islamabad. UK visa applications Pakistan mein VFS Global ke zariye process hoti hain — Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Faisalabad centres hain. Documents submission VFS par, decision UKVI karta hai.
