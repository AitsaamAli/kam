Education Expense Tax Credit Pakistan 2026 — Tuition Fee Guide

June 24, 2026Aitsaam Ali6 min readParents

Bachon ki school ya college ki fee bharna aasan kaam nahi — lekin ye aapki tax liability kam karne mein bhi madad kar sakta hai. Pakistan mein eligible individuals apni tuition fee expense par ek tax credit claim kar saktay hain. Ye guide eligibility, calculation formula, aur claim process cover karta hai.

TL;DR

Tuition fee par tax credit milta hai — lower of (5% of fee paid) ya (average tax rate x fee). Eligibility income threshold ke saath judi hai. Sirf core tuition fee qualify karti hai, hostel/transport nahi.

Tax Credit Kaise Calculate Hoti Hai

Formula (approximate): Credit = Lower of — (a) 5% of tuition fee paid, ya (b) Taxpayer ki average tax rate × tuition fee paid. Jo bhi amount kam ho, wahi credit milta hai.

ItemDetail
Eligible ExpenseSchool/college/university tuition fee (core fee only)
Not EligibleHostel charges, transport, uniform, books
Credit TypeTax credit (direct reduction in tax payable, not income)
EligibilityIncome threshold-based — high earners may not qualify

Kaun Eligible Hai

Ye credit generally un individuals ke liye designed hai jinki taxable income ek certain threshold se kam hai — maqsad middle-income families ko relief dena hai. High-income individuals ke liye ye credit limited ya unavailable ho sakta hai. Threshold amounts Finance Act ke saath change hotay rehtay hain — apni exact eligibility har saal confirm karwao.

Return Mein Kaise Claim Karein

  • School/college se official fee-paid certificate collect karo (bache ka naam, fee amount clearly mention ho)
  • IRIS return ke "Tax Credits" section mein tuition fee amount enter karo
  • System khud formula apply kar ke credit calculate karega
  • Bank payment proof bhi rakho documentation ke liye

Multiple bachon ke liye: Agar aapke multiple bache school/college mein hain, sab ki combined tuition fee ek sath calculate hoti hai — lekin overall formula (5% ya average rate, jo kam ho) still apply hoti hai total fee par.

Aksar Puchhe Gaye Sawal — Education Expense Tax Credit

Tuition fee par tax credit kaise milta hai Pakistan mein?
Individuals apne bachon ki tuition fee par tax credit claim kar saktay hain — credit ka amount fee paid ka ek chhota percentage (approximately 5%) ya taxpayer ki average tax rate x fee, jo bhi kam ho, us formula se calculate hota hai.
Kya ye credit sab taxpayers ko milta hai?
Ye credit generally un individuals ke liye hai jinki taxable income ek certain threshold se kam hai. High-income individuals ke liye eligibility limited ya na ho sakti hai — apni exact eligibility professional se confirm karwao har saal ke rules ke hisab se.
Kaunsi education fee is credit mein qualify karti hai?
Bachon ki school, college, ya university tuition fee generally qualify karti hai — lekin hostel charges, transport, uniform, aur books jaisi additional expenses is credit mein shamil nahi hoti. Sirf core tuition fee hi eligible hoti hai.
Education tax credit claim karne ke liye kya document chahiye?
School/college/university ka official fee paid receipt/certificate, jisme bache ka naam aur fee amount clearly mention ho. Bank statement se payment proof bhi rakhna faydemand hota hai.
