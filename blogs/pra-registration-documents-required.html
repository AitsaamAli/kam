PRA Registration Documents Required: The Complete Checklist From Form PST-01

21 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 10 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 21 November 2026
PRA does not publish a short list of documents. It publishes a form. This guide turns Form PST-01 into a checklist.

Ask five people what PRA needs and you will get five lists. The reliable answer is the form itself. Form PST-01 is appended to the 2012 Registration Rules, and the PRA IRIS screens follow it field by field. This checklist is built from that form, so nothing on it is guesswork and nothing the form asks for is missing.

Short answer

PRA registration is driven by Form PST-01, the prescribed form under rule 3 of the Punjab Sales Tax on Services (Registration and De-registration) Rules 2012. It asks for your category (individual, company or AOP), CNIC or registration number, name, address, the services you provide and your principal service, phone and email, every business place (owned or rented, the owner's CNIC or NTN if rented, electricity reference number, gas connection, start date) and every business bank account. An individual also needs an active FBR NTN, because PRA IRIS takes your basic details from FBR.

Identity and Category

Short answer: CNIC for an individual (passport for a non-resident), or the registration or incorporation number for a company or registered AOP, plus the FBR NTN.

Form PST-01 opens with who you are. Section 4 of the form asks for your category: company, individual or AOP. Section 5 asks for the basis: as per law, voluntary registration, or compulsory registration. Section 6 asks for your status: resident or non-resident (and the country if non-resident). Then:

The form carries an authorisation: by applying you authorise PRA to obtain your registration particulars from FBR. That is why your FBR record must be current before you apply. If you have no NTN yet, start with NTN registration.

Address, Phone and Email

Field 10 asks for the registered office address for a company, or the mailing or business address for an individual or AOP, "for all correspondence". It is split into office, shop, house, flat or plot number; street, lane, plaza, floor or village; province; district; and city or tehsil. Fields 14 and 15 ask for phone, mobile and email.

The declaration at the end of the form says notices sent to that email or address are accepted as legal notice. So use an email someone reads. The PRA IRIS screens also send verification codes and your password to the mobile and email, which must be live on the day you apply.

Services and Business Activity

Short answer: The services you provide with one principal service, every business place, and how each place is held: owned, rented or other.

Field 11 lists types of service, field 12 asks for the principal service, and field 19 for other activities. The IRIS manual adds that at least one activity must be marked principal and one business marked primary. Before you apply, write down in one line what you charge clients for. That line decides the activity you choose.

If an agent files on your behalf under section 71, field 13 records the agent's CNIC or NTN, name and address. When we file for you, this is where our details go, with your consent.

Each Business Place

Short answer: For each place: electricity reference number, whether gas is installed, phone, start date, and the owner's CNIC or NTN if rented.

Fields 20 to 25 repeat for every business, branch or outlet. For each one you need:

  • The branch type (head office, showroom, godown, sub-office and so on), name and trade name
  • The full address
  • Nature of possession: owned, rented or other, and the owner's CNIC, NTN or FTN if it is not yours
  • The electricity reference number and whether a gas connection is installed
  • The phone number and the date the business or branch started

In practice, the documents that prove these are a recent electricity bill for each place (the reference number is on it), and the ownership papers or rent agreement with the landlord's CNIC.

Bank Accounts

Short answer: Every bank account used in the business: number, title, bank, city and opening date.

Fields 26 to 30 ask for every bank account used in the business: account number, account title, bank name, city and the date the account opened. A bank account maintenance certificate or the first page of a statement shows all of these.

The Checklist to Send Us

Short answer: Send one message with all items at once; partial sets cause back-and-forth and delay.

For a sole proprietor providing a service from one place, the whole set is usually:

  1. CNIC (front and back)
  2. FBR NTN, or permission for us to register one (Rs 2,000)
  3. A latest electricity bill of the business place
  4. Rent agreement with the owner's CNIC, or ownership papers
  5. Bank account maintenance certificate
  6. One line on what services you provide, and since when
  7. Your active mobile number and email

Companies and AOPs add the incorporation or registration certificate and the directors' or partners' CNICs and NTNs. Several branches means items 3 and 4 for each branch. If you are not sure you must register at all, read who must register with PRA first; sole proprietors should also see PRA registration for sole proprietors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which form is used for PRA registration?
Form PST-01, prescribed by rule 3 of the Punjab Sales Tax on Services (Registration and De-registration) Rules 2012. The same form is used to report changes under rule 11.
Do I need a rent agreement for PRA registration?
Form PST-01 asks whether each business place is owned, rented or other, and for the owner's CNIC, NTN or FTN where it is not yours, so a rent agreement with the landlord's CNIC is the usual proof.
Is an electricity bill required?
The form asks for the electricity reference number of each business place and whether gas is installed. The reference number is printed on the bill.
Do I need to list every bank account?
Form PST-01 asks for the total bank accounts and details of each one used in the business: number, title, bank, city and start date.
Can someone else file for me?
Yes. Field 13 of Form PST-01 records an agent under section 71 with their CNIC or NTN, name and address.
Do I need photographs of the premises for PRA?
Form PST-01 does not ask for photographs. It asks for the address, how the place is held, the electricity reference number, gas connection, phone and start date of each place.
Does PRA need a business bank account in the trade name?
Form PST-01 asks for each bank account used in the business with its title; it does not say the title must be the trade name.
What if my FBR address is old?
Update it with FBR first. PRA IRIS takes basic details from FBR and the Basic Information screen cannot be edited there.
Do I need my CNIC copy if PRA pulls details from FBR?
Yes. Form PST-01 field 7 asks for the CNIC of an individual, and the codes and password go to the mobile and email you give, so keep the CNIC and live contact details ready.
What does a company need that an individual does not?
Form PST-01 asks companies and registered AOPs for the registration or incorporation number and the top ten directors, shareholders or partners with their NTN, CNIC or passport number.
