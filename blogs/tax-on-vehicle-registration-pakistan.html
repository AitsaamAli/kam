When you buy a new car in Pakistan, the advance income tax under Section 231B is one of the largest upfront costs after the vehicle price. Non-filers pay up to four times more than filers for the same car. In addition, provincial token tax is paid annually. This guide covers every tax a vehicle owner in Pakistan faces — from first registration to annual token, transfer, and commercial use.

Section 231B — Advance Tax on Vehicle Registration

Section 231B advance income tax is collected by the Excise department at the time of first registration of a private motor vehicle. The tax amount depends on engine capacity (cc) and filer/non-filer status:

Engine CapacityFiler RateNon-Filer Rate
Up to 1000ccRs. 10,000Rs. 30,000
1001cc – 1199ccRs. 15,000Rs. 45,000
1200cc – 1299ccRs. 25,000Rs. 75,000
1300cc – 1499ccRs. 40,000Rs. 120,000
1500cc – 1599ccRs. 70,000Rs. 210,000
1600cc – 1999ccRs. 100,000Rs. 300,000
2000cc and aboveRs. 150,000Rs. 450,000

Non-filers pay exactly 3× more than filers. On a 1600cc car, a filer saves Rs. 200,000 at registration alone. This Section 231B advance tax is adjustable — you claim it as a credit in your annual income tax return. If it exceeds your annual tax liability, the difference is refundable (though refunds on vehicle tax are slow to process).

Annual Token Tax — Provincial

Token tax is a provincial levy paid annually to the Provincial Excise department (not FBR). It is separate from income tax and is not deductible against income tax. Rates by province vary:

ProvinceToken Tax BasisApproximate Annual Rate
PunjabVehicle age and engine sizeRs. 1,500 – Rs. 15,000/year
SindhEngine size and vehicle valueRs. 2,000 – Rs. 20,000/year
KPKEngine sizeRs. 1,000 – Rs. 10,000/year
BalochistanEngine sizeRs. 800 – Rs. 8,000/year

Token tax is paid through the provincial Excise portal (Punjab: excise.punjab.gov.pk, Sindh: excise.gos.pk). Most banks also accept token tax payment using the vehicle registration number. Token tax is due annually and failure to pay attracts late payment surcharge plus inability to transfer the vehicle.

Advance Tax on Vehicle Transfer

When a registered vehicle is sold/transferred to a new owner, advance tax is collected again at the time of transfer. The rates at transfer are typically lower than first registration:

Engine CapacityFiler (Transfer)Non-Filer (Transfer)
Up to 1000ccRs. 5,000Rs. 15,000
1001cc – 1800ccRs. 10,000 – Rs. 25,000Rs. 30,000 – Rs. 75,000
Above 1800ccRs. 50,000Rs. 150,000

Both buyer and seller should be filers to minimize transfer costs. Like first registration tax, transfer advance tax is adjustable against annual income tax liability.

Electric Vehicle Exemptions

Pakistan's Electric Vehicle Policy provides incentives for EV purchases:

  • Electric vehicles (fully battery-powered) are exempt from Section 231B advance tax at first registration
  • Import duty and sales tax concessions apply on EVs under the EV Policy
  • Annual token tax is reduced for EVs (varies by province)
  • Commercial EVs (electric rickshaws, delivery bikes) also qualify for reduced advance tax

Hybrid vehicles do not get the full EV exemption. Only fully electric vehicles qualify for Section 231B exemption. Hybrid cars (petrol + electric motor) are taxed at normal rates based on their engine CC. Check vehicle classification with the manufacturer or Excise department.

Commercial Vehicle Tax Rules

Vehicles used for commercial purposes (goods transport, taxis, ride-hailing) face different tax treatment:

  • Goods transport vehicles: Subject to Section 234 fixed tax (annual WHT on route permits — see transporters blog)
  • Commercial passenger vehicles (taxis, ride-hailing): Advance tax under Section 234 applies
  • Vehicles owned by companies: Section 231B applies at company level; the company can claim the credit in its corporate return
  • Leased vehicles: If a leasing company purchases for lease, they pay 231B and pass the credit benefit to the lessee per their agreement

How to Claim Vehicle Tax Credit in Annual Return

  1. Obtain the vehicle registration certificate showing advance tax paid and the Excise department CPR number.
  2. In IRIS Form 114(I), go to Tax Credits → Adjustable WHT.
  3. Select Section 231B — Motor Vehicle as the WHT category.
  4. Enter the advance tax amount and the registration/CPR number.
  5. IRIS credits this amount against your annual tax liability.
  6. If total advance taxes (231B + bank WHT + any other adjustable WHT) exceed your annual liability, the balance can be claimed as a refund.

WHT on Used Car Purchase from Manufacturer / Dealer

When a vehicle is purchased from a manufacturer or authorized importer (brand-new), advance tax under Section 231B is collected at the excise department upon registration. For purchases from dealers or auctions of used/imported vehicles:

  • Locally-assembled brand-new vehicles: 231B WHT collected at excise registration in the buyer's name
  • Imported vehicles (Customs-cleared): Additional advance tax may apply at customs stage under other WHT provisions
  • Dealer / auction purchase (grey import): Buyer pays Section 231B at registration in their name
  • Used vehicle registered in another person's name: Transfer tax applies at transfer; previous 231B stays with original registrant

Step-by-Step: Vehicle Registration at Excise Office

  1. Purchase vehicle and obtain invoice from manufacturer/dealer.
  2. Confirm your NTN and Active Taxpayer List (ATL) — check via SMS to 9966 or iris.fbr.gov.pk.
  3. Visit provincial Excise and Taxation office or NADRA e-Sahulat centre with CNIC, invoice, and NTN.
  4. Excise officer calculates Section 231B advance tax based on engine size and your filer/non-filer status.
  5. Pay the advance tax via PSID generated by the Excise office. Payment options: 1-Link ATM, internet banking, or bank branch.
  6. Pay provincial token tax for the current registration year.
  7. Obtain registration certificate and number plate. CPR for 231B payment is retained as your credit proof.
  8. At year-end, submit the CPR to your tax consultant or enter in IRIS manually to claim the Section 231B credit.

Vehicle Leasing and Advance Tax

Many corporate employees use company lease cars. The tax treatment depends on who registers the vehicle:

  • Vehicle registered in company name and given to employee: Company pays 231B at lower corporate rate; employee is taxed on 5% of cost of vehicle as a perquisite each year under salary WHT
  • Leasing company (NBFC) registers vehicle for lease: Leasing company pays 231B and generally recovers it via lease pricing
  • Employee self-registers leased vehicle: Employee pays 231B as individual; claims as credit in personal return

Employer vehicle as perquisite: If your employer provides a company-registered vehicle for personal use, FBR treats 5% of the cost of the vehicle per year as a taxable perquisite added to your salary. This is in addition to the company paying 231B at registration. Understand both elements when negotiating vehicle allowance vs company car.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much advance tax do I pay on a 1300cc car in Pakistan as a filer?
For a 1300cc–1499cc vehicle, a filer pays Rs. 40,000 advance income tax under Section 231B at first registration. A non-filer pays Rs. 120,000 for the same car. This advance tax is adjustable — you claim it as a credit in your annual income tax return, reducing your net tax liability for that year.
Is Section 231B vehicle advance tax a final tax in Pakistan?
No. Section 231B advance tax is adjustable (not final). You claim it as a tax credit in your annual income tax return under WHT Credits. If the advance tax exceeds your computed annual income tax, the difference is refundable. This is why being a filer matters — filers pay less and then recover it anyway through annual return.
What is the difference between token tax and advance income tax on vehicles?
Advance income tax under Section 231B is a federal tax collected by FBR through the Excise department — it is adjustable against your annual income tax return. Token tax is a provincial annual fee for keeping a registered vehicle — it is not income tax, not adjustable against FBR obligations, and goes to provincial government revenue. Both must be paid separately.
Do electric vehicles pay advance tax on registration in Pakistan?
No. Fully electric vehicles are exempt from Section 231B advance income tax at first registration under Pakistan's Electric Vehicle Policy. However, hybrid vehicles (with both petrol and electric engines) are not fully exempt and are taxed based on engine size like conventional vehicles. Check with the Excise department for the latest classification of your specific model.
Can a company claim vehicle advance tax as a credit in Pakistan?
Yes. If a company purchases a vehicle in its name, the Section 231B advance tax paid at registration is credited against the company's corporate income tax return. Enter it under Adjustable WHT in the company's IRIS return. Companies with multiple fleet vehicles can accumulate substantial 231B credits to offset their annual corporate tax.
I bought a car two years ago but never filed a return - can I still claim the 231B credit?
Yes. File a back-year return for the relevant tax year (the year you registered the vehicle). Section 114(1)(b) allows filing back-year returns for up to 5 prior tax years. Include the 231B advance tax paid as a credit in that return. You may receive a refund or the credit reduces your tax liability for that year.
