🧳 UK Move Karne Ke Baad Pakistan Tax Filing, NTN Aur FBR Status Ka Kya Hota Hai — 2026 Guide

August 25, 2026Aitsaam Ali10 min readUKVI Verified

UK visa mil gaya, samaan pack ho gaya, flight bhi book ho gayi — lekin ek sawal jo zyada tar log tab tak nahi puchte jab tak koi masla khara nahi ho jata: UK move karne ke baad Pakistan mein NTN, tax filing, aur FBR status ka kya hota hai? Ye guide un logon ke liye hai jo already UK ja chuke hain ya jaane wale hain, aur specifically Pakistan-side ki ongoing tax responsibility samajhna chahte hain — non-resident status, property, aur future ke liye clean record kaise maintain karein.

TL;DR: UK move hone ka matlab Pakistan tax obligations khatam hona nahi. 182-din rule ke mutabiq residency status decide hoti hai, lekin Pakistan-source income (property rental, savings profit) non-resident hone ke baad bhi taxable rehti hai. UK-Pakistan DTA double taxation se bachati hai. Annual non-resident return file karte rehna — chahe minimal ho — future property transactions aur Pakistan wapasi ke liye zaruri hai.

Sabse Bara Misconception — "Visa Mil Gaya Matlab Tax Ka Rishta Khatam"

Kamboh Associates ke paas UK-based clients ka sabse common sawal ye hota hai — "maine toh visa le liya, ab Pakistan side kya karna hai?" Ye sawal khud is misconception ko reflect karta hai ke UK visa ka Pakistan tax obligations se koi seedha connection hai. Asal mein dono cheezein completely alag systems hain: UK visa ek immigration matter hai (Home Office/UKVI ka jurisdiction), jabke Pakistan tax filing ek financial matter hai (FBR ka jurisdiction) jo asset ownership aur residency days par based hoti hai — visa status par nahi. Ek applicant UK Skilled Worker visa le sakta hai aur phir bhi Pakistan mein tax-resident reh sakta hai agar wo saal ka zyada hissa Pakistan mein guzare (jaise ke initial transition period mein hota hai), aur isi tarah UK mein settle ho jane ke baad bhi agar Pakistan mein property ho toh filing obligation continue rehti hai — chahe non-resident hi kyun na ho.

182-Din Rule — Resident Ya Non-Resident Kaise Decide Hota Hai

Pakistan Income Tax Ordinance ke mutabiq, ek individual "resident" tab count hota hai jab wo tax year (July-June) mein Pakistan mein 183 din ya zyada rahe. Is se kam rahne par non-resident status apply hota hai. UK move karne wale logon ke liye ye important hai kyunke:

  • Resident status: worldwide income Pakistan mein taxable (UK salary bhi included)
  • Non-resident status: sirf Pakistan-source income taxable — UK salary, UK-based savings/investment income Pakistan mein taxable nahi
  • Transition year: jis saal move hui, agar mid-year hai, us saal ka din-count carefully calculate karna zaruri hai kyunke ye determine karta hai konsa status apply hoga

Bohat se log ye samajhte hain ke UK visa milte hi automatically non-resident ho jate hain — asal mein status har tax year ke actual din-count par based hota hai, visa status par nahi. Agar koi UK visa holder hai lekin abhi bhi Pakistan mein 183+ din guzar raha hai (jaise ke frequent travel wala pattern), wo us saal resident hi rahega.

NTN Aur FBR Registration — Kya Cancel Karni Chahiye?

Ek common galat fehmi ye hai ke UK move hone ke baad NTN "cancel" karwani chahiye ya khud-ba-khud inactive ho jati hai. Aisa nahi hota — NTN ek permanent tax identity hai, aur agar Pakistan mein koi bhi income-generating asset hai (property, savings account, business shareholding), NTN active rehni chahiye aur filing continue honi chahiye:

  • NTN cancel na karo: agar future mein Pakistan mein koi transaction karni ho (property, investment), active NTN zaruri hogi
  • Filer status maintain karo: ATL (Active Taxpayer List) mein rehne se property transactions par lower withholding tax rates apply hoti hain
  • Non-filer ban jane ke consequences: property kharidte/bechte waqt higher withholding tax, banking transactions par extra tax — chahe UK mein resident ho

Important: Filer status Pakistan mein kiye gaye financial transactions par directly asar dalta hai — chahe aap UK mein rehte ho aur Pakistan income taxable na ho, agar aap non-filer ban jate hain, property sale/purchase par extra tax lagti hai.

UK-Pakistan Double Taxation Agreement (DTA) — Kaise Kaam Karti Hai

UK aur Pakistan ke beech ek Double Taxation Agreement hai jo ek hi income par dono countries mein pura tax lagne se bachati hai. Practically ye kaise apply hota hai:

  • UK income: HMRC ko declare hoti hai, UK mein tax pay hota hai
  • Pakistan-source income (agar koi ho): Pakistan mein bhi declare honi chahiye — lekin agar UK mein bhi wahi income taxable ho rahi hai, Foreign Tax Credit (FTC) claim ki ja sakti hai taake double tax na lage
  • UK mein worldwide income declaration: HMRC resident individuals se worldwide income declare karwata hai — is mein Pakistan property rental ya savings profit bhi shamil hai

DTA automatically apply nahi hoti — applicant ko dono taraf correctly file karna hota hai aur FTC ka proper claim submit karna hota hai. Agar filing sirf ek taraf ki jaye (sirf UK ya sirf Pakistan), double taxation ka risk ya phir Pakistan side ki non-compliance ka risk reh jata hai.

Pakistan Mein Property — Non-Resident Hone Ke Baad Bhi Tax Obligations

Ye sabse common scenario hai jahan overseas Pakistanis ko ongoing FBR filing ki zarurat parti hai — Pakistan mein property rakhna:

  • Rental income: 15% flat rate par taxable, chahe applicant non-resident ho
  • Property sale par Capital Gains Tax (CGT): holding period ke hisaab se rate apply hoti hai — non-resident sellers ke liye bhi
  • Advance tax on property transactions: filer aur non-filer rates mein bara farq hota hai, is liye filer status maintain karna financially beneficial rehta hai

Agar property se koi income nahi bhi aa rahi (jaise ke khali plot), phir bhi wealth statement mein reflect honi chahiye — kyunke jis din bhi wo property sell ki jaye, uska FBR record mein consistent hona zaruri hai future scrutiny se bachne ke liye.

HMRC Self-Assessment Aur Pakistan Records Ka Connection

UK mein agar aap self-employed hain, high earner hain, ya foreign income rakhte hain, HMRC Self-Assessment tax return file karni parti hai — is mein worldwide income declare karna mandatory hota hai. Ye wahi jagah hai jahan Pakistan FBR records directly UK filing ko support karte hain:

  • Agar Pakistan property se rental income UK Self-Assessment mein declare ki ja rahi hai, us amount ka Pakistan FBR return se match hona chahiye
  • Foreign Tax Credit claim karne ke liye Pakistan mein pay ki gayi tax ka proof (payment challan, return acknowledgment) chahiye hoti hai
  • HMRC kabhi kabhi supporting documentation maang sakta hai agar declared foreign income significant ho — is waqt clean, consistent FBR records available hona case ko asaan banata hai

Is liye ye do filing systems (UK HMRC aur Pakistan FBR) technically independent hain, lekin practically ek dusre ko support karte hain jab bhi cross-border income involved ho. Inconsistent ya missing Pakistan records UK side ki filing ko bhi complicate kar sakte hain.

Section 8 Tax Clearance — Departure Se Pehle Ka Ek Achha Kadam

Agar move permanent ya long-term hai, Section 8 tax clearance certificate departure se pehle recommended hai. Ye certificate confirm karta hai ke koi outstanding tax liability nahi hai — aur ye future property transactions ya Pakistan wapasi ke waqt kaam aata hai. Legally UK visa ke liye mandatory nahi, lekin ek "clean slate" ke tor par valuable hai.

FAQs — Pakistan Tax Status After UK Move

UK move hone ke baad Pakistan mein tax return file karna zaruri hai?
Agar Pakistan mein koi income-generating asset hai — property, savings, business shareholding — toh haan, annual non-resident return file karna chahiye. Agar sirf UK income hai aur Pakistan mein koi asset nahi, generally filing zaruri nahi.
NTN cancel karwani chahiye UK move hone par?
Nahi, NTN cancel karwane ki zarurat nahi aur ye recommended bhi nahi. Agar future mein Pakistan mein koi transaction karni ho, active NTN aur filer status zaruri hoga.
Non-resident hone ke baad filer status maintain karna kyun zaruri hai?
Filer status property transactions, banking, aur dusre financial dealings par lower withholding tax rates deta hai — chahe aap UK mein rehte ho. Non-filer ban jane par ye rates significantly barh jaati hain.
UK mein Pakistan ki property income declare karni hoti hai?
Haan, HMRC UK residents se worldwide income declare karwata hai — Pakistan property se rental income bhi shamil hai. UK-Pakistan DTA ke under, agar Pakistan mein bhi tax pay hua hai, Foreign Tax Credit claim ki ja sakti hai double taxation se bachne ke liye.
182-day rule exactly kaise calculate hota hai?
Pakistan tax year (July-June) mein Pakistan mein guzare hue actual din count kiye jate hain. Agar total 183 din ya zyada ho, resident status apply hota hai; is se kam ho toh non-resident. Mid-year move ke case mein us specific saal ka calculation carefully karna zaruri hai.
Section 8 tax clearance UK move ke liye mandatory hai?
Nahi, UKVI ya Pakistan immigration authorities ise mandatory nahi karte. Lekin long-term ya permanent move ke liye ye recommended hai — clean tax record future property transactions ya Pakistan wapasi ke waqt valuable hota hai.
Kya UK citizenship milne ke baad bhi Pakistan filing zaruri reh sakti hai?
Haan, agar Pakistan mein koi asset (property, savings, business) hai, filing requirement citizenship status se nahi balke asset ownership se linked hoti hai. UK citizen ban jane ke baad bhi Pakistan-source income declare karni hoti hai agar wo maujood hai.
