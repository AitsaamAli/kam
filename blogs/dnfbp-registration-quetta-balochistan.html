DNFBP Registration in Quetta and Balochistan: FBR's Quetta Directorate

10 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 7 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 10 November 2026
Balochistan has its own FBR DNFBP directorate in Quetta, covering businesses within the territory of RTO Quetta.

For property dealers, jewellers and accountants in Balochistan, the supervising DNFBP office is in Quetta. This page sets out its coverage and the points that matter most for businesses there.

TL;DR

Balochistan's DNFBP supervisor is the Director (DNFBPs), Quetta. S.R.O. 1497(I)/2026 gives that office every DNFBP whose business lies in RTO Quetta's territory, plus residents of that area not assigned elsewhere. For cash-based trades the Rs 2 million rule is the key test: a single cash deal at that level makes a jeweller a DNFBP and requires a currency transaction report to FMU on goAML.

Quetta Directorate: Who Covers What

Test in the notificationResult
Business carried on in RTO Quetta's territorial jurisdictionSupervised from Quetta (Director and Additional Director, DNFBPs)
Residence in that area, where no other rule appliesAlso supervised from Quetta

Your RTO is shown in your IRIS profile. If it is RTO Quetta, the Quetta directorate supervises you for DNFBP purposes.

Cash-Heavy Markets

Where trade is largely in cash, the Rs 2 million thresholds matter most. A jeweller becomes a DNFBP through a single cash transaction of Rs 2 million or more with a customer, and every such transaction needs a CTR. Estate agents also file CTRs for cash of Rs 2 million or more. Splitting a payment into smaller cash amounts does not avoid reporting; FMU treats it as a red flag.

Cross-Border Risk and Enhanced Due Diligence

FBR's guidance treats customers and funds linked to countries that FATF has called out, or that have weak AML controls, as higher risk. For such customers, collect information on the source of funds, get senior management approval and monitor the relationship more closely. Screen every customer against the UN Security Council list and Pakistan's proscribed persons lists before the deal.

A Practical Setup for a Small Quetta Business

  1. Register as a DNFBP on IRIS and on goAML.
  2. As a sole owner without staff, you are the compliance officer (FBR Condition No. 3 of 2022).
  3. Use a one-page CDD form for every customer in scope, with a CNIC copy and a screening note.
  4. Keep a cash register that shows each large cash receipt and the CTR filed for it.
  5. Write a short risk assessment and policy; our compliance programme guide shows what to include.

A Quetta Scenario

A gold dealer in Quetta is offered Rs 4.5 million in cash for bullion by a customer who asks that no record be kept and says the money came from "trade" without further detail. Requests not to keep records, an unclear source of funds and large cash are all on FMU's red flag list for jewellers. The dealer asks for identification and an explanation; if suspicion remains, the dealer declines the transaction and files an STR, and because the cash offered is above Rs 2 million, any completed sale would also need a CTR.

The example is illustrative.

Doing It All Remotely From Balochistan

Businesses across Balochistan within RTO Quetta's territory do not need to travel to comply. DNFBP registration is on IRIS, goAML registration is online with FMU (with a hard copy of the authority letter posted to FMU), and FBR's DNFBP helpline is a telephone and email service. What does need to be on paper locally is the business's own file: CDD forms, cash register, screening notes and the policy.

Informal Money Transfers

Pakistan's anti-money laundering framework treats unregulated value transfer as a serious risk. For a property dealer or jeweller, the practical point is simple: money that arrives through an unlicensed channel, or is paid on the customer's behalf by someone who is not the owner of the funds, has no reliable trail. FMU's red flags for both real estate and jewellers include third-party payments without a legitimate reason and unclear sources of funds. Ask how the money reached the customer, prefer bank channels, and record the answer.

Gold Traders: Buying as Well as Selling

Many gold traders buy old gold from the public as often as they sell. The Rs 2 million test applies to cash transactions with a customer in either direction. When paying cash for gold, identify the seller, record where they say the gold came from, screen the name, and file a CTR when the threshold is met. A seller who brings large quantities often, with no explanation, is a pattern to note.

Quetta Business Checklist

  • ☐ Tax office confirmed as RTO Quetta in IRIS.
  • ☐ DNFBP registration on IRIS and goAML account approved.
  • ☐ Compliance officer named (the owner, in a one-person business).
  • ☐ CDD form, cash register and screening log in use.
  • ☐ Short risk assessment and policy signed and dated.

Property Dealing in Quetta

Property dealers in Quetta identify both buyer and seller, record how each payment is made, and file a CTR for cash of Rs 2 million or more. Where land is sold by several family members, each seller is a customer. Where a buyer pays through someone else, identify the payer and record why. These steps take minutes per deal and are what an inspector from the Quetta directorate will look for first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which FBR office supervises DNFBPs in Quetta?
The Director (DNFBPs), Quetta, under S.R.O. 1497(I)/2026, for businesses within the territorial jurisdiction of RTO Quetta.
Do I have to travel to Quetta's FBR office to register?
No. DNFBP registration is online on IRIS, and goAML registration is online with FMU.
Who is the compliance officer in a one-person business?
Under FBR's Condition No. 3 (2022), a sole proprietor with no employees is deemed to be the AML/CFT compliance officer.
Does splitting a cash payment avoid a CTR?
No. FMU lists several smaller transactions adding up to a large total as a red flag, and it may call for an STR.
Is the Balochistan Revenue Authority involved in DNFBP supervision?
No. BRA deals with provincial tax on services. Anti-money laundering supervision of DNFBPs in Balochistan sits with FBR's Quetta directorate.
Does buying old gold for cash count for the Rs 2 million test?
Yes. The test applies to cash transactions with a customer whether the trader is buying or selling.
What should I do if a customer's money came through an informal channel?
Ask how the funds reached the customer and record the answer. Third-party payments without a legitimate reason and unclear sources of funds are FMU red flags that may call for an STR.
Is a hard copy needed for goAML registration?
Yes. FMU's guideline requires the signed authority letter to be uploaded as a PDF and the hard copy to be sent to the Additional Director, FMU.
What will the Quetta directorate look for in a property dealer's files?
Typically CDD for both buyer and seller, payment records, CTRs for cash of Rs 2 million or more, and screening records.
Is there a separate FBR form for Balochistan businesses?
No. DNFBP registration uses the same online IRIS process nationwide; only the supervising directorate differs.
Who is the compliance officer in a Quetta family business with no employees?
If it is a sole proprietorship without employees, the owner is deemed the compliance officer under FBR Condition No. 3 (2022).
