Tax Return Ki Last Date (31 October 2026) Guzar Gayi — Ab Return Kaise File Karein

1 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 11 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Tax Consultant | NTN & Compliance Specialist · Facts checked 1 November 2026
Tax Year 2026 ki return ki last date 31 October 2026 thi. Return ab bhi file ho sakti hai, magar jitni der karenge, nuqsan utna barhta jayega.

Bohat se log 1 November ki subah ye soch kar pareshan hote hain: "Last date nikal gayi, ab kya hoga?" Pehli baat: ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi, return ab bhi file ho sakti hai. Doosri baat: ab har din ki der ka asar hai. Is guide mein hum asaan Roman Urdu mein batayenge ke late return kaise file karein, kaun se kaghzaat chahiye, kitna kharcha aa sakta hai aur kaun si ghaltiyon se bachna hai.

TL;DR

Tax return ki last date guzar gayi hai, magar return ab bhi IRIS par file ho sakti hai. Late return par Section 182 ke tehat jurmana lag sakta hai (zyada tar salaried logon ke liye kam az kam Rs 5,000, baqi ke liye Rs 40,000). ATL mein wapis aane ke liye Rs 25,000 surcharge dena hoga, ya agar IRIS mein Form ATL-U maujood ho to 6 mahine property na lene ka wada karna hoga. Sab se pehle documents ikatthe karein, wealth statement theek banayein aur jaldi file karein.

Last Date Kaun Si Thi Aur Ab Kya Badla?

Tax Year 2026 (1 July 2025 se 30 June 2026 tak ki aamdani) ki return pehle 30 September tak file honi thi. FBR ne pehle ise 15 October aur phir Section 214A ke tehat 31 October 2026 tak barhaya. Ye date salaried, karobari afraad, freelancers, kiraye ki aamdani walon aur AOPs ke liye thi. Companies ki date alag hoti hai.

1 November 2026 tak FBR ne mazeed kisi extension ka elaan nahi kiya tha. Is liye ab jo return file hogi wo "late return" kehlayegi. Late return bhi poori tarah valid return hai. Farq sirf itna hai ke iske saath teen cheezein judi hain: jurmana, ATL se bahar rehna aur non-filer wali katautiyan.

Pehla Kaam: Apne Kaghzaat Ikatthe Karein

Late return mein sab se zyada waqt kaghzaat dhoondne mein lagta hai. Apni category dekh kar ye cheezein tayyar karein:

Aap kaun hainZaroori kaghzaat
Salaried (naukri pesha)CNIC, salary certificate ya employer ki annual tax deduction statement, 1 July 2025 se 30 June 2026 tak ki bank statements
Dukandar ya karobariUpar wali cheezein, saath sales aur purchase ka record, kharchon ki details, aur suppliers ya buyers ki taraf se kati hui tax ki slips
Freelancer (Upwork, Fiverr waghera)Bank statements, foreign remittance ka record (PRC ya bank ka certificate), platform ki earning report
Kiraye ki aamdaniKirayanama, saal bhar mein mila kiraya, aur tenant ki taraf se kata hua tax (agar kata ho)
Overseas PakistaniCNIC ya NICOP, Pakistan mein aamdani aur assets ka record, safar ki dates (residency check karne ke liye)

Wealth statement ke liye ye bhi chahiye: pichle saal ki wealth statement, saal mein khareeda ya becha gaya plot, gaari, sona ya shares, aur loans ki details.

IRIS Par Late Return File Karne Ka Tareeqa

  1. Login karein: iris.fbr.gov.pk par apne CNIC aur password se login karein. Password bhool gaye hon to "Forgot Password" se registered mobile aur email par code mangwa lein.
  2. Sahi form kholein: Declaration mein Tax Year 2026 ka Return of Income (Section 114) form chunein. Agar draft pehle se save hai to wahi kholein, naya na banayein.
  3. Aamdani bharein: salary, karobar, kiraya ya foreign income, jo bhi ho, apni sahi category mein likhein. Andaze se figures na bharein.
  4. Kati hui tax darj karein: employer, bank, mobile company aur bijli ke bill par kati hui advance tax ki details darj karein. Ye aap ke tax mein se minus hoti hai.
  5. Wealth statement banayein: 30 June 2026 tak ke assets aur liabilities likhein, aur reconciliation check karein ke figures aapas mein milti hain.
  6. Baqi tax jama karwayein: agar form mein tax baqi aa raha hai to pehle payment slip (PSID) bana kar bank ya app se jama karwayein, phir submit karein.
  7. Submit karke acknowledgement save karein: return aur wealth statement dono submit karein aur acknowledgement ka PDF sambhal kar rakhein.

Ek mashwara: agar aap ne pichle saal ki wealth statement nahi bhari thi, ya is saal koi bara asset khareeda hai, to pehle kisi tajurbakar consultant se figures check karwa lein. Notices aksar yahin se shuru hote hain.

Late Return Par Kitna Jurmana Lag Sakta Hai?

FBR ki website par Section 182 ka jo text maujood hai (1 November 2026 ko check kiya), uske mutabiq:

  • Har din ki der par saal ke tax payable ka 0.1%.
  • Zyada se zyada tax payable ka 50%.
  • Kam az kam Rs 40,000, chahe tax zero ho.
  • Agar aap ki 75% ya zyada aamdani salary se hai aur salary Rs 50 lakh se kam hai, to kam az kam jurmana Rs 5,000 hai.

Yaad rakhein ke ye jurmana IRIS submit karte waqt khud nahi kat-ta. Tax officer notice bhej kar order ke zariye lagata hai, aur aap ko apni wajah batane ka mauqa milta hai. Agar der ki koi asal aur saabit wajah thi, jaise IRIS ka masla jiska screenshot aap ke paas ho, to wo jawab mein likhi ja sakti hai. Mazeed misalein hamari late filing penalty guide mein hain.

ATL Mein Wapis Kaise Aayen?

Late return file karne se aap khud ba khud Active Taxpayers List (ATL) mein nahi aate. Finance Act 2026 ke baad Section 182A ka surcharge ye hai:

KaunSurcharge
Individual (fard)Rs 25,000
AOP (partnership)Rs 50,000
CompanyRs 100,000

Individuals ke liye FBR ne October 2026 mein ek draft rule (Form ATL-U) bhi jari kiya tha. Is mein aap 6 mahine tak koi property na lene ka wada karte hain aur surcharge nahi dena parta. 1 November 2026 tak ki khabron mein ye draft hi tha, is liye file karte waqt IRIS mein check karein ke ye form maujood hai ya nahi. Dono raston ka mukammal muqabla hamari ATL surcharge ya Form ATL-U guide mein hai.

ATL status check karne ke liye 9966 par SMS karein: ATL, phir space, phir apna 13 hindson wala CNIC.

Late Return Mein Ye 6 Ghaltiyan Na Karein

  • Jaldi mein wealth statement andaze se bharna. Baad mein revise karna parta hai (dekhein revised return kab aur kaise file hoti hai).
  • Aamdani kam dikhana taake tax kam aaye. FBR ke paas bank, property aur katautiyon ka data hota hai. Is se masla aur bara ho jata hai.
  • Agle extension ke intezar mein baithe rehna. FBR ne koi elaan nahi kiya, aur jurmane ka hisab har din barhta hai.
  • Kisi aur ke IRIS account se ya ghalat CNIC par return file karna. Har shakhs ki return uske apne CNIC par aur uski apni aamdani ke mutabiq honi chahiye.
  • Notice aaye to nazar-andaz karna. Section 114(4) ke notice ka jawab na diya jaye to FBR apne data par assessment kar sakta hai. Hamari Section 114 notice guide dekhein.
  • Ye samajhna ke surcharge dene se jurmana khatam ho gaya. Surcharge sirf ATL ke liye hai. Jurmana alag cheez hai.

Frequently Asked Questions

Kya 31 October 2026 ke baad bhi tax return file ho sakti hai?
Ji haan. IRIS par Tax Year 2026 ki return ab bhi file ho sakti hai. Ye late return kehlayegi, is liye Section 182 ka jurmana lag sakta hai aur ATL mein aane ke liye surcharge dena hoga ya Form ATL-U ka rasta, agar maujood ho, istemal karna hoga.
Late return par kam az kam jurmana kitna hai?
FBR ki website par maujood Section 182 ke mutabiq kam az kam jurmana Rs 40,000 hai. Agar 75% ya zyada aamdani salary se ho aur salary Rs 50 lakh se kam ho to kam az kam Rs 5,000 hai. Is se upar har din tax payable ka 0.1% lagta hai, zyada se zyada 50% tak.
Kya FBR ne last date dobara barhai hai?
1 November 2026 tak FBR ne 31 October ke baad koi extension announce nahi ki thi. Kisi nayi khabar ke liye FBR ki official website ya X account dekhein.
ATL mein wapis aane ke liye kitne paise dene honge?
Late return ke baad individual ko Section 182A ke tehat Rs 25,000 surcharge dena hota hai, AOP ko Rs 50,000 aur company ko Rs 100,000. Individuals ke liye Form ATL-U ka draft rule bhi hai jis mein 6 mahine property na lene ka wada karna hota hai.
Agar mera tax employer ne poora kaat liya tha to kya phir bhi jurmana lagega?
Lag sakta hai, kyunke jurmane ka hisab saal ke poore tax payable par hota hai. Magar zyada tar salaried logon ke liye, jin ki salary Rs 50 lakh se kam hai, kam az kam jurmana Rs 5,000 hai.
Late return file karne mein kitne din lagte hain?
Agar saare documents mukammal hon to salaried aur freelancer ki return aam tor par ek se do working days mein tayyar ho kar manzoori ke liye bhej di jati hai. Karobari return mein record ke hisab se zyada waqt lagta hai.
Kya main ghar baithe late return file karwa sakta hoon?
Ji haan. Kamboh Associates poora kaam WhatsApp par karta hai. Aap documents bhejte hain, hum return aur wealth statement tayyar karke dikhate hain, aur aap ki manzoori ke baad IRIS par file karte hain.
ATL status kaise check karein?
9966 par SMS bhejein jis mein ATL likh kar space dein aur apna 13 hindson wala CNIC likhein. FBR ki website par online ATL search bhi maujood hai. Surcharge ya undertaking ke baad status update hone mein kuch waqt lag sakta hai.
