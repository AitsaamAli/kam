Politically Exposed Persons in Property and Jewellery Deals: FBR's PEP Rules for DNFBPs, Explained

26 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 11 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 26 November 2026
A PEP customer is not forbidden. But regulation 9 sets minimum steps that must be taken before the deal, not after it.

Pakistani property and gold markets serve a large number of public officials, politicians and their families. FBR's Regulations do not ask DNFBPs to turn them away. They ask for something narrower and more demanding: know when the customer is a PEP, get senior approval, find out where the money came from, and watch the relationship more closely. This guide explains each element from the text of regulations 2 and 9 and FBR's FAQs, with how it works in a property office or a jewellery shop.

Short answer

FBR's Regulations define a politically exposed person as anyone who is or has been entrusted with a prominent public function in Pakistan, abroad or in an international organisation, including heads of state or government, senior politicians, senior government, judicial or military officials, senior executives of state-owned corporations and political party officials. Family members (spouse, lineal ascendants and descendants, siblings) and close associates are covered too. Regulation 9 requires a system to identify PEPs before and during a relationship, senior management approval, reasonable measures to establish source of wealth and funds, and enhanced ongoing monitoring.

Who Counts as a PEP

Short answer: A person who is or has been entrusted with a prominent public function at home, abroad or in an international organisation.

Regulation 2(1)(m) of FBR's AML/CFT Regulations for DNFBPs 2020 defines “Politically Exposed Persons” as “any individual who is or has been entrusted with a prominent public function either domestically or by a foreign country, or in an international organization”, and includes, without limiting it:

TypeExamples named in the regulation
Domestic PEPsHeads of State or of government, senior politicians, senior government, judicial or military officials, senior executives of state owned corporations, political party officials
Foreign PEPsThe same functions held for a foreign country
International organisation PEPsMembers of senior management or individuals entrusted with equivalent functions

“Is or has been” matters: a retired senior official is within the definition. FBR's short guidance for real estate agents lists PEPs, their close associates and family members first among examples of higher-risk customers.

Family Members and Close Associates

Short answer: Family members (spouse, lineal ascendants and descendants, siblings) and close associates are treated with the PEP.

The Regulations extend PEP treatment beyond the office holder:

CategoryDefinition in the Regulations
Family member, reg. 2(1)(i)A spouse of the PEP; lineal ascendants and descendants; and siblings
Close associate, reg. 2(1)(e)Someone known to have joint beneficial ownership of a legal person or arrangement, or any other close business relations with the PEP; someone with sole beneficial ownership of a legal person or arrangement known to be set up for the PEP's benefit; or someone reasonably found or believed to be closely connected with the PEP, socially or professionally

In practice, the plot bought in a son's name, the jewellery paid for by a brother-in-law, and the company owned by a long-time business partner can all bring a deal within the PEP rules.

What Regulation 9 Requires

Short answer: Regulation 9(3): a system to identify PEPs, senior management approval, source of wealth and funds, and enhanced ongoing monitoring.

Regulation 9(1)(c) makes EDD compulsory for PEPs and their close associates and family members. Regulation 9(3) then sets the minimum:

  1. Identify. “Implement appropriate risk management systems to determine if a customer or beneficial owner is a PEP or a close associate or a family member of a PEP, both prior to establishing a business relationship or conducting a transaction, and periodically throughout the course of the business relationship.”
  2. Approve. Obtain senior management approval to establish or continue the relationship, including where an existing customer later becomes a PEP.
  3. Source. Take reasonable measures to establish the source of wealth and the source of funds of customers and beneficial owners identified as PEPs, family members or close associates.
  4. Monitor. Conduct enhanced ongoing monitoring of the relationship.

The general EDD measures in regulation 9(2) can be added: more information on the customer and the purpose of the transaction, more frequent updating of identification data, and more controls on the pattern of transactions.

How It Works in a Property Office or Jewellery Shop

StepProperty officeJewellery shop (DPMS)
IdentifyAsk on the CDD form whether the buyer, seller or beneficial owner holds or has held public office, or is a family member or associate of someone who hasAsk the same at any cash sale of Rs 2 million or more
ApproveSenior management, the proprietor for a sole proprietorship, signs off before token money is acceptedThe owner or manager signs off before the sale is completed
SourceBank statement, sale deed of a property sold, tax return, inheritance papersBank withdrawal record, tax documents, business accounts
MonitorReview the customer's further deals more closelyWatch repeat purchases and the use of cash

FBR's short guidance for real estate agents gives examples of evidence of source of wealth: “details of the bank account the funds came from or verification from evidence of sold property such as a sale deed, wealth return, inheritance payment or audited financials of a business.” A PEP who is also an FBR filer usually has a wealth statement; our wealth statement guide explains what it shows.

When Does a PEP Stop Being a PEP?

Short answer: FBR's FAQs: consider the influence the person can still exercise and links between past and current functions before declassifying.

FBR's FAQs (Q56) do not set a fixed period. They say the entity “should consider the level of influence that the individual could still exercise and whether the individual's previous and current function are linked in any way.” A senior official who retired last year and now advises a ministry is still a PEP in substance. A clerk-level appointment held decades ago was never within the definition. Record the reasoning when you declassify someone.

Example: A Plot Bought in a Daughter's Name

A buyer in Islamabad asks a dealer to arrange a Rs 45,000,000 plot in his daughter's name. The CDD form reveals that the buyer retired last year as a senior official in a federal ministry. The daughter is the customer; the father is a PEP who has held a prominent public function, and she is his family member. Under regulation 9, the dealer obtains senior management approval, asks for the source of funds (a bank transfer from the father's account) and the source of wealth behind it (pension commutation and the sale of an earlier property, with the sale deed), screens both names against sanctions lists, and marks the relationship for enhanced monitoring. If the explanation does not hold together, the dealer considers an STR on goAML; see our goAML guide.

When an Existing Customer Becomes a PEP

PEP status is not fixed at onboarding. Regulation 9(3)(a) requires systems to identify PEPs “both prior to establishing a business relationship or conducting a transaction, and periodically throughout the course of the business relationship”, and regulation 9(3)(b) requires senior management approval to continue a relationship where a customer “subsequently becomes a PEP”. FBR's short guidance for real estate agents gives the same instruction in plain terms: during ongoing monitoring, if there is a material change in the customer's profile, “like customer becomes a PEP, non-resident etc.”, update the CDD.

For a property office with repeat investors, or a jeweller with regular family customers, that means three habits: ask the PEP question again at each new deal, note public appointments and election results that affect known customers, and record the senior approval when a long-standing customer's status changes. FBR's FAQs (Q36) suggest review cycles of at least once a year for high-risk cases, every two years for medium and every three years for low risk; a PEP relationship sits in the first group.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is a politically exposed person in Pakistan?
Under FBR's Regulations, anyone who is or has been entrusted with a prominent public function domestically, abroad or in an international organisation, including senior politicians, senior government, judicial or military officials, senior executives of state owned corporations and political party officials.
Can a real estate agent deal with a PEP?
Yes. The Regulations do not prohibit it, but regulation 9 requires senior management approval, reasonable measures on source of wealth and funds, and enhanced ongoing monitoring.
Are a PEP's children covered?
Yes. Family members include the spouse, lineal ascendants and descendants and siblings of the PEP.
Who is a close associate of a PEP?
Someone with joint beneficial ownership of a legal person with the PEP or other close business relations, someone owning a vehicle set up for the PEP's benefit, or someone closely connected socially or professionally.
Is a retired official still a PEP?
The definition covers anyone who “is or has been” entrusted with a prominent public function. FBR's FAQs say to weigh remaining influence and links between past and current roles before declassifying.
What proof of source of wealth can a PEP customer give?
FBR's guidance mentions bank account details, a sale deed of property sold, a wealth return, an inheritance payment or audited business accounts.
How often should PEP customers be reviewed?
FBR's FAQs suggest at least once a year for very high or high-risk relationships, which is where PEP relationships sit under regulation 9.
What if a customer becomes a PEP after the deal?
Regulation 9(3) requires periodic identification during the relationship and senior management approval to continue it once the customer becomes a PEP.
