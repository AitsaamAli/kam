What is NTN and Why Do You Need It?

NTN (National Tax Number) is your unique tax identity issued by FBR. You need it to file income tax returns, open business bank accounts, apply for government tenders, import and export goods, and enjoy filer benefits like reduced withholding tax.

Who Needs NTN Registration in Quetta?

  • Salaried employees earning above Rs. 600,000 per year
  • Business owners and shopkeepers
  • Freelancers and consultants
  • Company directors and partners
  • Property buyers and sellers
  • Vehicle buyers (to get filer rate)
  • Anyone wanting active filer status

Documents Required for NTN

  • CNIC copy (both sides)
  • Mobile number registered on your CNIC
  • Email address
  • Employment letter or business address (for business NTN)
  • For company: SECP incorporation certificate and CNIC of all directors

Filer vs Non-Filer Benefits

  • Bank profit tax: Filer 15% vs Non-filer 30%
  • Property sale WHT: Filer 3% vs Non-filer 6%
  • Vehicle registration WHT: Filer 2% vs Non-filer 4-6%
