Who Must Register With PRA? Section 25, Voluntary Registration and the Recipient Rule

21 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 10 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 21 November 2026
The test in the Act is short: do you provide a taxable service from an office or place of business in Punjab?

"Do I need PRA?" is the first question every Punjab service business asks, and the answer is in four short sections of the Act. This guide reads them in order, with the questions they leave you to answer about your own business.

Short answer

Section 25(1) of the Punjab Sales Tax on Services Act 2012 says a person shall register who (a) provides any taxable service from his office or place of business in the Punjab, (b) is otherwise required to register under the Act or rules, or (c) meets criteria PRA prescribes. Section 26 allows voluntary registration by someone carrying on an economic activity who is not required to register, if PRA is satisfied on four conditions. Section 25(3) treats an unregistered recipient of certain taxable services as registered for that tax period. Rule 2(1) repeats the duty for every person providing taxable services.

The Rule: Section 25(1)

Short answer: You must register if you provide a taxable service from an office or place of business in Punjab (s.25(1)(a)), or another provision requires it.

Section 25(1) of the Punjab Sales Tax on Services Act 2012 lists three cases. A person shall register who:

  1. (a) "Provides any taxable service from his office or place of business in the Punjab"; or
  2. (b) "Is otherwise required to be registered under any of the provisions of the Act or the rules"; or
  3. (c) "Fulfills any other criteria or requirements which the Authority may prescribe" under section 25(2).

Section 25(2) adds that registration is regulated in the manner, conditions and restrictions PRA prescribes by Gazette notification. Rule 2(1) of the 2012 Rules repeats the duty: "Every person engaged in providing taxable service or services or pay tax under the Act is required to be registered."

Clause (a) has two parts, and you need both: the service is a taxable service, and you provide it from an office or place of business in Punjab. Which services are taxable is set by the Act's charging provisions and schedules, which PRA updates through Finance Acts; check the current list on PRA's website or ask us before you decide.

Voluntary Registration: Section 26

Short answer: Section 26 lets a person not required to register apply at any time; PRA registers them if four conditions are met.

Some businesses are asked for PRA registration by clients or in tenders before the law requires it. Section 26(1) allows "a person who carries on an economic activity but is not required to be registered" to apply "at any time". Under section 26(2), PRA may register the person if satisfied that:

  • (a) the person is providing, or shall provide, a service that would be taxable if the person were registered;
  • (b) the person has a place of business at which he carries on an economic activity;
  • (c) there are reasonable grounds to believe the person will keep proper records and file regular returns; and
  • (d) if the activity has already started, the person has kept proper records and complied with other tax laws.

Condition (d) is why an up-to-date FBR income tax record matters even for a voluntary applicant. Voluntary registration also brings every duty of a registered person, so register when you are ready to meet them.

When the Buyer Is Treated as Registered: Section 25(3)

Short answer: An unregistered recipient of a service made taxable by section 3(2) is treated as registered for that tax period (s.25(3)).

Section 25(3) covers the other side of a transaction. A person who receives a service that is taxable by virtue of section 3(2), and is not registered, "shall be deemed to be a registered person" for the tax period in which the service is received, invoiced or paid for, whichever is earlier, and all the Act and rules apply to that person for that period "as if that person had provided the service". In plain terms, buying certain services can bring an unregistered business into PRA's net for that period.

Working in More Than One Province

Short answer: Services rendered in Punjab need a separate Punjab registration even if you are registered in another province (rule 2(4)).

Rule 2(4) is direct: a person providing taxable services in more than one province including Punjab "is required to take a separate registration for the Punjab" for services rendered in Punjab. Registration in Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa or Balochistan does not cover Punjab. See our guide for freelancers serving other provinces.

If You Should Have Registered Already

Short answer: Ask three questions: is the service taxable, is it provided from a place of business in Punjab, and is any other provision catching you.

Section 27 lets PRA register a person who is required to register but has not applied, after advance notice and a hearing. It is better to register yourself first. If a notice has already arrived, read compulsory registration and e-Enrolment before doing anything else.

Do Not Charge PRA Tax Until You Know

Section 17 makes anyone who collects tax that was not payable, or more than was payable, and passes it to the customer, pay that amount to the Government, recoverable as an arrear (s.17(1)-(2)). Settle the section 25 question first; then invoice accordingly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PRA registration mandatory?
Yes for anyone who provides a taxable service from an office or place of business in Punjab, or is otherwise required by the Act, rules or a PRA notification (section 25(1)).
Can I register with PRA voluntarily?
Yes. Section 26 allows a person carrying on an economic activity who is not required to register to apply at any time, subject to four conditions.
I am registered with SRB in Sindh. Do I need PRA too?
For services rendered in Punjab, rule 2(4) requires a separate Punjab registration.
Can buying a service make me liable to PRA?
Section 25(3) treats an unregistered recipient of a service taxable under section 3(2) as registered for that tax period.
What happens if I do not register?
Section 27 lets PRA register you compulsorily after advance notice and an opportunity to be heard.
Does PRA registration depend on turnover?
Section 25(1)(a) turns on providing a taxable service from a place of business in Punjab. Any other criteria are those PRA prescribes under section 25(2).
Is a home-based service provider covered?
Section 25(1)(a) speaks of an office or place of business in Punjab. Form PST-01 records the place, so a home used for the business is listed as the business place.
Can PRA set extra criteria for registration?
Yes. Section 25(1)(c) and 25(2) let PRA prescribe other criteria, conditions and restrictions by notification in the official Gazette.
Does registering voluntarily carry the same duties?
Yes. A person registered under section 26 is a registered person, so duties such as showing the registration number on invoices (rule 2(5)) apply.
Who decides whether my service is taxable?
The Act and its schedules, as amended by Punjab Finance Acts. PRA publishes them; we check the current text on PRA's site before advising on your service.
