Benami Transaction Law Pakistan 2026 — Property Kisi Aur Ke Naam Par Rakhna Risky Kyun

July 23, 2026Aitsaam Ali8 min readLegal Awareness

"Property apni bhabhi ke naam kar do", "gaadi bhai ke naam register karwa lo" — Pakistan mein ye baatein aam hain, lekin 2017 ke baad se in mein se kai practices legally "benami transaction" ban gayi hain jinki bohat sakht saza hai. Ye guide Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Act 2017 explain karta hai — kya cheez benami hai, kya nahi, aur kaise is risk se bachein.

TL;DR

Benami = property jiski qeemat ek banda deta hai lekin naam kisi aur ka hota hai — true ownership chupane ke liye. Penalty: 1-7 saal jail, bhari fine, aur property confiscation. Spouse/child ke naam property tab tak safe hai jab tak unki apni declared income ho.

Benami Transaction Kya Hai

Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Act 2017 ke tehat, "benami transaction" wo hoti hai jahan:

  • Property ki qeemat ek banda (beneficial owner) deta hai
  • Property kisi aur ke naam (benamidar) par register hoti hai
  • Maqsad ye hota hai ke true ownership ko chupaya jaye — tax se bachne ke liye, unexplained wealth chupane ke liye, ya legal liability se bachne ke liye

Exceptions — kya benami NAHI hai: Agar property spouse, bachon, ya kisi close relative ke naam hai lekin unki apni declared/known income source se khareedi gayi hai, aur wealth statement mein sahi documented hai — ye generally benami nahi mani jati. Trustee-beneficiary aur fiduciary relationships (jaise legal guardian) bhi generally exempt hain.

Penalty aur Confiscation

Sakht Saza: Benami Act ke tehat penalty mein rigorous imprisonment (1 se 7 saal tak), property ki fair market value ka significant fine, aur sab se ahem — property ka confiscation (zabt) shamil hai. Dono benamidar (jiske naam property hai) aur beneficial owner (asal maalik) is penalty ke liable hote hain.

FBR Benami Zones — Enforcement Mechanism

FBR ne is Act ko implement karne ke liye specialized Benami Zones aur Adjudicating Authority establish ki hai. Ye zones property registration records, wealth statements, aur bank transactions ko cross-check karte hain — mismatch ya suspicious ownership pattern flag hone par investigation shuru hoti hai.

Red flags jo investigation trigger karte hain: Kam-income wale banday ke naam expensive property, wealth statement mein declared income se mismatch, aur ek hi source se multiple properties different logon ke naam par register hona.

Benami Risk Se Kaise Bachein

  • Property hamesha apne hi naam par khareedein jahan tak mumkin ho
  • Agar spouse/bachon ke naam property karni hai, unki apni income source clearly document karein
  • Wealth statement mein har property ka funding source transparently declare karein
  • Property purchase se pehle apne tax consultant se documentation review karwayein
  • Kabhi bhi domestic help, driver, ya low-income relative ke naam property na rakhein

Aksar Puchhe Gaye Sawal — Benami Transaction Law

Benami transaction kya hoti hai Pakistan mein?
Benami transaction wo hoti hai jahan property ki qeemat ek banda (beneficial owner) deta hai, lekin property kisi aur ke naam (benamidar) par register hoti hai, aur asal maqsad true ownership ko chupana hota hai. Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Act 2017 iss ko ghair-qanooni qarar deta hai.
Kya spouse ya bachon ke naam property rakhna benami hai?
Zaroori nahi. Agar spouse ya bache ke naam property unke apne known/declared income source se li gayi hai — aur wealth statement mein sahi tareeqay se documented hai — ye generally benami nahi mani jati. Risk tab hota hai jab funds ka source declared na ho ya spouse/child ki koi independent income hi na ho.
Benami transaction ki penalty kya hai?
Act ke tehat penalty mein rigorous imprisonment (1 se 7 saal tak), fine jo property ki fair market value ka ek significant hissa ho sakta hai, aur sabse ahem — property ka confiscation (zabt) shamil hai. Dono benamidar aur beneficial owner is penalty ke liable hote hain.
FBR Benami Zones kya hain?
FBR ne Benami Act ko implement karne ke liye specialized "Benami Zones" aur Adjudicating Authority establish ki hai jo suspected benami transactions ki investigation aur adjudication karti hai. Ye zones property records aur wealth statements cross-check karke mismatch flag karte hain.
