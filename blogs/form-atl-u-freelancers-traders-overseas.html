Form ATL-U for Freelancers, Shopkeepers, Sole Proprietors and Overseas Pakistanis: Late Return Without the Rs 25,000 Surcharge

19 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 12 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 19 November 2026
Business owners, freelancers and overseas Pakistanis each face the late-return problem differently. Here is how Form ATL-U applies to each.

For a freelancer the ATL affects bank profit and remittance accounts; for a shopkeeper it affects what suppliers and banks deduct; for an overseas Pakistani it affects property sales and bank profit at home. The fix is the same law, but the return behind it differs a lot. Here is what each group needs to know.

TL;DR

Form ATL-U, under Section 182A(3), is available to any individual who files the return after the due date and undertakes not to obtain ownership or beneficial interest in any property for six months. That includes freelancers, sole proprietors (who are individuals carrying on business in their own name) and overseas Pakistanis who file in Pakistan. It is not available to an association of persons or a company, which must pay Rs 50,000 or Rs 100,000 to join the ATL after a late return. For individuals who are not salaried, the Section 182 minimum penalty is Rs 50,000, reduced by 75% if the Tax Year 2026 return is filed by 30 November 2026.

Section 182A(3) begins "shall not apply to an individual". A partnership's partners are individuals, but the firm's return is the AOP's, and its ATL status is the AOP's. The undertaking cannot be given for the firm.

Freelancers

Most freelancers do not buy property within months of filing, which makes Form ATL-U a natural fit. What needs care is the return:

  • Foreign remittances received through banks, Payoneer-linked accounts or exchange companies should match the income declared.
  • Withholding on export proceeds, where your bank deducted it, is credited in the return.
  • If you are registered with PSEB, the treatment of your IT export income depends on the rules that apply for the year; we check this rather than assume.
  • The wealth statement must explain how bank balances grew.

Our freelancer tax return guide covers the return itself.

Shopkeepers and Sole Proprietors

A sole proprietor is an individual doing business in their own name, so the undertaking is available. Three cautions:

  • Buying a shop or godown for the business within six months is acquiring property. If expansion is planned, pay the surcharge.
  • Business assets like stock and equipment are part of running the business. The law's wide words ("any property") leave some doubt about major fixed-asset purchases; discuss them before signing.
  • Section 182 minimum penalty for non-salaried individuals is Rs 50,000, before the time-based reduction. Section 182 penalty, as it stands in the Ordinance amended up to 30 June 2026: the higher of 0.1% of tax payable per day or Rs 1,000 per day; minimum Rs 10,000 for an individual with 75% or more of income from salary and Rs 50,000 in other cases; maximum 200% of tax payable; reduced by 75%, 50% or 25% if the return is filed within one, two or three months after the due date.

Overseas Pakistanis

Whether you must file depends on your residence status and Pakistan-source income. Many non-residents file because they own property here, earn rent or profit here, or plan to sell. If you filed the Tax Year 2026 return late, you face the same Section 182A consequences as a resident.

The biggest stake is usually a property sale. Section 236C collects 2.75% from a seller on the ATL and 11.5% from one off it. On a Rs 2 crore house, that is Rs 550,000 against Rs 2,300,000. An overseas seller who is not buying in Pakistan in the next six months can furnish Form ATL-U and sell at the filer rate. Our guide on selling property as a late filer explains the timing.

Form ATL-U is furnished on IRIS, so it can be done from abroad. Our overseas Pakistani tax return guide covers residence and filing.

If You Are an AOP or Company

There is no undertaking route. The firm or company pays the surcharge to be included in the ATL after a late return, and the Section 182 penalty applies. If the business is really run by one person and registered as an AOP by mistake, that is a registration question to sort out for future years; it cannot be fixed by signing Form ATL-U now.

Steps for All Three Groups

  1. Gather income records: remittances, sales, rent, profit.
  2. Prepare the Tax Year 2026 return and wealth statement.
  3. File on IRIS; pay tax and any penalty.
  4. Furnish Form ATL-U; save the acknowledgement. See the IRIS steps.
  5. Check the ATL after the next update.

A graphic designer in Multan

Receives about USD 1,500 a month from clients abroad through a bank. Filed Tax Year 2026 late. No property plans. Route: Form ATL-U. The return must match the remittances in the bank statements, and the wealth statement must show how savings grew. With us: Rs 7,000.

A pharmacy owner in Gujranwala

Runs the pharmacy as a sole proprietor. Filed late. Planning to buy the shop he rents in April 2027. Route: pay the surcharge. Form ATL-U would be breached by the purchase, and he needs the filer rate under Section 236K when he buys.

Owns a plot in Islamabad and a house in Rawalpindi, files in Pakistan because of rent and a planned sale. Filed late. Selling the plot in February 2027; no purchase planned. Route: Form ATL-U from abroad on IRIS, so the sale is taxed at 2.75%. With us: Rs 8,000.

A Note on Residence for Overseas Filers

Your tax position in Pakistan depends heavily on whether you are resident for the tax year, which turns on the days you spent here and other tests in Section 82. A non-resident is taxed in Pakistan on Pakistan-source income; a resident on worldwide income. Get this right before filing, because it changes both the return and the wealth statement. It does not change your eligibility for Form ATL-U, which depends only on being an individual who filed late.

Avoiding This Next Year

Business and freelance returns take longer than salary returns because figures must be put together. Start in July, not September. The Tax Year 2027 due date under Section 118 is 30 September 2027, and an extension, if one comes, is never guaranteed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a freelancer use Form ATL-U?
Yes. A freelancer is an individual, and the undertaking is open to individuals who filed late and will not obtain property for six months.
Can a sole proprietor use Form ATL-U?
Yes. A sole proprietor is an individual. Buying a shop or other property for the business within six months would breach the undertaking.
Can a partnership firm use Form ATL-U?
No. An AOP must pay Rs 50,000 to be included in the ATL after a late return.
Can overseas Pakistanis use Form ATL-U?
Yes, if they file in Pakistan as individuals. It is furnished on IRIS, so it can be done from abroad.
What is the late-filing penalty for a freelancer?
The higher of 0.1% of tax payable or Rs 1,000 per day, minimum Rs 50,000 for non-salaried individuals, reduced by 75% if filed within one month of the due date.
Does Form ATL-U help an overseas seller of property?
Yes. If you are not buying in the next six months, it puts you on the ATL so that 2.75% applies under Section 236C instead of 11.5%.
Can I buy business equipment during the six months?
The law says 'any property'. Routine stock is part of the business; for major fixed assets, take advice before signing.
How much do you charge?
Freelancer or sole proprietor Rs 7,000 and overseas Rs 8,000, each including Rs 2,000 for Form ATL-U.
Does residence status affect Form ATL-U?
No. Eligibility depends on being an individual who filed after the due date. Residence affects what income goes in the return.
I plan to buy my rented shop. Should I use Form ATL-U?
No. Buying the shop is acquiring property. Pay the surcharge so you are on the ATL when you buy.
Do I need to visit your office?
No. Freelancers, traders and overseas clients can complete everything on WhatsApp and email.
