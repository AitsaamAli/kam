How to Add a Business to Your NTN on IRIS: Form 181 Guide for Sole Proprietors

10 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 10 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 10 November 2026
FBR lets you update business details yourself through Form 181 on IRIS, but some changes, such as discontinuing a business, still need a visit to your Regional Tax Office.

Many people register an NTN as salaried individuals and later start a business, or open a second shop, without updating FBR. The return then shows business income from a business FBR does not know about, and bank or marketplace records do not match. This guide explains how to add a business properly and what to do when the online route is blocked.

TL;DR

A sole proprietor's business is added to the owner's existing NTN through Registration Form 181 (filed for modification) on IRIS. FBR's own page lists what this form can update: mobile number, personal address, business address, business branches, legal representative under Section 87, and bank account. Discontinuing a business, deregistration, change of jurisdiction, CNIC, email and POC updates need a visit to your Regional Tax Office (RTO) with documents. Tax practitioners reported problems with the online Form 181 facility in 2026, so check whether it is working before you plan around it.

Why Your Business Must Be on Your NTN

Section 181 of the Income Tax Ordinance requires every taxpayer to register in the prescribed form, and for individuals the CNIC is the NTN. A sole proprietorship has no separate NTN; its details sit on the owner's registration. Keeping them current matters because:

  • Banks and payment processors match the business name and account to FBR records.
  • Customers who deduct withholding tax from your payments report it against your NTN, and you claim it in your return.
  • Online marketplaces and couriers must confirm vendors are registered (Section 181(1A)).
  • Notices and audits start from the registration data. Business income from an unregistered business invites questions.

What Form 181 Can and Cannot Change

FBR's page "Change your personal details" (checked 10 November 2026) splits changes into two groups:

Step-by-Step: Adding a Business on IRIS

IRIS screens change from time to time; use these steps as a guide and follow the labels on screen.

  1. Prepare the details: business name exactly as on your signboard and invoices, business address, the date the business started, nature of business, and the business bank account (if already opened).
  2. Log in to IRIS with your CNIC and password.
  3. Open the registration modification form (Form 181) for income tax.
  4. Add the business in the business section: name, start date and principal activity. Add each branch separately if you trade from more than one place.
  5. Add or update the business address and the bank account.
  6. Attach supporting documents where IRIS asks for them, such as a rent agreement or ownership proof for the premises and a utility bill.
  7. Submit and keep the acknowledgement. Check that the registration certificate shows the business once processed.

Documents by Type of Premises

Mismatches between the address on IRIS, the utility bill and the bank's records are the most common reason an update is questioned. Use the same spelling of the business name and address everywhere.

If the Online Form 181 Is Not Working

In August 2026 ProPakistani reported that the Pakistan Tax Bar Association asked FBR to restore the "change in particulars" (Form 181) facility on IRIS, which had been suspended for sales tax registered persons, with users reporting problems for individuals too. If you cannot submit online:

  • Keep screenshots of the error and the date you tried.
  • Visit or write to your Regional Tax Office with the documents, as FBR already requires for the changes in the right-hand column above.
  • Do not delay your return: declare the business income correctly in the return even while the registration update is pending, and keep proof that you tried to update.

After the Business Is Added

  • Use the same business name on invoices, the bank account and marketplace profiles.
  • Declare the business income and expenses in your next return; our sole proprietor return guide explains the schedules.
  • Keep records from the start date for six years, as Section 174 requires.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a sole proprietorship get its own NTN?
No. The business is added to the owner's registration, and for individuals the CNIC is the NTN under Section 181(4).
Which form adds a business to my NTN?
Registration Form 181, filed for modification, on IRIS. FBR lists business address, business branches and bank account among the details it updates.
Can I close a business online through Form 181?
No. FBR's page says discontinuance of business and deregistration require a visit to your Regional Tax Office with relevant documents.
Can I change my email on IRIS myself?
FBR's page lists email among the changes that require a visit to the RTO.
I have two shops. Do I need two NTNs?
No. Add the second location as a business branch on your registration through Form 181.
What if Form 181 shows an error?
Keep evidence of the error, contact or visit your RTO with documents, and declare the business correctly in your return in the meantime.
Should I add the business before opening a bank account?
It helps. Banks usually check tax registration, and once the account is open you can add it to IRIS through Form 181.
