Do Ya Zyada Rental Properties — Combined Rental Income Aur Expense Claims

September 2026 Kamboh Associates 10 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Tax Consultant | NTN & Compliance Specialist

Jin logon ke paas ek se zyada kiraye ki properties hain, unke liye return sirf "rent add kar do" jitna simple nahi hota. Har property ki apni alag entry, apna alag tenant, aur kabhi kabhi apna alag khali/bhara hua period hota hai. Yeh guide specifically un logon ke liye hai jinke paas do ya zyada rental properties hain — single property wale case se yeh kaafi mukhtalif hai.

TL;DR

Har rental property ki income return mein alag entry ke tor par ja sakti hai, phir sab ko mila kar total rental income par tax lagta hai. Expenses bhi per-property basis par claim hoti hain — ek property ka repair kharcha doosri property ki income ke khilaf minus nahi hota. Ek property khali reh jaye to us ka bhi apna treatment hai. Wealth statement mein har property ka apna record hona chahiye.

Har Property Ki Alag Entry — Lekin Ek Hi Return

Agar aapke paas do ya zyada properties kiraye par hain, to return ek hi hota hai — aap har property ke liye alag return nahi bharte. Lekin us ek return ke andar, "Income from Property" ke section mein har property ki apni alag entry hoti hai: alag gross annual rent, alag deductions, alag net income. Sab entries ko mila kar aapki total rental income banti hai, aur usi total par tax slab lagta hai.

Practical misaal: Ek sahab ke paas Lahore mein ek portion aur Islamabad mein ek flat kiraye par hai. Return mein dono ki alag alag entry jayegi — Lahore wali property ka apna rent aur apne deductions, Islamabad wali ka apna — aur phir dono ka net total mil kar tax calculation ka basis banega.

Expenses Per-Property Basis Par Claim Hoti Hain

Yeh woh nuqta hai jahan log sab se zyada confuse hote hain. Rental income par allowable deductions — repair allowance, property tax, insurance, loan ka interest, rent collection charges — har property ke liye alag se calculate hoti hain, us property ke apne rent aur apne kharche ke hisab se. Ek property ka bara repair kharcha doosri property ki rental income ke khilaf pool kar ke minus nahi kiya ja sakta jaise chahen.

Har property ka apna record rakhna — alag file ya alag folder mein — saal ke aakhir mein yeh calculation kaafi aasan bana deta hai.

Ek Property Khali Reh Jaye To

Agar saal ke kisi hisse mein koi property khali rahi (tenant nahi tha), to us arse ke liye us property se koi rental income declare nahi hoti — lekin us arse ke expenses (jaise property tax jo aapne khud ada kiya) abhi bhi claim ho sakte hain, qanoon ke mutabiq. Yeh farq document karna zaroori hai: property kab tak khali thi aur kab se tenant aaya, is ki tareekhen record rakhein.

Mukhtalif Tenants Aur Unki Withholding

Agar mukhtalif properties ke mukhtalif tenants hain — kuch withholding agent (company, bank) hain aur kuch individual — to har tenant ki taraf se hone wali katouti ka record alag rakhna zaroori hai. Return mein credit claim karte waqt har property ke tenant ki withholding ko sahi jagah match karna chahiye, taake koi credit miss na ho.

Har property ke tenant se quarterly withholding proof (challan/certificate) lein.
Agar koi tenant individual hai (withholding agent nahi), to us property par koi withholding credit nahi hoga — yeh farq samajhna zaroori hai.

Properties Alag Alag Sheheron Mein Hon To

Agar aapki properties mukhtalif sheheron mein hain — misal ke tor par ek Lahore, ek Islamabad, ek Karachi — to return ke liye yeh koi masla nahi banata, kyunke federal income tax return sab sheheron ki income ko ek hi jagah combine karta hai. Lekin practically records ko sheher ke hisab se organize karna faida-mand hota hai, khaas tor par jab har sheher ka apna property tax authority aur apna document-issuing office ho. Kisi bhi property ka record uske apne sheher ke hisab se rakhna, saal ke aakhir mein confusion se bachata hai.

Furnished/Serviced Property — Alag Consideration

Agar aapki koi ek property simple kiraye par hai lekin doosri furnished/serviced (jaise guest house ki tarah, cleaning/security shamil) hai, to yeh dono properties ka tax treatment mukhtalif ho sakta hai — pehli property income, doosri business income ban sakti hai. Is farq ko samajhna zaroori hai kyunke dono ka return mein alag section aur alag calculation hota hai.

Wealth Statement Mein Har Property Ka Record

Wealth statement mein bhi har property ki apni alag entry honi chahiye — apni cost, apna ownership record. Agar aapne is saal koi nayi property add ki hai (kiraye ke liye ya khud ke liye), to us ka funding source clear hona chahiye. Sab properties ka combined kiraya aur us se hone wala bank balance ka izafa reconciliation mein tally hona chahiye.

Ek practical tareeqa yeh hai ke wealth statement mein har property ke sath us ki current rent status (kiraye par hai, khali hai, ya khud istemal mein hai) bhi note kar len — chahe yeh column IRIS ka formal requirement na ho, aapke apne record ke liye yeh clarity aage chal kar bohat kaam aati hai, khaas tor par jab kisi saal koi property khali rahi ho.

Ek Worked Example

Farz karein ek sahiba ke paas teen rental properties hain: ek DHA mein (simple kiraya), ek Bahria Town mein (kuch mahine khali rahi), aur ek chhota commercial portion jahan tenant khud ek registered company hai. Unka return is tarah banega: teen alag entries — har ek ki apni gross rent aur apne allowable deductions. Bahria Town wali property ke khali mahinon ka record alag rakha jayega taake un mahinon ke liye zero rent declare ho lekin property tax jaisi expenses phir bhi claim ho sakein. Commercial tenant ki withholding ka credit sirf us property ke against milega, kyunke sirf wahi tenant registered withholding agent hai — baqi do properties ke individual tenants ki taraf se koi withholding credit nahi banega.

Teen properties ka net rental income mil kar ek total banayega, aur usi total par tax slab lagega — lekin calculation ka poora tareeqa per-property basis par hi hoga, sirf final number combine hoga.

Documents Checklist

Har property ka apna rent agreement, tareekh aur muddat ke sath.
Har property ka registry/ownership document.
Har tenant ki taraf se withholding proof (agar applicable ho).
Har property ke kharche — property tax, insurance, repair — alag alag record kiye hue.
Khali rahe mahinon ki tareekhen, agar koi property saal ke kisi hisse khali thi.

Common Ghaltiyan

Sab properties ka rent ek hi entry mein jama kar dena: har property ki alag entry honi chahiye, sirf total nahi.
Ek property ka bara kharcha doosri ki income se pool karna: deductions per-property basis par calculate hoti hain.
Khali period ka record na rakhna: baad mein yeh explain karna mushkil ho jata hai ke kis mahine kiraya kyun nahi aaya.
Kisi ek property ko chhupa lena: registry record system mein already mojood hai, mismatch turant nazar aata hai.

Frequently Asked Questions

Mere paas teen rental properties hain — kya teen alag return banenge?
Nahi, return ek hi hota hai. Lekin Income from Property section mein har property ki apni alag entry hogi — teen alag gross rent aur teen alag deductions, jo mil kar ek total banayenge.
Ek property ka repair kharcha doosri property ki income se minus kar sakte hain?
Nahi. Deductions har property ke apne rent ke khilaf, us property ke liye, calculate hoti hain. Ek property ka kharcha doosri ki income ke khilaf pool nahi hota.
Ek property saal ke kuch hisse khali rahi — kya karein?
Us arse ke liye rental income declare nahi hogi, lekin us arse ke allowable expenses (jaise property tax) abhi bhi claim ho sakte hain. Khali/bhara hua period ki tareekhen record rakhein.
Kya ek property chhupa kar sirf doosri declare kar sakte hain?
Bilkul nahi. Har property ka registry record FBR ke system mein already mojood hai. Kisi ek ko chhupana mismatch bana deta hai jo turant nazar aata hai.
Furnished aur simple rental property ka treatment alag hota hai?
Ho sakta hai. Simple kiraya property income hai, lekin furnished/serviced arrangement (cleaning, security shamil) business income ki taraf ja sakta hai. Dono ka return mein alag section hota hai.
Ek property ka tenant company hai, doosri ka individual — credit mein farq hoga?
Ji haan. Company/bank jaisa withholding agent tax kaat kar jama karwata hai, jo credit ban jata hai. Individual tenant withholding agent nahi hota, is liye us property par koi withholding credit nahi banega — sirf declared rental income hi taxable rahegi.
Teen properties ke expenses record karne ka asaan tareeqa kya hai?
Har property ke liye alag file ya digital folder rakhein jahan us property ka rent agreement, kharche ki receipts aur withholding proof mahine-ba-mahine save hote rahein. Saal ke aakhir mein yeh calculation kaafi aasan bana deta hai.
Ek property is saal bech di, doosri abhi bhi kiraye par hai — return mein kya farq hoga?
Bechi gayi property ki rental income sirf us arse tak declare hogi jab tak woh aapke paas thi, aur uski bikri ka apna alag capital gains/withholding treatment hoga. Doosri property normal tareeqe se apni entry mein chalti rahegi — dono ek hi return mein sath reflect hongi.
Naya property portfolio banate waqt shuru se kya tayyari karni chahiye?
Har property ki khareed ke waqt hi apna alag record shuru kar dein — registry, funding source aur agar kiraye ke liye hai to rent agreement. Yeh aadat saal ke aakhir mein return banate waqt bohat waqt bachati hai.
Multiple properties ka return banwane mein normal se zyada waqt lagta hai?
Thora zyada waqt lag sakta hai kyunke har property ki apni entry aur apna reconciliation hota hai, lekin agar records pehle se organized hon to yeh farq minimal hota hai.
Kya properties ki fee tadaad ke hisab se badhti hai?
Ji haan, individual return ki fee properties ki tadaad aur tafseel par depend karti hai. WhatsApp par apni properties ki tafseel bhej dein, hum exact quote de dete hain.
