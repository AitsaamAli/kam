How to File Income Tax Return on FBR IRIS 2026 - Step by Step

2027-04-04
Kamboh Associates
12 min read
By Aitsaam Ali — Tax Consultant | NTN • SECP • Sales Tax Specialist — FBR Active Filer
TL;DR — Key Takeaways
  • IRIS (iris.fbr.gov.pk) is FBR's official online portal for income tax return filing filing, NTN registration, and payment
  • IRIS username is your CNIC number or NTN — password is set during first-time registration
  • The return filing section in IRIS is under "Income Tax → Returns → Filing"
  • Most WHT credits auto-populate from CPR data — verify them against your WHT certificates
  • Always download and save the "Acknowledgment Receipt" after submission as proof of filing

FBR IRIS (Integrated Revenue Management System) is Pakistan's official online tax portal for filing income tax returns, registering NTN, making tax payments, submitting wealth statements, and responding to FBR notices. IRIS was redesigned in recent years to improve usability, though first-time users often find the navigation confusing. This guide provides a section-by-section walkthrough of filing your TY2026 income tax return on IRIS.

IRIS Login and Account Setup

To access IRIS for return filing:

  1. Go to iris.fbr.gov.pk in your browser (Chrome or Firefox recommended — avoid Internet Explorer)
  2. If you are a new user, click "Registration for Unregistered Person" and complete NTN registration first
  3. Existing users: enter your CNIC number (without dashes) as the username and your IRIS password
  4. If you have forgotten your password, click "Forgot Password" — a reset link is sent to the mobile number registered with FBR (the number you provided during NTN registration)
  5. After login, the IRIS dashboard shows your taxpayer name, NTN, tax circle, and registration status
Mobile number mismatch issue: The most common IRIS password reset failure is a mobile number that has changed since NTN registration. If the OTP does not arrive, visit your local FBR RTO with your CNIC to update the registered mobile number before the password can be reset online.

How to Enter Income on IRIS

Salary Income (Section 12): In the Income tab, select "Income from Salary." Enter employer name, NTN, and annual salary amount. Include bonus, allowances, and any perquisites received. If you had multiple employers during the year, add each separately. IRIS computes the tax on total salary using the applicable slab rates.

Business Income (Section 18): Enter gross revenue, cost of sales, and operating expenses to arrive at net profit. Ensure turnover is correctly entered — FBR crosschecks declared turnover against Section 113 minimum tax (1% of turnover). If you have books of accounts, prepare a simple profit and loss statement before starting the return.

Property Income (Section 15): Declare annual rental income received. Allowable deduction is 20% flat for repairs and maintenance. Enter tenant name and address if the rent is from commercial property. WHT certificates from tenants who are WHT agents should be entered in the WHT Credits tab.

Foreign Income: Foreign income earned by Pakistan residents must be declared under the relevant income head (salary, business, or other income). Enter in Pakistani rupees using the State Bank of Pakistan exchange rate on the date of receipt or the annual average rate.

Common IRIS Errors and How to Fix Them

  • "NTN/CNIC mismatch": Occurs when the NTN in your IRIS profile does not match your CNIC. Contact FBR helpline (051-111-772-772) or your RTO to align the records
  • "WHT credit not appearing": Some WHT credits take 7–14 days after bank payment to appear in CPR. If your employer's WHT deduction is not showing, enter it manually in the WHT Credits tab using the CPR number on your tax deduction certificate
  • "Wealth statement reconciliation error": IRIS flags when closing wealth does not reconcile with opening wealth + income − expenditure. Review each entry — most reconciliation errors are caused by forgotten prior-year assets or improperly entered bank balances
  • "Return already submitted": You can only submit once — to make corrections, use the "Revised Return" option under Income Tax → Returns. Revised returns can be filed within 5 years
  • Session timeout: IRIS sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity. Save your draft frequently using the "Save as Draft" button to avoid losing entered data

After Submission — What to Do

  1. Download the Acknowledgment Receipt: Immediately after submission, IRIS shows a confirmation page. Download and save the acknowledgment receipt (PDF) — this is proof of filing
  2. Verify on IRIS Filed Returns: Check Income Tax → Filed Returns to confirm your TY2026 return appears with status "Submitted"
  3. Check ATL status: Visit atl.fbr.gov.pk within 48 hours to verify you now appear as an active filer
  4. Inform your bank: If switching from non-filer to filer, inform your bank branch so they apply the reduced WHT rate (15% instead of 30%) on bank profit going forward
  5. File advance tax if required: If your annual tax exceeds Rs. 1 million and you are a business owner or professional, set up a quarterly advance tax payment schedule for TY2026–27

IRIS Pro Tips for Faster Filing:
  • Use Chrome or Firefox in incognito/private mode to avoid cached session conflicts with IRIS
  • Fill in income entries first, then deductions, then WHT credits — this order reduces reconciliation errors
  • If IRIS is slow during peak season (August-September), try filing during off-peak hours: early morning (5am-8am) or late night (11pm-1am)
  • Keep a PDF copy of each section as you complete it — if IRIS crashes, you have your entries documented
  • The wealth statement "Reconciliation" button at the bottom checks if your closing wealth is consistent with your declared income — use it before submitting

Frequently Asked Questions

What is my IRIS username?

Your IRIS username is your 13-digit CNIC number without dashes (e.g., 3520212345678). If you registered with an NTN before CNIC-based login was introduced, your username may be your 7-digit NTN. Try both if unsure. The password is what you set during initial IRIS registration — if forgotten, use the "Forgot Password" option to reset via registered mobile OTP.

My employer already deducted WHT — do I still need to file a return?

Yes. Employer WHT deduction under Section 149 satisfies the tax payment obligation but does not replace the return filing obligation under Section 114. You must still file your own annual return on IRIS declaring your income, wealth statement, and WHT credits. The filed return is what places you on the ATL as an Active Taxpayer List (ATL).

How do I save progress on IRIS without submitting?

Use the "Save as Draft" button in the IRIS return interface. IRIS sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity and unsaved data is lost. Save as draft frequently — drafts are accessible in Income Tax → Returns → In Progress. You can work on a draft over multiple sessions before final submission.

I made an error in my submitted return — can I correct it?

Yes. File a revised return under Section 114(6) of the Income Tax Ordinance 2001. In IRIS, go to Income Tax → Returns → Revised Return and select the tax year. You can revise within 5 years of the original filing due date. FBR accepts revised returns without penalty for genuine corrections — however, if the revision significantly increases income, FBR may review the reasons.

Why does IRIS show a different tax amount than I calculated?

IRIS applies automatic calculations based on your income entries and the latest tax slabs programmed into the system. Discrepancies usually arise from: (1) income entries being categorized under the wrong head, (2) deductions entered incorrectly, (3) WHT credits not matching CPR data, or (4) minimum tax under Section 113 being applied when declared profit is below the minimum threshold. Review each tab carefully before submission.

Can I file IRIS from outside Pakistan?

Yes. IRIS is accessible from any country with internet access. Overseas Pakistanis commonly file their Pakistan income tax returns directly on IRIS from abroad. If IRIS is inaccessible due to geo-blocking in some countries, use a VPN with a Pakistan server. Alternatively, authorize a tax consultant in Pakistan to file on your behalf via the IRIS agent access feature.
