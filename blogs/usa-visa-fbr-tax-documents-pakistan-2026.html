USA Visa Ke Liye Pakistan FBR Tax Documents — Mukammal Guide (2026)

28 August 2026Aitsaam Ali12 min read2026 Verified

USA Pakistani applicants ke liye sab se mushkil aur sab se ghair-yaqeeni manzil hai. Wajah yeh nahi ke wahan requirements zyada hain — balke yeh ke wahan requirements ek muqarrara number ki shakal mein hoti hi nahi. Germany aapko bata deta hai ke blocked account mein €11,904 chahiyen. Canada bata deta hai ke CAD 23,448 chahiyen. Amreeka aapko koi number nahi deta. Woh sirf yeh dekhta hai ke aapki poori kahani mantaqi hai ya nahi — aur us kahani ka wazan aapke kagazon se aata hai.

TL;DR:

  • US visa ke liye koi tay-shuda "proof of funds" figure nahi hota — is ki jagah consular officer ki apni raay hoti hai.
  • Zyada tar non-immigrant visas mein qanoon yeh farz karta hai ke aap wahan reh jayenge; is farz ko aapko todna hota hai — Pakistan ke sath apne rishtay dikha kar.
  • F-1 ke liye SEVIS I-901 fee $350 aur visa application (MRV) fee $185.
  • EB-5 mein source of funds ka standard sab se sakht hai — har dollar ka qanooni source aur us par tax compliance sabit karna hoti hai.
  • H-1B ke liye bahar se apply karne walon par ek $100,000 supplemental fee ka masla chala aa raha hai jo abhi adalaton mein hai — filing se pehle current status confirm karna zaroori hai.
  • Kamboh Associates: NTN Rs. 2,000, return Rs. 3,500–5,000/saal, wealth statement Rs. 2,500, SECP company registration Rs. 15,000.

USA ka nizam baqi mulkon se alag kyun hai

Is site par mojood baqi country guides mein ek cheez mushtarak hai: har mulk aapko ek number deta hai. Germany blocked account mein €11,904 maangta hai. Canada study permit ke liye CAD 23,448. Australia apni financial capacity ki figure deta hai. Aap us number ko poora karte hain aur us hisse se faarigh ho jate hain.

Amreeka aisa koi number nahi deta — aur yeh na dena ittefaqi nahi, jaan boojh kar hai.

US nizam mein faisla ek consular officer ke haath mein hota hai jo aapke saamne baitha hota hai aur aapki poori file — kagaz, jawab, halaat — ko mila kar ek raay banata hai. Koi checklist nahi jise tick kar ke aap "qualify" kar jayein.

Amali natija: Amreeka ke case mein documentation ka maqsad kisi threshold ko chhoona nahi hota. Maqsad yeh hota hai ke aapke bayan ke peeche wazan paida ho. Aur wazan ek jagah se aata hai — aise records se jo aapne pehle se, kisi aur maqsad ke liye, sarkar ke saamne jama karwa rakhe hon. Isi liye purani tax returns Amreeka ke case mein aksar naye bank statements se zyada qeemti hoti hain.

Sab kuch ek interview par

Doosra bara farq yeh hai ke US visa ka faisla aksar chand minute ke interview mein hota hai. Officer ke saamne aapki file to hoti hai, lekin uske paas har file ke liye bohat mehdood waqt hota hai.

Is ke do amali sabaq hain. Pehla: aapke kagazon ko foran samajh aane wala hona chahiye. Ek saaf, tarteeb wala document jo ek nazar mein poori tasveer dikha de, bikhri hui bees registryon se kahin behtar hai. Dusra: aapke zubani jawab aur aapke kagaz ek doosre se mail khane chahiyen — agar aap keh rahe hain ke walid ki dukan achhi chalti hai aur unki return mein mamooli income likhi hai, to yeh tazaad officer ko foran nazar aa jata hai.

Aur ek baat jo Pakistani applicants ke liye khaas ahem hai: zyada tar non-immigrant visas (student, visitor, kai work categories) mein qanoon ek farz se shuru hota hai — ke aap Amreeka ja kar wahin reh jayenge. Yeh farz aap ke khilaf hota hai aur ise todna aap ki zimmedari hoti hai. Aur woh todta hai Pakistan ke sath aapke rishtay se: jaidad, khandaan, karobar, aamdani ka silsila.

Char rastay — aur har ek ka alag taqaza

Pakistan se USA ke char bare rastay hain, aur in ki documentation ki nauiyat aapas mein kaafi mukhtalif hai:

F-1 Student Visa — yahan sawal funding ka hai: kaun paisa de raha hai aur uski gunjaish kya hai. SEVIS I-901 fee $350 aur MRV fee $185. Sponsor ki income ka saboot markaz mein hota hai.
H-1B aur employment-based — yahan zyada tar bojh employer par hota hai, lekin Pakistan se apply karne walon ke liye ek makhsoos masla chala aa raha hai jis par alag guide mein tafseel hai.
EB-5 Investor Visa — is poori site par yeh sab se document-heavy rasta hai. Har dollar ka qanooni source sabit karna hota hai, aur us mein tax compliance seedha shamil hai.
B1/B2 Visitor Visa — yahan sawal ulta hai: aap wapas kyun aayenge? Yahan aapki Pakistani jaidad aur wealth statement asal dalael banti hain.

Har rastay par mukammal, alag guide mojood hai. Yeh safha un sab ka nuqta-e-aaghaz hai.

Aur isi wajah se waqt sab se ahem factor hai. Teen saal purani returns ka silsila aap do hafte mein nahi bana sakte. Yeh wo cheez hai jo sirf pehle se ki gayi ho to kaam aati hai — aur yehi wajah hai ke Amreeka ke liye tayyari doosre mulkon se pehle shuru karni chahiye.

Filer status ka paisa wala pehlu

Bohat se applicants filer banne ko sirf visa ki formality samajhte hain. Amreeka jaane walon ke liye is ka ek seedha maali pehlu bhi hai, khaas tor par kyunki US ke akhrajat (tuition, EB-5 investment, safar) aksar jaidad bech kar poore kiye jate hain.

Apna ATL status kabhi bhi check kar sakte hain — 9966 par SMS bhejein, format ATL <13-digit CNIC>.

Pehle se banaya hua record kyun sab se qeemti hai

Is poori guide mein agar sirf ek baat yaad rakhni ho to yeh: Amreeka ke case mein aap ke documents ki taaqat un ki umar se aati hai, un ki tadaad se nahi.

Wajah samajhne layak hai. Ek consular officer ke saamne roz aisi files aati hain jo khaas taur par visa ke liye tayyar ki gayi hain — taza bank statements, naye letters, aur wo tamam kagaz jo pichhle mahine wujood mein aaye. In sab ki ek mushtarak kamzori hai: yeh sab is maqsad ke liye banaye gaye hain jis maqsad ke liye pesh kiye ja rahe hain.

Iske muqable ek tax return jo teen saal pehle, apne mamool ke waqt par, kisi aur wajah se file hui thi — us mein yeh kamzori hai hi nahi. Woh us waqt bani jab visa zehan mein bhi nahi tha. Yehi cheez usay wazan deti hai.

Amali natija yeh hai ke Amreeka ki tayyari ka faisla waqt ka faisla hai, paise ka nahi. Jo shakhs aaj se filing durust karna shuru karta hai, uske paas do saal baad woh cheez hogi jo koi bhi raqam kharch kar ke us waqt nahi khareed sakta — ek purana, fitri, tasalsul wala record. Aur jo shakhs interview se do mahine pehle sab kuch banata hai, uske paas kagaz to poore honge magar wazan koi nahi hoga.

Isi liye hum aksar aise logon se bhi baat karte hain jinhone abhi Amreeka ka faisla nahi kiya — sirf soch rahe hain. Kyunki jis cheez ko banne mein do saal lagte hain, us ka faisla do saal pehle hi lena hota hai.

Aam ghaltiyan

1. Sirf bank statement par bharosa. Ek achha balance apne aap mein kahani nahi banata. Officer yeh dekhta hai ke wo balance kaise bana.

2. Aakhri waqt mein ek saal ki return. Interview se do mahine pehle file ki gayi akeli return ulta sawal khari karti hai.

3. Zubani bayan aur kagaz ka tazaad. Interview mein bara karobar bata kar mamooli declared income dikhana sab se aam self-inflicted masla hai.

4. Wapas aane ki wajah pesh na karna. Non-immigrant visas mein yeh sab se ahem hissa hai aur sab se zyada nazar-andaz hota hai.

5. Fees ka confusion. SEVIS fee aur visa application fee do alag cheezein hain, alag jagah adaa hoti hain, aur dono ki rasid interview mein darkar hoti hai.

Kab shuru karein

18–24 mahine pehle: agar NTN nahi hai to abhi banwayein. Amreeka ke case mein purana record hi asal sarmaya hai.
12 mahine pehle: pending returns clear karwana shuru karein. Yaad rahe individual/AOP ki deadline 30 September hai, company ki 31 December.
6 mahine pehle: wealth statement finalize karwayein.
Jaidad bechne se pehle: filer status confirm karein — sale ke din nahi, sale ke faisle ke waqt.

USA Visa & FBR Documents — Aam Sawalat

USA visa ke liye kitna paisa dikhana parta hai?
Koi tay-shuda figure nahi hai, aur yehi US nizam ka farq hai. Germany blocked account mein €11,904 maangta hai aur Canada study permit ke liye CAD 23,448 — Amreeka koi number nahi deta. Wahan consular officer aapki poori file dekh kar raay banata hai ke funding mantaqi hai ya nahi. Is liye documentation ka maqsad kisi threshold ko chhoona nahi, apne bayan ke peeche wazan paida karna hota hai.
Kya US visa ke liye FBR tax return lazmi hai?
Consulate isay ek alag lazmi checklist item ke tor par list nahi karti. Lekin amali tor par yeh sab se wazandar document hota hai, kyunki return ek aisa record hai jo aapne pehle se, kisi aur maqsad ke liye sarkar ke saamne jama karwaya hota hai — visa ke liye banaya gaya kagaz nahi. EB-5 mein to source-of-funds ka standard tax compliance ko seedha shamil karta hai.
F-1 visa ki fees kitni hain?
Do alag fees hoti hain. SEVIS I-901 fee $350 hai, jo I-20 par mojood SEVIS ID ke zariye FMJfee.com par adaa hoti hai. Us se alag visa application (MRV) fee $185 hai, jo embassy ke apne payment nizam se adaa hoti hai. Dono ki rasid interview mein darkar hoti hai, aur SEVIS fee ki adaigi processing mein chand kaam ke din leti hai — is liye interview se aakhri waqt par na chhoren.
Non-immigrant visa mein "ties to Pakistan" ka kya matlab hai?
Zyada tar non-immigrant visas mein qanoon yeh farz karta hai ke aap Amreeka ja kar wahin reh jayenge, aur is farz ko todna aap ki zimmedari hoti hai. Yeh todta hai Pakistan ke sath aapke rishtay se — apne naam par jaidad, khandani zimmedariyan, karobar, aur aisi aamdani jo yahan aati rehti hai. Yehi wajah hai ke wealth statement in cases mein khaas tor par qeemti hota hai.
Mera NTN nahi hai — kitna waqt lagega?
NTN registration khud jaldi ho jata hai aur Kamboh Associates par is ki fee Rs. 2,000 hai. Lekin asal cheez NTN nahi, us ke baad banne wala record hai — teen saal ki returns ka silsila do hafte mein nahi ban sakta. Isi liye Amreeka ke liye tayyari doosre mulkon se pehle shuru karni chahiye, aam tor par visa se 18 se 24 mahine qabl.
