DNFBP Registration Certificate, Status and Verification: What FBR's Own Texts Say (2026)

26 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 11 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 26 November 2026
People search for a DNFBP certificate more than anything else about DNFBPs. Here is what FBR's published texts actually say, and what they do not.

“DNFBP certificate”, “DNFBP certificate download” and “DNFBP certificate verification” are among the most searched DNFBP questions in Pakistan. Property dealers want something to show a buyer, a society or a bank; jewellers want proof for a landlord or a supplier. Much of what circulates online is guesswork. This guide sticks to what FBR has published, separates it from what it has not, and explains how to get, keep and update proof of registration.

Short answer

FBR's DNFBP Regulations require every DNFBP to be registered with the Board and to keep the registration up to date (regulation 3). FBR's AML/CFT pages send the “FBR DNFBP Portal Login” to the IRIS login, so registration runs through your IRIS account. FBR's Condition No. 2 (2022) says a registered society may “obtain Registration Certificate from the concerned Director, DNFBPs”, so the certificate comes from the Directorate that supervises you under S.R.O. 1497(I)/2026. None of the FBR texts we read describe a public online verification tool or a certificate fee. To confirm status, use your IRIS record and the Directorate or FBR's DNFBP helpline.

What the Regulations Require

Short answer: Regulation 3: every DNFBP shall be registered with the Board, give the information FBR requires and keep the registration up to date.

Regulation 3 of FBR's AML/CFT Regulations for DNFBPs 2020 is titled “Registration and market entry control of DNFBPs”. It has three parts:

Sub-regulationRequirement
3(1)Every DNFBP shall be registered with the Board
3(2)The DNFBP shall provide any information or documentation the Board requires for registration or for keeping the registration up to date, including criminal records of senior management and beneficial owners
3(3)The DNFBP shall notify the Board if it ceases operations as a DNFBP within thirty business days, and the Board shall deregister it if the appropriate information is provided

So registration is not a one-time event. It is a record that must stay accurate for as long as you operate, and it ends only through a notice from you.

Where Registration Happens: IRIS

Short answer: FBR's AML/CFT pages link the “FBR DNFBP Portal Login” to the IRIS login page.

FBR's AML/CFT pages for DNFBPs carry a link labelled “FBR DNFBP Portal Login”. It points to FBR's IRIS login page, the same system used for returns and NTNs. Registration therefore runs through the IRIS account of the person or entity that carries on the business: the individual for a sole proprietor, the AOP for a partnership, the company for a private limited company. If that account does not exist yet, the first step is an NTN. The steps inside IRIS are covered in our complete DNFBP guide.

The same pages list a separate link for reports: “File STR or CTR with the FMU via GoAML”. goAML belongs to the Financial Monitoring Unit, not FBR, and needs its own registration; see our goAML guide.

The Registration Certificate: Who Issues It

Short answer: FBR's Condition No. 2 refers to obtaining a Registration Certificate from the concerned Director DNFBPs once registered.

The clearest official reference to a certificate is in FBR's Condition No. 2 (2022), addressed to public sector development authorities. After requiring housing societies and real estate development authorities to register, it says they “may also be informed to obtain Registration Certificate from the concerned Director, DNFBPs once registered as a DNFBP with FBR.” Two points follow:

  • the certificate is issued by the Director DNFBPs who supervises you, not generated by a public website; and
  • it comes after registration, as evidence of it.

Which Director supervises you is fixed by S.R.O. 1497(I)/2026 (2 September 2026): Directors at Lahore, Islamabad, Karachi and Quetta, each for listed Regional Tax Offices, under the Director General's all-Pakistan jurisdiction. Our regional guides list the offices: Lahore and Punjab, Islamabad and Rawalpindi, Karachi and Sindh, Peshawar and KP, Quetta and Balochistan.

Status and Verification: What Is and Is Not Published

Short answer: FBR's published texts do not describe a public online verification tool. Confirm status through IRIS and the Directorate or helpline.

Question people askWhat FBR's published texts we read say
Is there an online DNFBP certificate download?No such facility is described. The certificate is referred to as obtained from the Director DNFBPs
Is there a public verification page?None is described in the Regulations, FAQs, conditions or FBR's AML/CFT pages we read
Is there a fee for registration or the certificate?None of those texts mention one
Where can a DNFBP ask?FBR's FAQs (Q25) give help.dnfbp.fbr@gmail.com and +92 51 9106606 for AML/CFT compliance queries
Is there an app?Yes. FBR publishes a DNFBP Mobile Application on Google Play and the Apple App Store; FBR's guidance says sanctions lists are available on it

In practice, your own proof of status is the registration record in your IRIS account plus the certificate from your Directorate. A counterparty, such as a society, bank or landlord, that wants to confirm your registration can be shown both; if they want confirmation from FBR itself, the Directorate is the authority to ask.

Keeping the Registration Current

Short answer: Keep registration details current; if you stop operating as a DNFBP, notify FBR within thirty business days so it can deregister you.

Regulation 3(2) requires information for “keeping the DNFBP registration up to date”. The changes that matter most are:

  1. a new owner, partner, director or beneficial owner, whose details and criminal-record information FBR may require;
  2. a new compliance officer, whose contact details FBR's FAQs (Q26) say must be shared with FBR;
  3. a new business address, which may also change your supervising Directorate under S.R.O. 1497(I)/2026;
  4. a change of category, for example a jeweller who stops taking cash of Rs 2 million or more, or a dealer who starts.

If you stop operating as a DNFBP, regulation 3(3) requires notice within thirty business days in the form FBR requires, and FBR then deregisters you. Until that happens, you remain a registered DNFBP and within FBR's supervision.

When You Will Be Asked for Proof

  • Development authorities processing a society's NOC or approval, under Condition No. 2; see developers and societies.
  • FBR inspections under regulation 15, where the inspector starts from your registration and compliance officer.
  • Counterparties such as banks and larger developers that run their own AML checks on the agents they work with.

Registration alone does not make a business compliant. The written programme, CDD files and goAML access are what an inspection tests; see our compliance programme guide and penalties guide.

Choosing the Right Category, and Businesses With More Than One

Registration records the category of DNFBP you are, and the category decides your duties. FBR's Regulations, as amended by S.R.O. 1439(I)/2026, apply to three groups:

CategoryWhen you fall in itThe duty that changes most
Real estate agentBuilders, developers, title transferring authorities, brokers and dealers when they execute, participate in or carry out transactional activity for property transfersCDD on buyers and sellers in every property deal (reg. 8(1)(a))
DPMSBullion dealers and sellers of jewellery, precious metals and stones, in a cash transaction of Rs 2 million or moreCDD triggered by cash of Rs 2 million or more (reg. 8(1)(b))
AccountantNon-ICAP/ICMAP practitioners carrying out the specified activities in the AML ActCDD when preparing for or carrying out those activities (reg. 8(1)(c))

Some businesses span two. A jeweller who also arranges plot sales for clients, or an accountant who forms companies and brokers property, carries on two DNFBP activities. The Regulations do not provide a combined category; regulation 3(2) requires any information FBR needs for registration, so the safe course is to declare every activity at registration and apply each set of CDD rules to the deals it covers. A category chosen too narrowly is a registration that is not “up to date”.

FBR's FAQs (Q25) give a dedicated email, help.dnfbp.fbr@gmail.com, and helpline, +92 51 9106606, for questions on AML/CFT compliance, including which category applies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a DNFBP registration certificate in Pakistan?
Yes. FBR's Condition No. 2 (2022) refers to obtaining a Registration Certificate from the concerned Director DNFBPs once registered as a DNFBP with FBR.
Can I download my DNFBP certificate online?
None of the FBR texts we read describe an online download. The certificate is obtained from the Director DNFBPs who supervises you.
How can I check my DNFBP registration status?
Through your IRIS account, which FBR's DNFBP portal link opens, and by asking your Directorate or FBR's DNFBP helpline (help.dnfbp.fbr@gmail.com, +92 51 9106606).
Is there a public DNFBP verification website?
FBR's Regulations, FAQs, conditions and AML/CFT pages that we read describe none. Confirmation comes from the Directorate.
Is there a fee for DNFBP registration?
None of the FBR texts we read mention a government fee for registration or the certificate. Our professional fee is fixed and confirmed before work starts.
What happens if I close my business?
Under regulation 3(3) you must notify FBR within thirty business days of ceasing operations as a DNFBP; FBR then deregisters you.
What is the FBR DNFBP helpline number?
FBR's FAQs give +92 51 9106606 as the dedicated helpline and help.dnfbp.fbr@gmail.com as the dedicated email for AML/CFT compliance queries.
Which DNFBP office covers Lahore?
Under S.R.O. 1497(I)/2026, the Director DNFBPs Lahore covers businesses in the jurisdiction of RTO-I and RTO-II Lahore, CTO Lahore, LTO Lahore and Multan, and the RTOs of Sialkot, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Sargodha, Multan, Sahiwal and Bahawalpur.
