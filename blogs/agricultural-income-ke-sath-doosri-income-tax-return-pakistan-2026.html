Agricultural Income Ke Sath Doosri Income — Provincial Aur Federal Return Ka Farq

September 2026 Kamboh Associates 10 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Tax Consultant | NTN & Compliance Specialist

Bohat se log jinke paas salary ya business income ke sath khandani zameen se agricultural income bhi hai, is baat mein confuse rehte hain ke apne federal tax return mein agricultural income ka kya karein. Kya usay dikhana hai? Kya us par tax lagega? Is guide mein hum specifically is combination — agricultural income + doosri income — ka federal return ke lehaaz se treatment dekhte hain.

TL;DR

Agricultural income Pakistan mein federal income tax se exempt hai, lekin provincial agricultural income tax authority ke tehat alag se taxable ho sakti hai — yeh do bilkul mukhtalif systems hain. Federal return mein agricultural income ko "exempt income" ke tor par declare karna zaroori hai taake wealth statement reconciliation match kare, chahe us par federal tax na lage. Doosri income (salary/business) par normal tareeqe se tax lagta hai. Agricultural income ko galat tarah "cover" ke tor par istemal karna FBR scrutiny ki wajah ban sakta hai.

Federal Exempt Lekin Provincial Taxable — Yeh Farq Kyun Hai

Pakistan ka constitutional structure agricultural income ko federal aur provincial governments ke darmiyan taqseem karta hai. Federal Income Tax Ordinance ke tehat, "agricultural income" ki definition mein aane wali income federal tax se exempt hai — yani FBR ka income tax is par nahi lagta.

Lekin is ka matlab yeh nahi ke agricultural income poori tarah tax-free hai. Har province apna alag Agricultural Income Tax (AIT) qanoon rakhta hai — Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, aur Balochistan ka apna apna system hai, apni apni relevant provincial revenue authority ke zariye collect hota hai. Yeh dono systems — federal aur provincial — bilkul alag hain, alag returns, alag authorities.

Yaad rakhein: "agricultural income exempt hai" ka matlab sirf "federal income tax se exempt" hai. Provincial AIT ka apna alag hisaab aur apni alag filing zaroorat ho sakti hai, jo aapki zameen ke province par depend karti hai. Har province ke rates aur structure mukhtalif hain — is topic ki gehrai mein jaane ke liye humari Punjab-Sindh-KP AIT comparison guide dekh lein.

Exempt Hone Ke Bawajood Declare Karna Kyun Zaroori Hai

Yahan sab se aam ghalat-fehmi hoti hai. Log sochte hain "agricultural income exempt hai to return mein zikr hi nahi karna." Yeh soch masla banati hai. Wealth statement ka poora usool yeh hai ke aapki total income (exempt aur taxable dono) aapki assets ke izafe ko justify kare. Agar agricultural income return mein bilkul zikr na ho lekin aapke bank balance ya assets mein izafa nazar aaye, to system yeh nahi jaan sakta ke yeh izafa kahan se aaya.

Is liye return mein ek specific "Exempt Income" ka section hota hai jahan agricultural income (aur doosri exempt categories) ko declare kiya jata hai. Yeh declaration tax nahi lagati — bas aapki wealth reconciliation ko complete karti hai.

Salary Ya Business Income Par Normal Tareeqa

Agar aapke paas agricultural income ke sath salary ya business income bhi hai, to woh doosri income normal tareeqe se hi taxable hai — agricultural income ki exemption sirf agricultural income tak mehdood hai, aapki baqi income ko "cover" nahi karti. Salary par employer ki withholding lagegi, business par apna advance tax aur record-keeping usool lagega — bilkul waise hi jaise agricultural income bilkul na hoti.

Agricultural Income Ko "Cover" Banane Ka Khatra

Kyunke agricultural income federal tax se exempt hai, kuch log isay ek "convenient explanation" ke tor par istemal karne ki koshish karte hain — yani jo income kahin aur se aayi hai, usay "agricultural income" ke tor par declare kar dena taake tax na lage. Yeh practice FBR ki nazar mein bohat aam aur bohat risky mani jati hai.

FBR agricultural income ke claims ko scrutinize karta hai — khaas tor par jab claim ki gayi raqam zameen ke size, crop type, aur us area ke typical yield se match na kare. Agar aapki agricultural income asal zameen se justify nahi hoti, to yeh sirf agricultural income ka masla nahi banta — poori wealth reconciliation par sawal aa jata hai.

Khabardar: agricultural income ka claim hamesha aapki zameen ke record, crop ki tafseel, aur us area ke reasonable yield se justify honi chahiye. Bila wajah bara claim karna asaan tarika nahi, khatarnak tarika hai.

Kaun Se Documents Zaroori Hain

Zameen ki ownership documents (registry, intiqal, fard).
Zameen kitne acres ki hai aur us par kya crop hoti hai, iski tafseel.
Agar zameen kisi aur ko kaash-e-karari (lease/rent) par di hai, to us ka agreement.
Crop bikri ka record — mandi receipt, ya jo bhi record maujood ho.
Provincial AIT ki filing/payment ka record, agar applicable ho.
Agar zameen inheritance mein mili hai, us ka succession/intiqal ka record — is se ownership aur funding dono ka sawal ek sath clear ho jata hai.

Provincial Obligation Alag Se Poori Karna

Federal return mein agricultural income declare karna provincial obligation ko poora nahi karta — yeh dono bilkul alag zaroorat hain. Agar aapki zameen ki income muqarrar threshold se zyada hai, to provincial AIT authority ke paas alag se filing ya payment ka apna process ho sakta hai, jo province se province mukhtalif hai. Is topic par mukammal tafseel ke liye Punjab, Sindh aur KP ke darmiyan farq samajhna zaroori hai.

Ek Worked Example

Farz karein ek sahab Lahore mein private job karte hain aur unki khandani zameen se agricultural income bhi hai, jo unke bhai zameen dekh kar bhejte hain. Unka federal return is tarah banega: salary normal tareeqe se "Salary" head mein aayegi, employer ki withholding credit ke sath. Agricultural income "Exempt Income" section mein declare hogi — chahe us par federal tax na lage, yeh declaration wealth statement ko complete karti hai, kyunke zameen se aane wali raqam unke bank balance ka hissa banti hai.

Agar unki zameen Punjab mein hai aur income threshold se zyada hai, to Punjab Land Revenue/AIT ke tehat ek alag provincial filing bhi zaroori ho sakti hai — jo federal return se bilkul mukhtalif process aur mukhtalif authority ke sath hoti hai. Dono ko sath-sath dekhna hi mukammal compliance banata hai.

Documentation Ka Practical Pehlu

Agricultural income ki documentation aksar us tarah organized nahi hoti jaise salary ya business ki hoti hai — khaas tor par jab zameen family ke naam par ho aur ek member sab dekh raha ho. Behtar practice yeh hai ke saal ke shuru mein hi ek simple record banayein: kitni zameen hai, us saal kya crop hui, kitni bikri hui aur kis tareekh ko paisa kis account mein aaya. Yeh record baad mein — chahe federal exempt-income declaration ho ya provincial filing — poori tarah kaam aata hai, aur agar FBR ya provincial authority ki taraf se kabhi sawal aa jaye, to jawab tayyar hota hai.

Ek aur baat jo aksar nazar-andaz hoti hai: agar zameen kai bhaion ke naam mushtarka hai, to agricultural income ka wealth statement mein declaration bhi usi tanasub se taqseem honi chahiye jis tanasub se malikiyat hai — bilkul waise jaise joint property ke rental income ka usool hota hai. Sirf ek bhai ke account mein poora paisa aana aur baqi bhaion ki wealth statement mein us ka koi zikr na hona, mismatch ki wajah ban sakta hai.

Common Ghaltiyan

Agricultural income ko bilkul declare na karna: exempt hone ke bawajood yeh wealth reconciliation ka hissa hai.
Ghair-mutabiq raqam claim karna: zameen ke size aur crop se match na karne wala claim scrutiny bulata hai.
Provincial AIT ko bhool jana: federal exemption provincial obligation ko khatam nahi karti.
Doosri income ko agricultural income ke naam par chhupana: yeh sab se risky ghalti hai.

Frequently Asked Questions

Agricultural income par federal tax lagta hai ya nahi?
Nahi, agricultural income federal income tax se exempt hai. Lekin provincial Agricultural Income Tax alag se lag sakta hai, jo province ke apne qanoon ke mutabiq hota hai.
Agricultural income exempt hai to return mein zikr karna zaroori hai?
Ji haan, zaroori hai. Wealth statement reconciliation ke liye agricultural income ko exempt income ke tor par declare karna hota hai, warna aapki assets mein izafa unexplained lag sakta hai.
Salary ke sath agricultural income ho to salary par tax kam lagega?
Nahi. Agricultural income ki exemption sirf agricultural income tak mehdood hai. Salary ya business income par normal tareeqe se hi tax lagta hai.
Kya doosri income ko agricultural income ke naam par declare kar sakte hain?
Bilkul nahi. Yeh FBR scrutiny ka sab se aam sabab hai. Agricultural income ka claim hamesha zameen ke size, crop type aur us area ke reasonable yield se match hona chahiye.
Provincial agricultural income tax ka federal return se koi taalluq hai?
Nahi, yeh bilkul alag system hai — alag authority, alag filing. Federal return mein exempt declaration provincial obligation ko poora nahi karti.
Zameen bhai dekhte hain, paisa sirf saal mein ek dafa mujhe milta hai — record kaise rakhoon?
Simple hai: kitni zameen, kya crop, kitni bikri aur kis tareekh ko paisa aaya — yeh chaar cheezein note kar len. Yeh record exempt-income declaration aur provincial filing dono ke liye kaafi hota hai.
Agar zameen ka koi hissa kiraye par (kaash) diya ho to kya farq hai?
Agar zameen kisi doosre kashtkar ko rent/lease par di hai, to us se milne wali raqam ka treatment agricultural income se mukhtalif ho sakta hai. Apna exact arrangement bata kar confirm karayein.
Zameen se hone wali income har saal alag hoti hai — kya har saal wealth statement mein farq nazar aana chahiye?
Ji haan, aur yeh bilkul theek hai. Crop ki paidawar mausam ke mutabiq har saal badalti hai, is liye rental/agricultural inflow ka har saal alag hona natural hai. Zaroori sirf yeh hai ke jo bhi raqam declare ho, us saal ke bank record se match kare.
Kya agricultural income ke liye NTN hona zaroori hai?
Agar aapki koi doosri income (salary, business) hai jis ki wajah se NTN zaroori hai, to agricultural income bhi usi return ke exempt-income section mein chali jaati hai. Sirf agricultural income wale case mein NTN ki zaroorat alag se assess honi chahiye.
