How We Handle a Less-Than-Perfect Experience

Not every case is straightforward, and being transparent about this matters more than pretending every interaction is flawless. When a client raises a concern — a delay, a miscommunication, or a document that was misplaced in the process — the standard response is:

  • Acknowledge the issue directly on WhatsApp rather than deflecting or delaying a response
  • Identify whether the cause was internal (team error) or external (FBR system delay, bank processing time) and communicate that honestly
  • Fix the underlying problem at no additional cost if the fault was internal
  • Follow up after resolution to confirm the client is satisfied with the outcome

This is also why referrals remain the primary source of new clients — a firm that handles problems well when they occur earns more trust than one that has simply never been tested. Prospective clients are encouraged to ask directly, during the initial WhatsApp conversation, how a similar past issue was resolved for another client in a comparable situation.
