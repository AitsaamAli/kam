Agriculture & Cotton Tax in Bahawalpur

Bahawalpur's economy is heavily agriculture-based — cotton, wheat, and livestock. Tax treatment of agricultural income is a common source of confusion:

Cotton Ginners — WHT on Purchases: When cotton ginners purchase raw cotton from farmers, they must deduct WHT at 5% under Section 153 if the farmer is a non-filer. This WHT is deposited to FBR monthly. We help cotton ginners maintain compliance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a Bahawalpur cotton farmer need to file FBR taxes?
Pure agricultural income is taxed provincially under Punjab AIT, not by FBR. However, if you also have non-agricultural income (e.g., a shop, rent from urban property), you must file FBR return. Also, if you sell agricultural land, FBR capital gains tax applies.
What taxes apply when buying/selling property in Bahawalpur?
Buyer pays 4% WHT on purchase value if non-filer, 2% if filer (Section 236K). Seller may pay capital gains tax — 10% if held under 3 years, 0% after 4 years (for open plots). Being on ATL halves your property transaction tax.
