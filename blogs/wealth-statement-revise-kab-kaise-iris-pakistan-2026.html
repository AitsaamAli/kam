Wealth Statement Revise Karna — Kab Zaroori Hai Aur IRIS Mein Kaise Hota Hai

September 2026 Kamboh Associates 10 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Tax Consultant | NTN & Compliance Specialist

Return theek ho sakta hai lekin us ke sath jaane wali wealth statement mein ghalti reh sakti hai — kisi asset ki value ghalat likhi gayi, koi liability chhoot gayi, ya reconciliation ka koi hissa match nahi kar raha. Log aksar samajhte hain ke sirf return revise karna kaafi hai, lekin wealth statement ka apna alag mahal hai aur usay bhi zaroorat par khud revise karna parta hai.

TL;DR

Wealth statement ek alag dastawez hai jo return ke sath file hoti hai lekin apni ghaltiyon ki correction bhi khud mangti hai. Return theek hone ke bawajood agar wealth statement mein koi asset, liability ya reconciliation ka hissa ghalat reh gaya hai, to usay bhi revise karna zaroori hai — warna dono kaghazat aapas mein match nahi karte. IRIS mein wealth statement ki revision return ke revision se juri hui hai, lekin dono ko sath sath check karna zaroori hai. Jitni jaldi ghalti pakri jaye, utna asaan hota hai usay theek karna, kyunke muddat guzarne ke baad revision ka rasta bhi tang ho jata hai.

Ek saal ki filing ka masla aksar akela nahi hota — is ki jarrh doosre saal tak phaili ho sakti hai, is liye jaldi mein sirf woh ek entry theek kar ke aage barh jana kaafi nahi hota.

Wealth Statement Aur Return Do Alag Dastawez Hain

Bohat se log return aur wealth statement ko ek hi cheez samajhte hain, jab ke qanooni tor par yeh do alag dastawez hain jo sath sath file hote hain. Return aapki income aur us par lagne wale tax ka hisaab hai. Wealth statement aapki tamam assets, liabilities aur saal bhar ke reconciliation ka bayaan hai. Dono ek doosre se juray hue hain — income wealth mein izafe ki wajah banti hai — lekin har ek apni jagah alag file hoti hai aur apni alag ghaltiyan bhi rakh sakti hai.

Isi wajah se yeh mumkin hai ke aapka return bilkul sahi ho lekin wealth statement mein koi asset ghalat value par likha ho, ya koi liability likhna reh gayi ho. Aisi soorat mein sirf return check karna kaafi nahi, wealth statement ka bhi alag se jaiza lena zaroori hai.

Kab Wealth Statement Revise Karna Zaroori Hai

Kisi asset ki value ghalat likhi gayi ho (jaise property ki cost galat number par).
Koi bank account, gaari, ya investment likhna reh gaya ho.
Koi liability (jaise koi loan) darj karna reh gaya ho.
Reconciliation match nahi kar rahi — assets ka izafa aapki declared income se justify nahi ho raha.
Koi gift ya inheritance record mein aana chahiye tha lekin reh gaya.

Return Revise Kiya To Wealth Statement Ka Kya Hoga

Agar aap return revise kar rahe hain kyunke koi income source chhoot gaya tha, to us tabdeeli ka wealth statement par bhi seedha asar hota hai — jaise agar naya income declare ho raha hai, to us se banaya gaya asset ya us se hui bachat bhi wealth mein reflect honi chahiye. Sirf return revise kar ke wealth statement ko purani halat mein chhor dena galat hai, kyunke ab dono kaghazat ek doosre se match nahi karenge — jo apne aap mein ek naya sawal khara kar sakta hai.

Yaad rakhein: return aur wealth statement ki revision ko hamesha jode kar sochein, alag alag nahi. Jab bhi ek revise ho, doosre ka bhi jaiza le lein.

Sirf Wealth Statement Mein Ghalti, Return Theek Hai

Kuch cases mein return ki income sahi hoti hai lekin wealth statement mein koi asset ya liability ghalat likhi ja chuki hoti hai — misaal ke tor par kisi ne apni gaari ki value galti se do dafa likh di, ya koi purana asset jo ab bech diya gaya hai, us ki entry hatanamull bhool gaye. Aisi soorat mein sirf wealth statement ki revision se maamla theek ho sakta hai, return ko dobara chhedna zaroori nahi. Is soorat mein bhi zaroori hai ke revision se reconciliation dobara match ho jaye, warna sirf ek galti theek karte karte doosri ban sakti hai.

Ek Saal Ki Revision Agle Saal Ko Bhi Chhoo Sakti Hai

Wealth statement ki khaasiyat yeh hai ke har saal ki closing position agle saal ki opening ban jati hai. Agar aap ek purane saal ki wealth statement revise karte hain, to zaroori hai ke dekhein kahin us tabdeeli ka asar agle saal (ya us se bhi agle saal) ki opening position par to nahi para. Agar para hai to un saalon ki wealth statement bhi consistency ke liye check karni parti hai — sirf ek saal theek kar ke chhor dena, agle saalon ka reconciliation tor sakta hai.

IRIS Mein Practical Process

Pehle pehchanein ke wealth statement ki kaun si entry ghalat hai aur sahi number kya hona chahiye.
Agar return bhi revise ho raha hai, to dono ka data aapas mein match karke rakhein.
IRIS mein "revise wealth statement" ka option istemal karein, poore statement ko dobara review karein, sirf ek entry nahi.
Reconciliation ka total zero ya sahi figure par aana chahiye — agar nahi aa raha to koi aur entry bhi ghalat ho sakti hai.
Submit karne ke baad naya acknowledgement download karein.

Aam Ghaltiyan

Sirf return revise karna, wealth statement bhool jana.
Ek saal ki revision karke agle saalon ka asar check na karna.
Reconciliation match kiye bina hi revision submit kar dena.
Sirf ek entry theek karna jab ke poore statement ka jaiza lena chahiye tha.

Wealth Statement Revision Ke Sath Documentation Rakhein

Jab bhi wealth statement mein koi tabdeeli ki jaye — chahe asset ki value theek ki ja rahi ho ya koi naya liability shamil kiya ja raha ho — us tabdeeli ka asal sabot apne paas rakhein. Agar property ki value theek kar rahe hain to us naye number ka source (registry, valuation) rakhein. Agar koi liability shamil kar rahe hain to loan agreement ya bank statement rakhein. Yeh documentation sirf is waqt ke liye nahi, balke agar kabhi aainda is saal ka jaiza liya jaye, tab bhi aapki position ko foran saaf kar deti hai.

Ek Ghalti Doosri Ghalti Ki Taraf Ishara Kar Sakti Hai

Jab aap ek galti theek karne ke liye wealth statement khol rahe hon, to yeh mauqa hai ke poore statement ka ek baar mukammal jaiza le lein, sirf woh ek entry nahi jo aap ko nazar aayi. Aksar aisa hota hai ke ek ghalti dhoondte waqt koi doosri chhoti si cheez bhi nazar aa jati hai jo pehle miss ho gayi thi. Ek hi baar mein sab theek kar lena, baar baar revision karne se kahin behtar hai.

Joint Assets Ki Revision Mein Ehtiyat

Agar wealth statement mein koi asset joint ownership mein hai — jaise property ya bank account kisi aur family member ke sath sanjha hai — to revision karte waqt yeh dhyan rakhein ke aapka hissa sahi tarah reflect ho, poori cheez nahi. Kabhi kabhi log galti se poori asset apne naam par likh dete hain jab ke asal mein sirf ek hissa unka hai. Revision ke doran yeh mauqa hai ke aisi galtiyan bhi pakri jayein aur theek ki jayein, taake sab co-owners ka record aapas mein match kare.

Revision Ke Baad Agle Saal Ki Filing Par Nazar Rakhein

Jab bhi kisi purane saal ki wealth statement revise ki jaye, agle saal ki filing (agar woh already ho chuki ho) par bhi ek nazar dalna zaroori hai. Agar us mein bhi wohi purani ghalti dohrai gayi thi, to sirf ek saal theek karne se poora silsila theek nahi hoga. Behtar hai ke revision ke turant baad agle saal ka bhi jaiza le liya jaye, taake continuity ka silsila mukammal tor par saaf ho jaye.

Wealth Statement Revision Ki Bhi Apni Muddat Hai

Bilkul return ki tarah, wealth statement ki revision ke liye bhi ek muqarrar muddat hoti hai jis ke andar yeh process mumkin hota hai. Yeh muddat return ki muddat se juri hui ho sakti hai, is liye jab bhi return revise karne ka waqt aaye, us ke sath sath wealth statement ka bhi jaiza le lein — alag alag waqt par dono ko revise karne ki koshish karne se behtar hai ke ek hi baar mein dono ka mukammal jaiza liya jaye. Muddat guzarne ke baad revision mushkil ho sakta hai, is liye jitni jaldi ghalti nazar aaye utni jaldi qadam uthana chahiye.

Har Saal Ki Wealth Statement Ka Apna Digital Record Rakhein

Ek practical aadat yeh hai ke har saal ki submitted wealth statement ka apna alag PDF ya screenshot sambhal kar rakhein, sirf IRIS ke record par bharosa na karein. Agar kabhi IRIS ke apne system mein koi technical masla ho ya aapko purana record foran dekhna ho, to apni khud ki file se turant reference mil jata hai. Yeh aadat khaas tor par tab kaam aati hai jab aapko kisi bank ya visa application ke liye purane saalon ki wealth statement ka hawala dena ho.

Aakhir mein, wealth statement ko sirf ek "annual formality" ki tarah nahi, balke apni maali zindagi ka ek saalana snapshot samajhein. Jitna zyada dhyan aap is dastawez ko dete hain, utna hi asaan har agle saal ki filing hoti jati hai, kyunke record ki continuity khud ba khud sahi rehti hai aur ghaltiyan bhi kam hoti jati hain.

Yeh khidmat sirf ek dafa ki correction nahi — hum aainda saalon ke liye bhi aapka record consistent rakhne mein madad karte hain, taake yeh masla dobara na ho aur har saal ki filing pehle se asaan hoti jaye.

Frequently Asked Questions

Return theek hai lekin wealth statement mein ghalti hai — kya sirf wealth statement revise ho sakti hai?
Ji haan. Yeh do alag dastawez hain aur agar sirf wealth statement mein koi asset ya liability ghalat likhi gayi hai, to sirf usay revise karna kaafi ho sakta hai, return ko chhedna zaroori nahi.
Return revise kar diya, kya wealth statement bhi revise karni chahiye?
Aam tor par ji haan. Agar return ki tabdeeli (jaise nayi income) wealth mein bhi asar dalti hai, to wealth statement ko bhi is asar ke sath match karna zaroori hai, warna dono kaghazat aapas mein tazad karenge.
Kya ek purani wealth statement revise karne se agle saalon par bhi asar parta hai?
Ho sakta hai. Har saal ki closing position agle saal ki opening banti hai. Agar purane saal mein tabdeeli aayi hai to agle saal(on) ki wealth statement bhi consistency ke liye check karni chahiye.
Reconciliation match nahi ho rahi — kya karoon?
Yeh signal hai ke koi asset, liability, ya income ka izhaar adhoora hai. Poore wealth statement ka dobara jaiza lein, sirf ek entry nahi — aksar ek se zyada jagah masla mil jata hai.
IRIS mein wealth statement revise karne ka process kya hai?
Pehle ghalat entry pehchanein, phir IRIS mein "revise wealth statement" option se poora statement dobara review karke sahi karein. Reconciliation ka total sahi figure par aana chahiye, phir submit kar ke naya acknowledgement download karein.
Wealth statement revision ki fee kya hai?
Simple revisions normal wealth statement fee (Rs. 2,500) ke daire mein hoti hain. Agar case multi-year ya zyada paicheeda hai to andaza case dekh kar diya jata hai.
