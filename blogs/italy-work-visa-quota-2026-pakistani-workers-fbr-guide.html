Italy Ka Naya Work Visa Quota — Pakistani Workers Ke Liye Mukammal Guide (2026)

28 August 2026Aitsaam Ali11 min read2026 Verified

Italy ne Pakistan ke liye ek makhsoos work visa quota mukarrar kiya hai — reports ke mutabiq 2026 se 2028 tak kul 10,500 visas, yani tagreeban 3,500 har saal. Yeh Pakistani workers ke liye Europe ka sabse khula qanooni darwaza hai jo is waqt maujood hai. Lekin is ke sath ek aisi cheez juri hui hai jis par tagreeban koi baat nahi karta: aap ka Pakistan-side ka record, jo aap ke jaane ke baad bhi chalta rehta hai.

TL;DR:

  • Italy ka 2026 ka kul quota 164,850 workers ka hai, jo Decree-Law 146/2025 (baad mein Law 179/2025) ke tehat set hua.
  • Pakistan un mulkon mein shamil hai jinke sath Italy ka migration cooperation agreement hai — is liye Pakistan ke liye alag reserved allocation maujood hai.
  • Process employer se shuru hota hai (nulla osta), aap se nahi. Koi blocked account ya proof-of-funds nahi chahiye.
  • Nulla osta milne ke baad worker ke paas 180 din hote hain ke Italian Embassy/Consulate se visa ki darkhwast de.
  • Italy aapse FBR documents nahi maangta — lekin remittance, property aur wapsi ke liye aapka Pakistani tax record aage chal kar zaroori ho jata hai.

Quota mein asal mein hai kya

Italy har saal ek decree jaari karta hai — Decreto Flussi — jismein woh tay karta hai ke us saal kitne non-EU workers ko kaam ke liye aane ki ijazat hogi. 2026 ke liye yeh tadaad 164,850 workers hai, jo Decree-Law 146/2025 ke zariye set hui aur baad mein Law 179/2025 mein tabdeel ho gayi.

Is bare number ke andar chhote chhote hisse hain. Kuch hissa seasonal kaam ke liye, kuch non-seasonal ke liye, kuch khaas tor par domestic care workers ke liye (2026 mein 13,600, jo 2027 mein 14,000 aur 2028 mein 14,200 ho jayega). Aur ek hissa un mulkon ke liye reserve hai jinhone Italy ke sath migration cooperation ka agreement kiya hua hai.

Pakistan un mulkon ki fehrist mein shamil hai. Pakistani reports ke mutabiq is framework ke tehat Pakistan ke liye 2026 se 2028 tak 10,500 slots mukarrar kiye gaye hain — tagreeban 3,500 saalana — aur yeh Pakistan Ministry of Overseas Pakistanis and Human Resource Development ki koshishon ka natija hai.

Yahan aik ehtiyat zaroori hai. 10,500 ka figure Pakistani sarkari aur media source se aata hai. Italian decree khud country-wise breakdown is tafseel se publish nahi karta. Is liye isay ek mustanad indication samjhein, ek guaranteed contract nahi — aur apne case ke liye hamesha Italian Embassy ya official Gazzetta Ufficiale notification se tasdeeq karein.

Decreto Flussi kaise kaam karta hai — aur "click day" kya cheez hai

Yeh system Pakistan ke aam visa process se bilkul mukhtalif hai, aur yahi wo jagah hai jahan zyada tar log dhoka khate hain.

Sabse ahem baat: process aap se shuru nahi hota, employer se hota hai. Aap khud jaa kar "Italy work visa" apply nahi kar sakte. Ek Italian employer ko aapke liye nulla osta (work authorisation) ki darkhwast deni parti hai. Aur woh darkhwast sirf usi din di ja sakti hai jab quota portal khulta hai — jise "click day" kehte hain.

Click day par applications pehle aao pehle pao ki bunyad par process hoti hain. Bare demand waale categories ghanton mein khatam ho jate hain. Isi liye Italian employers apne documents mahino pehle tayyar rakhte hain taake portal khulte hi submit kar sakein.

Iska ek seedha natija hai: agar koi aap se yeh keh raha hai ke woh "guaranteed Italy work visa" dilwa dega, to woh aisi cheez ka wada kar raha hai jo uske ikhtiyar mein hai hi nahi. Nulla osta ek Italian employer aur Italian authorities ke darmiyan ka mamla hai. Koi Pakistani agent iski guarantee nahi de sakta.

Kaunse sectors khule hain

2026-2028 ke decree ne pehle ke muqable sectors ka daira kaafi barha diya hai. Non-seasonal work permits ab tagreeban har bare economic sector mein mumkin hain:

Construction — Pakistani workers ke liye sab se bara aur sab se realistic sector.
Agriculture, forestry aur fishing — khaas tor par seasonal work ka bara hissa yahin hai.
Manufacturing — food processing, textiles, metal products waghera.
Transport aur logistics — warehouse, delivery, driving.
Hospitality aur tourism — hotels, restaurants, tourism services.
Personal care aur domestic work — buzurgon aur maazoor afraad ki dekhbhaal, housekeeping. Is category ka apna alag, bara quota hai.

2026 mein tagreeban 76,000 se zyada visas non-seasonal jobs ke liye hain. Seasonal category alag hai aur uska apna hisaab hai — us par tafseeli baat humne alag guide mein ki hai, kyunki seasonal workers ke liye Pakistan ke tax qawaid ka asar bilkul mukhtalif tareeqe se parta hai.

Ek haqeeqat jo koi agent nahi batata

Quota ka number aur asal mein jaari hone wale visas ka number ek nahi hota — aur farq mamooli nahi hai.

2024 mein Italy ne 119,890 slots available kiye thay. Un mein se asal mein tagreeban 9,331 work permits hi jaari hue — yani tagreeban 7.8%. Baqi quota istemal hi nahi hua, kyunki employer-side documentation, verification, aur process ki peechidgi ne zyada tar applications ko mukammal hone hi nahi diya.

Pakistani applicants ke liye ek khaas nuqta: Pakistan se aane wali applications par Questura aur National Labour Inspectorate ki taraf se additional verification hoti hai. Reserved quota hone ka matlab yeh nahi ke process tez hai ya approval aasan hai — balke checks aam category se zyada ho sakte hain.

Yeh baat hausla shikni ke liye nahi likhi ja rahi. Yeh is liye likhi ja rahi hai ke aap kisi aise shakhs ko paisa na dein jo aapse yeh keh raha ho ke "quota khul gaya hai, seat pakki hai." Quota khulna aur aapka visa lagna do bohat door ki cheezein hain.

Italy aapse FBR documents nahi maangta — phir bhi yeh kyun zaroori hain

Yahan hum saaf baat karte hain, kyunki is site par kai country guides hain aur unmein se kuch mein tax documents visa ke liye lazmi hote hain. Italy ka work visa un mein shamil nahi hai.

Germany ke student visa mein aapko €11,904 ka blocked account dikhana parta hai aur us paise ka source sabit karna parta hai. UK visa mein UKVI khud NTN aur tax returns maangti hai. Italy ke Decreto Flussi route mein aisa koi step hai hi nahi — kyunki aapki maali zimmedari employer utha raha hai, aap nahi. Is liye agar koi aapse yeh keh raha hai ke "Italy visa ke liye tax return lazmi hai," to woh theek nahi keh raha.

To phir yeh guide is site par kyun hai? Kyunki asal masla visa ke baad shuru hota hai:

Aap paisa ghar bhejenge. Yeh hi is poore safar ka maqsad hai. Aur jo raqam Pakistan aati hai, uska record hona chahiye — khaas tor par agar us se property khareedni ho.
Aapke naam Pakistan mein kuch na kuch hoga. Zameen mein hissa, walidain ka ghar, bank account. In sab ka tax treatment aapke bahar jaane ke baad bhi chalta rehta hai.
Aap wapas aayenge. Seasonal workers har saal, aur permanent workers bhi aksar kuch saal baad. Us waqt aapka Pakistani record wahi hoga jo aap chhor kar gaye thay.
Filer aur non-filer ka farq paison ka hai. Property bechte waqt filer par 2.75% withholding lagti hai, non-filer par 5.5% — poora double.

Yani Italy ke case mein tax documentation "visa requirement" nahi, ek financial planning ka faisla hai. Aur usay jaane se pehle karna baad mein karne se kahin aasan hai.

Italy Work Visa & Pakistan Documentation — Aam Sawalat

Italy ne Pakistan ke liye kitne work visas reserve kiye hain?
Pakistani sarkari aur media reports ke mutabiq 2026 se 2028 tak 10,500 slots, yani tagreeban 3,500 saalana. Pakistan un mulkon mein shamil hai jinke sath Italy ka migration cooperation agreement hai, is liye Pakistan ke liye reserved allocation maujood hai. Italy ka 2026 ka kul quota 164,850 workers ka hai. Country-wise tafseel Italian decree khud is tafseel se publish nahi karta, is liye apne case ki tasdeeq Italian Embassy se karein.
Kya Italy work visa ke liye FBR tax return lazmi hai?
Nahi. Decreto Flussi route employer-sponsored hai — aapki maali zimmedari Italian employer utha raha hota hai, is liye blocked account ya proof-of-funds jaisa koi step nahi hai. Yeh Germany ya UK se bilkul mukhtalif hai. Lekin jaane ke baad remittance, Pakistan mein property aur wapsi ke waqt aapka tax record ahem ho jata hai — is liye NTN aur filing ka faisla visa ka nahi, financial planning ka masla hai.
Kya main khud Italy work visa apply kar sakta hoon?
Nahi, process employer se shuru hota hai. Ek Italian employer ko aapke liye nulla osta (work authorisation) ki darkhwast deni parti hai, aur woh bhi sirf us din jab quota portal khulta hai — jise click day kehte hain. Nulla osta approve hone ke baad aapke paas 180 din hote hain ke Italian Embassy ya Consulate se visa ki darkhwast dein.
Quota khulne ka matlab kya visa pakka hai?
Bilkul nahi, aur yeh farq bohat bara hai. 2024 mein Italy ne 119,890 slots available kiye thay lekin asal mein tagreeban 9,331 work permits hi jaari hue — yani tagreeban 7.8%. Sath hi Pakistan se aane wali applications par Questura aur National Labour Inspectorate ki additional verification hoti hai. Reserved quota ka matlab tez ya aasan process nahi hai.
Italy jaane se pehle kaunse Pakistani documents banwa lene chahiyen?
Kam az kam NTN registration (Rs. 2,000) aur agar koi pending returns hain to unhein clear karwa lena (Rs. 3,500 se 5,000 per saal). Wealth statement (Rs. 2,500) jaane se pehle assets ka baseline bana deta hai, jo baad mein Italy ki kamai se property khareedte waqt kaam aata hai. Permanently ja rahe hain to Section 8 tax clearance certificate (Rs. 5,000) bhi consider karein.
