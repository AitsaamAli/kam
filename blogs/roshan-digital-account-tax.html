Roshan Digital Account Tax Pakistan 2026 — NRP Tax Benefits

June 2026 Kamboh Associates 6 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Tax Consultant | NTN • SECP • Sales Tax Specialist

Tax benefits of Roshan Digital Account for non-resident Pakistanis 2026. Profit exemptions, repatriation, WHT rates — complete guide.

Main guide: for the complete overview, see our Roshan Digital Account tax guide.

TL;DR

Profit earned through a Roshan Digital Account, including Naya Pakistan Certificates, is generally subject to withholding tax that is final for non-residents, and the rate can differ by product from what resident savers pay — confirm the current rate for each product with your bank before investing.

Naya Pakistan Certificates and RDA Investment Products

Beyond simple deposit accounts, Roshan Digital Account holders can invest in Naya Pakistan Certificates (RDA's flagship savings instrument), Roshan Equity Investment for buying shares on the Pakistan Stock Exchange, and Roshan Samaaji Khidmat for social investment. Each of these carries its own tax treatment layered on top of the base RDA account rules: Naya Pakistan Certificate profit is taxed through withholding at the rate notified for these certificates, whether held in dollars, pounds, euros or rupees, while capital gains on PSX shares purchased through Roshan Equity Investment are subject to the standard capital gains tax rules applicable to any investor, filer status included, regardless of the RDA wrapper the investment came through. A common misconception among overseas Pakistanis is assuming the RDA's overall favorable framework means every product accessed through it is automatically tax-advantaged — in reality, each underlying investment type retains its own tax character, and the RDA is simply the access mechanism, not a blanket tax shelter covering everything purchased through it. Before investing through any RDA product, it's worth confirming the specific tax treatment for that exact instrument rather than assuming it inherits the treatment of the underlying deposit account — Roshan Equity Investment gains, for instance, are taxed the same way a resident filer's PSX trading gains would be, following the standard capital gains schedule by holding period rather than benefiting from any special reduced rate simply because the investor happens to be an overseas Pakistani accessing the market through an RDA account. This distinction trips up a fair number of first-time RDA investors who read about the account's deposit treatment and assume it extends automatically to every product available under the same umbrella, when in fact each individual investment vehicle needs to be evaluated on its own distinct tax merits, rules, and reporting requirements that a careful filer must understand thoroughly before investing rather than after the fact, once the next tax filing season eventually arrives.

Who Can Open an RDA

Roshan Digital Accounts are open to non-resident Pakistanis, holders of NICOP or POC cards, and resident Pakistanis who have foreign assets declared to FBR. The account is opened online without visiting a branch, and funds can be repatriated abroad without separate approval.

Buying Property Through an RDA

Overseas Pakistanis increasingly pay for houses and plots from RDA funds. The payment trail from the RDA is useful evidence of where the money came from. The usual property taxes still apply at registration: advance tax under section 236K on the buyer at 1.25% for persons on the Active Taxpayers List and 2.5% for others, for transfers from 1 July 2026. Filing a Pakistani return to appear on the list therefore reduces the cost of a purchase.

Do RDA Holders Need to File in Pakistan?

A non-resident whose only Pakistani income is RDA profit with tax deducted at source may not be required to file. Filing becomes worthwhile when you own Pakistani property, receive rent, sell assets or want the lower filer rates on purchases. We assess this for each overseas client.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is profit on a Roshan Digital Account tax-free?

Generally no. Profit on RDA deposits and Naya Pakistan Certificates is subject to withholding tax, which is usually final for non-residents. Rates vary by product.

Can I buy property in Pakistan with RDA funds?

Yes. The RDA payment trail helps prove the source of funds, but advance tax at registration still applies, with lower rates for active filers.

Do I need an NTN to open a Roshan Digital Account?

Non-residents do not need to be filers to open an account, but filing in Pakistan lowers withholding on property and other transactions.
