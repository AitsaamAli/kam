Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to file taxes online through a consultant?
Yes — Kamboh Associates uses FBR's official Iris portal for all filings. We handle your credentials with complete confidentiality. After filing, you receive the official FBR acknowledgement receipt directly. You can also verify your return status on the FBR portal using your NTN.
What documents do I need to share for online tax filing?
For salaried: CNIC + salary slip or Form 16 from employer. For business: CNIC + income/expense summary + bank statements. For property: CNIC + rental receipts or sale deed. All documents shared via WhatsApp are kept strictly confidential.
Can I track the status of my return after you file it?
Yes — we send you the FBR acknowledgement receipt (PDF) after filing. You can also log into fbr.gov.pk with your NTN to view your filed return anytime. We also keep records and can resend receipts if needed.
Do you offer online tax filing for overseas Pakistanis?
Yes — many of our clients are Pakistanis living in UAE, UK, USA, Saudi Arabia, and Europe. They share documents via WhatsApp or email and we handle all FBR compliance from Pakistan on their behalf. NRP returns, foreign income declaration, and ATL maintenance — all handled online.
