Italy Seasonal Work Visa Aur 182-Day Rule — Aapka Pakistani Tax Status (2026)

28 August 2026Aitsaam Ali11 min read2026 Verified

Italy ka seasonal work visa un logon ke liye hai jo saal ka ek hissa Italy mein kaam karte hain aur baqi Pakistan mein guzarte hain — aam tor par agriculture aur tourism mein, chhe se nau mahine tak. Aur yahin ek aisa tax sawal paida hota hai jo permanent migrants ka kabhi nahi hota: agar aap saal ka aadha waqt idhar aur aadha udhar guzarte hain, to Pakistan ki nazar mein aap resident hain ya nahi? Iska jawab seedha aapki jeb par asar daalta hai, aur zyada tar workers ko iska ilm hi nahi hota.

TL;DR:

  • Seasonal permits par Italy mein saal ke 6 se 9 mahine tak kaam kiya ja sakta hai, mainly agriculture aur tourism mein.
  • 2026 ke seasonal quota mein se 5,000 visas un workers ke liye reserve hain jo pichhle 5 saal mein kam az kam ek dafa Italy mein kaam kar chuke hain (2027 mein 6,000, 2028 mein 7,000).
  • Pakistan mein residency ka faisla 182-day rule par hota hai — aur seasonal workers aksar is line ke bilkul upar ya neeche baithte hain.
  • Agar aap Pakistan mein 182 din ya zyada rahe to us saal aap resident hain — aur resident ki poori income Pakistan mein zer-e-ghaur aati hai.
  • Pakistan ka tax year 1 July se 30 June hai, calendar year nahi — aur seasonal workers ke liye yeh farq bara asar daalta hai.

Seasonal visa kya hai aur kis ke liye hai

Italy ke Decreto Flussi mein seasonal category alag rakhi gayi hai. Iska maqsad un industries ko workers dena hai jinka kaam saal bhar nahi chalta — khaas tor par khet-baari (fasal ka mausam) aur tourism (garmiyon ka season). Permit aam tor par chhe se nau mahine ka hota hai, jiske baad worker apne mulk wapas jata hai.

Pakistani workers ke liye yeh route kai wajah se pur-kashish hai. Employer commitment chhota hota hai, is liye employers zyada aasani se hire karte hain. Aur worker ka Pakistan se rishta tootta nahi — ghar, khandaan, zameen sab yahin rehti hai.

Lekin isi khoobi mein ek uljhan chhupi hui hai. Permanent migrant ka case saaf hota hai: woh chala gaya, non-resident ho gaya, baat khatam. Seasonal worker ka case har saal naye sire se banta hai.

Multi-year seasonal permit — barhta hua faida

2026 ke decree mein ek cheez khaas tor par qabil-e-zikr hai: seasonal quota ke andar 5,000 visas un workers ke liye mukarrar hain jo pehle bhi Italy mein seasonal kaam kar chuke hain — sharat yeh ke pichhle paanch saal mein kam az kam ek dafa aaye hon. Yeh tadaad 2027 mein 6,000 aur 2028 mein 7,000 ho jayegi.

Iska matlab yeh hai ke pehla season sab se mushkil hai. Ek dafa aap system mein aa gaye, to agle saalon ke liye multi-year clearance ka darwaza khulta hai aur wapsi asaan ho jati hai.

Tax ke lehaz se yeh ahem kyun hai: agar aap ek dafa ka worker hain to yeh ek saal ka mamla hai. Lekin agar aap multi-year route par chal parte hain, to aap agle paanch se saat saal tak har saal Italy jayenge aur wapas aayenge. Us soorat mein aapka Pakistani tax status har saal dobara tay hoga — aur ek saal ki laparwahi ka asar agle saalon tak chalta hai.

Asal sawal: aap resident hain ya non-resident?

Pakistan mein residency ka faisla neeyat se nahi, dinon se hota hai. Bunyadi usool yeh hai: agar aap ek tax year mein Pakistan mein 182 din ya us se zyada maujood rahe, to us saal aap Pakistan ke resident samjhe jayenge.

Do baatein jo seasonal workers ke liye khaas ahem hain:

Pakistan ka tax year 1 July se 30 June tak chalta hai, calendar year nahi. Yeh Italian season ke sath match nahi karta, is liye din ginte waqt aapko Pakistani calendar par ginna hoga, Italian contract ke hisaab se nahi.
Yeh har saal alag se dekha jata hai. Aap ek saal resident ho sakte hain aur agle saal non-resident, sirf is bunyad par ke us saal aap kitne din kahan thay.

Aur yahan seasonal workers ki asal soorat-e-haal samne aati hai: chhe mahine ka permit matlab tagreeban 180 din Italy mein — yani tagreeban 185 din Pakistan mein. Yeh 182 ki line ke bilkul upar hai. Nau mahine ka permit matlab tagreeban 270 din Italy mein — yani tagreeban 95 din Pakistan mein, jo line se kaafi neeche hai.

Yani seasonal workers ke liye jawab ek nahi hota — woh permit ki lambai par badalta hai. Yeh baat aam guides mein kahin nahi milti, kyunki wo permanent migrants ke liye likhi gayi hoti hain.

Din ka hisaab — teen misalein

9 mahine ka seasonal permit~95Non-resident
7 mahine ka permit, 1 mahina Pakistan chhutti~180Line ke bilkul qareeb — hisaab zaroori

Teesri misaal sab se ahem hai, kyunki wohi sab se aam hai. Bohat se workers season ke beech mein ek do hafte ke liye ghar aate hain — shaadi, bimari, Eid. Har aisa chakkar aapke Pakistani dinon mein izafa karta hai aur aapko 182 ki line ke qareeb ya us ke paar le ja sakta hai.

Is liye ek aadat bana lein: apne passport ki entry aur exit stamps ka record rakhein. Har saal ke aane jaane ki tareekhein ek jagah likhein. Jab sawal uthta hai to yehi record aapka jawab hota hai — yaad-dasht nahi.

Resident rehne ka amali matlab kya hai

Bohat se seasonal workers yeh samajhte hain ke resident hona koi buri cheez hai. Aisa nahi hai — lekin uska matlab jaanna zaroori hai.

Resident hone ki soorat mein Pakistan aapki income ko wusee daire mein dekhta hai, jabke non-resident ki sirf Pakistan-source income zer-e-ghaur aati hai. Seasonal workers ke case mein iska amali natija yeh hota hai ke aapki Italy ki kamai ka Pakistani tax treatment us saal ke status par depend karta hai.

Yahan Pakistan aur Italy ke darmiyan maujood double taxation agreement ki ahmiyat aati hai. Pakistan ke 60 se zyada mulkon ke sath aise agreements hain aur Italy un mein shamil hai. In agreements ka maqsad yehi hota hai ke ek hi income par dono mulkon mein poora poora tax na lage — relief aam tor par tax credit ya exemption ki shakal mein milta hai.

Ek zaroori ehtiyat: treaty ka faida khud ba khud nahi milta. Uske liye aapko apni residency ka status document karna parta hai aur aksar residency certificate ki zaroorat parti hai. Har case ke halaat alag hote hain — permit ki lambai, kamai ka size, Pakistan mein baqi income — is liye apne case ka hisaab kisi tax professional se karwana behtar hai bajaye kisi aam qaida par bharosa karne ke.

Sabse mehngi ghalti: filing bilkul band kar dena

Yeh sab se aam ghalti hai jo seasonal workers karte hain, aur yeh mehngi is liye hai ke iska nuqsan tax se nahi, ATL se bahar ho jane se aata hai.

Soch yeh hoti hai: "main to Italy mein kama raha hoon, Pakistan mein kya file karun." Phir teen chaar season ke baad, jab aap ne Italy ki kamai se gaon mein zameen ka tukra khareedna ya bechna hota hai, tab pata chalta hai ke aap non-filer hain.

Hisaab lagayein. Farz karein aap paanch season Italy mein kaam kar ke gaon mein 50 lakh ka plot khareedte hain. Filer ke tor par Section 236K ki withholding 62,500 rupay banti hai. Non-filer ke tor par 1,25,000. Farf: 62,500 rupay — ek hi transaction par.

Aur saalana return file karte rehne ki cost? Rs. 3,500 se 5,000 per saal. Yani paanch saal ki poori filing ki cost tagreeban 25,000 rupay hai, jo ek hi property purchase ki bachat se kam hai. Aur agar aap woh plot aage chal kar bechte hain to farq aur bhi bara ho jata hai.

Har saal ka record kaise rakhein

Seasonal workers ke liye ek simple saalana routine kaafi hai:

Passport stamps ka record. Har season ki entry aur exit date ek copy par likh lein. Yeh aapke residency status ka bunyadi saboot hai.
Har remittance ka bank record. Paisa hamesha qanooni banking channel se bhejein. Ghair-rasmi zariye se aane wale paise ka koi record nahi hota — aur jab wohi paisa zameen khareedne mein lagta hai, tab uske source ka sawal uth sakta hai. Section 111 (unexplained income) ka poora daira isi qism ki suraton ke liye hai.
Saalana return, 30 September se pehle. Yeh Italy se bhi ho jati hai. Aapko season ke beech Pakistan aane ki zaroorat nahi.
ATL status ki tasdeeq. Return file karne ke baad 9966 par SMS bhej kar check karein — format: ATL <13-digit CNIC>. Return file hona aur ATL par naam aana do alag cheezein hain.

Poora kaam WhatsApp par hota hai. Na aapko season chhor kar aana parta hai, na Lahore mein hona zaroori hai — hamare kaafi clients kabhi daftar aaye hi nahi.

Seasonal Work & Pakistani Tax Status — Aam Sawalat

Italy mein 6 mahine kaam karne se kya main Pakistan mein non-resident ho jata hoon?
Zaroori nahi. Pakistan mein residency ka faisla 182-day rule par hota hai — agar aap us tax year mein Pakistan mein 182 din ya zyada rahe to aap resident hain. Chhe mahine Italy mein guzarne ka matlab tagreeban 185 din Pakistan mein hai, jo 182 ki line se upar hai. Yani chhe mahine ke seasonal permit par aam tor par aap resident hi rehte hain. Nau mahine ke permit par soorat ulti ho jati hai.
Pakistan ka tax year kab se kab tak hai?
1 July se 30 June tak. Yeh calendar year nahi hai, aur seasonal workers ke liye yeh farq ahem hai kyunki Italian season aur Pakistani tax year aapas mein match nahi karte. Din ginte waqt hamesha Pakistani tax year par ginna hai, apne Italian contract ke hisaab se nahi.
Multi-year seasonal permit kya hota hai?
2026 ke seasonal quota mein se 5,000 visas un workers ke liye reserve hain jo pichhle paanch saal mein kam az kam ek dafa Italy mein seasonal kaam kar chuke hain — yeh tadaad 2027 mein 6,000 aur 2028 mein 7,000 ho jayegi. Ismein multi-year clearance milti hai jo har saal wapas aana asaan bana deti hai. Yani pehla season sab se mushkil hota hai.
Kya Pakistan aur Italy ke darmiyan double taxation agreement hai?
Ji haan, Italy un mulkon mein shamil hai jinke sath Pakistan ka double taxation agreement maujood hai — Pakistan ke aise 60 se zyada mulkon ke sath treaties hain. Iska maqsad yeh hai ke ek hi income par dono jagah poora tax na lage, aur relief aam tor par tax credit ya exemption ki shakal mein milta hai. Lekin yeh faida khud ba khud nahi milta — residency status document karna parta hai, aksar residency certificate ke sath.
Seasonal worker hone ke bawajood filer rehna zaroori hai?
Qanooni zimmedari aapke us saal ke status aur Pakistan mein maujood income par depend karti hai. Lekin amali tor par filer rehna aapke apne faide mein hai — property khareedte waqt filer ko 1.25% withholding lagti hai aur non-filer ko 2.5%, aur bechte waqt 2.75% ke muqable 5.5%. 50 lakh ke plot par yeh farq 62,500 rupay ka hai, jabke saalana return ki cost Rs. 3,500 se 5,000 hai.
