🧳 Australia Move Karne Ke Baad Pakistan Tax Filing, NTN Aur FBR Status Ka Kya Hota Hai — 2026 Guide

August 25, 2026Aitsaam Ali10 min read2026 Verified

Australia PR mil gaya, samaan pack ho gaya — lekin ek sawal jo zyada tar log tab tak nahi puchte jab tak koi masla khara nahi ho jata: Australia move karne ke baad Pakistan mein NTN, tax filing, aur FBR status ka kya hota hai? Ye guide un logon ke liye hai jo already Australia ja chuke hain ya jaane wale hain, aur specifically Pakistan-side ki ongoing tax responsibility samajhna chahte hain.

TL;DR: Australia move hone ka matlab Pakistan tax obligations khatam hona nahi. 182-din rule ke mutabiq Pakistan residency status decide hoti hai, lekin Pakistan-source income (property rental, savings profit) non-resident hone ke baad bhi taxable rehti hai. Pakistan-Australia DTA double taxation se bachati hai. Annual non-resident return file karte rehna future property transactions ke liye zaruri hai.

"PR Aa Gaya" Aur "Tax Khatam Ho Gaya" — Do Alag Baatein Hain

Perth ya Sydney mein settle hote hi zyada tar naye migrants ka dhyan naya bank account kholne, Medicare register karwane, aur job dhoondhne par hota hai — Pakistan ki filing peeche reh jati hai, kyunke assumption ye hota hai ke PR mil jane se woh chapter automatically close ho gaya. Reality thodi different hai: Australian Home Affairs jo PR grant karta hai, uska Pakistan FBR ke record se koi automatic link nahi. FBR sirf ye dekhta hai ke aap ke naam par Pakistan mein kya assets registered hain aur aap ne tax year mein kitne din Pakistan mein guzare — kisi bhi doosre mulk ka visa ya residency status is calculation mein seedha factor nahi banta.

182-Din Rule Se Residency Kaise Nikaalte Hain

FBR ka test simple hai — ek tax year (1 July se 30 June) mein Pakistan mein guzare din ginenge. 183 ya usse zyada din ho toh resident, kam ho toh non-resident:

  • Resident classify hua: duniya bhar ki income Pakistan mein bhi taxable ban jati hai
  • Non-resident classify hua: sirf Pakistan mein kamaya gaya paisa taxable rehta hai, Australia ki tankhwah nahi
  • Jis saal move hui ho: us saal ka count karna zaruri hai kyunke half-saal Pakistan mein guzarna bhi resident bana sakta hai

Kai families PR flight book hote hi khud ko "non-resident" maan leti hain — jabke asal fact ye hai ke agar wapas aake kisi saal 183+ din Pakistan mein guzar jayen (jaise extended family visit ya business setup ke liye), wo poora saal firse resident tax treatment mein aa jata hai.

Purani NTN — Band Karein Ya Chalti Rehne Dein

Iska seedha jawab hai — chalti rehne dein. NTN band karwane ka koi practical fayda nahi, aur agar Pakistan mein zameen, ghar, ya kisi bhi tarah ki savings hai, ye zaruri hai:

  • ATL (filer list) mein naam rehne do: property khareedne-bechne par tax rate significantly kam hoti hai filers ke liye
  • NTN band ho jaye toh: Australia se paisa transfer karke Pakistan mein property lene mein bhi mushkil aa sakti hai, kyunke registration authorities NTN maangte hain

Reality check: Non-filer status Australia mein baithe ho toh bhi Pakistan ke financial transactions par asar dalta hai — property registry, bank ke bare transactions, sab jagah filer/non-filer ka farq lagu hota hai.

Ghar Do Hisso Mein Bant Gaya Ho Toh Residency Kaise Dekhen

Ek bohat common Australia-specific pattern — pehle husband ya wife akela move karta hai job set karne ke liye, baaki family (spouse, bacchon ki schooling ke saath) Pakistan mein 1-2 saal rukti hai. Is scenario mein residency status har family member ka apna-apna hota hai, poore ghar ka ek combined status nahi hota. Jo member Australia mein 183+ din guzar raha hai, wo non-resident ho sakta hai us saal ke liye, jabke Pakistan mein reh raha spouse resident hi rahega — aur uski apni income (agar koi ho) normal resident rate par taxable rahegi. Ye distinction important hai kyunke bohat se log galti se sochte hain ke "ek banda chala gaya toh poori family ka status badal gaya" — aisa nahi hota, har individual ka apna tax file hota hai.

Do Countries, Ek Income — DTA Kya Karti Hai

Australia aur Pakistan ke darmiyan ek treaty hai jo yehi ensure karti hai ke ek hi paisa par dono taraf pura tax na lag jaye:

  • Australia mein kamaya paisa ATO ko declare hota hai, normal tarike se
  • Agar Pakistan mein bhi koi income aa rahi hai (rent, profit), wo bhi ATO ko declare karni parti hai — kyunke Australia worldwide income par tax lagata hai apne residents se
  • Jo tax Pakistan mein pehle se pay ho chuka hai, uska credit Australia mein claim ho sakta hai — taake dono jagah full tax na kaTe

Ye process khud-ba-khud nahi hoti — dono returns sahi tarike se file karni parti hain, aur credit ka claim manually lagana parta hai, tabhi ye kaam karti hai.

Ghar Ya Plot Chhod Aaye Ho? Uska Tax Chapter Abhi Baaki Hai

Sabse zyada overseas Pakistanis ko FBR filing ki zarurat isi wajah se parti hai — Pakistan mein koi property reh gayi ho:

  • Kiraya aata hai toh: 15% flat tax lagta hai, chahe malik Australia mein baithe ho
  • Property bech do toh: Capital Gains Tax lagti hai, jitna arsa property rakhi thi us hisaab se rate kam-zyada hoti hai
  • Har transaction par: filer hone se advance tax ka rate seedha kam ho jata hai

Ek pattern jo baar-baar dikhta hai — Australia mein settle hone ke kuch saal baad, log apni Australian income se Pakistan mein naya plot ya flat le lete hain family ke liye. Wo remittance khud tax-free hai, lekin us paisay se khareedi gayi property wealth statement mein show honi chahiye — kyunke jab bhi wo aage bechi jaye, uske funds ka origin puchha ja sakta hai.

Wapasi Se Pehle — Section 8 Clearance Lena Kyun Sahi Rehta Hai

Agar Australia ka move permanent nature ka hai, departure se pehle Section 8 tax clearance certificate le lena ek smart move hai — ye certify karta hai ke FBR ki taraf se koi bakaya nahi. Ye koi qanooni zarurat nahi Australia visa ke liye, lekin ye ek "sab kuch clear hai" wala record chhor jata hai, jo Pakistan wapas aane ya property deal karne ke waqt kaam aata hai.

ATO Ko Jawab Dete Waqt Pakistan Ke Documents Kyun Chahiye

Agar Australia mein khud ka kaam ya foreign income ho, ATO worldwide income declare karwata hai. Us declaration ko support karne ke liye Pakistan side ki paper trail chahiye — jitni Pakistan mein rental income declare ho rahi hai, utni hi FBR ke record mein bhi honi chahiye, aur DTA credit lene ke liye Pakistan mein tax pay hone ka proof dena parta hai. Agar Pakistan record missing ya inconsistent ho, ATO ki taraf ki filing bhi atak sakti hai.

Rawana Hone Se Pehle — Ek Chhoti Si To-Do List

  1. Jo bhi saal ki return baaki reh gayi hai, wo complete karo
  2. Wealth statement mein current assets update karo
  3. Apna ATL (filer) status verify kar lo
  4. Property hai toh uski ownership documents order mein rakho
  5. Permanent move hai toh Section 8 clearance lene par ghor karo
  6. Ek reliable consultant ko authorize kar do taake har saal ki filing remotely ho jaye

Ek baar ye list complete ho jaye, uske baad har saal sirf ek routine return file karni hoti hai — jo WhatsApp aur email se hi manage ho jati hai, Pakistan wapas aaye bina.

Time-zone ka farq (Australia Pakistan se karib 4-5 ghante aage) practically koi masla nahi banta, kyunke communication asynchronous hoti hai aur FBR deadline (September 30) kaafi pehle se pata hoti hai. Real challenge time nahi — priority hai. Overseas rehte hue Pakistan ki chhoti filing ko yaad rakhna mushkil ho jata hai jab tak koi reminder system set na ho.

Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane aur Perth mein Pakistani community kaafi connected hai, aur log ek dusre ko informal tarike se guide bhi karte hain. Lekin har ghar ka case alag hota hai — kisi ke paas sirf ek bank account hai, kisi ke paas do-teen properties aur ek family business bhi. Behtar yehi hai ke apni specific situation ek professional se confirm karwa li jaye, doston ki general advice par poori tarah rely na kiya jaye.

FAQs — Pakistan Tax Status After Australia Move

Australia move hone ke baad Pakistan mein tax return file karna zaruri hai?
Agar Pakistan mein koi income-generating asset hai — property, savings, business shareholding — toh haan, annual non-resident return file karna chahiye. Agar sirf Australia income hai aur Pakistan mein koi asset nahi, generally filing zaruri nahi.
NTN cancel karwani chahiye Australia PR milne par?
Nahi, NTN cancel karwane ki zarurat nahi. Agar future mein Pakistan mein koi transaction karni ho, active NTN aur filer status zaruri hoga.
Non-resident hone ke baad filer status maintain karna kyun zaruri hai?
Filer status property transactions par lower withholding tax rates deta hai — chahe aap Australia mein rehte ho. Non-filer ban jane par ye rates significantly barh jaati hain.
Australia mein Pakistan ki property income declare karni hoti hai?
Haan, ATO Australian tax residents se worldwide income declare karwata hai — Pakistan property se rental income bhi shamil. Pakistan-Australia DTA ke under Foreign Tax Credit claim ki ja sakti hai.
182-day rule exactly kaise calculate hota hai?
Pakistan tax year (July-June) mein Pakistan mein guzare hue actual din count kiye jate hain. 183 din ya zyada ho toh resident status; is se kam ho toh non-resident.
Kya PR ya citizenship milne ke baad bhi Pakistan filing zaruri reh sakti hai?
Haan, agar Pakistan mein koi asset (property, savings, business) hai, filing requirement PR/citizenship status se nahi balke asset ownership se linked hoti hai.
