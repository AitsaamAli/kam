DNFBP Registration in Peshawar and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: FBR's KP Directorate

10 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 7 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 10 November 2026
Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has its own FBR DNFBP directorate, covering businesses within the territory of RTO Peshawar and RTO Abbottabad.

Property dealers, jewellers and accountants in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa have a directorate of their own rather than one based in Islamabad or Lahore. This page explains its coverage and the points KP businesses most often ask about.

TL;DR

Under S.R.O. 1497(I)/2026, the Director (DNFBPs), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa supervises all DNFBPs doing business within the territorial limits of the Regional Tax Offices Peshawar and Abbottabad, and DNFBPs not otherwise covered who reside there. An Additional Director, Deputy or Assistant Directors and inspectors work under the Director. Registration is online on IRIS and goAML.

KP Directorate: Who Covers What

If your business is within…Your DNFBP officer
The territory of RTO PeshawarDirector / Additional Director (DNFBPs), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
The territory of RTO AbbottabadDirector / Additional Director (DNFBPs), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
Not covered above, but you reside in those areasDirector (DNFBPs), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Cases are assigned by the Director or Additional Director to Deputy or Assistant Directors (the notification lists two) and inspectors.

Which RTO Covers Your District?

The notification names the two RTOs, not the districts within them. Your income tax RTO appears in your IRIS profile; if it is RTO Peshawar or RTO Abbottabad, the KP directorate supervises you for DNFBP purposes.

Points for KP Businesses

SectorWhat to watch
Property dealersIdentify both parties; record how each payment is made; watch for third-party payers and purchases in the names of relatives other than a spouse or child, which FMU lists as red flags.
Jewellers and gold tradersA cash transaction of Rs 2 million or more makes the business a DNFBP and requires a CTR. Keep the customer's CNIC and CDD form.
Cross-border linksCustomers or funds linked to jurisdictions FATF has called out need enhanced due diligence. Screen names against the UN and domestic proscribed lists before any deal.
AccountantsCompany formation, managing client money and registered-address services are specified services.

Sanctions Screening Is Not Optional

FBR expects every DNFBP to screen customers, their beneficial owners and agents against the UN Security Council consolidated list and Pakistan's proscribed persons lists. On a true match the business must freeze the funds without delay, refuse the transaction, file an STR with FMU and inform FBR immediately. Write down how and when you screen, and keep the result in the customer file.

Two KP Scenarios

A property dealer in Peshawar is asked to arrange the purchase of a house in the name of a buyer's nephew, with payment coming from a third person the dealer has never met. Purchases in the name of a relative other than a spouse or child, and third-party payments, are both FMU red flags. The dealer identifies the payer, asks about the source of funds, and if the answers do not hold up, declines the deal and files an STR without telling the parties.

A jeweller in Abbottabad sells a gold set for Rs 2.2 million in cash during the wedding season. The business is a DNFBP, files a CTR on goAML, and is supervised by the KP directorate because it is within RTO Abbottabad's territory.

The examples are illustrative.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which FBR office supervises DNFBPs in Peshawar?
The Director (DNFBPs), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, under S.R.O. 1497(I)/2026, for businesses within the territory of RTO Peshawar.
Is Abbottabad under the KP DNFBP directorate?
Yes. The KP directorate covers DNFBPs within the territorial limits of both RTO Peshawar and RTO Abbottabad.
Do KP jewellers need to register as DNFBPs?
A jeweller is a DNFBP when it engages in a cash transaction of Rs 2 million or more with a customer, and must then register and file CTRs.
What should I do if a customer matches a sanctions list?
Freeze the funds without delay, refuse the transaction, file an STR with FMU on goAML and inform FBR immediately.
Does KPRA registration cover DNFBP requirements?
No. KPRA collects provincial tax on services; it does not supervise anti-money laundering compliance, which for DNFBPs in KP is FBR's job.
