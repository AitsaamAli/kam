DNFBP Registration in Lahore and Punjab: The FBR Directorate, Its Offices, and Which One Supervises Your Business

10 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 9 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 10 November 2026
Under S.R.O. 1497(I)/2026, one Lahore-based directorate supervises DNFBPs across most of Punjab, split between three Additional Directors by tax office.

Property dealers in Lahore, jewellers in Faisalabad and accountants in Multan all ask the same practical question: which FBR office is mine for DNFBP matters? The answer comes from the jurisdiction table FBR replaced on 2 September 2026. This page sets it out for Punjab outside the Islamabad directorate's area.

TL;DR

FBR's Director (DNFBPs) Lahore supervises DNFBPs whose income tax cases sit with CTO Lahore, LTO Lahore or LTO Multan, and all DNFBPs doing business within the territory of RTO-I and RTO-II Lahore and the RTOs of Sialkot, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Sargodha, Multan, Sahiwal and Bahawalpur (S.R.O. 1497(I)/2026, 2 September 2026). Additional Director-I covers Lahore, Additional Director-II covers Sialkot, Gujranwala, Faisalabad and Sargodha, and Additional Director-III covers Multan, Sahiwal and Bahawalpur. Registration itself is online on IRIS, wherever you are.

Your Tax Office Decides Your DNFBP Officer

S.R.O. 1497(I)/2026 assigns DNFBPs by the income tax office that holds their case, or by the area in which they carry on business. Where neither fits, the place of residence decides. For the Lahore directorate:

If your business or tax case is with…Your DNFBP officer (Lahore)
Corporate Tax Office Lahore or Large Taxpayers' Office LahoreAdditional Director-I (DNFBPs), Lahore
RTO-I Lahore or RTO-II LahoreAdditional Director-I (DNFBPs), Lahore
RTO Sialkot, RTO Gujranwala, RTO Faisalabad or RTO SargodhaAdditional Director-II (DNFBPs), Lahore
Large Taxpayers' Office Multan, or RTO Multan, RTO Sahiwal or RTO BahawalpurAdditional Director-III (DNFBPs), Lahore

All of them report to the Director (DNFBPs), Lahore. Day-to-day cases are assigned to Deputy or Assistant Directors (the notification lists eight) and inspectors working under the Director and Additional Directors. The Director General of DNFBPs has jurisdiction over the whole country.

Rawalpindi is not in this list: it falls under the Islamabad directorate.

How to Find Your Income Tax Office

Your RTO or LTO is shown in your IRIS profile and on your registration details. If you are not sure, check IRIS before writing to FBR; correspondence sent to the wrong officer slows replies.

Registering From Punjab

There is no separate Punjab form. Registration is done on FBR's IRIS portal; the system assigns your case by jurisdiction. Then register on goAML with the Financial Monitoring Unit. Most delays we see in Punjab come from mismatched details: a business name on the signboard that differs from IRIS, or a partnership registered with the Registrar of Firms under a slightly different name.

The registration, CDD, screening, reporting and record-keeping rules do not change between Punjab cities; only the supervising officer does. Start with our DNFBP registration guide, then the guide for your trade: real estate agents, jewellers or accountants.

Two Punjab Scenarios

A dealer in Johar Town, Lahore, whose income tax case is with RTO-II Lahore, receives a letter about DNFBP registration. It comes under Additional Director-I. The dealer registers on IRIS, appoints the owner as compliance officer, registers on goAML and adopts a buyer-and-seller CDD form before the next deal.

A jeweller in Faisalabad sells a bridal set for Rs 2.6 million in cash. That one sale makes the business a DNFBP and requires a CTR. Supervision sits with Additional Director-II, because the business falls within RTO Faisalabad's territory.

The examples are illustrative.

Contacting FBR About DNFBP Matters

For general questions, FBR's DNFBP helpline is +92 51 9106606 and its help email is help.dnfbp.fbr@gmail.com (FBR FAQs, February 2023). Letters from your supervising officer will state the officer's designation; reply to that office and keep a copy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which FBR office handles DNFBPs in Lahore?
The Director (DNFBPs), Lahore. Under S.R.O. 1497(I)/2026, Additional Director-I covers DNFBPs with CTO or LTO Lahore and those in the territory of RTO-I and RTO-II Lahore.
Who supervises DNFBPs in Faisalabad?
Additional Director-II (DNFBPs), Lahore, who covers businesses within the territory of RTOs Sialkot, Gujranwala, Faisalabad and Sargodha.
Who supervises DNFBPs in Multan?
Additional Director-III (DNFBPs), Lahore, covering LTO Multan and the RTOs of Multan, Sahiwal and Bahawalpur.
Is Rawalpindi under the Lahore DNFBP directorate?
No. RTO Rawalpindi falls under the Director (DNFBPs), Islamabad.
Do I register at the Lahore office in person?
No. DNFBP registration is done online on FBR's IRIS portal, and goAML registration is online with FMU.
What if my business is in one city and I live in another?
The notification assigns DNFBPs mainly by tax office or place of business; place of residence applies to DNFBPs not otherwise covered.
When did these jurisdictions take effect?
S.R.O. 1497(I)/2026 was issued on 2 September 2026 and replaced the earlier jurisdiction table.
