Greece Digital Nomad Visa — Pakistani Freelancers Ke Liye

Greece ka Digital Nomad Visa remote workers, IT professionals aur freelancers ke liye ek growing opportunity hai:

  • Income source: Foreign clients se honi chahiye (Pakistan, UAE, USA etc.) — Greece se nahi
  • FBR se prove karo: Pakistan mein income tax returns ye prove karte hain ke aap ka income legitimate foreign source se hai
  • Health insurance: Greece mein private health insurance zaruri
  • Exact income threshold: Consulate se current amount confirm karo — ye periodically update hoti hai

IT professionals ke liye: Pakistan mein PSEB registered ya FBR ke saath file karne wale IT freelancers ke liye Greece Digital Nomad Visa ek achha option hai. Income FBR mein declared hai — consulate ko prove karna aasaan ho jata hai. Kamboh Associates IT export income ki filing bhi handle karta hai.

Greece Golden Visa — Source of Funds Prove Karo

Greece Golden Visa property investment ke zariye milta hai — investment threshold location ke hisab se different hai (high-demand areas jaise Athens, Thessaloniki, aur popular islands mein zyada, baaki areas mein kam). FBR ka role specifically funds ka source prove karna hai:

  • Wealth statement mein declared savings + investments ka history dikhata hai funds ka source
  • Tax returns prove karte hain ke income legal business/salary se thi
  • Business owners ke liye: company tax returns + audited accounts
  • Property owners ke liye: FBR property records + capital gains history

Important: Greece Golden Visa ki minimum investment amounts periodically change karti hain area ke hisab se. 2026 mein exact current threshold Greek consulate ya immigration lawyer se confirm karo before proceeding.

FAQs — Greece Visa FBR

Greece Schengen visa ke liye FBR return zaruri hai?
Haan. Greek consulate financial sufficiency prove karne ke liye Pakistan FBR tax returns maangta hai. NTN, last 1–3 saal ki returns, wealth statement aur bank statements standard documentation hai. Non-filer ka matlab consulate ke liye verifiable income ka record nahi — rejection risk high ho jaata hai.
Greece Golden Visa ke liye kitna investment chahiye?
Greece Golden Visa property investment ke zariye milta hai — minimum investment amount area ke hisab se different hai (kam demand areas mein kam, Athens/Thessaloniki/major islands jaisi high-demand areas mein zyada). Exact current threshold Greek consulate ya immigration lawyer se confirm karna zaroori hai kyunke ye periodically update hoti hai.
Greece Digital Nomad Visa ke liye kya requirement hai?
Greece Digital Nomad Visa remote workers aur freelancers ke liye hai jinka income foreign clients se ata ho (Greece-based nahi). Minimum monthly income requirement hoti hai jo consulate confirm karta hai. Pakistan mein FBR returns + bank statements + client contracts income prove karte hain.
Greece mein Pakistani community kitni badi hai?
Greece mein estimated tens of thousands Pakistanis rehte hain — mostly Athens aur agricultural areas jaise Manolada mein, jahan bohat se Pakistani labor aur farming sector mein kaam karte hain. Ye ek established, purani community hai jo decades se Greece mein settle hai.
Greece jane ke baad Pakistan mein tax dena hoga?
Non-resident hone ke baad (182 din se kam Pakistan mein) Greece se income Pakistan mein taxable nahi. Pakistan mein property rent ya savings income taxable rahega. Greece-Pakistan ke beech tax treaty verify karo apne case ke hisab se — double taxation se bachne ke liye professional advice lein.
