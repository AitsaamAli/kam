Filer Kaise Banein 2026: Late Return Ke Baad Form ATL-U Se Bina Rs 25,000 Surcharge ATL Mein Naam

19 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 12 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 19 November 2026
Return ki aakhri tareekh 31 October 2026 guzar gayi? Agar agle 6 maah property nahi khareedni to Rs 25,000 diye baghair filer ban sakte hain.

Har saal deadline ke baad hazaron log yahi poochte hain: ab filer kaise banein? Is saal jawab badal gaya hai, kyun ke surcharge Rs 1,000 se Rs 25,000 ho gaya aur saath hi aik naya, sasta raasta bhi aa gaya. Yeh guide qanoon ki buniyad par, aasan zubaan mein, poora tareeqa batati hai.

Short answer

Filer banne ke liye FBR koi fees nahi leta. Filer ka matlab hai Active Taxpayers List (ATL) mein naam, aur yeh naam return file karne se aata hai. Lekin Section 182A ke mutabiq jo shakhs return deadline ke baad file kare, woh us saal ATL mein nahi aata jab tak surcharge na de: individual Rs 25,000, AOP Rs 50,000, company Rs 100,000. Finance Act 2026 ne Section 182A(3) shamil kiya: individual agar undertaking de ke 6 maah tak koi property nahi khareedega ya hasil nahi karega, to surcharge ki shart lagu nahi hoti. FBR ne 10 November 2026 ko SRO 2097(I)/2026 ke zariye is ka form, Form ATL-U, jari kiya. Late-filing penalty phir bhi alag se lagti hai.

Filer Ka Asal Matlab

Short answer: Filer ka matlab hai FBR ki Active Taxpayers List mein naam; is se bank, property aur gaari par kam tax katta hai.

"Filer" koi certificate nahi. Bank, property registrar aur gaari ki registration wale yeh dekhte hain ke aap ka naam FBR ki Active Taxpayers List mein hai ya nahi. Naam hai to kam tax katta hai, nahi hai to Tenth Schedule ke rule 1 ke mutabiq zyada tar withholding rates 100% barh jate hain.

Late Return Ka Masla

Short answer: Deadline ke baad return file karne wala us saal ATL mein nahi aata jab tak surcharge na de ya Form ATL-U na de.

Tax Year 2026 ki return ki aakhri tareekh barha kar 31 October 2026 ki gayi thi. Is ke baad file karne wala "late filer" hai. Section 182A(1) kehta hai ke aisa shakhs us saal ke ATL mein shamil nahi hoga. Saath mein:

  • Jab tak ATL se bahar hai, refund nahi milta (182A(1)(c)).
  • Us saal ka nuqsan agle saalon mein carry forward nahi hota (182A(1)(b)).
  • Late filing ki penalty Section 182 ke tehat alag se lagti hai.

Qanoon Kya Kehta Hai

Short answer: Section 182A(3) individual ko 6 maah property na lene ki undertaking par surcharge se chhoot deta hai.

Section 182A(3) ka khulasa: surcharge ki shart us individual par lagu nahi hogi jo Commissioner ke saamne undertaking de ke woh undertaking ki tareekh se 6 maah tak kisi property ko "purchase, acquire, or otherwise obtain ownership or beneficial interest" nahi karega. Yani na khareedna, na kisi aur tareeqe se maalik banna, aur na kisi doosre ke naam par apne faide ke liye property rakhna.

Yeh shart 1 July 2026 se qanoon mein thi lekin form na hone ki wajah se kaam nahi kar rahi thi. 10 November 2026 ko FBR ne Income Tax Rules 2002 ke rule 81B mein tarmeem karke Form ATL-U jari kiya.

Form ATL-U Kis Ke Liye Theek Hai

Short answer: Salaried, pensioners, freelancers, dukandar aur property bechne wale jo 6 maah kuch nahi khareed rahe.

  • Naukri pesha log aur pensioners jinhon ne deadline miss ki. Tafseel hamari salaried guide mein hai.
  • Freelancers, dukandar aur sole proprietors; dekhein yeh guide.
  • Bahar rehne wale Pakistani jo yahan property bechna chahte hain.
  • Woh log jin ke bank profit par double tax kat raha hai; dekhein bank profit guide.

Kis Ke Liye Nahi

Short answer: Jis ne 6 maah mein property leni hai, aur har AOP ya company.

  • Jis ne agle 6 maah mein plot, ghar, flat, dukaan ya file leni hai.
  • AOP ya company: qanoon sirf individual ki baat karta hai.
  • Jise yaqeen nahi. Gaari, gift ya wirasat ke sawalon par hamari 6 maah wali guide parhein.

Tareeqa: Qadam Ba Qadam

Short answer: Return aur wealth statement file karein, tax jama karein, IRIS par Form ATL-U dein, acknowledgement rakhein.

  1. IRIS par login karein. Password bhool gaye hain to reset karein.
  2. Tax Year 2026 ki return aur wealth statement file karein. Draft nahi, Completed status zaroori hai.
  3. Tax aur penalty (agar bane) PSID challan se jama karein.
  4. Form ATL-U IRIS par bharein: naam, CNIC, tax year 2026, return ki tareekh aur acknowledgement number, phir declaration. Screens ki tafseel hamari IRIS guide mein hai.
  5. Acknowledgement download karke sambhal lein. Is ki tareekh se 6 maah gine jayenge.
  6. ATL status agli update ke baad check karein: online ya SMS se.

Penalty Kitni Hai

Short answer: Tax payable ka 0.1% ya Rs 1,000 rozana, jo zyada ho; 30 November 2026 tak file karne par 75% kami.

30 June 2026 tak amended Ordinance ke mutabiq Section 182 ki penalty yeh hai: har din ke liye tax payable ka 0.1% ya Rs 1,000, jo zyada ho. Kam az kam Rs 10,000 agar aamdani ka 75% ya zyada salary se ho, warna Rs 50,000. Zyada se zyada tax payable ka 200%. Aur agar return deadline ke 1, 2 ya 3 maah ke andar file ho to penalty 75%, 50% ya 25% kam ho jati hai.

Is ka matlab: 30 November 2026 tak return file karne par penalty mein 75% kami milti hai. Der na karein.

Aam Ghaltiyan

Short answer: Sirf return file karna, 6 maah ghalat tareekh se ginna aur penalty maaf samajhna aam ghaltiyan hain.

  • Sirf return file karke samajhna ke ATL mein naam aa jayega.
  • 6 maah return ki tareekh se ginna, jab ke yeh undertaking ki tareekh se shuru hote hain.
  • Yeh samajhna ke Form ATL-U se penalty bhi maaf ho gayi.
  • 6 maah ke andar biwi ya bhai ke naam par apne paison se plot lena. Yeh "beneficial interest" hai.

Zaroori Kaghzat

Short answer: CNIC, IRIS password, aamdani ke kaghzat, 30 June 2026 ke assets aur tax certificates.

  • CNIC aur IRIS ka password (ya registered mobile number).
  • Salaried: employer ka salary certificate (July 2025 se June 2026).
  • Freelancer: bank statements jin mein bahar se aaya paisa nazar aaye.
  • Dukandar: saal bhar ki sale aur kharche ka khulasa, business account ki statement.
  • Sab ke liye: 30 June 2026 ko bank balance, plot, ghar, gaari, sona aur cash ki tafseel.
  • Bank aur bijli/mobile ke tax certificates, taake kata hua tax return mein credit ho.

Property Bechni Hai To?

Short answer: Bechna mana nahi; Form ATL-U ke baad 11.5% ke bajaye 2.75% advance tax lagta hai.

Form ATL-U sirf khareedne aur hasil karne par pabandi lagata hai, bechne par nahi. Is liye agar aap ne apna plot ya ghar bechna hai aur naya nahi lena, to Form ATL-U de kar ATL mein aa jayein aur 11.5% ke bajaye 2.75% advance tax dein. Rs 1 crore ki property par yeh farq Rs 875,000 banta hai. Tafseel hamari property sale guide mein.

Frequently Asked Questions

Filer banne ki fees kitni hai?
FBR filer banne ki koi fees nahi leta. Late return par penalty aur ATL ke liye Rs 25,000 surcharge ya Form ATL-U ka raasta hai.
Form ATL-U kya hai?
Section 182A(3) ke tehat undertaking ka form, jo SRO 2097(I)/2026 se jari hua. Individual 6 maah property na lene ka wada karta hai aur surcharge ke baghair ATL mein aata hai.
Kya company ya AOP Form ATL-U de sakti hai?
Nahi. Yeh sirf individual ke liye hai. AOP Rs 50,000 aur company Rs 100,000 surcharge deti hai.
6 maah kab se shuru hote hain?
Undertaking jama karne ki tareekh se, jo IRIS acknowledgement par likhi hoti hai.
Kya Form ATL-U ke baad property bech sakte hain?
Haan. Pabandi khareedne ya hasil karne par hai, bechne par nahi.
Agar 6 maah mein plot le liya to kya hoga?
Notify kiye gaye rules ke mutabiq sunwai ke baad breach ki tareekh se riayat wapas ho jati hai aur surcharge wala qanoon lagu hota hai.
Penalty kab kam hoti hai?
Deadline ke 1 maah ke andar file karne par 75%, 2 maah mein 50%, 3 maah mein 25% kami. Tax Year 2026 ke liye 30 November 2026 tak 75%.
Aap kitni fees lete hain?
Salaried return Rs 3,500 + Form ATL-U Rs 2,000 = Rs 5,500. Freelancer ya dukandar Rs 7,000, overseas Rs 8,000.
Kya main khud Form ATL-U jama kar sakta hoon?
Haan, IRIS par koi bhi individual khud jama kar sakta hai. Log hamari madad tab lete hain jab return tayyar karni ho ya IRIS mein masla ho.
Biwi ke naam par plot le sakte hain?
Agar paisa aap ka hai aur plot aap ke liye hai to yeh beneficial interest hai aur undertaking toot jati hai. Agar biwi apne paison se apne liye le to woh un ki khareed hai.
Office aana zaroori hai?
Nahi. Saara kaam WhatsApp aur email par ho jata hai, Pakistan mein kahin se bhi ya bahar se.
