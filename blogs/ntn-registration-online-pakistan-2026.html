NTN Registration Online Pakistan 2026
Free FBR IRIS Guide — Get Your NTN in 1-3 Days

June 17, 2026 7 min read Kamboh Associates

What is NTN and Why Do You Need It?

NTN (National Tax Number) is your unique tax identification number issued by FBR Pakistan. It is required for filing income tax returns, registering for sales tax, opening corporate bank accounts, entering government tenders, and for many business transactions.

For individual taxpayers (salaried, freelancers, rental income earners), your CNIC number itself serves as your NTN. For businesses, companies, and partnerships, a separate NTN is issued for the business entity.

Good News: NTN registration is completely FREE on FBR IRIS. No government fee is charged. The process is online and can be done from home without visiting any FBR office.

Step-by-Step NTN Registration on FBR IRIS

  • Step 1: Go to FBR IRIS portal: iris.fbr.gov.pk
  • Step 2: Click "Registration for Unregistered Person" on the login page
  • Step 3: Enter your CNIC number, email address, and mobile phone number
  • Step 4: You will receive an OTP on your mobile — enter it to verify
  • Step 5: Set a password for your IRIS account
  • Step 6: Login and complete the Registration Form (181) with your personal details, income sources, business information
  • Step 7: Enter your bank account details and property/assets information
  • Step 8: Submit the form — FBR reviews and activates your NTN within 1-3 working days
  • Step 9: Download your NTN Certificate from IRIS after activation

NTN for Business vs Individual — Key Differences

  • Individual NTN: Your 13-digit CNIC number is your NTN. No separate registration needed — just activate on IRIS
  • Business/Company NTN: A separate 7-digit NTN is issued for your business entity, different from your personal NTN
  • You can have both a personal NTN (as individual) and a business NTN (for your company) simultaneously
  • For filing returns, use the NTN corresponding to the income head — personal income on personal NTN, company income on company NTN

After NTN — Get on the Active Taxpayers List (ATL)

Having an NTN alone is not enough. To enjoy lower tax rates as a "filer," you must appear on FBR's Active Taxpayers List (ATL). To get on ATL:

  • File your income tax return for the latest completed tax year
  • ATL is updated every Sunday by FBR
  • You will appear on ATL approximately one week after filing your return
  • ATL status can be checked at atl.fbr.gov.pk or via SMS (Type ATL [space] CNIC to 9966)

Frequently Asked Questions

How to get NTN number in Pakistan online?
Register on FBR IRIS portal (iris.fbr.gov.pk), click "Registration for Unregistered Person," enter your CNIC, verify via OTP, and complete the registration form. NTN is issued within 1-3 working days.
Is NTN registration free in Pakistan?
Yes. FBR NTN registration has no government fee. It is completely free. Professional consultants may charge a service fee for handling the process on your behalf, but the NTN itself costs nothing.
What is my NTN if I am an individual taxpayer?
For individuals, your CNIC number (13 digits) is your NTN. You do not need a separate number — just activate your IRIS account using your CNIC and you are registered as a taxpayer.
How can I download my NTN certificate from FBR?
Login to FBR IRIS portal, go to Registration > Draft, and open your registration form. Once approved, you can print/download the NTN certificate directly from IRIS.
