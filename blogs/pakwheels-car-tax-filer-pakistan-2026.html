PakWheels Par Car Khareedna ya Bechna — Vehicle Tax Guide Pakistan 2026 | Filer vs Non-Filer

PakWheels ya OLX par car dhundhna — sabki routine hai Pakistan mein. Lekin car khareedne ya bechne ke waqt vehicle registration tax (WHT) ka hisaab lagana bhool jaate hain. Section 231B — Vehicle Registration WHT 2026

Naya ya used vehicle register karwane par Section 231B advance income tax lagta hai. Ye tax excise office (ya dealership mein) registration ke waqt pay karna padta hai — adjustable hai annual tax return mein.

Car Dealer Ke Liye Tax — PakWheels Dealer Ya OLX Dealer

Agar aap car dealing business kar rahe hain — PakWheels ya OLX par regularly cars buy-sell karte hain — tou aap ka tax case individual car buyer se bahut alag hai:

  • Business income: Cars ki buying-selling se profit business income hai — annual return mein declare karna zaroori hai
  • NTN zaruri: Business NTN aur annual tax return — incomplete compliance par FBR notice possible
  • 231B WHT: Har car registration par WHT — filer hone se annually lakhs bachte hain
  • Sales Tax (if applicable): Agar turnover Rs 100 million+ hain — sales tax registration
  • Section 153 WHT: Agar mechanicals / accessories khareedne par service payments — WHT kaatni padti hai

Car dealer non-filer hona: Monthly 10 cars buy-sell karne wala dealer — non-filer hone par sirf 231B mein Rs 3,75,000/month extra deta hai (10 Corolla-class cars). Annually yeh Rs 45 lakh extra tax hai. Filer banna dealer ke liye absolutely essential business decision hai.

Budget 2026-27 — Non-Filer Vehicle Restriction

FBR ne Budget 2026-27 mein non-filers par nayi vehicle restrictions lagayi hain:

  • 1300cc se zyada naya car: Non-filer directly (first hand) register nahi kar sakta — dealer ya excise may reject
  • Import karda vehicles: Non-filer import license restrict hai
  • Commercial vehicles: Non-filer commercial fleet registration mushkil ho rahi hai

Practically — agar 1300cc se zyada car lena hai (Honda City, Corolla, Civic, Sportage, Prado — sab is range mein hain) — tou filer hona zaroori ho gaya hai.

231B Tax Adjustable Hai — Return Mein Deduct Hoga

Section 231B advance tax final tax nahi hai — adjustable hai. Jab aap annual income tax return file karte hain, ye amount aapki final tax liability se deduct ho jaata hai. Agar aapki total income kam hai aur final tax liability zero ya negative hai — tou 231B amount refund claim ho sakta hai.

Is liye car registration ke baad annual return file karna bhoolna nahi chahiye — bahut se log Rs 25,000–50,000 ke refund miss kar jaate hain sirf is liye ke return file nahi ki. Kamboh Associates annual return filing mein 231B adjustment properly karta hai.

FAQs — PakWheels Car Tax

Car registration ke din ATL mein nahi hoon — kya filer rate milegi?
Nahi — registration ke waqt aap ka ATL status check hota hai (atl.fbr.gov.pk). Agar ATL mein naam nahi tou non-filer rate lagegi. Is liye registration se 2-3 din pehle filer banna zaroori hai. Kamboh Associates urgent case mein same day ATL confirm karta hai — car khareedne se pehle WhatsApp karo.
Husband filer hai — wife ke naam par car register karein tou kya rate?
Car jis ke naam par register hogi — us person ka ATL status dekha jaayega. Agar wife ATL mein nahi hai tou non-filer rate lagegi chahe husband filer ho. Best option: dono ka NTN aur return file karo — family mein sab filer hone se sab property/vehicle transactions mein savings milti hain.
Old car bechne par tax dena hoga?
Personal use ki apni car bechne par seller par WHT nahi lagti — ye Section 231B sirf buyer par hai. Seller ki liability zero hoti hai (capital gains on personal vehicle exempt). Lekin agar aap car dealer ho — buy-sell business ho — tou profit business income hai aur annual return mein declare karna zaroori hai.
Electric vehicle (EV) par 231B tax lagta hai?
Electric vehicles ke liye FBR ne alag provisions ki hain — Budget 2026-27 mein EVs ko promote karne ke liye concessions hain. EV registration tax conventional cars se kam ho sakta hai ya exemption ho — specific EV ka case Kamboh Associates se verify karwao kyunke rates change hote rehte hain.
Car dealer ka tax kaise handle karein — monthly multiple cars?
Car dealer ka tax complex hai — business NTN, annual return, possibly quarterly advance tax. Monthly 10+ cars deal karte hain tou advance tax planning zaroori hai. Kamboh Associates car dealers ke liye complete tax compliance handle karta hai — monthly WHT compliance, annual return, FBR notice defense — sab. WhatsApp karo aur case discuss karo.
