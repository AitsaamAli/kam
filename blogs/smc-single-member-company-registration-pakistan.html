SMC Registration Pakistan 2026 — Akele Company Bana Sakte Hain

July 22, 2026Aitsaam Ali8 min readSolo Entrepreneurs

Bohat se freelancers, YouTubers, aur solo entrepreneurs sochte hain ke company banane ke liye ek partner zaroori hai. Ye galat hai — Single Member Company (SMC) Pakistan mein akele banayi ja sakti hai, poori limited liability ke sath. Ye guide SMC kya hai, kaise bante hai, aur normal Pvt Ltd se kaise different hai — sab explain karta hai.

TL;DR

SMC = ek shareholder, poori company, limited liability. Minimum authorized capital Rs 100,000, no minimum paid-up. Nominee director declare karna zaroori. Registration 7-10 working days. Kamboh Associates total service Rs 15,000 se shuru.

SMC Kya Hai — Single Member Company Explained

Companies Act 2017 se pehle Pakistan mein company banane ke liye minimum do members chahiye hote thay. SMC iss requirement ko khatam karta hai — ek hi banda 100% shares hold kar sakta hai, sole director bhi ban sakta hai, aur phir bhi corporate limited liability protection milti hai jaisay normal Pvt Ltd mein milti hai.

  • Sirf ek shareholder — koi partner ya co-founder zaroori nahi
  • Limited liability — personal assets business liabilities se protected hain
  • Separate legal entity — company khud contracts, bank accounts, property hold kar sakti hai
  • Corporate credibility — clients, banks, aur investors ke saamne zyada trust

Nominee Director — Kyun Zaroori Hai

Nominee director ka role: Kyunke SMC mein sirf ek hi member hota hai, SECP requirement hai ke ek nominee declare kiya jaye — agar sole member ka intiqal ho jaye ya wo incapacitated ho jaye, nominee company ko legally continue chala sakta hai jab tak proper transfer na ho. Nominee normal operations mein involve nahi hota — sirf ek legal safeguard hai.

SMC Registration Requirements

  • Ek Pakistani resident individual (18+ saal, valid CNIC)
  • Company name (SECP se pehle reserve karna hoga)
  • Nominee director ka CNIC aur consent letter
  • Registered office address (Pakistan mein)
  • Memorandum of Association (MoA) aur Articles of Association (AoA)
  • Minimum authorized capital Rs 100,000 (paid-up zaroori nahi)

SMC Registration — Step by Step

Name Reservation

SECP eServices portal par company ka naam check aur reserve karo (1-3 din).

Documents Tayyar Karo

MoA, AoA, apna CNIC, nominee director ka CNIC aur consent letter tayyar karo.

SECP Par Incorporation File Karo

Online form fill karke documents upload karo, authorized capital ke hisab se fee pay karo.

Certificate of Incorporation Milega

7-10 working days mein SECP certificate issue kar deta hai — company legally exist karti hai.

NTN aur Bank Account

FBR se company NTN register karo, phir business bank account kholo.

Yaad rakhein: SMC banane ke baad bhi annual SECP filing (Form A, financial statements) aur annual FBR corporate tax return zaroori hai — chahe company active ho ya dormant. In requirements ko miss karna penalty ka sabab banta hai.

Aksar Puchhe Gaye Sawal — SMC Registration

SMC (Single Member Company) kya hoti hai Pakistan mein?
SMC ek aisi private limited company hai jiska sirf ek hi shareholder aur director hota hai — Companies Act 2017 ke tehat allowed hai. Normal Pvt Ltd mein minimum do members chahiye hote hain, lekin SMC mein solo entrepreneur bhi corporate structure aur limited liability le sakta hai.
SMC banane ke liye kitna paisa chahiye?
Minimum authorized capital Rs 100,000 hai lekin koi minimum paid-up capital requirement nahi — yani aap sirf Rs 100,000 declare kar sakte hain bina actually deposit kiye. SECP filing fee capital ke hisab se Rs 1,000 se shuru hoti hai.
SMC mein nominee director kya hota hai aur kyun zaroori hai?
Kyunke SMC mein sirf ek hi member hota hai, SECP requirement hai ke ek "nominee director" declare kiya jaye — agar sole member ka intiqal ho jaye ya wo incapacitated ho jaye, nominee company ko continue chala sakta hai. Nominee actual business mein involve nahi hota jab tak zaroorat na paray.
SMC registration mein kitna time lagta hai?
Online SECP registration process mein 7-10 working days lagte hain — name reservation se certificate of incorporation tak. Kamboh Associates NTN aur bank account setup ke sath total process 2-3 weeks mein complete karti hai.
