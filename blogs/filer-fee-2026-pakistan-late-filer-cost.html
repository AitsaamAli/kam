Filer Fee 2026 in Pakistan: The Real Cost of Becoming a Filer After the Deadline, Line by Line

19 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 11 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 19 November 2026
People ask 'how much is the filer fee?' The honest answer is that FBR charges no fee for being a filer. Here is what a late filer actually pays in 2026, and how to cut it.

'Filer fee' is one of the most searched tax phrases in Pakistan, and also one of the most misunderstood. Some agents quote a single number that hides what is inside it. This guide breaks the cost into its real parts, using the law as it stands after the Finance Act 2026, so you can see what you must pay and what you can avoid.

Short answer

FBR does not charge any fee to become a filer. Filing a return on IRIS is free. A person becomes a filer in practice by appearing on the Active Taxpayers List, which requires a return filed for the relevant tax year. Costs arise only in three places: the tax on your income, if any; the Section 182 penalty if the return is late; and, for a late filer, the Section 182A surcharge of Rs 25,000 (individual), Rs 50,000 (AOP) or Rs 100,000 (company) to be included in the ATL. An individual who will not obtain property for six months can replace the surcharge with Form ATL-U, which costs nothing at FBR. Professional fees, where you use a consultant, are separate: ours are published.

FBR Charges No Fee to Become a Filer

Short answer: FBR charges no fee to become a filer; NTN registration and filing on IRIS are free.

There is no "filer registration fee" in the Income Tax Ordinance. NTN registration on IRIS is free for an individual, and so is filing a return. The word "filer" is shorthand for a person on the Active Taxpayers List, and the only way onto that list is a filed return. Anyone quoting a fixed "filer fee" is quoting their own service charge, possibly with FBR amounts folded in.

The Five Possible Cost Lines

Short answer: A late filer can face five cost lines: tax, the Section 182 penalty, the ATL surcharge, the wealth statement work and any professional fee.

The Surcharge Line in Detail

Short answer: From 1 July 2026 the ATL surcharge is Rs 25,000 for an individual, Rs 50,000 for an AOP and Rs 100,000 for a company.

The proviso to Section 182A(1)(a), as amended by the Finance Act 2026, says a person who files after the due date is included in the ATL on paying a surcharge of:

PersonSurcharge before Finance Act 2026Surcharge from 1 July 2026
IndividualRs 1,000Rs 25,000
Association of personsRs 10,000Rs 50,000
CompanyRs 20,000Rs 100,000

For an individual, a twenty-five-fold increase turned the surcharge from a nuisance into a real decision. That is why the same Finance Act added Section 182A(3), the undertaking that FBR has now put into Form ATL-U.

The Penalty Line in Detail

Short answer: The penalty is the higher of 0.1% of tax payable or Rs 1,000 a day, minimum Rs 10,000 (salaried) or Rs 50,000, cut by 75% if filed by 30 November 2026.

Section 182 penalty, as it stands in the Ordinance amended up to 30 June 2026: the higher of 0.1% of tax payable per day or Rs 1,000 per day; minimum Rs 10,000 for an individual with 75% or more of income from salary and Rs 50,000 in other cases; maximum 200% of tax payable; reduced by 75%, 50% or 25% if the return is filed within one, two or three months after the due date.

The reductions are time-sensitive. For Tax Year 2026 the extended due date was 31 October 2026, so:

Return filed byReduction in penalty
30 November 2026 (within one month)75%
31 December 2026 (within two months)50%

After thatNone

Costs People Forget

Short answer: Doubled withholding while you wait, blocked refunds and unreconciled earlier years cost more than the visible fees.

  • Doubled withholding while you wait. Every month off the ATL, a bank deducts tax on profit at the increased rate. That money comes back only through a refund claim, and Section 182A(1)(c) blocks refunds while you are off the ATL.
  • Earlier years. If earlier returns are missing, the wealth statement for 2026 may not reconcile. Fixing that later costs more than doing it now.
  • Breaking the undertaking. If you buy property within six months, the waiver is withdrawn and the surcharge rule applies. Pick the route that fits your real plans.

How to Read an Agent's Quote

Short answer: Ask for every quote in separate lines: professional fee, FBR penalty and FBR surcharge.

Ask for every quote in lines: professional fee, FBR penalty, FBR surcharge (if any). Ask whether the surcharge is included and whether you qualify for Form ATL-U instead. Ask for the IRIS acknowledgement of everything filed in your name. A consultant who cannot separate these lines is asking you to trust a number you cannot check.

Non-Filer to Filer: The Full Path

Short answer: NTN, Tax Year 2026 return and wealth statement, tax and penalty, then Form ATL-U or the surcharge.

  1. NTN on IRIS, if you have never registered: free at FBR (our fee Rs 2,000 if you want it done).
  2. Tax Year 2026 return and wealth statement.
  3. Tax and any penalty paid.
  4. Form ATL-U (no property for six months) or surcharge.
  5. ATL checked after the next update.

How the Penalty Is Paid

Short answer: The penalty is paid to FBR by PSID challan, never to the consultant.

The Section 182 penalty is paid to FBR through a payment slip (PSID) generated on IRIS, the same way income tax is paid. It is not paid to us and it is not part of our fee. We work out the figure from your return before filing, show it to you, and generate the PSID for you to pay through your bank, mobile app or ATM. Keep the paid challan with your return papers.

Short answer: Filing missing years now lets every wealth statement reconcile; we quote all years before starting.

If you have never filed, or skipped years, the cheapest moment to fix them is now. A wealth statement for 2026 that starts from nothing can make savings, property or a vehicle look unexplained. Filing the missing years in order lets each wealth statement reconcile with the one before. Each late year carries its own penalty question, so we look at the full picture and quote before starting. A Two-Question Decision

Short answer: Individual and no property for six months: Form ATL-U. Otherwise: surcharge.

  1. Are you an individual? If you are filing for an AOP or company, Form ATL-U is not available; the surcharge is Rs 50,000 or Rs 100,000.
  2. Will any property come to you in six months? If no, Form ATL-U costs Rs 2,000 with us instead of Rs 25,000 to FBR. If yes, or maybe, pay the surcharge.

That is the whole decision. Everything else on the bill (tax, penalty, return fee) is the same either way.

Why Not Just Stay a Non-Filer?

Short answer: Staying a non-filer costs more in doubled withholding than filing does.

Some people weigh these costs and decide to skip filing. The arithmetic rarely supports it. A non-filer pays increased withholding on every bank profit payment, dividend and vehicle registration, and 11.5% instead of 2.75% on any property sale. Section 114C of the Ordinance, added by the Finance Act 2025, also sets out restrictions on certain transactions by 'ineligible persons', which come into force from dates the Federal Government notifies. The direction of the law is clear: filing gets more valuable each year.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the filer fee in Pakistan in 2026?
FBR charges no fee to become a filer. Late filers may owe the Section 182 penalty and, to join the ATL, a surcharge of Rs 25,000 (individual) unless they use Form ATL-U.
What is the non-filer to filer fee?
There is no FBR fee for it. You file the return; if it is late, the penalty and the ATL route (surcharge or Form ATL-U) apply.
Is the ATL surcharge the same as the late-filing penalty?
No. The penalty is under Section 182 for missing the due date; the surcharge is under Section 182A for inclusion in the ATL after a late return.
How much is the ATL fee for an individual in 2026?
The surcharge is Rs 25,000 from 1 July 2026, up from Rs 1,000. An individual who furnishes Form ATL-U does not pay it.
How much do you charge for Form ATL-U?
Rs 2,000 for preparing and furnishing it, added to the published return fee: Rs 5,500 total for a salaried return.
Can I reduce the late-filing penalty?
Yes, by filing quickly. Under the current text it falls by 75% if the return is filed within one month after the due date, 50% within two months and 25% within three.
Is NTN registration free?
Yes, at FBR. We charge Rs 2,000 if you want us to register you.
Do I pay the surcharge and Form ATL-U both?
No. They are alternative routes; the undertaking replaces the surcharge for an individual.
Do I pay the penalty to you or to FBR?
To FBR, through a PSID challan. Our fee is separate and covers preparing and filing.
Is the Rs 2,000 for Form ATL-U paid to FBR?
No. FBR charges nothing for the undertaking. Rs 2,000 is our fee for preparing and furnishing it.
Is there a discount if I file several years together?
Our pricing page offers a combined quote for multiple years. Message us with the years you need and we confirm the total before starting.
