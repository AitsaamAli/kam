Contractor Income Tax Pakistan 2026 — Section 153 Guide

June 24, 2026Aitsaam Ali6 min readContractors

Contractors aur sub-contractors — construction, IT, services, ya kisi bhi field mein — ek unique tax situation face karte hain: har payment par WHT deduct hoti hai, phir bhi annual return file karna zaroori hai. Ye guide Section 153 WHT, self-assessment, aur filing process poora cover karta hai.

TL;DR

Contracts par Section 153(1)(c) WHT — filer ~7%, non-filer ~14% — client deduct karta hai. Ye adjustable tax hai. Har contractor ko NTN, annual return, aur WHT certificates collect karna zaroori hai.

Kaun Affected Hai

  • Construction contractors — buildings, roads, infrastructure
  • IT/software service contractors
  • Sub-contractors kaam karne wale principal contractor ke through
  • Government aur private sector dono ke contracts par ye rule apply hota hai

Self-Assessment aur Return Filing

Chahe WHT already deduct ho chuki ho, contractor ko apni annual income tax return file karna zaroori hai — return mein total contract income declare karke, deducted WHT ko credit ke taur par claim kiya jata hai. Agar actual tax liability WHT se kam hai, refund bhi claim ho sakta hai.

Registered firm vs individual: Agar contractor ek registered company/AOP hai, corporate/AOP tax rates apply hongi. Individual contractor ke liye normal slab rates apply hoti hain. Structure choose karte waqt tax impact zaroor consider karo.

Records Maintain Karna

  • Har contract ka agreement/purchase order
  • WHT certificates har client se
  • Bank statements — payment receipts verify karne ke liye
  • Material/labor expenses ke invoices (business expense claim ke liye)

Aksar Puchhe Gaye Sawal — Contractor Tax

Contractors par kaunsi withholding tax lagti hai Pakistan mein?
Contracts execution par Section 153(1)(c) ke tehat WHT lagti hai — filer ke liye approximately 7%, non-filer ke liye 14%. Ye tax client/principal ne payment karte waqt deduct karni hoti hai.
Contractor ka WHT adjustable hai ya final tax?
Zyada tar contractor WHT adjustable hoti hai — annual return mein credit milta hai. Certain categories (jaise companies ke against specific contracts) mein final tax bhi ho sakta hai — apni situation professional se confirm karwao.
Sub-contractor ka tax treatment kya hai?
Sub-contractor bhi principal contractor jaisi hi WHT treatment ke liye eligible hai — jo bhi payment sub-contractor ko milti hai us par bhi Section 153 WHT apply hogi, aur usko bhi apni annual return file karni hogi.
Contractor ko NTN aur return filing zaroori hai?
Haan. Contractor ho ya sub-contractor, agar koi bhi business income hai toh NTN registration aur annual income tax return filing zaroori hai — return na file karne se ATL status miss ho jata hai aur WHT rate double lagti hai.
