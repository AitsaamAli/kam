🎓 UK Student Visa Ke Liye Pakistan FBR Tax Documents — Financial Evidence Checklist 2026

August 25, 2026Aitsaam Ali10 min readUKVI Verified

2024 mein 35,500 se zyada Pakistani students ne UK student visa liya — 14% growth year-on-year — aur Pakistan ab UK ke liye 3rd-largest international student source country ban chuka hai, jabke 2019 mein ye ranking 11th thi. Lekin isi waqt UK visa approval rate 82% (2023) se girkar 74% reh gaya hai — matlab refusals barh rahe hain, aur zyada tar refusal ki wajah financial documentation hoti hai, na ke academic eligibility. Ye guide specifically student visa ke financial evidence — maintenance funds, parents ki FBR history, aur CAS-linked requirements — par focus karti hai, taake application submit hone se pehle har financial gap identify aur fix ho sake.

TL;DR: UK student visa mein maintenance funds London ke liye £1,334/month aur baaki UK ke liye £1,023/month (9 months tak) chahiye — 28 consecutive days tak bank mein hone chahiye. Agar parents ya koi financial sponsor funds provide kar rahe hain, unki FBR tax returns aur wealth statement funds ki legitimacy prove karne ka sabse strong tareeqa hai. CAS issue hone se pehle ye sab arrange hona chahiye.

Undergraduate, Postgraduate Aur PhD — Financial Requirement Mein Farq

Bohat se families ye samajhti hain ke financial requirement har education level par same hoti hai — asal mein aisa nahi hai. Maintenance amount ka formula (£1,334 ya £1,023 per month) sab par apply hota hai, lekin tuition fee ka scale kaafi different hota hai jo total required amount ko significantly badal deta hai:

  • Undergraduate courses: tuition typically £13,000-£25,000/year — is liye total required funds (tuition + 9 months maintenance) sabse zyada hote hain, aur zyada tar cases mein parents hi sponsor hote hain
  • Postgraduate (Masters): tuition £15,000-£35,000 depend karta hai course par — funding scholarship, partial university funding, ya self-sponsorship se bhi aa sakti hai
  • PhD/Research programs: aksar fully-funded ya stipend-based hote hain, jis mein financial evidence ka requirement kam ho jata hai agar funding letter university se mil jaye

PhD aur fully-funded Masters students ke liye, agar university ya koi external scholarship (HEC, Commonwealth, Chevening) tuition aur living cost dono cover kar rahi hai, toh sponsor ki FBR documentation ki zarurat kam ho jati hai — lekin partial funding ke cases mein remaining amount ke liye wahi maintenance rules apply hote hain jo self-funded students par hote hain.

Maintenance Funds — Kitna Paisa Aur Kitne Din Tak

UK student visa (Student Route) ka financial requirement seedha CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) se linked hai. University CAS issue karne se pehle tuition fee aur living cost ka combination confirm karti hai, aur applicant ko wo maintenance funds apne ya sponsor ke bank account mein 28 consecutive days tak hold karni hoti hain — CAS issue hone se pehle nahi, balke application se pehle wale window mein.

  • London mein padhne wale students: £1,334 per month, 9 months tak = £12,006 (tuition ke ilawa)
  • London se bahar UK mein: £1,023 per month, 9 months tak = £9,207 (tuition ke ilawa)
  • Agar tuition ka ek saal already paid ho: sirf living cost ka maintenance chahiye, tuition dobara nahi jorna
  • 28-day rule: balance 28 din se kam ke liye fluctuate nahi hona chahiye — ek din bhi threshold se neeche jaye toh dobara 28-day window shuru karni parti hai

Ye 28-day rule wahi jagah hai jahan zyada tar Pakistani applicants mistake karte hain. University fee deadline nazdeek dekh kar parents last moment mein FDR break karte hain ya property se paisa nikal kar account mein daal dete hain — is se balance toh dikh jata hai, lekin 28 consecutive days ka pattern nahi banta, aur case worker isay reject kar sakta hai chahe amount sahi ho.

Financial Sponsor Kaun Ho Sakta Hai — Aur Kya Prove Karna Hota Hai

UKVI rules ke mutabiq funds applicant ke apne account mein ho sakte hain, ya phir ek "official financial sponsor" ke account mein — jo generally parents, legal guardian, ya spouse hote hain. Agar funds sponsor ke account mein hain (jo Pakistani students ke case mein zyada common hai), do cheezein additionally prove karni hoti hain:

  • Relationship proof: birth certificate, family registration certificate (B-form), ya CNIC ke through parent-child relationship
  • Consent letter: sponsor ki taraf se ek signed letter ke funds student ke education ke liye available hain

Lekin asal sawal ye hai — case worker kaise satisfy ho ke sponsor ke paas genuinely itna paisa hai, aur ye paisa unki legitimate income se aaya hai? Yahin par FBR documentation kaam aati hai. Agar sponsor (aksar father ya mother) salaried hain, business owner hain, ya property se rental income lete hain — unki FBR tax return aur wealth statement is baat ka strongest proof hoti hai ke declared income aur bank balance ek dusre se match karte hain.

Practical tip: Agar sponsor non-filer hain ya unki returns purani hain, application submit karne se pehle unki NTN registration aur pending returns complete karwao — sirf ek fresh bank statement se funds ka source establish nahi hota.

Refusal Ki Sabse Aam Wajahein — Student Visa Specific

Kamboh Associates ke paas student visa clients mein ye galtiyan sab se zyada dekhi jaati hain:

  • 28-day window break hona: deadline se just pehle paisa jama karna, jis se continuous holding period disturb ho jata hai
  • Sponsor non-filer hona: parents ki achi income hai lekin kabhi tax return file nahi ki — bank balance ka source unexplained reh jata hai
  • Currency conversion mismatch: PKR se GBP conversion galat rate par calculate karna, jis se required amount se kam show hota hai
  • Multiple sponsors ka overlap: ek se zyada logon (chacha, mamu) ka paisa mix karna bina proper documentation ke — UKVI sirf defined "financial sponsor" categories accept karta hai
  • Wealth statement outdated hona: sponsor ki wealth statement purani filing year ki ho, current assets reflect na ho rahe hon

Inme se zyada tar galtiyan avoidable hain agar planning early shuru ki jaye. Kamboh Associates ka observation ye hai ke jo families application se 2-3 mahine pehle apni FBR documentation review karwati hain, unka refusal rate un families se kaafi kam hota hai jo sirf CAS milne ke baad last-minute mein sab kuch arrange karti hain. Financial documentation ek "checkbox" nahi hai — ye ek consistent, verifiable history honi chahiye jo case worker ko genuinely convince kare.

Bank Statement Format — Kya UKVI Accept Karta Hai

Bank statement sirf balance dikhana kaafi nahi — UKVI ke specific format requirements hain jo aksar families miss kar deti hain:

  • Statement bank ke official letterhead par honi chahiye, ya digitally-generated ho toh bank ki verification stamp/seal ke saath
  • Account holder ka naam, account number, aur statement date clearly visible honi chahiye
  • Statement application date se 31 din se zyada purani nahi honi chahiye
  • Personal savings account ya current account acceptable hai — lekin joint account ho toh dono holders ka naam aur relationship clear hona chahiye

Agar sponsor ka account FBR mein declared assets ke saath match karta hai — yani wealth statement mein jo bank balance declare kiya gaya, wahi ya us se consistent amount statement mein bhi reflect ho — ye case ko significantly strengthen karta hai. Mismatch (jahan wealth statement kam dikhati hai lekin bank statement zyada) red flag ban sakta hai, kyunke case worker sochta hai ke funds kahin aur se recently transfer hue hain.

FAQs — UK Student Visa Financial Evidence

UK student visa ke liye khud student ki FBR return chahiye ya sirf parents ki?
Zyada tar cases mein student khud earning nahi karta, is liye focus sponsor (parents/guardian) ki FBR return aur wealth statement par hota hai. Agar student khud bhi income rakhta hai (part-time business, freelance), toh uski apni filing bhi additional support deti hai.
28-day rule mein agar ek din bhi balance kam ho jaye toh kya hota hai?
Agar balance threshold se neeche jata hai chahe ek din ke liye, 28-day continuous holding period dobara shuru karni parti hai. Isi liye funds ko application se kaafi pehle stable rakhna zaruri hai, last-minute adjustment se bacho.
Sponsor non-filer hai — kya student visa reject ho sakta hai?
Directly non-filer hone ki wajah se visa reject nahi hota, lekin agar bank balance ka source explain nahi ho pata, case worker suspicious ho sakta hai. Sponsor ki NTN registration aur pending returns complete karwana strongly recommended hai — 5-8 working days mein ho sakta hai agar urgent basis par kiya jaye.
Ek se zyada log milkar funds provide kar rahe hain — kya ye acceptable hai?
UKVI generally ek ya do defined financial sponsors accept karta hai (parents/legal guardian). Agar extended family (chacha, mamu, dost) paisa de rahe hain, ye complications create kar sakta hai — behtar hai funds ko ek clear sponsor ke account mein consolidate karo jiska FBR record bhi saaf ho.
London aur non-London maintenance amount mein kya farq hai?
London mein padhne wale students ke liye £1,334/month aur London se bahar ke liye £1,023/month, dono 9 months tak calculate hote hain. Ye university ki location par depend karta hai, student ke apne residence par nahi.
Wealth statement mein sponsor ki property include karni chahiye?
Haan, wealth statement sponsor ke tamam assets — property, savings, investments, gold — ka snapshot honi chahiye. Ye funds ke overall financial background ko strengthen karti hai, sirf bank balance akela utna convincing nahi hota jitna asset-backed wealth history.
