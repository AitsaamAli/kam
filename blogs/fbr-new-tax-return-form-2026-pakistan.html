FBR Naya Tax Return Form 2026 — IRIS 2.0 Mein Kya Badla Hai

July 22, 2026Aitsaam Ali8 min readTax Year 2026

IRIS 2.0 par Tax Year 2026 ka return form pehle se kaafi different hai. FBR ne salaried filers ke liye process simplify kiya hai — withholding tax data auto-populate hota hai, wealth statement reconciliation ek dedicated wizard mein hoti hai, aur ek quick single-page mode bhi add hua hai chhoti income walon ke liye. Is guide mein hum poora comparison, naya form kaise use karein, aur kin cheezon mein galti se bachein — sab dekhtay hain.

TL;DR

Naya IRIS 2.0 form: auto-populated WHT data, easy wealth reconciliation wizard, salaried-only quick mode. Business/property/capital gain walon ke liye detailed form same hai. Auto-data hamesha verify karo submission se pehle. Last date 30 September 2026.

Kya Badla Hai — Naye Form Ka Overview

Har saal ki tarah FBR IRIS return form ko update karta hai, lekin Tax Year 2026 ke liye tabdeeliyan structural hain — sirf naye fields ka izafa nahi. Maqsad ye hai ke zyada tar filers ke liye return process tez aur kam error-prone ho jaye.

  • Auto-populated Withholding Tax: Employer aur bank ke record se WHT data khud-b-khud form mein aa jata hai
  • Wealth Reconciliation Wizard: Opening vs closing wealth ka gap khud calculate hokar dikhata hai — koi manual subtraction nahi
  • Quick Mode (Salary-Only): Agar sirf salary income hai, ek chhota single-page form available hai
  • Bank IBAN Validation: Refund ke liye IBAN system verify karta hai submission se pehle
  • Draft Auto-Save: Har kuch minutes mein progress khud save hoti hai — session timeout se data loss nahi hoga

Kis ke liye best hai: Quick Mode sirf un filers ke liye hai jinki income sirf salary se hai aur koi property, business, ya capital gain nahi. Multiple income sources walon ke liye detailed form hi use hoga.

Quick Mode — Salaried Filers Ke Liye

Agar aapki sirf salary income hai — koi rental property, business, ya capital gain nahi — IRIS 2.0 ka naya Quick Mode sabse tez rasta hai:

Quick Mode Select Karein

Login ke baad "Return of Income" mein "Quick Filing — Salaried" option choose karein agar sirf salary income hai.

Employer Data Verify Karein

Aapke employer ka NTN, salary amount, aur withheld tax (Section 149) auto-populate ho jayega. Apni salary slip se match karke confirm karein.

Bank Accounts Add Karein

Sab bank accounts list karein — profit auto-fetch hone ki koshish karta hai lekin manually bhi verify zaroor karein.

Wealth Wizard Complete Karein

Property, vehicle, gold, cash — declare karein. System opening se closing wealth ka gap khud calculate karega.

Review aur Submit

Final summary screen par sab numbers ek nazar mein dekhein, phir submit karein. Acknowledgment receipt turant milegi.

Important: Quick Mode mein bhi galti se galat data submit ho sakta hai agar auto-populated figures verify na kiye jayein. Employer ya bank ka data IRIS tak der se pohnchne ki wajah se kabhi incomplete ho sakta hai.

Wealth Reconciliation Wizard

Sabse bara pain-point jo purane form mein tha — opening aur closing wealth ka manual reconciliation — ab wizard se automatic ho gaya hai:

StepKya Hota Hai
1. Opening WealthPichlay saal ki closing wealth khud carry-forward hoti hai
2. Income DeclarationIs saal ki income (salary, business, rental, capital gain) enter karo
3. Expenses EstimateGhar ke expenses ka reasonable estimate dena hota hai
4. Closing WealthAssets/liabilities ki current position declare karo
5. Auto-ReconciliationSystem khud check karta hai: Opening + Income − Expenses = Closing?

Agar reconciliation match nahi karta, system ek warning dikhata hai submission se pehle — ye feature naye form ka sabse useful hissa hai kyunke unexplained wealth increase hi FBR notice ka sabse bara trigger hota hai.

Naye Form Mein Common Mistakes

Bachne ki cheezein: Auto-populated WHT ko bina verify kiye submit karna, spouse/bachon ki assets apni wealth mein include na karna, quick mode use karte hue property/business income ko chupa dena (ye galat ATL status de sakta hai), aur reconciliation warning ko ignore kar ke force-submit karna.

Aksar Puchhe Gaye Sawal — Naya Tax Return Form 2026

FBR ka naya tax return form 2026 mein kya tabdeeli aayi hai?
IRIS 2.0 par Tax Year 2026 ka return form pehle se zyada simplified hai — withholding tax certificates automatically employer aur bank record se populate hotay hain, wealth statement reconciliation ek dedicated wizard mein hoti hai, aur salary-only filers ke liye ek chhota single-page quick mode add hua hai. Purana form manual data entry par zyada depend karta tha.
Kya salaried person ke liye return filing ab aasan ho gayi hai?
Haan. Agar sirf salary income hai aur koi property/business/capital gain nahi — IRIS 2.0 ka quick mode employer ka WHT data auto-fetch karta hai aur zyada tar fields pre-filled dikhata hai. Filer ko sirf verify karke submit karna hota hai. Complex cases abhi bhi detailed form use karte hain.
Auto-populated fields galat ho toh kya karein?
Auto-populated data hamesha verify karo — kabhi kabhi employer ya bank ka record IRIS tak der se pohnchta hai ya galat hota hai. Agar figure match nahi karta apni salary slip / bank statement se, manually correct karke sahi amount enter karo. Submission se pehle ek dafa professional se cross-check zaroor karwao.
Purana return form (Tax Year 2025) abhi bhi use ho sakta hai?
Nahi. Tax Year 2026 ki return sirf naye IRIS 2.0 form format mein hi file hogi. Purana format sirf pichlay tax years (2025 aur pehle) ki late/revised returns ke liye available rehta hai jahan wo already shuru ho chuki thi.
