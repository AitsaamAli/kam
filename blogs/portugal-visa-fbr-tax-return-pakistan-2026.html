🇵🇹 Portugal Visa Ke Liye Pakistan FBR Tax Return aur NTN — D7 aur Digital Nomad Guide 2026

July 23, 2026Aitsaam Ali9 min readD7 Verified

Portugal mein Pakistani community tezi se barh rahi hai — mostly Lisbon aur surrounding areas mein settle. D7 Passive Income Visa ho, Digital Nomad Visa ho, ya Golden Visa (jo ab real estate se hat kar dusray routes par shift ho chuki hai) — Pakistan mein FBR tax returns, NTN aur wealth statement documentation ka lazmi hissa hain. Ye guide specifically Portugal ke liye hai.

D7 Passive Income Visa — Kis Ke Liye Hai

Portugal D7 Visa "Passive Income Visa" ke naam se mashhoor hai — un logon ke liye jinke paas regular passive income hai:

  • Eligible income: Rental income, pension, dividends, ya stable remote work income
  • FBR se prove karo: Pakistan mein income tax returns aur wealth statement se ye income legitimate prove hoti hai
  • Minimum threshold: Portuguese minimum wage ke multiple ke barabar — exact current amount consulate se confirm karo
  • Bank statements: Consistent income history last 6-12 mahinay ki dikhani hoti hai

Property owners ke liye: Agar Pakistan mein rental property se stable income hai, D7 Visa ek natural fit hai — FBR rental income tax return aur wealth statement sath mein consulate ko strong case dete hain.

Portugal Digital Nomad Visa — Freelancers Ke Liye

Portugal ka Digital Nomad Visa remote workers aur IT professionals ke liye hai jinka income foreign clients se ata ho (Portugal-based clients se nahi). Minimum income Portuguese minimum wage ke multiple ke hisab se set hoti hai — exact figure consulate se confirm karein. Pakistan mein FBR returns + client contracts income prove karte hain.

Portugal Golden Visa — 2023 Ke Baad Ki Tabdeeliyan

Important update: Portugal ne 2023 mein "Mais Habitação" law ke zariye real estate investment route ko Golden Visa ke liye band kar diya. Ab investment fund route, job creation, ya cultural donation jaisay alternative routes available hain. Exact current thresholds immigration lawyer ya consulate se confirm karein.

FBR ka role in sab alternative routes mein bhi same hai — funds ka source prove karna wealth statement aur tax returns ke zariye:

  • Wealth statement mein declared savings + investments ka history dikhata hai funds ka source
  • Tax returns prove karte hain ke income legal business/salary se thi
  • Business owners ke liye: company tax returns + audited accounts

D7 Ke Liye Pakistani Passive Income Ka Saboot

D7 visa ka sab se mazboot case woh hai jahan passive income Pakistan ke documents se saaf nazar aaye. Har qism ki aamdani ke liye alag saboot chahiye:

Passive incomePakistani saboot
Ghar ya dukaan ka kirayaTenancy agreement, tenant ke tax certificates, return mein property income
PensionPension certificate, bank statement
Shares ka dividendBroker ya CDC statement, dividend tax certificates
Bank ya savings certificates ka munafaBank ke tax deduction certificates

Consulate ye dekhta hai ke aamdani mustaqil hai. Is liye kam az kam do saal ki filed returns jin mein yehi aamdani dikhayi gayi ho, sirf bank statement se zyada wazan rakhti hain.

Portugal Mein NIF Aur Tax Residence

Portugal pohanchne se pehle hi NIF (Portuguese tax number) lena parta hai. Portugal mein 183 din se zyada rehne par aap wahan ke tax resident ban jate hain aur Pakistani kiraya ya munafa wahan bhi declare hota hai. Portugal ka purana NHR scheme naye logon ke liye band ho chuka hai, is liye kisi bhi tax chhoot par bharosa karne se pehle Portuguese adviser se baat karein.

Pakistan Chhorne Ke Baad

Pakistan mein rental property rakhne walon ko return file karte rehna chahiye, kyunke kiraya Pakistan mein taxable hai aur Active Taxpayers List par rehna property ki future farokht mein advance tax kam karta hai.

Frequently Asked Questions

D7 visa ke liye Pakistani kiraya kaise prove karein?

Tenancy agreement, tenant ke tax deduction certificates aur filed returns jin mein property income dikhayi gayi ho, sab se mazboot saboot hain.

Portugal ka NHR scheme ab bhi milta hai?

Purana NHR scheme naye applicants ke liye band ho chuka hai. Kisi bhi chhoot se pehle Portuguese tax adviser se confirm karein.
