Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need both FBR and PRA registration in Lahore?
If you sell goods, only FBR Sales Tax (STRN) applies. If you provide services in Punjab (including Lahore), PRA registration is required if turnover exceeds Rs. 5 million. Many businesses in Lahore need BOTH — for example, a restaurant supplying packaged food items.
Can you file my PRA monthly return from outside Lahore?
Yes. PRA returns are filed online on the PRA portal. We handle your monthly PRA return remotely — you just share your sales invoices via WhatsApp and we handle the rest. No need to visit any office.
What is the penalty for late PRA return in Lahore?
PRA imposes a penalty of Rs. 10,000 per late return plus default surcharge at 1.5% per month on unpaid tax. Multiple late returns can result in PRA de-registration and FBR action on income tax. File by the 15th of each month.
