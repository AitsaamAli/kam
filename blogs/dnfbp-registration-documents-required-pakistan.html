DNFBP Registration Documents: What FBR Needs From You, and What You Must Collect From Every Customer

26 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 12 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 26 November 2026
There are two document lists in DNFBP compliance: one you give FBR to register, and one you collect from every customer. Both are set by FBR's Regulations.

“What documents are required for DNFBP registration?” is usually asked as one question, but the Regulations answer two. The first list is short and goes to FBR once, to register the business. The second is long and is collected from every customer, for as long as you operate, because it is what an FBR inspector asks to see. This guide gives both lists from the text of FBR's Regulations and guidance, with the practical forms they take in a property office, a jewellery shop or an accounting practice.

Short answer

To register as a DNFBP you need an active NTN and IRIS access for whoever runs the business, details of owners, partners, directors and beneficial owners, the compliance officer's details, and any information FBR requires, including criminal records of senior management and beneficial owners (regulation 3(2)). After registration, regulation 8(11) lists what counts as reliable identification for customers: a CNIC, NICOP, POC, Form B or ARC/passport for individuals; board resolution, memorandum, articles, incorporation certificate and director lists for companies; the trust instrument and registration papers for trusts. Copies are stamped “Original seen” (regulation 6).

List 1: What You Provide to Register

Short answer: An NTN and IRIS access, details of owners and beneficial owners, the compliance officer, and information FBR requires, including criminal records of senior management and beneficial owners.

Regulation 3(2) of FBR's AML/CFT Regulations for DNFBPs 2020 says the DNFBP “shall provide any information or documentation that may be required by the Board for the purposes of registration or keeping the DNFBP registration up to date, including but not limited to criminal records of the senior management and beneficial owners”. FBR's DNFBP portal link opens the IRIS login, so registration starts from an IRIS account. In practice that means:

List 2a: Documents From an Individual Customer

Short answer: CNIC; for overseas Pakistanis a NICOP and/or passport; POC for former nationals; Form B for minors; ARC or passport with valid visa for foreigners.

Regulation 8(11)(a) sets the reliable and independent documents for a natural person:

CustomerIdentity document
Pakistani residentComputerized National Identity Card (CNIC) issued by NADRA
Overseas Pakistani or dual nationalNICOP and/or a machine-readable passport
Pakistani who has given up nationalityPakistan Origin Card (POC) and/or passport
Child under 18Form B or smart/juvenile card issued by NADRA
Foreign nationalAlien Registration Card (ARC) from NARA, or passport with a valid visa or other proof of legal stay

FBR's short guidance for real estate agents adds the information to record alongside: full name, date of birth, residential address, identity number, whether the customer acts for someone else, beneficial ownership and the purpose of the relationship. For higher-risk customers and PEPs it adds source of wealth or funds, which may be shown by an NTN, bank statement, tax documents, salary slips or business information. Its CDD form suggests a recent utility bill for address and NADRA's online verification services for identity.

List 2b: Documents From a Company

Short answer: Board resolution, memorandum and articles, incorporation certificate, commencement declaration and directors list, and identity documents of directors and beneficial owners.

Regulation 8(11)(b) asks for certified copies of:

  1. the board resolution naming who is authorised to act (not needed for a single member company);
  2. the memorandum of association and, where applicable, the articles;
  3. the certificate of incorporation;
  4. the SECP-registered declaration for commencement of business, where the Companies Act 2017 requires one;
  5. the list of directors filed under the Companies Act 2017, where applicable;
  6. identity documents of all directors, beneficial owners and authorised persons, as in List 2a; and
  7. any other documents needed to understand the company's activities and sources and uses of funds, including annual accounts.

Regulation 8(8) also requires the company's name, legal form and proof of existence, the powers that bind it, the names of its senior managers, and its registered office and principal place of business.

For a trust, regulation 8(10) adds the persons whose identity must be known: settlor, trustees, protector, beneficiaries or class of beneficiaries, and anyone exercising ultimate effective control.

When Someone Acts for the Customer

Property deals in Pakistan are often signed by an attorney or a family member. Regulation 8(4) requires you to identify every person acting on the customer's behalf, verify that person's identity from reliable documents, and verify their authority to act, for example a registered power of attorney. FBR's FAQs (Q31) list “any person acting on behalf of a customer” among the people CDD is conducted on.

How the Documents Must Be Kept

Short answer: Stamp copies “Original seen”, keep a list of refused customers, and retain CDD records at least five years after the relationship ends.

For a real estate agent, FBR's short guidance lists the core deal file as the CNIC of seller and purchaser, the sale deed, the bank draft and the CDD form.

When the Documents Must Be in Hand

Regulation 8(12) says identity of the customer and beneficial owner is verified before establishing a business relationship or conducting an occasional transaction. Regulation 8(13) allows verification shortly after only if it happens as soon as reasonably practicable, is essential not to interrupt normal business, and the risks are effectively managed. A customer who will not produce documents should be refused, recorded on the refused list, and considered for an STR, as FBR's FAQs (Q41) suggest.

Relying on Someone Else's CDD

Property deals often involve two dealers, one for each side, and sometimes a bank or another regulated firm. Regulation 12 lets a DNFBP rely on a third party for identifying the customer and CDD, but only on four conditions: the relying DNFBP remains liable for any failure; it immediately obtains the CDD information from the third party; it keeps copies of the identification documents as regulation 6 requires; and it satisfies itself that the third party is supervised by an AML/CFT regulatory authority, or an equivalent foreign authority, and has CDD and record-keeping measures in place. FBR's FAQs (Q40) put it simply: you can authorise another person to carry out CDD, “however, you are still legally responsible”. In practice, take copies of the other dealer's CDD file for your customer, and check that the dealer is itself a registered DNFBP.

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are required for DNFBP registration?
An NTN and IRIS access for the business, details of owners, partners or directors and beneficial owners, the compliance officer's details, and any information FBR requires, including criminal records of senior management and beneficial owners under regulation 3(2).
Which ID can I accept from an overseas Pakistani customer?
Regulation 8(11)(a) allows a NICOP and/or a machine-readable passport for non-resident or overseas Pakistanis and dual nationals.
What documents does a company customer need to give?
Certified copies of the board resolution, memorandum and articles, certificate of incorporation, commencement declaration and directors list where applicable, and identity documents of directors, beneficial owners and authorised persons.
What does “Original seen” mean?
Regulation 6 requires copies of identity and verification documents to be stamped “Original seen” by the real estate agent, DPMS or accountant after viewing the originals.
How long must CDD documents be kept?
At least five years after the business relationship ends; records relating to STRs and CTRs at least ten years from filing, per FBR's FAQs.
Can I complete the deal before collecting documents?
Only in the limited case regulation 8(13) allows: verification as soon as reasonably practicable, essential to avoid interrupting business, with risks managed.
Can I use another dealer's KYC documents for my customer?
Regulation 12 allows reliance on a supervised third party, but you remain liable, must immediately obtain the CDD information and keep copies of the identification documents.
Is NADRA verification accepted for CDD?
FBR's short guidance for real estate agents refers to electronic verification through NADRA's identity verification services alongside original documents.
