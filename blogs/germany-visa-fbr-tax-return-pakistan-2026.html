Opportunity Card — Pakistan Se Germany Naya Rasta

Germany ka Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte) June 2024 mein launch hua — ye un logon ke liye hai jo Germany mein job dhundhna chahte hain lekin abhi concrete job offer nahi hai. Points system:

  • Qualification (2 points): University degree ya vocational qualification (Pakistan HEC recognized ya evaluated)
  • Language German (1 point): A1 level — baaki Germany mein seekhna hoga
  • Language English (1 point): C1 level
  • Work Experience (1 point): 5+ saal experience in your field
  • Pakistan mein Germany connection: Previous Germany stay ya partner in Germany (bonus)
  • Age (1 point): Under 35 years old
  • Minimum required: 6 points total

Financial requirement: €1,027/month ke hisaab se adequate funds (blocked account recommended). FBR wealth statement ye funds ka source prove karta hai.

IT professionals ke liye: Pakistan mein software engineer, developer ya IT professional jo English mein fluent hai — Opportunity Card ke liye strong candidate hai. FBR mein declared IT income (especially PSEB registered ya freelance income) employment history aur income levels prove karta hai. Kamboh Associates IT income filing bhi handle karta hai.

EU Blue Card — Salary aur Qualification

EU Blue Card Germany ka premium work visa hai — university degree wale professionals ke liye:

  • Salary threshold 2026: €45,552/year general, €41,042/year shortage occupations (IT, engineering, medicine)
  • Job offer: German employer se formal offer zaruri — Chancenkarte ke bina Blue Card ke liye job pehle dhundhna hota hai
  • Degree evaluation: Pakistan degree German anabin database ya Uni-Assist se evaluate karwani hogi
  • FBR returns: Previous employment aur salary history prove karne ke liye — especially agar German employer background check maange

FAQs — Germany Visa FBR

Opportunity Card ke liye kitne funds chahiye aur FBR kaise help karta hai?
Opportunity Card ke liye €1,027/month savings required hain — typically 12 months ke liye €12,324. Pakistan mein FBR wealth statement bank balance aur savings declare karta hai — ye German embassy ke liye funds ka documented source hai. Bank statement ke saath FBR wealth statement strongest combination hai.
Germany Ausbildung ke liye parents ki returns chahiye?
Haan. Ausbildung ke doran typically €649/month stipend milti hai — lekin ye visa processing ke waqt prove nahi hota. Parents ya sponsor ki FBR returns + bank statement ye prove karte hain ke backing available hai. Plus blocked account (€11,208) usually required hota hai for Ausbildung visa.
German embassy Islamabad mein appointment kaise milti hai?
German Embassy Islamabad: Ramna 5, Diplomatic Enclave. Appointment online book hoti hai — visumantrag.de ya embassy website se. Germany ka appointment backlog significant hai — 3–6 months pehle book karo. VFS Global bhi Germany visa services handle karta hai Pakistan mein.
Germany mein IT professionals ke liye Blue Card salary threshold kya hai?
IT sector Germany mein shortage occupation hai — isliye reduced threshold €41,042/year (2026) apply hoti hai instead of regular €45,552. Software engineers, developers, data scientists, IT architects — sab qualify karte hain. Pakistan mein comparable salary FBR returns mein declared honi chahiye for credibility.
Germany jane ke baad Pakistan mein property hai — kya karna chahiye?
Pakistan mein property se rental income non-resident ke liye 15% flat rate par taxable hai Pakistan mein. Germany mein bhi declare karna hoga — Germany-Pakistan DTA ke zariye double taxation se bacha ja sakta hai (credit method). Pakistan mein non-resident return file karo sirf Pakistan-source income declare karte hue.
