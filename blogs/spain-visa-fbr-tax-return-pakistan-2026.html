🇪🇸 Spain Visa Ke Liye Pakistan FBR Tax Return aur NTN — Schengen aur Digital Nomad Guide 2026

Spain mein 80,000+ registered Pakistanis hain aur ye number tezi se barh raha hai — especially 2023-2026 ke beech Digital Nomad Visa launch hone ke baad. Schengen visa ho, Digital Nomad Visa ho, ya Spain Golden Visa — Pakistan mein FBR tax returns, NTN aur wealth statement documentation ka lazmi hissa hain. Spanish consulate financial proof ke bagair visa nahi deta. Ye guide specifically Spain ke liye hai.

Spain Digital Nomad Visa — Pakistani Freelancers Ke Liye

Spain ka Digital Nomad Visa (Ley de Startups ke zariye 2023 mein launch) Pakistani freelancers, IT professionals aur remote workers ke liye ek new opportunity hai:

  • Income requirement: €2,646/month (200% Spanish minimum wage, 2026) — approximately Rs 10 Lakh/month
  • Income source: Foreign clients se honi chahiye (Pakistan, UAE, USA etc.) — Spain se nahi
  • FBR se prove karo: Pakistan mein income tax returns ye prove karte hain ke aap ka income legitimate foreign source se hai
  • Health insurance: Spain mein private health insurance zaruri
  • Pakistan mein clients: Pakistan mein clients se income FBR mein declare hoti hai — ye prove karna Digital Nomad Visa ke liye best scenario hai

IT professionals ke liye: Pakistan mein PSEB registered ya FBR ke saath file karne wale IT freelancers ke liye Spain Digital Nomad Visa ideal option hai. Income FBR mein declared hai — spain consulate ko prove karna aasaan ho jata hai. Kamboh Associates IT export income ki filing bhi handle karta hai.

Spain Golden Visa Khatam Ho Chuka Hai

Spain ne property investment par milne wala golden visa 2025 mein khatam kar diya. Jo log ab bhi €500,000 property investment ke zariye residence ka soch rahe hain, unhein immigration lawyer se maujooda raste confirm karne chahiyein. Pakistani documents ka kirdar ab zyada tar Digital Nomad, work aur family visas mein hai.

Barcelona Aur Spain Ki Pakistani Community

Spain mein Pakistanion ki sab se bari tadaad Catalonia, khaas tor par Barcelona mein hai, jahan bohat se log retail, restaurants aur transport mein kaam karte hain. Spain mein kaam shuru karne par NIE (foreigner identification number) milta hai, aur saalana Spanish return (IRPF) bhi file hoti hai.

Digital Nomad Visa ke liye foreign clients se aamdani ka saboot chahiye. Pakistan mein PSEB registration, bank ke foreign remittance certificates aur filed returns is aamdani ki tasdeeq karte hain.
