Spain mein 80,000+ registered Pakistanis hain aur ye number tezi se barh raha hai — especially 2023-2026 ke beech Digital Nomad Visa launch hone ke baad. Schengen visa ho, Digital Nomad Visa ho, ya Spain Golden Visa — Pakistan mein FBR tax returns, NTN aur wealth statement documentation ka lazmi hissa hain. Spanish consulate financial proof ke bagair visa nahi deta. Ye guide specifically Spain ke liye hai.

Spain Digital Nomad Visa — Pakistani Freelancers Ke Liye

Spain ka Digital Nomad Visa (Ley de Startups ke zariye 2023 mein launch) Pakistani freelancers, IT professionals aur remote workers ke liye ek new opportunity hai:

  • Income requirement: €2,646/month (200% Spanish minimum wage, 2026) — approximately Rs 10 Lakh/month
  • Income source: Foreign clients se honi chahiye (Pakistan, UAE, USA etc.) — Spain se nahi
  • FBR se prove karo: Pakistan mein income tax returns ye prove karte hain ke aap ka income legitimate foreign source se hai
  • Health insurance: Spain mein private health insurance zaruri
  • Pakistan mein clients: Pakistan mein clients se income FBR mein declare hoti hai — ye prove karna Digital Nomad Visa ke liye best scenario hai

IT professionals ke liye: Pakistan mein PSEB registered ya FBR ke saath file karne wale IT freelancers ke liye Spain Digital Nomad Visa ideal option hai. Income FBR mein declared hai — spain consulate ko prove karna aasaan ho jata hai. Kamboh Associates IT export income ki filing bhi handle karta hai.

Spain Golden Visa — Source of Funds Prove Karo

Spain Golden Visa requires €500,000 property investment in Spain. FBR ka role specifically funds ka source prove karna hai — anti-money laundering requirements ke liye Spanish authorities aur property sellers check karte hain:

  • Wealth statement mein declared savings + investments ka history dikhata hai funds ka source
  • Tax returns prove karte hain ke income legal business/salary se thi
  • Business owners ke liye: company tax returns + audited accounts
  • Property owners ke liye: FBR property records + capital gains history

Spain Golden Visa 2024 update: Spain ne 2024 mein Golden Visa program ki review announce ki thi — some property investment routes pe restrictions aaye hain especially Barcelona aur Madrid region mein. 2026 mein current status verify kar ke Spain consulate ya immigration lawyer se confirm karo before proceeding.

FAQs — Spain Visa FBR

Spain Schengen visa ke liye FBR return zaruri hai?
Haan. Spanish consulate financial sufficiency prove karne ke liye Pakistan FBR tax returns maangta hai. NTN, last 1–3 saal ki returns, wealth statement aur bank statements standard documentation hai. Non-filer ka matlab consulate ke liye verifiable income ka record nahi — rejection risk high ho jaata hai.
Spain Digital Nomad Visa ke liye kitni income chahiye?
Minimum €2,646/month (2026 rate — 200% of Spanish minimum wage). Ye income foreign clients se honi chahiye — Spain-based clients se nahi. Pakistan mein FBR returns + bank statements + client contracts ya employment letter ye income prove karte hain. Income consistently last 3–6 months se honi chahiye.
Spanish consulate Islamabad mein visa apply hoti hai?
Spain ki Pakistan mein Embassy Islamabad mein hai — Spanish Embassy, Street 6, Diplomatic Enclave, Islamabad. Schengen visa VFS Global ke zariye bhi apply hoti hai. Spain ke consulate ka appointment usually advance mein book karna padta hai — especially peak season mein.
Spain mein freelancer Pakistani bhi Spain ka tax denge?
Haan. Spain mein resident hone ke baad (183 din se zyada raho) Spain mein worldwide income IRPF ke under taxable hogi. Freelancers ko autónomo (self-employed) register karna hoga Spain mein. Pakistan income bhi Spain mein declare karni hogi. Tax consultant Spain mein bhi rakho.
Spain jane ke baad Pakistan mein return file karna hoga?
Non-resident hone ke baad (Pakistan mein 182 din se kam) Pakistan return file karna legally required hai agar Pakistan-source income hai — rental, savings, business income. Agar koi Pakistan income nahi toh return optional hai lekin advisable — future property transactions ke liye useful hoti hai.
