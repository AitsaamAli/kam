Faisal Town Block B

Faisal Town's main roads have a steady presence of medical businesses that aren't doctors' clinics: diagnostic labs and sample collection points, physiotherapy centres, dental labs, radiology and ultrasound units. They're capital-heavy, staff-heavy and paid by a mix of patients, panels and companies — a combination that needs careful records.

Referring doctorsReferral arrangementsPayments to doctors may need withholding

Panel and insurance payments arrive with tax deducted at source; collect the certificates. Referral payments to doctors are a sensitive area ethically and for tax — if you pay any, they must be recorded, and withholding may apply.

Equipment is the big cost

Analysers, centrifuges, X-ray and ultrasound machines, dental chairs and physiotherapy equipment are capital assets. Record the cost, depreciate them over their useful life, and keep the import or purchase documents. Leased equipment is different: lease rentals are expenses, and the equipment isn't your asset until transferred.

Reagents and consumables

Reagents and test kits are a lab's main running cost and are usually bought from distributors with invoices. Expired reagents are a cost too. Keeping purchase records by month makes the lab's cost structure easy to show.

Sales tax on medical services

Medical and healthcare services are generally treated favourably under provincial sales tax on services, with many exempt. The position for specific services — diagnostic tests, physiotherapy, cosmetic procedures — can differ, and the Punjab Revenue Authority's schedules decide it. We check the services you offer before advising.

Staff

Labs employ technologists, phlebotomists, receptionists and riders for home sample collection. Pay through the bank, withhold tax for anyone above the threshold, and register for EOBI once you meet the threshold.

Labs that collect samples in Lahore and send them to a central lab elsewhere are often franchisees or agents. Check whether your income is the full test fee or a commission — your agreement decides what goes in your return.

Structure

Single-owner labs often start as sole proprietorships. With several branches or partners, an AOP or company is usually more practical.

Home sample collection and online reports

Labs that offer home collection and online booking receive payments through payment gateways and wallets. Those receipts are reported electronically and should match your booking system. Riders' fuel and mobile allowances are business costs. If you run a website or app for bookings, its development and subscription costs are expenses or assets depending on their nature and life.

Faisal Town Block B — questions we get asked

Panel companies deduct tax from our lab's invoices. How do we claim it?
Collect each payer's withholding certificate and claim the deductions in your return.
Can we claim our ultrasound machine as an expense?
It's a capital asset, depreciated over its useful life rather than expensed in one year.
Are diagnostic services subject to PRA sales tax?
Many medical services are exempt or favourably treated, but specific services can differ. We check PRA's current schedules for your services.
We're a collection point for a bigger lab. What is our income?
It depends on your agreement — either the full test fee with a cost to the main lab, or a commission. The agreement decides.
