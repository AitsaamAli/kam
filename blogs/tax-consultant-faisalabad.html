Textile Industry Tax in Faisalabad

Faisalabad's textile sector has unique FBR tax treatment that differs from regular businesses. Understanding these rules is critical to avoid overpaying or facing FBR notices:

Business TypeTax Treatment
Textile Exporter1% final tax on export proceeds (Section 154)
Yarn / Fabric Local TraderNormal income tax slabs on net profit
Garment Manufacturer (local)Normal rates + minimum turnover tax
DTRE Scheme UsersDuty-free import of raw material for export
Sales Tax on TextileZero-rated on exports; 18% on local supply

Zero-Rating for Exporters: Textile exporters in Faisalabad are entitled to zero-rated sales tax on inputs. Proper STRN registration and monthly sales tax return filing is required to claim input tax refunds.

Traders & Manufacturers Tax Obligations

  • Advance tax on purchases under Section 236G
  • WHT on utility bills (electricity for factories)
  • Minimum turnover tax: 1.25% if net profit below threshold
  • Monthly sales tax return (18th of each month)
  • Annual income tax return by September 30
  • Wealth statement — machinery, vehicles, factory assets

Frequently Asked Questions

Do textile businesses in Faisalabad need separate sales tax registration?
Yes. All manufacturers and traders with turnover above Rs. 10 million, and all exporters regardless of turnover, must register for sales tax (STRN) with FBR. We handle registration in 5-7 working days.
What is the tax rate for Faisalabad textile exporters?
1% of export proceeds is the final tax under Section 154. This is a final discharge of liability — no additional income tax on export income. However, you must file an annual return to declare this income.
Can you file our monthly sales tax returns from Faisalabad?
Yes — we handle monthly FBR sales tax returns for Faisalabad businesses remotely. Share your purchase/sales summary and we file by the 18th of each month.
How much does income tax filing cost for a Faisalabad business?
Business / trader return starts at Rs. 7,000. Textile manufacturer with accounts: Rs. 10,000-15,000. Company audit and return: Rs. 20,000+. All packages include wealth statement.
