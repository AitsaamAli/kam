Frequently Asked Questions

Is Kamboh Associates available for clients outside Lahore?

Yes. The firm serves clients from all major cities in Pakistan — Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Multan, Faisalabad, and Sialkot — as well as overseas Pakistanis from UAE, UK, Canada, Saudi Arabia, and Australia. All services are handled digitally via WhatsApp and email. No physical visit is required.

How long does NTN registration take with Kamboh Associates?

NTN registration is completed on the same day in most cases. After you share your CNIC (and business registration documents for business NTN), the team applies on IRIS and you receive your NTN number within hours. Active taxpayer list (ATL) appearance follows within 24–48 hours after the first return is filed.

What documents do I need to file my income tax return?

For salaried employees: salary slip or Form 12 from employer, CNIC, and bank statements. For freelancers: bank statements showing remittance, Payoneer/Wise annual report, and CNIC. For business owners: bank statements, purchase/sale records, and any WHT certificates from clients. The team guides you on specific documents after an initial WhatsApp consultation.

Does Kamboh Associates handle FBR notices?

Yes. The firm handles all types of FBR notices including Section 111 (unexplained income), Section 122 (amendment/best judgment assessments), Section 176 (information requests), Section 114 (return filing notices), and audit notices under Section 177. Response strategy and documentation are prepared, and where required, the team files formal replies through IRIS or via registered post.

What is the fee for income tax return filing?

Fees vary by return complexity. Simple salaried returns start from Rs. 2,500–Rs. 5,000. Freelancer returns with foreign income declarations are priced higher due to the additional complexity of remittance reconciliation and 0.25% rate election. Business returns are priced based on turnover and number of transactions. WhatsApp for an exact quote after describing your situation.

Can Kamboh Associates file my return after the September 30 deadline?

Yes, late returns can be filed with a penalty under Section 182 (Rs. 1,000 per day for individuals, capped at Rs. 50,000 for salaried employees). The firm also helps assess whether a deadline extension has been granted by FBR for TY2026 and whether a penalty waiver application under Section 182(2) is appropriate in your case.
