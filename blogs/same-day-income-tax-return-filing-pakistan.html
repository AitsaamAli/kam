Complete Document Checklist by Taxpayer Type

Send these documents on WhatsApp to get started. Clear photos on your phone camera are fine — you do not need to scan anything.

Salaried Employee Checklist

  • CNIC (front and back)
  • Annual salary certificate from employer (or Form 16 / tax deduction certificate) — shows gross salary and WHT deducted
  • Bank account closing balance as of June 30
  • Bank profit / WHT certificate from your bank(s) — available from bank app or branch
  • Property list (any plots, houses, flats you own — with purchase price)
  • Vehicle registration certificates
  • Any outstanding loans (bank or personal)

Freelancer / Overseas Income Checklist

  • CNIC (front and back)
  • Bank statement showing all foreign remittances received (Wise, Payoneer, bank account credits)
  • Payoneer / Upwork / Fiverr annual earnings report (if available)
  • Bank profit certificate if you have savings
  • Property and vehicle information
  • Any expenses related to freelance work (internet, equipment, software) — helps reduce taxable income

Landlord / Rental Income Checklist

  • CNIC
  • Rental agreement(s) showing monthly rent
  • Bank statement showing rent received (ideally)
  • Property deed / allotment letter with purchase price
  • Property tax receipts paid during the year
  • Any mortgage / loan against the property
  • If a company paid rent: Form 155 or company deduction certificate

Business Owner (Sole Proprietor) Checklist

  • CNIC
  • Business revenue for the year (July 2025 – June 2026)
  • Major expense categories (rent, salaries, purchases, utilities)
  • Any WHT certificates received from clients (if they deducted at source)
  • Bank statements showing business income and expenses
  • NTN of the business (if separate from personal NTN)

Most common document missing: Bank profit certificate. Many filers forget this and miss a WHT refund worth tens of thousands of rupees. Ask your bank for the “Annual Tax Deduction Certificate” or check your banking app under “Documents” or “Statements.”

What the Acknowledgment Receipt Is Used For

The FBR acknowledgment receipt (ITR-V) is more useful than many people realise. You will need it for:

  • Visa applications: UK, Schengen, Canada, USA, and most embassies require 3 years of FBR acknowledgments as proof of income and tax compliance
  • Bank account operations: Some banks require acknowledgment before releasing large amounts or granting loans
  • Property registration: Sub-registrars may request proof of filer status — the receipt is your evidence
  • Government tenders: Contractors and suppliers must show tax filings for government contract eligibility
  • Loan applications: Mortgage and personal loan applications typically require 2–3 years of filed returns
  • Company directorship: SECP requires directors to be tax filers — acknowledgment proves filing

Keep all acknowledgment receipts permanently — not just for the current year. Embassy interviewers and loan officers typically ask for the last 2–3 years.

Why File Before September 30 — Not at the Last Minute

September 30 is the standard FBR deadline. Here is why filing early is better than waiting:

  • ATL status kicks in within 48–72 hours of filing — if you file September 27, your WHT savings start September 29. Every month you delayed all year, you paid non-filer rates
  • Last-minute filing risks IRIS server overload — FBR’s system often gets congested in September
  • If your return shows tax due, you must pay before the deadline — last-minute payment processing can fail
  • You need time to gather documents — getting a salary certificate from HR or a profit certificate from a bank can take a few days

What happens if you miss September 30? A late filing penalty of Rs. 1,000 per month applies under Section 182. If there was also tax due that you did not pay, default surcharge at 12% per annum on the unpaid amount adds up quickly. File immediately after missing the deadline — the penalty stops accruing once you file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is same-day filing possible even if I’ve never filed before?
Yes. If you have a CNIC but no NTN, we first register you on FBR’s IRIS (NTN registration is included in the filing package). Then we file your return the same day. First-time filers usually need 30–60 extra minutes for NTN registration, but same-day completion is standard. NTN + first return together costs Rs. 5,500.
What if I have income from multiple sources — salary, rent, and bank profit?
All income sources are declared in a single return on IRIS Form 114(I). Multiple income sources add 1–2 hours to preparation time but same-day filing is still achievable if all documents are received before 2 PM. Bring salary certificate, all bank profit certificates, and rental income documentation.
Can you file returns for previous years the same day?
Yes. Late returns for previous years are filed the same way on IRIS. Each prior year is a separate filing. We can process all outstanding years in one session — a common approach for clients clearing a 3–5 year backlog. Each prior year adds 1–2 hours to the session.
Is my personal and financial information safe if I share documents on WhatsApp?
Kamboh Associates is a registered tax consulting firm. Client documents are used solely for FBR filing purposes and are not shared with any third party. We follow the same confidentiality standards as major accounting firms. WhatsApp business-to-client communication is end-to-end encrypted.
How quickly will I appear on the FBR Active Taxpayer List after filing?
FBR updates the Active Taxpayer List within 48–72 hours of return submission. You can verify your status at fbr.gov.pk under Online Verification → Active Taxpayer Status. Once you appear on the ATL, banks immediately apply the lower 15% WHT rate on profit (instead of 30%) and property WHT drops from 6% to 3%.
What documents will I receive after filing?
You receive: (1) FBR Acknowledgment Receipt (ITR-V) — a PDF confirming your return was submitted, with a unique acknowledgment number. (2) Tax computation summary showing your income, deductions, WHT credits, and final liability or refund. We deliver both on WhatsApp as PDFs. Keep these permanently for embassy and loan applications.

Filer Status — What Changes Immediately After Filing

The moment your return is accepted and your name appears on the Active Taxpayer List (ATL), the following WHT rates change automatically — banks and registrars verify your ATL status in real time:

  • Bank profit WHT: Drops from 30% (final, non-refundable) to 15% (adjustable). On Rs. 5 lakh profit, this saves Rs. 75,000 per year.
  • Property sale WHT (236C): Drops from 6% to 3%. On a Rs. 50 lakh sale, this saves Rs. 1,50,000.
  • Property purchase WHT (236K): Drops from 6% to 3%. On a Rs. 50 lakh purchase, this saves Rs. 1,50,000.
  • Vehicle registration: Non-filer advance tax on new vehicles is higher. Filer rates apply once on ATL.
  • Cash withdrawal tax (Section 231A): Reduced for filers on large withdrawals.
  • National Savings profit: WHT on DSC, SSC, RIC drops from 30% to 15% for filers.

None of these changes require any application or separate process. Appearing on the ATL is sufficient — institutions check the ATL automatically when processing your transactions.
