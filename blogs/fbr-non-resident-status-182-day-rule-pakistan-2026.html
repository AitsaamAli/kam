FBR Non-Resident Status — 182 Din Ka Qanoon Pakistan 2026 | Overseas Tax Guide

July 15, 2026Aitsaam Ali8 min read2026 Verified

Bahut se Pakistanis jo abroad ja rahe hain ya ja chuke hain — unhe pata nahi ke Pakistan mein non-resident kab ban jate hain aur is ka kya faida milta hai. 182 din ka qanoon Pakistan tax law ka ek important pillar hai. Ise samjhna overseas Pakistanis ke liye taxes save karne aur compliance maintain karne ke liye zaruri hai.

182 Din Ka Qanoon — Kya Hai?

Pakistan Income Tax Ordinance 2001, Section 82 ke mutabiq:

  • Resident: Agar koi shaks Pakistan mein ek tax year mein 183 din ya zyada rehta hai — resident consider hoga
  • Non-Resident: Agar 182 din ya kam Pakistan mein rehta hai — non-resident consider hoga
  • Tax Year: Pakistan ka tax year July 1 se June 30 tak — is mein din count hote hain
  • Arrival/Departure: Aane ka din aur jane ka din — dono count hote hain

Practical example: Agar aap July 1, 2025 ko abroad gaye aur December 31, 2025 ko wapas aaye — aap December 31 tak 184 din Pakistan se bahar the. Lekin agar aap ne July 1 se pehle Pakistan mein kuch din bitaye — total count check karna hoga. Tax year July 1 – June 30 hai isliye part year bhi count karta hai.

Overseas Income — Pakistan Mein Taxable Nahi

Non-resident ke liye sabse bada faida ye hai — abroad se kamayi income Pakistan mein taxable nahi:

  • UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, Italy, Spain mein salary — Pakistan mein zero tax
  • Abroad freelance/business income — Pakistan mein exempt
  • Abroad property rental — Pakistan mein exempt
  • Abroad investments dividends — Pakistan mein exempt

Sirf Pakistan mein koi property rental hai ya Pakistan bank mein savings interest aa raha hai — wohi taxable hai, aur wo bhi preferential rates par.

Non-Resident Ko Return File Karna Chahiye?

Mandatory ya not — dono cases:

  • Mandatory hai agar: Pakistan mein koi taxable income hai — property rental, bank interest, etc.
  • Mandatory nahi agar: Koi Pakistan-source income nahi
  • Lekin file karo agar: Pakistan mein property hai ya future mein Pakistan transactions expected hain — ATL status ke liye. Property buy/sell par non-filer ko double WHT (1.25% vs 2.5% buyer, 2.75% vs 5.5% seller)

ATL trap: Bahut se overseas Pakistanis sochte hain ke non-resident hain isliye return file nahi karna. Lekin Pakistan mein property dealing ya bank transactions mein non-filer pe zyada withholding tax lagta hai. ATL mein rehne ke liye return file karte raho — sirf Rs 200 fee aur basic return kafi hai agar overseas income nahi hai.

Remittances — Pakistan Mein Tax?

Abroad se Pakistan ko bhejne wali remittances — overseas Pakistanis ki family ko ghar chalaane ke liye:

  • Foreign currency remittances: Tax-exempt in Pakistan — FBR ne remittances ko encourage kiya hua hai
  • ROZE (Remittance-originated) accounts: Special benefits for routed-through-bank remittances
  • Cash remittances: Tax-exempt but hawala/hundi illegal — always use banking channels

Non-Resident Return Kaise File Karein

  • FBR IRIS portal — iris.fbr.gov.pk — NTN aur password se login
  • Return form: "Tax Return for Non-Residents" select karo
  • Pakistan source income declare karo — overseas income fields blank ya "N/A"
  • Wealth statement — Pakistan aur overseas assets dono declare karne hote hain
  • Due date: September 30 (TY 2025 deadline September 30, 2025 tha)

Dual filer: Agar aap abroad reh rahe hain lekin tax year mein kuch waqt Pakistan mein bhi tha — count carefully karo. Agar 183 din ya zyada Pakistan mein hain — resident return. Agar 182 ya kam — non-resident return. Ek saath dono countries mein tax resident hona double taxation risk create karta hai — DTA ke baghair.

FAQs

Pakistan aur abroad dono mein tax dena padega?
Agar Pakistan-Pakistan DTA (Double Taxation Agreement) hai to dohar tax se bacha ja sakta hai — UK, Australia, Germany ke saath Pakistan ka DTA hai. Spain ke saath nahi hai (2026). DTA ke zariye ek country mein pay ki tax doosri country mein credit milti hai — net mein double tax nahi lagta.
Pakistan wapas aa gaya kuch mahine ke liye — kya non-resident status khatam hogi?
Tax year (July-June) mein total 183+ din Pakistan mein hain tou us saal ke liye resident. Baaki saal ke liye non-resident. Status har saal alag ho sakti hai — isliye har saal days carefully count karo. PartYear resident bhi hona possible hai agar visit lambi ho jaaye.
Abroad earn kar raha hoon lekin Pakistan mein Roshan Digital Account hai — kya hoga?
Roshan Digital Account (RDA) overseas Pakistanis ke liye — foreign currency mein Pakistan mein invest/save karna. RDA interest/profit par concessional tax rates applicable hain. Non-resident ke liye Pakistan-source income consider hoga — return mein declare karna hoga. Lekin overseas earnings jo RDA mein transfer ki hain — wo tax-free hain Pakistan mein.
Non-resident hone ke liye koi FBR se declaration karna hoga?
FBR se koi formal non-resident declaration zaruri nahi — ye status automatically tax year ke din count se determine hoti hai. Return file karte waqt non-resident form use karo — ye declare kar deta hai. Agar koi specifically non-resident certificate maange (embassy/bank ke liye) tou Kamboh Associates ye prepare karta hai.
