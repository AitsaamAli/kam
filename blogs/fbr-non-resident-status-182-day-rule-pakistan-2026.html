FBR Non-Resident Status — 182 Din Ka Qanoon Pakistan 2026 | Overseas Tax Guide

July 15, 2026Aitsaam Ali8 min read2026 Verified

Bahut se overseas Pakistanis samajhte hain ke Pakistan se 182 din bahar rehna hi non-resident banne ke liye kaafi hai. Qanoon is se zyada sakht hai. Section 82 ki clause (d), jo Finance Act 2022 ne shamil ki, Pakistani citizen ke liye do aur shartein lagati hai: woh kisi doosre mulk mein 182 din se zyada maujood ho aur kisi doosre mulk ka resident taxpayer ho. Ye guide ITO 2001 (amended upto 30 June 2026) se batati hai ke resident aur non-resident kaun hai, kis aamdani par Pakistan mein tax lagta hai, remittances ka qanoon kya hai, aur return kab aur kaise file hoti hai.

Short answer

Section 82 ke mutabiq tax year (1 July – 30 June) mein Pakistan mein 183 din ya zyada maujood shakhs resident hai. Pakistani citizen us waqt bhi resident hai agar woh kisi doosre mulk mein 182 din se zyada maujood na raha ho, ya kisi doosre mulk ka resident taxpayer na ho (section 82(d)). Non-resident ki sirf Pakistan-source aamdani par tax lagta hai (section 11(6)).

182 Din Ka Qanoon — Kya Hai?

Section 82 ke mutabiq koi individual kisi tax year ke liye resident hai agar in mein se koi ek baat sach ho:

  • (a) 183 din: tax year mein Pakistan mein ek ya kai dafa mila kar 183 din ya zyada maujood raha.
  • (c) Sarkari mulazim: Federal ya Provincial Government ka employee ya official jo us tax year mein abroad posted ho.
  • (d) Pakistani citizen: jo kisi doosre mulk mein tax year ke dauran 182 din se zyada maujood na raha ho, ya jo kisi doosre mulk ka resident taxpayer na ho.

Tax year: 1 July se 30 June tak. Misaal ke tor par tax year 2026 ka matlab hai 1 July 2025 se 30 June 2026.

Pehle (Finance Act 2019 se 2021 tak) 120 din aur pichle 4 saal mein 365 din wala ek aur rule bhi tha (clause (ab)). Finance Act 2021 ne woh khatam kar diya.

Misaal: ek Pakistani citizen 1 July se 15 December 2025 tak Pakistan mein raha (168 din), phir baqi tax year (197 din) Dubai mein raha aur wahan ka resident taxpayer hai. Pakistan mein 183 din pure nahi huay. Woh doosre mulk mein 182 din se zyada raha aur wahan ka resident taxpayer bhi hai, is liye clause (d) bhi lagu nahi hoti. Yani tax year 2026 ke liye woh non-resident hai. Agar Dubai mein sirf 150 din guzarta, ya doosre mulk ki tax residency na hoti, to clause (d) ke tehat resident hota.

Overseas Income — Pakistan Mein Taxable Nahi

Section 11(6) ke mutabiq non-resident ki aamdani sirf Pakistan-source amounts se compute hoti hai. Is liye jo shakhs section 82 ke tehat non-resident hai, us ki ye aamdani Pakistan mein taxable nahi:

  • Abroad naukri ki salary, jab kaam abroad kiya ho
  • Abroad freelance ya business ki aamdani
  • Abroad property ka rent
  • Abroad investments ka dividend ya profit

Pakistan mein rent, Pakistani bank ka profit, Pakistan mein property ki farokht aur Pakistani companies ka dividend Pakistan-source hain, aur non-resident par bhi taxable hain. Lekin agar aap section 82 ke tehat resident hain, to aap ki worldwide aamdani Pakistan mein taxable hai, chahe aap saal ka bara hissa bahar rahe hon.

Non-Resident Ko Return File Karna Chahiye?

  • Pakistan-source taxable aamdani ho: rent, bank profit ya property sale jaisi aamdani par return ki zaroorat section 114 ki shartein dekh kar tay hoti hai.
  • ATL ka faida: ATL se bahar ho to Pakistan mein withholding rates bohat zyada hain. Bank profit 40% (20% ki jagah). Property seller 11.5% (2.75% ki jagah). Property buyer 10.5% se 18.5% (1.25% ki jagah). Gaari ka 231B teen guna.
  • Property ka khaas qanoon: non-resident individual (POC, NICOP ya CNIC) ne property FCVA ya NRVA account se khareedi ho, to 236K final discharge hai. Bechne par 236C capital gains ki jagah final tax hai.

ATL trap: deadline ke baad return file karne par ATL mein aane ke liye section 182A ka surcharge dena hota hai (individual Rs 25,000). Is liye agar Pakistan mein koi deal ya bank profit hai, to return waqt par file karein.

Remittances — Pakistan Mein Tax?

Foreign remittance ke baare mein ITO mein ye provision hai:

  • Section 111(4): normal banking channel se aayi foreign exchange, jo scheduled bank ne rupees mein encash ki ho, section 111 (unexplained income) ke daire se bahar hai. Ye riayat ek tax year mein Rs 5 million tak hai, aur is ke liye bank ka certificate dikhana hota hai.
  • Exchange companies: section 111(4) ki Explanation (Finance Act 2024) ke mutabiq money service bureaus, exchange companies aur money transfer operators ke zariye aayi remittance bhi normal banking channel mani jati hai.
  • Hundi/hawala: ye normal banking channel nahi. Is se aaya paisa section 111(4) ki riayat mein nahi aata.

Non-Resident Return Kaise File Karein

  1. IRIS login: iris.fbr.gov.pk par CNIC/NTN aur password se login karein. Registration nahi to pehle NTN banwayein.
  2. Residential status: return mein section 82 ke mutabiq resident ya non-resident status chunein. Doosre mulk ki tax residency aur travel record ka saboot apne paas rakhein.
  3. Pakistan-source aamdani: rent, bank profit, dividend aur property sale declare karein, aur kata hua tax (withholding) claim karein.
  4. Wealth statement: resident ke liye zaroori hai. Non-resident ke liye lazmi nahi, magar Commissioner notice se maang sakta hai.
  5. Deadline: individuals ki return section 118 ke tehat 30 September tak hoti hai. Tax year 2026 ke liye FBR ne ye deadline 31 October 2026 tak barhai thi.

Double taxation: resident shakhs foreign aamdani par abroad diye gaye tax ka credit section 103 ke tehat le sakta hai. Mulkon ke darmiyan double taxation agreements section 107 ke tehat hote hain. Kis mulk ke saath DTA hai, is ki list FBR ki website par dekhein.

FAQs

Pakistan aur abroad dono mein tax dena padega?
Agar aap section 82 ke tehat non-resident hain, to Pakistan sirf Pakistan-source aamdani par tax leta hai. Agar resident hain aur abroad bhi tax diya, to section 103 ke tehat foreign tax credit ka raasta hai. Double taxation agreement section 107 ke tehat hota hai, aur list FBR ki website par hai.
Pakistan kuch mahine ke liye wapas aaya hoon — kya status badal jayega?
Status har tax year ke liye alag tay hota hai. Agar us tax year mein Pakistan mein 183 din ya zyada ho gaye, ya doosre mulk mein 182 din se zyada nahi rahe, ya kisi doosre mulk ke resident taxpayer nahi, to us saal ke liye resident honge. Qanoon mein "part-year resident" ka koi tasawwur nahi.
Roshan Digital Account se property khareedi hai — kya farq padta hai?
Agar non-resident individual (POC, NICOP ya CNIC) ne property FCVA ya NRVA account ke zariye khareedi, to 236K final discharge hai. Bechne par 236C, section 37 ke capital gains ki jagah final tax hai. Account ke profit par Pakistan ka tax qanoon (Division IIIA) lagu hota hai.
Non-resident hone ke liye FBR se koi declaration lena hoga?
Section 82 mein koi alag declaration ya registration nahi. Status har tax year ke din aur shartein dekh kar tay hota hai, aur return mein declare kiya jata hai. Agar FBR sawal kare, to passport ki entries aur doosre mulk ki tax residency ka saboot kaam aata hai.
