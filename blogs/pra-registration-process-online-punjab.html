PRA Registration Process Online in Punjab: Every Step on PRA IRIS, Explained (2026)

21 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 11 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 21 November 2026
PRA registration is free and fully online, but one wrong screen sends the application back. Here is each step in order.

Most people who get stuck on PRA registration are not stuck on the law. They are stuck on a screen: a utility they did not have to hand, a business activity they could not find, or an address that FBR holds differently. This guide walks the PRA IRIS registration module in the order the official PRAL user manual describes it, and tells you what to prepare before each screen.

Short answer

You register with the Punjab Revenue Authority on its IRIS portal by choosing New Registration, picking the basis (voluntary or on a notice), confirming one-time codes sent to your mobile and email, and completing the Basic Information, Address, Business, Utilities and Verification screens. Basic details are pulled from your FBR record and cannot be edited there, so an FBR NTN comes first. Rule 3 of the Registration Rules 2012 gives you a provisional certificate at once and a final certificate (Form PST-03) after verification, preferably within thirty days. PRA charges no fee (rule 15).

Before You Start: What PRA IRIS Will Ask For

Short answer: Have an active FBR NTN, the mobile number and email you control, your business address, an electricity reference number and your bank account details ready.

The PRA registration module runs on IRIS, the same system design FBR uses, and it is built on top of your FBR record. The official PRAL user manual (January 2024) says the information on the Basic Information screen is "pre-filled from the records fetched from FBR" and "cannot be edited". That one line decides your order of work: fix your FBR profile first, then register with PRA.

Form PST-01, the prescribed registration form appended to the 2012 Rules, shows what PRA wants to know about you. Read together with the IRIS screens, you need:

  • An active NTN with FBR, and the CNIC of the individual (or the registration or incorporation number for a company or registered AOP)
  • A mobile number and an email address you can receive codes on today
  • Your business or mailing address, with province, district and city or tehsil
  • The services you provide, with one of them as your principal service
  • Each business place or branch: whether it is owned or rented (and the owner's CNIC or NTN if rented), its electricity reference number, whether a gas connection is installed, its phone and its start date
  • Each bank account used in the business: account number, title, bank, city and opening date

Our documents checklist turns these fields into a list you can send to a client or collect yourself.

The Screens, In Order

Short answer: New Registration, verification codes, Basic Information, Address, Business, Utilities, a further details screen, then Verification and confirm.

The manual describes the voluntary route screen by screen. Here is what each one does and what to watch for.

1. New Registration

On the PRA portal, click New Registration. Fill the starred fields. From the Registration Basis drop-down you choose one of two options: voluntary, or notice/compulsory. If PRA has already served you a notice, choose the notice option; the system then asks for the Notice Bar Code and verifies it. Then choose the Person type (individual, company or AOP), enter the captcha and submit.

2. Verification codes

A verification code goes to the mobile number and another to the email address you entered. Enter both, with the captcha, and click Next. If you are registering for someone else, they must be on the phone with you at this point.

3. Basic Information

This screen arrives filled from FBR and is locked. Two rules from the manual matter here: the "Withholding Agent" box is ticked automatically and cannot be changed for a company user, and the Business section becomes mandatory only if you tick Service Provider. Most applicants registering a service business tick it.

4. Address

Your address may already come from FBR's database; if not, add it with the "+" button, choose its type and save. At least one address must be selected (ticked) before you can move on.

5. Business

Add each business and its activities. The manual sets two hard rules: at least one activity must be marked Principal, and one business must be marked Primary Business. Pick addresses already saved or add new ones from this screen.

6. Utilities

Add the utility connections for the business place (Form PST-01 asks for the electricity reference number and whether gas is installed). Each entry is added with "+" and shows on a small dashboard.

7. Further details and Verification

One more screen asks for a link-type detail, added the same way. The last screen shows an undertaking; clicking Confirm means you declare the information authentic. The manual then says a password is sent to the registered email and mobile, and the registration process is complete on your side.

What Happens After You Submit

Short answer: Rule 3 gives a provisional certificate immediately; PRA verifies and issues Form PST-03, preferably within thirty days.

Rule 3 of the Registration and De-registration Rules 2012 sets the legal timetable:

StageWhat the rule saysRule
On applyingThe applicant is "immediately" issued a provisional certificate of registration3(1)
VerificationPRA may verify the particulars and the genuineness of documents3(2)
Final certificateIssued in Form PST-03 with a registration number, "preferably within thirty days"3(2)
RejectionReasons given within thirty days of a complete application3(3)

The e-PRA login page adds a practical note: an application is reviewed by a tax officer and you are told the status "within 48 working hours". Read both together: you hear back quickly, and the final certificate follows verification. See our guide to the PST-03 certificate for what it shows and how to check it.

Two duties start with registration. Rule 2(5) requires you to show your registration number on invoices, bills, vouchers and letters to PRA. And section 25(4) lets PRA publish a list of registered persons on its website; under section 25(5) a customer may reasonably rely on that list, which is why being on it matters to clients who check.

One Registration or Several?

Rule 2 answers the questions businesses ask most:

  • Several services: one application can list all of them, and the certificate shows all of them (rule 2(3)).
  • Several premises: if billing or accounting is centralised in Punjab, you may take one registration (rule 2(2)). Each branch still goes on the form.
  • Several provinces: a business providing services in Punjab and another province needs a separate Punjab registration for services rendered in Punjab (rule 2(4)). Our guide on which provincial authority applies covers the other side.

Mistakes That Hold Applications Up

Short answer: Wrong principal activity, an address that does not match FBR, and a phone or email you cannot receive codes on cause most delays.

  1. FBR details out of date. PRA pulls them and you cannot edit them there. Update your FBR profile first.
  2. No principal activity, or the wrong one. The system will not move on without one, and the wrong choice follows you.
  3. Codes going to an old number. The mobile and email must be live for both the codes and the password.
  4. Choosing voluntary when a notice exists. If PRA has served a notice, use the notice route and its bar code; see compulsory registration and e-Enrolment.
  5. Missing branch or bank details. Form PST-01 asks for every branch and every bank account. Leaving one out means a change application later under rule 11.

If you already hold FBR sales tax registration, read the P+NTN rule first: rule 10 may mean you are already registered with PRA.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I register with PRA without an NTN?
PRA IRIS fills your Basic Information from FBR's records and that screen cannot be edited, so in practice you need an active FBR NTN before you start.
How long does PRA registration take?
Rule 3 gives a provisional certificate immediately on applying and the final PST-03 certificate after verification, preferably within thirty days. The e-PRA page says status is notified within 48 working hours of review.
Is there a government fee for PRA registration?
No. Rule 15 of the Registration and De-registration Rules 2012 says registration and de-registration are free of any fee or service charge.
Can one registration cover all my branches?
Yes, if billing or accounting is centralised in Punjab (rule 2(2)). All branches are still listed in the application.
What if I also work in Sindh or KP?
Rule 2(4) requires a separate registration for Punjab for services rendered in Punjab, alongside the other province's registration.
Who receives the password?
The PRAL manual says the password is sent to the registered email address and mobile number once you confirm the undertaking.
Do I have to visit a PRA office to register?
No. Rule 3(1) allows the application electronically or manually, and the PRAL manual describes the full process online on PRA IRIS.
What does 'Registration Basis' mean on PRA IRIS?
It is the drop-down where you choose voluntary registration or the notice/compulsory route; the notice route asks for the Notice Bar Code.
