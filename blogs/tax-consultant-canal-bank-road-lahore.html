Canal Bank Road, Lahore — Swapping One Property for Another

Updated October 2026
Kamboh Associates
3 min read
Canal Bank Road

Property swaps are more common in Lahore than people think: a house exchanged for two plots, a commercial unit swapped for a residence with cash on top, family members trading properties to suit their needs. Because no full cash price changes hands, owners often assume nothing needs to be declared. In tax terms, a swap is usually two transactions at once.

A swap is a sale and a purchase

When you exchange your property for another, you are generally treated as:

  1. selling your property for a consideration equal to the value of what you receive (plus or minus any cash); and
  2. buying the other property at that value.
Your sideTreatment
Property you giveDisposal — gain = value received − your cost
Property you receiveAcquisition — cost = value given (+ cash paid)
Cash you payPart of the cost of the new property
Cash you receivePart of the consideration for the old one

Advance tax at transfer

Each transfer in a swap is a separate registration or society transfer, with advance tax under 236C (as seller) and 236K (as buyer) collected on each side, on the higher of FBR valuation and the declared value. Both parties should be on the Active Taxpayer List to pay the lower rates.

Valuing the swap

Since there's no single cash price, agree and document a value for each property in the exchange agreement. Valuations far below market can be questioned, particularly since FBR valuation tables set minimum values for advance tax.

Family swaps

Swaps between relatives — a brother takes the shop, a sister takes the flat — are often informal. Partition among co-owners is different from a swap, and gifts are different again. Get the documents right first; the tax follows them.

Wealth statement

In the year of the swap:

  • the old property comes off at cost;
  • the new property goes in at its acquisition value;
  • cash paid or received moves through your bank balance;
  • any gain is reported in the return.

Write a simple exchange agreement stating both properties, the agreed values and any cash adjustment, signed by both parties. Without it, nobody can later explain how either property was acquired.

Canal Bank Road — questions we get asked

Is a property swap taxable?
A swap is usually treated as a sale of your property and a purchase of the other, with any gain on your old property reported.
Is advance tax collected on a swap?
Yes. Each transfer is separately registered, with 236C and 236K collected on each side.
How do I value properties in a swap?
Agree and document values in an exchange agreement. Values far below market can be questioned.
My sister and I exchanged inherited properties. Is that a swap?
It may be a partition among co-owners rather than a swap. The documents decide; get advice first.
