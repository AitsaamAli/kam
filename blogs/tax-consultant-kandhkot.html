Kandhkot

Kandhkot, in Kashmore district of upper Sindh, sits near one of Pakistan's older gas fields and amid rice and wheat farms watered from the Guddu barrage. Gas field contractors, landowners, traders and government employees are its main taxpayers.

The Gas Field and Its Contractors

The Kandhkot gas field and its processing facilities engage local contractors for transport, security, maintenance and labour. Every bill paid to these contractors carries income tax withholding, at a lower rate for active filers. Contractors should collect the deduction certificates each month, because they are credits only if claimed in the return.

Landowners and Rice

Canal-irrigated land around Kandhkot grows rice and wheat. Crop income from family land is agricultural, outside federal income tax; larger holdings pay the Sindh agricultural income tax. The federal wealth statement still lists the land.

Traders and Rice Mills

Rice mills and grain traders buy at harvest and sell to buyers in Sukkur, Karachi and Balochistan. Their returns rest on purchase and sale records and year-end stock.

Government Employees

Teachers, police and officials have salary tax withheld.

Services Under SRB

Restaurants, hotels and many other services fall under the Sindh Revenue Authority.

Security and Documentation

Insecurity in parts of the district means many businesses rely on cash. Regular bank deposits and simple daily records protect them when FBR or a bank asks questions.

Doctors and Clinics

Private clinics and pharmacies serving the district keep fee registers and distributor invoices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Kandhkot: which FBR office handles income tax?

Kandhkot filers: FBR lists no RTO in Kashmore; Kandhkot NTNs show theirs in IRIS.

Kandhkot: who collects sales tax on services?

For Kandhkot, the provincial authority is the Sindh Revenue Board (SRB); FBR handles sales tax on goods.

Do gas field contractors need to file?

Yes, to claim withholding and get lower rates.

Is rice from my land taxed by FBR?

No, agricultural income is outside federal tax.

Who taxes restaurants in Kandhkot?

The Sindh Revenue Authority.
