🇮🇹 Italy Jane Se Pehle FBR Tax Return, NTN Aur Wealth Statement — Pakistan 2026 Guide

August 28, 2026Aitsaam Ali11 min read2026 Verified

Italy mein already 120,000+ Pakistanis rehte hain, aur 2026 mein ek naya, formal work-visa quota (10,500 visas, 2026-2028 ke doran) is number ko aur barhane wala hai. Lekin Italy jaane wale Pakistanis do bilkul alag systems se guzarte hain — Schengen visa (tourist, family, student) jahan financial proof central hai, aur naya Decreto Flussi work-visa quota jahan koi proof-of-funds requirement hi nahi. Ye guide dono ko clearly separate karti hai, taake aap sahi documents par waqt aur paisa lagayein.

TL;DR:

  • Schengen visa (tourist, family reunion, student, kuch long-stay categories) mein Italian consulate financial sufficiency check karta hai — Pakistan mein iska sabse credible proof FBR NTN, tax returns aur wealth statement hai.
  • Naya 2026-2028 Decreto Flussi work-visa quota (Pakistan ke liye 10,500 slots, ~3,500/saal) employer-sponsored hai — is route mein koi blocked account ya sponsor tax-return requirement NAHI hai, process seedha employer ke nulla osta se shuru hota hai.
  • In dono ko mix mat karo — galat route ke liye galat documents taiyar karna time waste karta hai.
  • Chahe visa type kuch bhi ho, Italy jaane ke baad Pakistan mein NTN aur filer status maintain karna — remittance, property, aur future wapsi ke liye — dono routes mein equally zaruri hai.

Schengen Visa Aur Decreto Flussi — Do Bilkul Alag Systems

Italy jaane ke liye Pakistanis do fundamentally different rastay use karte hain, aur inki financial documentation requirement bhi utni hi alag hai:

  • Schengen visa (tourist, family reunion, student, kuch long-stay): applicant khud apply karta hai, aur Italian consulate (Islamabad ya Karachi) financial sufficiency verify karta hai — is mein FBR documents strongly relevant hain
  • Decreto Flussi work-visa quota: ek Italian employer applicant ko sponsor karta hai (nulla osta ke zariye), aur employer financial responsibility uthata hai — is route mein koi blocked account, maintenance funds, ya sponsor tax-return requirement nahi hoti

Important correction: agar aap Decreto Flussi quota ke zariye ja rahe hain (2026 ka 10,500-visa Pakistan allocation), Italy aap se FBR documents visa ke liye nahi maangta — employer ka nulla osta hi central requirement hai. Ye Schengen visa se bilkul alag hai, jahan financial proof central hoti hai. Agla section dono ko detail mein cover karta hai.

Yeh Farq Samajhna Kyun Zaruri Hai

Bohat se Pakistani families ye galti karti hain ke Italy ke liye "ek hi" checklist follow karte hain — chahe bhai job offer ke zariye Decreto Flussi se ja raha ho ya beti student visa par. Is confusion ki wajah se do tarah ka nuqsan hota hai: ya toh log apply karne se pehle mahino unnecessary FBR paperwork mein waqt lagate hain jab employer ka nulla osta hi kaafi tha, ya phir Schengen route par jaate waqt financial documentation ko halka le lete hain aur application weak reh jati hai. Sabse pehla qadam hamesha yehi hona chahiye — pehle route confirm karo, phir uske mutabiq documents taiyar karo.

Schengen Visa — Yahan FBR Documents Kyun Central Hain

Tourist, family reunion, student, aur kuch long-stay Schengen categories mein Italian consulate applicant ki financial sufficiency dekhta hai — ke aap Italy mein reh kar khud ka kharcha uthaa sakte hain. Pakistan mein formal income prove karne ka sabse credible zariya FBR ke saath registered hona aur return file karna hai:

  • Non-filer applicants: unke paas verifiable income ka koi government-endorsed record nahi hota — consulate ke liye red flag
  • Family reunion cases: applicant ki apni return + Italy mein sponsor ki Italy tax return (Modello Redditi/730) dono chahiye
  • Student visa: parents/sponsor ki returns financial guarantee ko support karti hain

Non-filer hona automatically rejection ka reason nahi banta, lekin ye application ko significantly kamzor karta hai — jab bhi financial proof ka sawal aata hai, FBR-backed documentation sabse strong evidence hoti hai.

2026 Decreto Flussi Quota — Yahan FBR Documents Kyun Central NAHI Hain

Italy ka 2026 kul work-visa quota 164,850 workers ka hai (Decree-Law 146/2025, baad mein Law 179/2025 mein convert hua), aur Pakistan un countries mein shamil hai jinke sath Italy ka migration cooperation agreement hai — is liye ek reserved allocation maujood hai. Pakistani government/media sources ke mutabiq ye allocation 10,500 visas hai, 2026-2028 ke doran (~3,500/saal) — is exact number ki Italian decree mein country-wise breakdown publicly available nahi, is liye ye figure Pakistani-side sources se hai, hedge ke sath.

Is route ka process employer se shuru hota hai — Italian employer applicant ke liye nulla osta (work authorization) apply karta hai, Questura aur National Labour Inspectorate verification karte hain, aur worker ko nulla osta milne ke baad 180 din milte hain visa apply karne ke liye. Poore process mein kahin bhi applicant ke Pakistan-side financial documents ka formal requirement nahi hai.

Common Mistakes

  • Galat route ke liye documents taiyar karna: Decreto Flussi applicants jo unnecessarily blocked-account-style financial proof arrange karte hain jab employer ka nulla osta hi kaafi hai
  • Schengen applicants ka non-filer hona: achi income hai lekin FBR mein declare nahi ki — financial proof kamzor reh jati hai
  • Agents ke overpromise par bharosa karna: koi bhi agent jo "guaranteed visa" promise kare, chahe Schengen ho ya Decreto Flussi quota — 2024 mein sirf 7.8% uptake rate (9,331 permits issued out of 119,890 available) khud ye dikhata hai ke quota milna guarantee nahi hai
  • Italy jaane ke baad Pakistan filing bhool jana: dono routes se jaane wale is galti mein equally shamil hote hain

Sabse zyada nuqsan-deh galti asal mein pehli hi hai — galat route ke liye documentation. Har saal Kamboh Associates ke paas aise clients aate hain jinhone Decreto Flussi quota ke liye apply kiya hota hai lekin unhone pehle se hi mahino tak Schengen-style blocked-account jaisi financial proof jama karne mein waqt zaya kiya hota hai, sirf ye samajh kar ke "Europe jane ke liye yehi standard process hai." Route confirm karna sabse pehla, aur sabse sasta, qadam hai.

Italy Jaane Ke Baad — Har Route Par Equally Zaruri

Chahe Schengen visa se jayein ya Decreto Flussi quota se, ek baar Italy pohanchne ke baad Pakistan-side obligations same hain:

  • Non-resident status: 182-din rule ke mutabiq — us tax year mein Pakistan mein 182 din se kam rahe toh non-resident, Italy income Pakistan mein taxable nahi
  • NTN active rakho: Pakistan mein property ya savings hon toh filer status maintain karna future transactions ke liye zaruri
  • Pakistan-source income: property rental 15% flat rate par taxable rehti hai, non-resident hone ke baad bhi
  • Italy mein: resident hone ke baad worldwide income Italy mein taxable, Italian IRPEF rates apply hoti hain

Poori detail — remittance, property, aur filer-vs-non-filer ka practical farq — humari alag guide mein hai: Italy Se Pakistan Remittance Aur Property Tax Guide.

Ek scenario jo especially Decreto Flussi seasonal workers ke saath hota hai — wo saal ka 6-9 mahina Italy mein guzarte hain aur baaqi Pakistan mein, is liye unki residency status har saal alag ho sakti hai depending on actual din-count. Ye group ke liye 182-din rule ka calculation especially important hai, kyunke ek saal resident aur agle saal non-resident classify ho sakte hain. Is specific pattern ki poori detail humari seasonal-work guide mein hai.

FAQs — Italy Visa FBR Documentation

Italy visa ke liye Pakistan mein tax return hamesha zaruri hai?
Nahi — ye depend karta hai route par. Schengen visa (tourist, family, student) mein financial proof central hai aur FBR returns strongest evidence hain. Naye 2026 Decreto Flussi work-visa quota mein employer sponsorship hoti hai aur FBR documents visa ke liye zaruri nahi.
Decreto Flussi quota se ja raha hun — kya mujhe FBR documents chahiye?
Visa ke liye nahi — employer ka nulla osta hi central requirement hai. Lekin Italy pohanchne ke baad Pakistan mein filer status maintain karna, remittance aur property ke liye phir bhi zaruri hai.
Schengen family visa ke liye kiski return chahiye?
Applicant ki apni Pakistan FBR return, plus Italy mein sponsor (family member) ki Italy tax return (730 ya Modello Redditi). Dono ka combination consulate financial stability assess karne ke liye use karta hai.
Italy 2026 quota mein Pakistan ko kitne visa mile hain?
Pakistani government/media sources ke mutabiq 10,500 visas, 2026-2028 ke doran (~3,500/saal). Ye exact figure Italian decree mein publish nahi hoti, is liye Pakistani-side sources par based hai.
NTN nahi hai — Schengen visa apply ho sakta hai?
Technically ho sakta hai, lekin financial proof incomplete rahegi. NTN 1-2 din mein ban sakta hai, phir returns file ho sakti hain — application se pehle complete karwana behtar hai.

NTN registration Rs. 2,000, pending return Rs. 3,500-5,000 per saal, wealth statement Rs. 2,500. First-time filer ke liye total typically Rs. 8,000-10,000.
