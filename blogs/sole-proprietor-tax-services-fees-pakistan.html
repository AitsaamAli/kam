Tax Services for Sole Proprietors in Pakistan: What Your Business Needs Each Year, and What It Costs

10 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 11 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 10 November 2026
Most small businesses need the same few tax services every year. Knowing which ones apply, and when, is what keeps penalties away.

Owners of small businesses often pay for tax help one emergency at a time: a notice here, a late return there. That is the most expensive way to do it. This guide lists what a sole proprietorship actually needs across a year, which of those you can do yourself, and where professional help pays for itself, with the fees we publish.

TL;DR

A sole proprietor in Pakistan typically needs: registration on IRIS with the business added (once), an annual income tax return with wealth statement (every year, due 30 September for individuals under Section 118), quarterly advance tax once latest assessed income reaches Rs 1 million (Section 147), withholding statements once you employ staff, pay rent of Rs 1.5 million a year or cross Rs 100 million turnover, and help with notices when they arrive. Our published fees are Rs 5,000 for sole proprietorship registration, Rs 5,000 for the annual return, Rs 2,000 for a wealth statement, Rs 3,000 a month for withholding statements, and from Rs 5,000 for a notice response.

What Missing a Step Costs

  • Late return: 0.1% of tax payable for each day of default, up to 50%, minimum Rs 40,000 (Section 182).
  • Rejoining the Active Taxpayers List after a late return: Rs 25,000 for an individual (Section 182A).
  • Missing wealth statement or reconciliation: 0.1% of taxable income per week or Rs 100,000, whichever is higher (Section 182).
  • Unpaid advance tax: default surcharge at 12% a year (Section 205(1A)).
  • Late withholding statement: Rs 2,500 a day, minimum Rs 10,000, or Rs 50,000 if filed within 90 days after the tax was paid (Section 182).
  • Records not kept: Rs 10,000 or 5% of the tax, whichever is higher (Section 182).

Against these, the annual cost of doing it right is small.
