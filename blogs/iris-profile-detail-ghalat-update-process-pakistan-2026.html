IRIS Profile Mein CNIC, Address Ya Business Detail Ghalat — Update Ka Process

September 2026 Kamboh Associates 10 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Tax Consultant | NTN & Compliance Specialist

IRIS profile ka woh hissa jo shayad sab se kam dhyan paata hai wahi profile detail hai — address, contact number, business activity ki category. Yeh cheezein filing ke waqt seedha nazar nahi aatin, is liye log inhein saalon tak update nahi karte. Lekin jab koi notice ghalat address par ja rahi ho, ya sahi business category na hone ki wajah se galat return form khul raha ho, tab yeh chhoti si cheez bara masla ban jati hai.

TL;DR

IRIS profile mein CNIC ki tafseel, address, contact number aur business activity — sab kuch waqt ke sath update rehna chahiye. Purana address hone se notices sahi jagah nahi pohanchtin. Business activity ghalat honi se galat return form aata hai. Update ka process seedha hai lekin kuch tabdeeliyan (jaise CNIC ki bunyadi tafseel) ke liye supporting documents chahiye hote hain. Overseas Pakistanis aur ek se zyada filer rakhne wale khandaano ke liye yeh ehtiyat aur bhi ahem hai, kyunke inconsistent record confusion barhata hai.

Profile Detail Kyun Ahem Hai

IRIS profile sirf ek "account settings" wala hissa nahi — yeh woh bunyad hai jis par bohat sari cheezein khadi hoti hain: kaun sa return form aapko dikhega, kis address par notices bheji jayengi, aur agar FBR ko rabta karna ho to kis number ya email par karega. Jab yeh maloomat purani ho jati hai, to yeh cheezein khamoshi se ghalat hoti rehti hain — aap ko pata bhi nahi chalta jab tak koi masla khara na ho.

Address Update Karna

Ghar badalna, kisi naye shehar mein shift hona, ya business ki jagah badalna — yeh sab IRIS profile mein update karna zaroori hai. Address sirf ek formality nahi, yeh legal notices ke liye official correspondence address bhi hota hai. Agar aap ne address update nahi kiya aur koi notice purane pate par bheji gayi, to qanooni tor par yeh maan liya jata hai ke notice sahi tarah dee di gayi — chahe aap ko woh kabhi mili hi na ho. Isi liye address ko current rakhna sirf sahulat nahi, ek zaroori ehtiyat hai.

Mobile Number Aur Email Update Karna

Mobile number badalna ek aam waqia hai — naya SIM, naya operator, ya sirf number kho jana. IRIS profile mein registered mobile number OTP aur important alerts ke liye istemal hota hai. Agar purana number band ho chuka hai aur aap ne update nahi kiya, to login ya important verification ke waqt masla aa sakta hai. Isi tarah email bhi current rakhein, kyunke bohat si important updates email par bhi jati hain.

Business Activity Ki Category Update Karna

Agar aap ne apna business badal liya hai — jaise pehle trading karte thay ab services de rahe hain, ya business bilkul band kar diya hai — to IRIS profile mein business activity ki category bhi update honi chahiye. Yeh category seedha asar dalti hai ke aapko kaun sa return form dikhega aur kis tarah ke fields fill karne parenge. Purani category rehne se galat form khulta hai, jo aage chal kar filing mein masail paida karta hai.

Misaal: agar aapne apni dukan band kar di hai aur ab sirf salaried hain, lekin profile mein abhi bhi business active dikha raha hai, to IRIS aapse business-related fields maangta rahega jo aap ke liye ab relevant nahi.

CNIC Ki Bunyadi Tafseel Mein Tabdeeli

Kabhi kabhi CNIC khud renew hota hai aur us mein kuch tafseel (jaise address ya naam ki spelling) tabdeel ho jati hai. Aisi soorat mein IRIS profile ko NADRA ke record se match karna zaroori hai. Yeh update aam tor par seedha nahi hota — is ke liye NADRA se verification ka process chalta hai, aur kabhi FBR ke concerned office se rabta bhi karna par sakta hai. Is qism ki tabdeeli ke liye naye CNIC ki copy aur zaroorat par ek darkhwast tayyar rakhein.

Purana Address Hone Ka Khatra

Yeh sab se practical aur sab se ziyada nuqsan-deh masla hai. Agar aapka address IRIS mein purana hai aur us par koi notice bheji jati hai, to qanooni tor par yeh maan liya jata hai ke aap ko notice mil chuki. Agar aap jawab nahi dete (kyunke aap ko pata hi nahi chala), to Commissioner ke paas mojood maloomat ki bunyad par ex-parte faisla ho sakta hai — jo aksar taxpayer ke liye zyada mehnga sabit hota hai. Yeh poori mushkil sirf is liye khari ho sakti hai kyunke profile mein address update nahi thi.

Update Ka Practical Process

IRIS mein login kar ke profile section mein jayein.
Jo bhi tafseel purani hai (address, mobile, email, business activity) usay edit karein.
Zaroorat parne par supporting document upload karein (jaise CNIC ki copy).
Update save karne ke baad confirm karein ke tabdeeli reflect ho gayi hai.
Agar CNIC ki bunyadi tafseel mein masla ho, to NADRA se pehle apna record verify karwaein.

Har Saal Filing Se Pehle Profile Ek Baar Check Karein

Sab se practical aadat yeh hai ke har saal filing shuru karne se pehle, IRIS profile ka ek baar jaiza le lein — chahe koi tabdeeli nahi bhi hui ho. Yeh chhota sa qadam yaqini banata hai ke agar saal ke doran koi cheez (address, business activity) chup-chap badal chuki hai jo aapko yaad hi nahi, woh update ho jaye. Yeh check karna sirf 2-3 minute ka kaam hai lekin aainda kisi masle se bacha sakta hai.

NTN Aur Profile Ka Aapas Mein Taalluq

Kuch log samajhte hain ke NTN aur IRIS profile do alag cheezein hain, jab ke haqeeqat mein NTN wohi shanakht hai jis par poora IRIS profile khada hota hai. Agar NTN ke basic record (jaise naam ki spelling) aur profile ke detail mein koi mismatch ho, to yeh kai jagah confusion paida kar sakta hai — bank verification, visa document verification, ya kisi bhi aisi jagah jahan aapka NTN check kiya jata hai. Isi liye zaroori hai ke NTN ka bunyadi record aur IRIS profile ki tafseel hamesha ek doosre se match karein.

Bahir Rehne Walon Ke Liye Profile Ki Ehmiyat

Overseas Pakistanis ke liye IRIS profile update rakhna aur bhi zyada zaroori hai, kyunke unka address aksar Pakistan aur bahir donon jagah munsalik hota hai. Agar profile mein Pakistan ka koi purana pata reh gaya hai jab ke woh khandan wahan se shift ho chuka hai, to notices ghalat jagah pohanchne ka khatra aur zyada barh jata hai, kyunke woh khud waha maujood bhi nahi hote ke wapasi mein pata chal jaye. Aisi soorat mein IRIS profile mein ek aisa contact number ya email rakhein jo hamesha check hota rahe, chahe woh khud kisi bhi mulk mein hon.

Employer Ke Record Aur IRIS Profile Ka Farq

Kabhi kabhi log samajhte hain ke unke employer ne unki tafseel HR record mein update kar di hai to IRIS mein bhi khud-ba-khud ho jayegi. Yeh ghalat-fehmi hai. Employer ka record aur IRIS ka apna profile do alag system hain jo aapas mein automatically link nahi hote (sirf kuch khaas withholding statements ke zariye income ka data jata hai, personal profile detail nahi). Agar aapka naam, CNIC ki spelling, ya address kahin badla hai, to IRIS profile khud jaa kar alag se update karna zaroori hai.

Khandan Ke Sab Filers Ki Profile Consistency

Agar ek khandan mein kai log filer hain — walid, walida, bhai, bahen — to behtar hai ke sab ki IRIS profile ka address consistent ho, khaas tor par agar sab ek hi ghar mein rehte hain. Kabhi aisa hota hai ke ek fard ne address update kar liya lekin baqi ka purana hi reh gaya, jis se khandan ke record mein bila wajah farq nazar aata hai. Ek sath sab ki profile ka jaiza lena is qism ki chhoti si inconsistency ko rokta hai.

Update Ke Baad Screenshot Rakhein

Jab bhi koi profile detail update karein, us waqt ka ek screenshot ya PDF sambhal kar rakhein jismein tareekh aur nayi maloomat saaf nazar aaye. Yeh sirf ehtiyat hai — agar kabhi yeh sawal aaye ke koi tabdeeli kab hui thi, to yeh record foran jawab de deta hai, khaas tor par un cases mein jahan address ki tabdeeli kisi qanooni maamle se juri ho, jaise koi property transfer ya koi visa application.

In sab chhoti chhoti aadaton ka majmoo yeh hai ke IRIS profile ek "set it and forget it" wali cheez nahi, balke ek zinda dastawez hai jo aapki maujooda haisiyat ko sahi tarah reflect karti rehni chahiye. Jitna updated aur consistent yeh record rahega, utna hi kam khatra hoga ke koi ghair-zaroori confusion ya notice ka masla khara ho.

Profile update sunne mein maamooli lagta hai, lekin isi maamooli si cheez ki wajah se bara masla ban sakta hai — ya bilkul nahi bhi ban sakta, agar waqt par yeh chhota sa kaam kar liya jaye.

Agar aap apna NTN kai saal pehle banwa chuke hain aur us ke baad se profile ko kabhi dobara nahi dekha, to yeh ek achha waqt hai ke ek baar poori profile khol kar dekh lein — address, mobile, email aur business activity, sab kuch. Yeh chhota sa muayna aksar aisi cheezein saamne la deta hai jo saalon se update hone se reh gayi thin.

Aakhir mein, IRIS profile update karwane mein koi sharm ya jhijhak ki baat nahi — yeh sirf ek routine maintenance hai jo har taxpayer ko apni filing ki tarah hi hamesha nazar mein rakhni chahiye.

Chhoti si baat, bara asar — yehi IRIS profile ki asal kahani hai. Ek do minute ka update, saalon ki ghair-zaroori uljhan se bacha sakta hai.

Simple profile update ki koi alag fee nahi, yeh hamari normal filing ke sath included hoti hai — is liye is chhote se kaam ko kabhi taalne ki zaroorat nahi, jab bhi mauqa mile theek karwa lein.

Frequently Asked Questions

Address badal gaya hai lekin IRIS mein update nahi kiya — kya masla ho sakta hai?
Ji haan, bara masla ho sakta hai. Agar FBR koi notice purane pate par bhejti hai to qanooni tor par maan liya jata hai ke aap ko woh mil chuki, chahe waqai na mili ho. Address current rakhna ek zaroori ehtiyat hai.
Business band kar diya hai — kya IRIS profile update karni zaroori hai?
Ji haan. Purani business activity category rehne se galat return form khulta hai aur ghair-zaroori fields fill karni parti hain. Profile update karne se sahi form khud khulta hai.
Mobile number badal gaya hai — IRIS profile update na karne se kya farq parta hai?
Registered number OTP aur important alerts ke liye istemal hota hai. Purana number band ho to login ya verification ke waqt masla aa sakta hai. Naya number turant update kar lena behtar hai.
CNIC renew hua aur usme kuch tafseel badal gayi — IRIS mein kaise update karein?
Is qism ki tabdeeli ke liye NADRA ke record se match karwana zaroori hota hai, jo seedha nahi hota. Naye CNIC ki copy aur zaroorat par darkhwast tayyar rakhein, aur agar masla ho to concerned FBR office se rabta karein.
Profile update karne ki koi alag fee hai?
Aam tor par nahi, jab yeh normal filing ke sath ki jati hai. Agar sirf profile update chahiye, bina filing ke, to apna case WhatsApp par bhej dein, hum rehnumai kar dete hain.
Profile update karne ke baad kya confirm karna chahiye?
Save karne ke baad profile dobara khol kar dekhein ke tabdeeli reflect ho gayi hai. Kabhi kabhi update process mein thora waqt lag sakta hai, is liye dobara check karna behtar hai.
