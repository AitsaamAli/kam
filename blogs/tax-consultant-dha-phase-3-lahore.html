DHA Phase 3 Lahore — Tax Compliance for Restaurants, Cafés and Food Businesses

Updated October 2026
Kamboh Associates
3 min read
DHA Phase 3

Phase 3's commercial strips — Y Block's food street above all — are one of Lahore's busiest restaurant clusters. A food business there answers to two tax authorities at once: the Punjab Revenue Authority for sales tax on the service, and FBR for income tax on the profit. Owners usually have one of them under control and the other drifting.

Two authorities, two different questions

  • Punjab Revenue Authority (PRA) treats restaurant and catering services as taxable services in Punjab. A registered restaurant charges sales tax on its bills, files monthly PRA returns and, in many cases, is expected to issue invoices through an integrated system so PRA can see sales in real time.
  • FBR is concerned with income tax: whether the business, its owner or its partners declare the profit the restaurant actually makes, and whether salaries and rent paid by the restaurant have had tax withheld where required.

The two sets of figures are increasingly compared. A restaurant reporting strong monthly sales to PRA but a thin profit to FBR — or the other way round — is the kind of file that gets a query. Our overview of provincial sales tax on restaurants covers the PRA side in depth.

The structure question comes first

Before any return is filed, we settle what the business legally is:

Many restaurants start as "a few of us put money in" without any partnership deed. That leaves each investor unsure what to declare. A short partnership deed and an AOP registration usually fix it, and our AOP return service handles the annual filing.

What a clean year looks like for a Phase 3 outlet

  1. Monthly PRA returns filed on time, with sales matching the POS or invoicing system.
  2. Purchases of food supplies, gas and packaging recorded with invoices, so profit is not overstated.
  3. Staff salaries recorded, and tax withheld for anyone above the taxable threshold.
  4. Rent paid to the landlord documented; if the business is a company or AOP of the prescribed size, tax withheld from rent where the law requires it.
  5. Annual income tax return of the proprietor, AOP or company, with the profit tying back to sales reported to PRA.

Restaurants that pay rent in Y Block or the surrounding commercial lanes often have a lease in the owner's personal name. If the business is an AOP or company, update the lease or document why the business pays it — otherwise the rent expense may not be accepted.

Catch-up for outlets that started without registering

Some Phase 3 businesses opened quickly and registered later. If you have been operating without PRA or FBR registration, the sensible path is to register now, start filing correctly from the current period, and assess the earlier months honestly with us. Waiting for an inspection makes every option more expensive.

DHA Phase 3 — questions we get asked

Does my restaurant in DHA Phase 3 register with PRA or FBR for sales tax?
Restaurant and catering services in Punjab fall under the Punjab Revenue Authority for sales tax. Income tax on the business profit is still filed with FBR, so most restaurants deal with both.
We are three partners without a written deed. How do we file?
The cleanest route is a short partnership deed and registering the business as an AOP. The AOP then files its own return and each partner shows their share of profit. We can draft the deed and handle the registration.
Our POS sales and our bank deposits don't match because of cash. Is that a problem?
It can be if nobody can explain the gap. Cash sales are normal, but they should be recorded in the POS and deposited or accounted for. We reconcile POS, PRA returns and bank statements before filing.
Can a small café just file a personal return without PRA registration?
If it provides a taxable restaurant service in Punjab above PRA's thresholds, it is expected to register. We check your turnover and setup and tell you plainly whether registration is required.
