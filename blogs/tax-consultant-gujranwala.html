Industrial & Manufacturing Tax in Gujranwala

Gujranwala's diverse industrial base — from ceramic tiles to electric fans — each has specific FBR tax obligations:

IndustryKey Tax Obligation
Ceramics / Tiles ManufacturersSales tax @ 18%, WHT on purchases from non-filers
Steel / Iron Re-rollingFED + sales tax; minimum tax on turnover
Electric Fan ManufacturersSales tax on local supply; export = 1% WHT final

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my Gujranwala manufacturing business need to be on ATL?
Yes. Being on Active Taxpayer List (ATL) is critical for Gujranwala manufacturers to avoid higher WHT deductions by their buyers. You must file your annual return by September 30 each year to remain on ATL. We ensure timely filing to protect your ATL status.
How is sales tax handled for a ceramics factory in Gujranwala?
Ceramic tiles and bathroom fixtures are subject to 18% sales tax on local supply. The manufacturer charges sales tax to buyers, claims input tax on raw material purchases, and files a monthly return. Net tax payable is output tax minus input tax.
Can you file our monthly sales tax returns remotely?
Yes — we handle monthly sales tax filing for Gujranwala businesses entirely online. Share purchase/sale summary by the 15th and we file by the 18th deadline each month. Starting at Rs. 3,000/month.
What are the tax benefits of being a filer in Gujranwala?
Lower WHT rates on purchases and sales (2% vs 4%), lower property transaction tax, access to banking benefits, ability to import goods at reduced advance tax, and avoidance of penalties. Becoming a filer typically saves Gujranwala businesses more than the cost of filing.
