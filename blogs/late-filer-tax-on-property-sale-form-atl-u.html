Late Filer Tax on Property Sale in 2026: Why the Rate Is 2.75% or 11.5%, and How Form ATL-U Gets You the Lower One

19 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 12 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 19 November 2026
On a Rs 1 crore sale, the advance tax is Rs 275,000 if you are on the ATL and Rs 1,150,000 if you are not. Late filers who are only selling can usually close that gap with Form ATL-U.

Most late filers who call us in November have one urgent question: 'My property transfer is next month. How much tax will I pay?' The answer depends almost entirely on whether your name is on the ATL on the day of transfer. Here is the law, the numbers, and the order of steps that protects you.

Short answer

When immovable property is sold, Section 236C requires advance tax to be collected from the seller at registration or transfer. Under Division X of Part IV of the First Schedule, as substituted by the Finance Act 2026, the rate for a person on the Active Taxpayers List is 2.75% of the gross consideration. The Tenth Schedule sets 11.5% for a person not on the ATL. A late filer is off the ATL until the surcharge is paid or, for an individual, Form ATL-U is furnished. The undertaking restricts purchasing, acquiring or obtaining ownership or beneficial interest in property for six months; it says nothing against selling. A seller with no purchase planned can therefore join the ATL without the Rs 25,000 surcharge and sell at 2.75%.

The Rates in the Law

Short answer: Section 236C collects 2.75% from a seller on the ATL and 11.5% from one who is not.

Seller's statusSection 236C rateWhere it comes from
On the ATL2.75% of gross considerationFirst Schedule, Part IV, Division X (Finance Act 2026)
Not on the ATL11.5% of gross considerationTenth Schedule, rule 1, third proviso table

Two changes in the Finance Act 2026 matter for sellers. First, Division X now has a single 2.75% rate for filers, replacing the earlier slab table of 4.5%, 5% and 5.5% by value. Second, rule 1A of the Tenth Schedule, which charged separate higher rates to people who were on the ATL but had filed late, was omitted. A late filer who reaches the ATL is now charged exactly what an on-time filer pays.

What the Difference Looks Like

Short answer: On a Rs 1 crore sale that is Rs 275,000 on the ATL against Rs 1,150,000 off it.

Sale valueAdvance tax at 2.75%Advance tax at 11.5%Extra paid off the ATL
Rs 50 lakhRs 137,500Rs 575,000Rs 437,500
Rs 1 croreRs 275,000Rs 1,150,000Rs 875,000
Rs 2.5 croreRs 687,500Rs 2,875,000Rs 2,187,500

Against those numbers, Rs 25,000 for the surcharge looks small, and many sellers pay it out of fear. If you are not buying anything in the next six months, there is no need: Form ATL-U achieves the same ATL inclusion.

Why Selling Is Compatible With Form ATL-U

Short answer: Form ATL-U restricts acquiring property, not selling it, so a seller with no purchase planned can use it.

Section 182A(3) asks an individual to declare that he "shall not purchase, acquire, or otherwise obtain ownership or beneficial interest in any property" for six months. Each verb is about something coming to you. Disposing of property you already own is not among them. The undertaking was designed for exactly the person who needs ATL status without buying.

The risk is what happens to the sale money. If it goes into a new plot, a booking, a file or a flat within six months, the undertaking is broken. Plan the reinvestment for after the six months, or pay the surcharge instead.

The Order of Steps That Protects You

Short answer: File the return, furnish Form ATL-U, wait for your name on the ATL, then transfer.

  1. Check your status now with the online or SMS check.
  2. File the Tax Year 2026 return and wealth statement. The property you are selling should already be in the wealth statement at its cost.
  3. Furnish Form ATL-U and keep the acknowledgement.
  4. Wait for your name to appear on the ATL. The rate is applied according to the list at the time of transfer.
  5. Proceed with the transfer. Ask for the challan showing tax at 2.75%.

If the buyer is pushing for a date earlier than the next ATL update, you have two choices: move the date, or pay the surcharge and accept the cost for certainty. We help you weigh that on the day.

Is the 236C Tax Final?

Short answer: 236C tax is adjustable against tax on the gain, and refunds are blocked while you are off the ATL.

Advance tax under Section 236C is adjustable against your tax on the capital gain for the year of sale, not a final tax in itself. That is another reason to be on the ATL: Section 182A(1)(c) blocks refunds while a person is not on the ATL, so excess tax collected at the higher rate can stay locked. The capital gain itself is reported in the return for the tax year in which the sale happens, the Tax Year 2027 return for a sale now.

Overseas Sellers

Short answer: Non-resident sellers face the same rates and can furnish Form ATL-U on IRIS from abroad.

Non-resident Pakistanis who own property here face the same Section 236C rates. If you have not filed, or filed late, the same two routes apply. Our guide for freelancers, traders and overseas Pakistanis covers the overseas angle, including filing from abroad.

What About the Buyer?

Short answer: Buyers pay 236K at 1.25% on the ATL and 10.5% to 18.5% off it; Form ATL-U is not for buyers.

The buyer pays advance tax under Section 236K: 1.25% on the ATL, and 10.5%, 14.5% or 18.5% by value off it. If you are the buyer and a late filer, Form ATL-U is not for you: buying is what it forbids. Pay the surcharge, or use our comparison guide to think it through.

Mistakes Sellers Make

Short answer: The costly mistakes are transferring before your name appears and reinvesting in property within six months.

  • Filing the return but not the ATL route, then discovering at the registrar that they are still off the list.
  • Assuming 'active late filer' rates still apply. Rule 1A is gone since 1 July 2026.
  • Selling one property and booking another within the six months.
  • Omitting the property from the wealth statement and creating a mismatch with the registrar's records.

Our main guide on tax on property sale in Pakistan covers gains, holding periods and the buyer's side in more depth.

A Worked Example

Short answer: Filing late and using Form ATL-U before a Rs 1.8 crore transfer saved Rs 1,575,000 in advance tax in our example.

Ahmed filed his Tax Year 2026 return on 12 November 2026, after the deadline. He has agreed to sell a house in Lahore for Rs 1.8 crore, with transfer planned for mid-January 2027. He has no plans to buy property before summer.

StepDateResult
Return and wealth statement filed (house declared)12 November 2026Return complete, but Ahmed is not on the ATL

Name appears on the ATLAfter FBR's next updateFiler rates now apply

Had Ahmed transferred while still off the ATL, Section 236C would have collected 11.5%: Rs 2,070,000. The difference, Rs 1,575,000, is far more than any fee or surcharge. His only discipline is not to put the sale money into another property before 20 May 2027.

Jointly Owned Property

Short answer: Each co-owner's own ATL status decides the rate on their share; each needs their own Form ATL-U.

When two or more people sell a jointly owned property, each owner's share is a separate seller's share for advance tax, and each owner's own ATL status matters. A brother on the ATL and a sister off it may pay different rates on their halves. If any co-owner filed late, they should complete their own ATL route before the transfer. Form ATL-U is personal: one co-owner's undertaking does not cover another.

Selling Through a Power of Attorney

Short answer: When an attorney sells for an overseas owner, it is the owner's ATL status that counts.

Overseas owners often sell through an attorney in Pakistan. The tax is collected with reference to the owner, not the attorney, so it is the owner's ATL status that counts. The owner can file the return and furnish Form ATL-U on IRIS from abroad. Check the status before the attorney goes to the registrar.

What to Keep Ready for the Transfer

Short answer: Keep an ATL screenshot, the Form ATL-U and return acknowledgements, and your purchase papers.

  • A screenshot of your ATL status on the day before transfer.
  • Your Form ATL-U acknowledgement, in case the official asks how a late filer appears on the list.
  • The Tax Year 2026 return acknowledgement.
  • Purchase documents, which you will need for the capital gain calculation in next year's return.

Seven Days Before the Transfer: A Checklist

Short answer: Check status, the gross consideration, your CNIC and the challan before the transfer date.

  1. Check your ATL status online or by SMS. If you are not on the list, do not go ahead on the planned date.
  2. Confirm the gross consideration figure that will appear on the transfer papers; Section 236C applies to the gross amount.
  3. Make sure your CNIC on the transfer papers matches the CNIC on IRIS, so the tax collected is credited to you.
  4. Ask the society or registrar how the advance tax will be collected and get the challan copy afterwards.
  5. Keep the purchase deed or allotment letter for the property you are selling; the gain is worked out from its cost.
  6. Note the sale in your records for the Tax Year 2027 return.
  7. Re-read your Form ATL-U end date before you discuss any new purchase with the buyer or a dealer.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the tax on property sale for a late filer in 2026?
It depends on the ATL. On the ATL, Section 236C collects 2.75%; off it, 11.5%. Rule 1A, which set separate rates for late filers on the ATL, was omitted by the Finance Act 2026.
Can I sell property after furnishing Form ATL-U?
The undertaking forbids purchasing, acquiring or obtaining ownership or beneficial interest in property for six months. Selling is not among those, so a sale does not breach it.
Do I need to pay Rs 25,000 to sell at the filer rate?
Not if you are an individual who can keep the six-month undertaking. Form ATL-U replaces the surcharge.
When is the rate decided?
At the time of transfer, according to whether you appear on the ATL. Make sure your name is on the list before the transfer date.
Is the 236C advance tax final?
No. It is adjustable against tax on the capital gain in the return for the year of sale.
Can I reinvest the sale proceeds in a new plot?
Not within the six months if you used Form ATL-U. That would be acquiring property. Wait, or pay the surcharge instead.
What did 'active late filer' sellers pay before?
From 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2026, rule 1A of the Tenth Schedule charged them separate higher rates. That rule no longer applies.
Does the buyer's status affect my tax?
No. The seller pays 236C based on the seller's status; the buyer pays 236K based on the buyer's.
Does each co-owner need their own Form ATL-U?
Yes. The undertaking is personal to the individual who furnishes it. Each co-owner's ATL status decides the rate on their share.
Can an overseas owner selling through an attorney use Form ATL-U?
Yes. The owner files the return and furnishes the form on IRIS from abroad; the owner's ATL status decides the rate.
What if the transfer date comes before my name appears on the ATL?
Section 236C will collect 11.5%. Either move the date, or pay the surcharge if that gets you on the list sooner for an urgent transfer.
