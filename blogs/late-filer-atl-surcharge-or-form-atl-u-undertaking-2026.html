Late Filer in 2026? Rs 25,000 ATL Surcharge or the Form ATL-U Undertaking

1 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 10 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Tax Consultant | NTN & Compliance Specialist · Facts checked 1 November 2026
Filing after the deadline does not put you on the Active Taxpayers List. Individuals have two possible routes back: a Rs 25,000 surcharge, or a six-month property undertaking (draft rule).

Every November, the same question arrives on our WhatsApp from people who filed late: "My return is submitted, so why am I still not on the ATL?" In 2026 the answer comes with a bigger price tag than before, along with a possible way to avoid it. This guide sets out both routes, who each one suits, and the risks in the fine print.

TL;DR

After the 31 October 2026 deadline, filing a return alone does not put you on the Active Taxpayers List. Under Section 182A, individuals pay a Rs 25,000 surcharge (AOPs Rs 50,000, companies Rs 100,000). In October 2026 FBR published a draft Form ATL-U: an individual who promises not to acquire any property for six months could join the ATL without the surcharge. Pay the surcharge if any property deal is possible within six months. Consider the undertaking only if it is live in IRIS and you are certain no property will come to you in that period.

Why a Late Return Does Not Put You on the ATL

The Active Taxpayers List is the list banks, property registrars and vehicle registration authorities check before choosing which withholding rate to apply. On-time filers appear on it once their return is processed. Section 182A treats late filers differently. A person who files after the due date (31 October 2026 for most Tax Year 2026 returns) is included only after paying a surcharge. Your return can be complete and accepted while you still show as inactive.

Route 1: Pay the Section 182A Surcharge

The Finance Act 2026 raised the surcharge from 1 July 2026:

TaxpayerSurcharge nowBefore 1 July 2026Increase
IndividualRs 25,000Rs 1,00025 times
Association of personsRs 50,000Rs 10,0005 times
CompanyRs 100,000Rs 20,0005 times

Once you pay, your status updates and nothing else is required of you. There are no conditions on what you buy or sell afterwards. The increase has been controversial. The Pakistan Tax Bar Association asked FBR to withdraw the higher amount for Tax Year 2025 returns, arguing it cannot apply retrospectively. For Tax Year 2026 returns filed after 31 October 2026, the higher amount is the current rule.

Route 2: The Form ATL-U Undertaking (Draft)

On 6 October 2026, FBR published draft amendments to Rule 81B of the Income Tax Rules, 2002, through SRO 1691(I)/2026, and invited comments within seven days. As reported by Dawn and Business Recorder, the draft would let an individual who files the latest return after the due date join the ATL without the surcharge by submitting Form ATL-U through IRIS. The form records your name, CNIC or NTN, tax year, filing date and return acknowledgement number.

The undertaking has three parts:

  • The promise: you will not purchase, acquire or otherwise obtain ownership or beneficial interest in any property for six months.
  • The clock: the six months run from the date on the IRIS electronic acknowledgement for Form ATL-U.
  • The consequence: FBR can verify compliance with registries, authorities and financial institutions. If a breach is established after a hearing, the surcharge-free benefit is withdrawn from the date of the breach.

Status check: the reports we found up to 1 November 2026 describe this as a draft, and we have not seen a final notification. If Form ATL-U is not available in IRIS when you file, Route 1 is the only route.

Which Route Fits Your Situation

Pay the surcharge if:

  • You are buying, booking or might inherit property, a plot file or a share in property in the next six months. The draft wording "any property" is broad, so do not assume it covers only land or houses.
  • You are partway through a property transaction, such as an instalment plan, a transfer in progress or a family settlement.
  • You want certainty, and Rs 25,000 is small next to the withholding difference on a single transaction.

The undertaking may suit you if:

  • Form ATL-U is actually live in IRIS when you file.
  • You are, for example, a salaried employee or a freelancer who needs filer rates mainly for banking, and you are certain nothing of this kind will happen in the next six months.
  • You keep a written note of the acknowledgement date, so you know exactly when the six months end.

Not an option: AOPs and companies. The draft applies only to individuals, so they pay Rs 50,000 or Rs 100,000.

Situations to Think Through Before You Sign

An undertaking is a promise to FBR, and the draft attaches a real consequence to breaking it. Before choosing it, go through the next six months honestly. These are the situations we ask clients about:

  • An instalment plan already running. If you are paying instalments on a plot, flat or file in a housing scheme, a transfer, allotment or possession letter could fall inside the six months. That can look like acquiring an interest in property. Pay the surcharge.
  • Family transfers and inheritance. A gift from a parent, or a share in inherited property being transferred to heirs, is still a change in ownership. If a family settlement is underway, do not give the undertaking without advice on whether it would count.
  • Balloting and new bookings. Booking a unit in a new project, or winning a balloting draw, can create a beneficial interest even before full payment. Anyone planning to apply for a scheme should take Route 1.
  • Vehicles and other assets. The draft wording, as reported, says "any property" without listing types. Until the final rule defines it, the cautious reading is that the promise may reach further than land and houses. Plan accordingly, and check the final wording before you rely on a narrower reading.

If you are unsure about even one of these, Rs 25,000 buys certainty. A broken undertaking takes back the benefit and draws attention to your file, so it can cost more.

Why the Surcharge Is Often Worth Paying

The real comparison is not Rs 25,000 against zero. It is Rs 25,000 against what being off the ATL costs you on your actual transactions. Property transfers, vehicle registration and some banking transactions carry much higher withholding for persons not on the ATL. On one property deal, that difference can be many times the surcharge. Our filer and non-filer withholding rates guide has the current rates. If even one such transaction is likely, pay the surcharge.

Steps After Filing Your Late Return

  1. File the Tax Year 2026 return and wealth statement in IRIS and save the acknowledgement.
  2. Choose your route. For the surcharge, generate the payment slip (PSID) for the Section 182A surcharge and pay it through your bank or banking app. For the undertaking, submit Form ATL-U in IRIS, if it is available, and save its acknowledgement.
  3. Allow time for processing, then check your status online or by SMS (see our ATL status check guide).
  4. Keep the payment receipt or the undertaking acknowledgement with your tax records.

The Penalty Still Applies

Neither route settles the Section 182 penalty for filing late. That penalty can still be imposed by order. It is 0.1% of tax payable per day, with a minimum of Rs 40,000, or Rs 5,000 for most salaried individuals below Rs 5 million in salary. Frequently Asked Questions

What is Form ATL-U?
Form ATL-U is an undertaking proposed by FBR in draft rules published on 6 October 2026. An individual who files late promises not to acquire any property for six months and, in return, joins the Active Taxpayers List without paying the Section 182A surcharge.
Is Form ATL-U available in IRIS now?
The reports we found up to 1 November 2026 describe Form ATL-U as a draft. Whether it is usable depends on FBR finalising the rule and enabling the form in IRIS, so check IRIS on the day you file.
How much is the ATL surcharge for late filers in 2026?
From 1 July 2026 under the Finance Act 2026, the Section 182A surcharge is Rs 25,000 for individuals, Rs 50,000 for associations of persons, and Rs 100,000 for companies.
What counts as property under the ATL-U undertaking?
The draft wording, as reported, covers purchasing, acquiring or otherwise obtaining ownership or beneficial interest in any property. That is broad. Until the final rule is published, do not assume it is limited to houses or land.
What happens if I break the six-month undertaking?
Under the draft, FBR can verify compliance using records from registries, authorities and financial institutions. If a breach is established after a hearing, the surcharge-free ATL benefit is withdrawn from the date of the breach.
Can an AOP or company use Form ATL-U?
No. The draft applies to individuals only. AOPs pay Rs 50,000 and companies pay Rs 100,000 to be placed on the Active Taxpayers List after a late return.
Does paying the surcharge remove the late filing penalty?
No. The surcharge only restores ATL status. The Section 182 penalty for filing after the due date is a separate matter and can still be imposed by order.
How do I check whether I am back on the ATL?
Check FBR's online ATL search with your CNIC or NTN, or send an SMS with ATL, a space and your CNIC to 9966. Allow some processing time after paying or submitting the undertaking.
