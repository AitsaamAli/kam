USA F-1 Student Visa — FBR Documents Aur Affidavit of Support Checklist (2026)

28 August 2026Aitsaam Ali12 min read2026 Verified

F-1 visa Pakistani students ke liye us liye mushkil nahi ke uski requirements zyada hain — balke is liye ke uski requirements ek muqarrara number ki shakal mein hoti hi nahi. Koi blocked account nahi, koi GIC nahi, koi "itni raqam dikhayein" wali line nahi. Sirf ek officer, chand minute, aur yeh faisla ke aapki funding ki kahani mantaqi lagti hai ya nahi. Aur us kahani ka poora wazan aapke sponsor ke kagazon se aata hai.

TL;DR:

  • SEVIS I-901 fee $350 aur visa application (MRV) fee $185 — do alag fees, alag jagah adaa hoti hain.
  • F-1 mein koi tay-shuda proof-of-funds figure nahi hota; aapko pehle saal ka poora kharcha aur baqi saalon ka mantaqi plan dikhana hota hai.
  • Affidavit of Support akela kaafi nahi — uska wazan sponsor ke tax record se aata hai.
  • Sab se aam masla yeh hai ke interview mein bataya gaya karobar aur return mein likhi income aapas mein mail nahi khate.
  • 17 July 2026 ko DHS ne F aur J students ke liye "duration of status" ki jagah ek muqarrara admission period ka final rule jaari kiya.

I-20 se interview tak — process ka naqsha

F-1 ka process tarteeb se chalta hai aur har qadam agle par munhasir hai:

QadamKya hota hai
1. AdmissionSEVP-certified school se daakhla aur Form I-20 ka issue hona
2. SEVIS feeI-20 par darj SEVIS ID se $350 ki adaigi (FMJfee.com)
3. DS-160Online visa application form
4. MRV fee$185 visa application fee, embassy ke nizam se
5. InterviewUS Embassy Islamabad ya Consulate mein

Interview mein aap ke saath passport, I-20, DS-160 confirmation, SEVIS fee ki rasid, admission letter aur financial documents hone chahiyen. Officer interview ke doran aap ka SEVIS record real time mein dekhta hai — agar fee adaa nahi hui ya abhi process nahi hui, to us din uska koi hal nahi hota.

Do alag fees jo log gadd-madd kar dete hain

Yeh chhoti si baat lagti hai lekin har saal bohat se applicants ka waqt zaya karti hai.

SEVIS I-901 fee ($350) DHS ko jati hai aur us nizam ko chalati hai jo international students ka record rakhta hai. Yeh I-20 par mojood SEVIS ID se juri hoti hai, is liye I-20 haath mein aaye baghair adaa nahi ho sakti.

MRV visa application fee ($185) Department of State ki taraf ki fee hai jo interview ka appointment lene ke liye adaa hoti hai.

Do amali baatein. Pehli: SEVIS fee ki adaigi ko system mein process hone mein kaam ke chand din lagte hain — interview se ek din pehle adaa karna khatarnak hai. Doosri: yeh fee wapas nahi hoti. Agar visa refuse ho jaye ya aap na jayein, to yeh raqam khatam. Isi tarah MRV fee bhi wapas nahi hoti.

Kitna paisa? Yahan koi number nahi hota

Yeh sawal har Pakistani student poochta hai aur is ka koi seedha jawab mojood nahi. US consulate koi threshold nahi deti.

Iski jagah, aapko yeh dikhana hota hai ke:

Pehle saal ka poora kharcha — tuition aur living dono — aap ke paas mojood hai ya committed hai.
Baqi saalon ke liye ek mantaqi plan hai — yani sponsor ki aamdani itni hai ke woh agle saalon mein bhi yeh bojh utha sakta hai.

Doosri baat pehli se zyada ahem hai aur zyada tar log usay bilkul nazar-andaz kar dete hain. Aap ke paas pehle saal ke liye paisa jama ho sakta hai — shayad ek plot bech kar. Lekin agar sponsor ki saalana aamdani us se bohat kam hai, to officer ke zehan mein foran yeh sawal aata hai ke doosre aur teesre saal ka kya hoga. Aur us sawal ka jawab sirf ek cheez deti hai: sponsor ki saalana income ka documented silsila.

Yahi wajah hai ke Amreeka ke liye tax returns ki ahmiyat baqi mulkon se zyada hai. Jahan ek fixed number dikhana ho, wahan ek dafa ka bara balance kaam kar jata hai. Jahan "gunjaish" sabit karni ho, wahan saalon ka silsila chahiye — aur wo silsila sirf returns se banta hai.

Affidavit of Support kya hai aur uska wazan kahan se aata hai

Affidavit of Support aap ke sponsor ka baaqaida bayan hota hai ke woh aap ki taleem ka kharcha uthayega. F-1 ke context mein yeh aam tor par Form I-134 ki shakal mein hota hai.

Ab ahem baat: yeh form apne aap mein tagreeban be-wazan hai. Yeh sirf ek waada hai. Officer ke liye sawal yeh nahi ke aap ke walid ne waada kiya ya nahi — sawal yeh hai ke kya woh us waade ko poora kar sakte hain.

Aur us sawal ka jawab affidavit ke sath lage huye documents dete hain. Amreeka mein rehne wale sponsors ke liye yeh IRS tax returns aur employment letter hote hain. Pakistan mein rehne wale sponsors ke liye theek isi cheez ka Pakistani mutabadil hota hai — FBR ki tax returns, wealth statement aur bank record.

Yani Affidavit of Support ka poora wazan un kagazon se aata hai jo uske sath lagte hain, khud form se nahi.

Business owner sponsors ke liye SECP registration documents aur company ki apni tax return bhi shamil ho jati hai — kyunki us soorat mein aamdani ka source ek individual nahi, ek registered entity hoti hai.

Ek baat khaas tor par: teen saal ka silsila ek saal se kahin behtar hai. Aur teen saal ka silsila us waqt sab se mazboot hota hai jab uski har kari apne waqt par file hui ho — na ke teenon saal ek hi hafte mein.

Sab se aam self-inflicted masla

Yeh Pakistani F-1 applications ka sab se aam aur sab se qabil-e-ihtiraz masla hai, aur is par saaf baat karni chahiye.

Interview mein student batata hai ke uske walid ka achha khasa karobar hai. Officer file mein dekhta hai ke walid ki declared income mamooli hai. Ab do sarkari-darjay ke bayanat aapas mein tazaad kar rahe hain: ek zubani, ek tehreeri. Officer ke paas is ka koi hal nahi bacha — kyunki agar karobar waqai bara hai to record ghalat hai, aur agar record theek hai to interview mein di gayi tasveer ghalat hai.

Aur is ka hal jaldi mein nahi ho sakta. Ek saal achanak zyada income declare kar dena apni jagah ek naya sawal bana deta hai. Wahi record wazan rakhta hai jo waqt ke saath, apne fitri andaz mein bana ho. Isi liye hum kehte hain ke Amreeka ka faisla jis din zehan mein aaye, filing usi din se durust honi chahiye — chahe apply do saal baad karna ho.

Wapsi ka iraada bhi sabit karna hota hai

F-1 ek non-immigrant visa hai, aur us mein qanoon yeh farz karta hai ke aap Amreeka ja kar wahin reh jayenge. Yeh farz aap ke khilaf hota hai aur ise todna aap ki zimmedari hai.

Student ke case mein yeh khaas tor par mushkil hai, kyunki bees saal ka noujawan aksar khud koi jaidad nahi rakhta. Is liye yahan wazan khandaan ke rishtay par jata hai: walidain yahan hain, khandaani jaidad yahan hai, aur us jaidad ke sath aap ka mustaqbil juda hua hai.

Wealth statement is silsile mein khamoshi se kaam karta hai. Woh sirf funding ki gunjaish nahi dikhata — woh yeh bhi dikhata hai ke is khandaan ki jaren Pakistan mein hain, aur yahan aisi cheezein hain jin ki taraf wapas aana mantaqi hai.

Ek nayi tabdeeli: fixed admission period

17 July 2026 ko DHS ne ek final rule jaari kiya jis ke tehat F academic students aur J exchange visitors ke liye ek muqarrara admission period aur us mein toseeh ka baaqaida tareeqa qaim kiya gaya hai.

Is se pehle F students aam tor par "duration of status" par admit hote thay — yani jab tak aap apna program jari rakhein. Naye nizam mein admission ki ek tay shuda muddat hoti hai aur us se aage rehne ke liye toseeh ki darkhwast deni hoti hai.

Yeh Pakistan-side documentation par kaise asar daal sakta hai: agar aage chal kar toseeh ki darkhwast deni ho to us waqt bhi aap ki funding aur halaat dobara dekhe ja sakte hain. Yani sponsor ka record sirf pehli dafa ke liye nahi — usay chalte rehna behtar hai. Yeh rule naya hai aur is ke amali asraat waqt ke sath saamne aayenge, is liye apni current status apni university ke international office se confirm karte rahein.

F-1 Visa & FBR Documents — Aam Sawalat

F-1 visa ke liye kitna bank balance chahiye?
Koi tay-shuda figure nahi hai — US consulate threshold nahi deti. Aapko pehle saal ka poora kharcha (tuition aur living) aur baqi saalon ke liye ek mantaqi plan dikhana hota hai. Doosri baat zyada ahem hai: officer yeh dekhta hai ke sponsor ki saalana aamdani agle saalon ka bojh utha sakti hai ya nahi, aur us ka jawab sirf documented income history se milta hai.
SEVIS fee aur visa fee mein kya farq hai?
Yeh do alag fees hain. SEVIS I-901 fee $350 hai, DHS ko jati hai, aur I-20 par darj SEVIS ID ke zariye FMJfee.com par adaa hoti hai. MRV visa application fee $185 hai aur embassy ke apne nizam se interview appointment ke liye adaa hoti hai. Dono ki rasid interview mein darkar hoti hai aur dono wapas nahi hotin. SEVIS fee ko process hone mein kaam ke chand din lagte hain.
Affidavit of Support kaafi hai ya sath mein tax return bhi chahiye?
Affidavit akela tagreeban be-wazan hai — woh sirf ek waada hai. Officer ka sawal yeh nahi ke sponsor ne waada kiya, balke yeh ke woh us waade ko poora kar sakta hai. Us sawal ka jawab affidavit ke sath lage documents dete hain: Pakistani sponsors ke liye FBR ki tax returns, wealth statement aur bank record.
Mere walid ka karobar bara hai lekin return mein income kam hai — kya karun?
Yeh Pakistani F-1 applications ka sab se aam masla hai. Interview mein bara karobar batana aur file mein mamooli declared income dikhana ek aisa tazaad paida karta hai jiska officer ke paas koi hal nahi hota. Aur is ka ilaaj jaldi mein nahi ho sakta — ek saal achanak zyada income declare karna apni jagah naya sawal bana deta hai. Wahi record wazan rakhta hai jo waqt ke sath bana ho, is liye filing us din se durust karni chahiye jis din Amreeka ka iraada bane.
Duration of status khatam hone ka kya matlab hai?
17 July 2026 ko DHS ne ek final rule jaari kiya jis ke tehat F academic students aur J exchange visitors ke liye muqarrara admission period aur toseeh ka baaqaida tareeqa qaim kiya gaya. Pehle F students aam tor par program ki muddat tak admit hote thay. Ab ek tay-shuda muddat hoti hai aur us se aage rehne ke liye darkhwast deni hoti hai. Yeh rule naya hai, is liye apni current position university ke international office se confirm karte rahein.

Sponsor Ki Maali File Abhi Se Mazboot Karwayein

NTN Rs. 2,000, teen saal ki returns Rs. 3,500 se, wealth statement Rs. 2,500. Amreeka ke case mein purana record hi asal sarmaya hai — aur woh do hafte mein nahi banta.
