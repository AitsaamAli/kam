Sole Proprietorship Registration in Lahore: How to Set Up Your Business, Which Tax Office Covers You, and Local Help

10 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 10 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 10 November 2026
Registering a business in Lahore is done online, but the Lahore tax offices still matter for some changes. Local help saves repeat visits.

Lahore has a huge number of small businesses, from shops in its markets to workshops, agencies and online stores run from home. Most start as sole proprietorships. This guide explains how registration works for a Lahore business, what differs locally, and how to avoid the mistakes we see most often in the city.

TL;DR

A sole proprietorship in Lahore is registered online on FBR's IRIS portal, like anywhere in Pakistan: the owner's CNIC is the NTN and the business is added through Form 181. Lahore businesses fall under one of the city's income tax offices named in FBR notifications, including RTO-I and RTO-II Lahore, the Corporate Tax Office and the Large Taxpayers' Office. Changes such as discontinuing a business, deregistration or a change of jurisdiction need a visit to your RTO. Registration Is Online, Even in Lahore

There is no separate Lahore registration. The process is the same national one:

  1. Create or activate your IRIS profile with your CNIC; under Section 181(4) the CNIC is your NTN.
  2. Add the business name, start date, address and nature of business through Form 181.
  3. Add any branches, such as a second shop or a warehouse, separately.
  4. Open a business bank account and add it to IRIS.

Our national guide to sole proprietorship registration explains each step, and our Form 181 guide covers the form itself.

Which Lahore Tax Office Covers You

FBR assigns each taxpayer to an income tax office. FBR's notifications name the Lahore offices, including RTO-I Lahore, RTO-II Lahore, the Corporate Tax Office Lahore and the Large Taxpayers' Office Lahore. Most sole proprietors fall under one of the two RTOs. Your office is shown in your IRIS profile; check it before you write to FBR or plan a visit.

Your office matters for three reasons:

  • Notices about your returns come from it.
  • Changes FBR does not allow online, such as discontinuing a business or deregistration, are made there.
  • Applications and replies should be addressed to it, or they are delayed.

Documents to Bring or Send

  • Original CNIC (and a copy for any RTO visit).
  • Rent agreement or ownership document for the premises, and a recent utility bill.
  • Business name exactly as it will appear on the signboard and bank account.
  • Start date of the business and a short description of the activity.
  • Bank account details, once opened.

Moving Your Business Within or Into Lahore

A new address within your current office's area is updated through Form 181. Moving to Lahore from another city, or between offices, can change your jurisdiction, which FBR lists among changes that need an RTO visit. Apply early so notices do not keep going to the old office.

Professional Tax in Punjab

Separately from federal income tax, Punjab levies professional tax, collected by the Excise, Taxation and Narcotics Control Department, which can be paid online. Whether and how much a business pays depends on the provincial rules for its category. Keep the paid receipt each year: licence renewals, tenders and some registrations ask for proof that it has been paid. Paying professional tax does not settle income tax, and vice versa.

Sole Proprietorship or Partnership?

Many Lahore businesses are run by relatives together. If two or more people share ownership and profits, the business is an association of persons, not a sole proprietorship, and it registers and files separately. Partnerships in Punjab can also register with the Registrar of Firms. Getting this right at the start avoids re-registering later; see our guide to the Registrar of Firms and FBR registration for partnerships.

Online Stores Run From Lahore

Lahore has many home-based online stores. Because marketplaces and couriers must not serve unregistered vendors under Section 181(1A), register the business first, with the store name you will use, and use the same name and CNIC on the seller and courier accounts. Keep the monthly settlement and cash-on-delivery statements as your sales records.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register a sole proprietorship in Lahore?
Online on FBR's IRIS portal: your CNIC is your NTN, and you add the business through Form 181 with its name, address, start date and branches.
Which FBR office covers my Lahore business?
Most sole proprietors fall under RTO-I or RTO-II Lahore. Your office is shown in your IRIS profile.
Do I need to visit an FBR office to register?
No. Registration is online. Visits are needed only for changes FBR does not allow online, such as discontinuing a business or a change of jurisdiction.

What does registration cost?
FBR charges no fee for IRIS registration. Our published fee for registering a sole proprietorship is Rs 5,000.
I run two shops in different Lahore markets. How do I register?
Register one business on your NTN and add the second shop as a branch with its own address.
My brother and I run the shop together. Is it a sole proprietorship?
If you both genuinely own it, it is a partnership (AOP), which registers separately. A sole proprietorship has one owner.
Do Lahore businesses pay professional tax?
Punjab levies professional tax through the Excise, Taxation and Narcotics Control Department, depending on the category of business or profession. It is separate from federal income tax.
Can you register my business the same day I visit?
When the IRIS profile and documents are in order, the business can usually be added in the same visit. Problems with the profile may need extra steps.
Can I register an online store run from home in Lahore?
Yes. Use your home address as the business address and register before opening marketplace and courier accounts.
