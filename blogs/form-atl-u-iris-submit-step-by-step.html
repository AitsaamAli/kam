How to Submit Form ATL-U on IRIS: A Step-by-Step Guide for Late Filers (2026)

19 November 2026 Kamboh Associates 12 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Author · Figures from FBR’s official website · Facts checked 19 November 2026
Form ATL-U is furnished electronically on IRIS. Here is what to prepare, the order to do things in, and how to confirm it worked.

Most people who come to us about Form ATL-U have already understood the idea. What they want is the practical sequence: what to open, what to fill, and how to know it worked. This guide follows that order, and flags the points where people go wrong.

Short answer

Form ATL-U is furnished on FBR's IRIS portal after the late return has been filed. Prepare four things first: working IRIS login, the filed Tax Year 2026 return, its IRIS acknowledgement number and filing date, and certainty that you will not obtain any property for six months. The form, prescribed by SRO 2097(I)/2026 under rule 81B of the Income Tax Rules 2002, records your name, CNIC or NTN, tax year, return filing date, return acknowledgement number and the declaration. Once submitted, IRIS issues an acknowledgement; its date starts the six-month period under Section 182A(3). Your name should then appear on the ATL after FBR's next update.

Before You Open IRIS: A Five-Point Check

Short answer: You need an IRIS login, a completed Tax Year 2026 return with its acknowledgement number, and certainty that no property comes to you for six months.

Do not start the form until each of these is true. Each one has stopped a client at some point.

You have the return acknowledgement number and filing dateThe form asks for themOpen the filed return or the acknowledgement PDF

Step 1: Get Into IRIS

Short answer: Log in at iris.fbr.gov.pk with your CNIC and password; reset codes go to your registered mobile and email.

Go to iris.fbr.gov.pk and log in with your CNIC and password. If the password is lost, use the reset option; the code goes to the mobile number and email registered with FBR. If you no longer have that number, the registered contact details must be changed first, which FBR handles through its own procedure, so allow time for it.

Step 2: Make Sure the Late Return Is Complete

Short answer: The return and wealth statement must show Completed, not Draft, with any tax paid by PSID.

Open the list of completed tasks and find the return for Tax Year 2026. Three things to verify:

  • The status is Completed, not Draft. A draft is not a filed return.
  • The wealth statement for Tax Year 2026 has also been filed. A return without its wealth statement is an incomplete filing for an individual who must file one.
  • Any tax shown as payable on the return has been paid through a challan (PSID) and the payment reference is entered.

If the return is still pending, file it first. Our salaried return guide and freelancer return guide cover the return itself.

Step 3: Open Form ATL-U

Short answer: Form ATL-U is an electronic IRIS form; if you cannot see it, use the IRIS search or forms area, never a paper copy.

FBR has added Form ATL-U to IRIS as an electronic form verified the same way as other IRIS submissions. Its exact menu location may change as FBR updates the portal, so if you cannot see it, use the IRIS search or the declaration/forms area rather than guessing. Do not use any paper or emailed version: the law requires the undertaking in the prescribed form, and IRIS is where it is furnished.

Step 4: Fill the Fields

Short answer: Enter your name and CNIC exactly as on IRIS, tax year 2026, the return filing date and its acknowledgement number, then read the declaration.

FieldWhat to enterCommon mistake

CNIC / NTNYour 13-digit CNIC (for individuals this is the NTN)Entering a family member's CNIC
Tax year2026 for the return due on 31 October 2026Selecting 2027, the current tax year
Date of filing the returnThe date on the return acknowledgementThe date you started the draft
Return acknowledgement / reference numberFrom the filed returnThe PSID of the tax payment instead
DeclarationRead it fully before confirmingTicking without reading the property clause

The declaration is the heart of the form. It records that you will not purchase, acquire or otherwise obtain ownership or beneficial interest in any property for six months from the date of the undertaking, and that you understand the benefit depends on keeping that promise.

Step 5: Verify and Submit

Short answer: Verify with your PIN or code, re-check the tax year, then submit.

IRIS asks for verification before submission, usually with your PIN or a code sent to your registered mobile and email. Submit only after re-reading the tax year and the return details: once furnished, the undertaking is on the record.

Step 6: Download and Keep the Acknowledgement

Short answer: Download the IRIS acknowledgement; its date starts your six months.

IRIS issues an electronic acknowledgement. Download the PDF and keep it with your return acknowledgement. It is your proof of three things:

  • That the undertaking was furnished, if your ATL status is ever questioned by a bank or registrar.
  • The date the six months began.
  • The date the six months end, which is when you can safely book or buy property again.

Write the end date somewhere you will see it. People forget, and the most common breach we see is innocent: a plot booking in month five.

Step 7: Confirm Your ATL Status

Short answer: Your name appears after FBR's next ATL update; check online or by SMS.

The ATL is updated periodically, not the minute you submit. After the next update, check your status online or by SMS, as explained in our ATL status check guide. If your name does not appear after an update, the usual reasons are:

  • The return is not in Completed status, or the wealth statement is missing.
  • The undertaking was furnished against the wrong tax year.
  • The form was saved but not submitted.

Short answer: Return and Form ATL-U can be done in one to two days; ATL inclusion follows the next update.

File sooner rather than later. Under the current text of Section 182, the late-filing penalty is reduced by 75% if the return is filed within one month after the due date, 50% within two months and 25% within three. For Tax Year 2026, one month after 31 October 2026 is 30 November 2026.

If Form ATL-U Is Not Visible on Your IRIS

Short answer: File the return anyway and check again; pay the surcharge only if you need filer status for a transaction this week.

New forms are sometimes rolled out in stages. If the form is not visible, do not pay the surcharge in a panic unless you need ATL status for a transaction this week. File the return, keep it complete, and check again, or ask us to check the account. If you do need filer status immediately, for example for a property sale next week, paying the surcharge may be the faster route; our comparison guide helps with that call.

The File You Should Keep

Short answer: Keep the return, wealth statement, challans, Form ATL-U acknowledgement and an ATL screenshot together for at least six years.

Keep a single folder, paper or digital, with these items for at least six years:

  • The Tax Year 2026 return acknowledgement and the return itself.
  • The wealth statement and its reconciliation.
  • The PSID challans for tax and any penalty paid.
  • The Form ATL-U acknowledgement, with the six-month end date written on it.
  • A screenshot of your ATL status once your name appears.

If a bank, a housing society or a registrar later questions your filer status for that year, this folder answers them in minutes.

Filing for a Parent or Spouse

Short answer: You can submit for a parent or spouse with their permission, but the six-month promise is theirs, so explain it first.

Many people handle IRIS for an elderly parent or a spouse. That is allowed in practice when you act with their permission, but remember whose undertaking it is. The declaration is made in the taxpayer's name; they are the one promising not to obtain property for six months. Explain the promise to them in plain words before you submit, and make sure no family property transfer into their name is planned. A parent who is about to receive a plot from a sibling's share of an inheritance, for example, may be better served by the surcharge.

After You Submit: The Next Six Months

Short answer: Set reminders for the ATL check and the day after your six months end.

Set two reminders: one for the date FBR usually updates the ATL, to check your status, and one for the day after your six months end. Between those dates, if anyone in the family discusses buying or transferring property to you, pause and check before agreeing to anything. This is the stage where most innocent breaches happen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Form ATL-U on IRIS?
It is an electronic form on IRIS. FBR can move menu items, so if you cannot find it, use the IRIS search or forms area, or ask us to check your account.
Can I submit Form ATL-U before filing my return?
It relates to a return filed after the due date, and it asks for the return's filing date and acknowledgement number. File the return first.
Which tax year do I select?
Tax Year 2026 for the return that was due on 31 October 2026.
Is there a challan or fee on IRIS for Form ATL-U?
No payment is made to FBR for the undertaking. The Rs 25,000 surcharge is what the undertaking replaces.
How long until my name appears on the ATL?
After FBR's next ATL update. Check online or by SMS after the update.
I lost the acknowledgement. Can I download it again?
Yes, completed submissions remain in your IRIS account; download the acknowledgement again and keep a copy.
Can someone else submit Form ATL-U for me?
A tax practitioner can prepare and submit it through your IRIS account with your authorisation. The undertaking remains yours, so read the declaration yourself.
What if I selected the wrong tax year?
Contact us or your tax office promptly. An undertaking against the wrong year does not help with the late Tax Year 2026 return.
Do I need a tax practitioner to submit Form ATL-U?
No. Any individual can furnish it on IRIS. People use a consultant when the late return itself needs preparation, or when IRIS access is a problem.
Can I edit Form ATL-U after submitting?
Treat a submitted undertaking as final. If you entered a wrong detail, contact us or your tax office promptly rather than submitting a second form without advice.
