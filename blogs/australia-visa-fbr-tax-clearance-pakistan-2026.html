🇦🇺 Australia Visa Ke Liye Pakistan FBR Tax Return — PR aur Skilled Migration Guide 2026

Australia Pakistan se emigration ke liye top destinations mein se hai — aur 2026 mein skilled migration points system mein significant changes aaye hain. Subclass 189 (independent), 190 (state nominated), aur 491 (regional) sab SkillSelect system ke zariye process hote hain. In sab ke liye Pakistan mein FBR tax compliance — NTN, returns, wealth statement — financial credibility aur income history prove karta hai. Australia High Commission Islamabad documentation mein koi shortcut nahi chalti.

Skills Assessment aur Points — Pakistan Mein Preparation

SkillSelect EOI mein points declare karte hain — invitation milne ke baad full documentation. Pakistan se common point boosters:

  • Age (30 points): 25-32 years — maximum points
  • English (20 points): IELTS 8.0 each band (Superior)
  • Work Experience Pakistan (5-15 points): 3-8+ years in nominated occupation
  • Australian Work Experience: On-shore experience strong boost (+5-20)
  • Skills Assessment: VETASSESS, Engineers Australia, ACS, AIQS etc. — mandatory
  • Partner English: +5 points if partner also competent English

Pakistan IT professionals ke liye: ACS (Australian Computer Society) skills assessment Pakistan ke software engineers, developers aur IT managers ke liye process karta hai. Pakistan mein FBR returns employment history aur salary levels prove karte hain — ACS ke liye experience letters ke saath FBR returns strong supporting evidence hain. Character requirement: Australia visa character requirement ke liye Penal Clearance Certificate (Pakistan Police clearance) zaruri hai — NADRA se issue hoti hai. Plus health examination from Australian-approved panel physician. Ye process 4–8 weeks le sakta hai — pehle shuru karo.

Pakistan mein FBR Returns — Verification Process

Australia High Commission mein FBR documents submit karte waqt in chezon ka khayal rakho:

  • Original + copy: Original documents Islamabad mein submit karte hain — copies apne pass rakhein
  • Translation: Urdu ya regional language documents ka certified English translation zaruri
  • Consistency: Bank statement mein income must match FBR declared income
  • Gaps explain karo: Gap years ya irregular income explain karne ke liye cover letter helpful
  • Kamboh Associates: FBR ke liye AHC acceptable format mein affidavit/letter bhi provide karta hai

Australia Jane Ke Baad Pakistan Tax

  • Non-resident status: Australia mein 183 din se zyada rahne par Australian tax resident — worldwide income ATO ko declare
  • Pakistan-Australia DTA: Double Taxation Agreement available — Pakistan mein pay ki tax Australia mein credit milti hai
  • Pakistan rental income: 15% flat rate — Pakistan mein non-resident return file karo
  • ATL maintain karo: Pakistan mein property ya assets hain tou ATL (Active Taxpayer List) mein rehna beneficial — property dealings mein lower WHT

Australia High Commission Islamabad: Plot No. 2, Sector G-5/4, Islamabad. Walk-in nahi — appointment VFS Australia Pakistan se book hoti hai. Processing time 189/190: 1-3 years depending on points score. Student visa: 3–8 weeks.

FAQs — Australia Visa FBR

Australia 189 visa ke liye kitne points chahiye 2026 mein?
Minimum 65 points required hai — lekin invitation typically 80-90+ points par milta hai depending on occupation aur current round. Pakistan se common occupations like software engineers, nurses, accountants, civil engineers — ye sab high-demand occupations mein hain aur state nomination (190) bhi available hai jo +5 points deta hai.
ACS assessment ke liye Pakistan FBR returns sufficient hain employment proof ke liye?
ACS (Australian Computer Society) Pakistan mein experience verify karne ke liye employment letters primary document maanta hai — company letterhead, role, duration, duties. FBR returns is ko support karte hain income levels aur tax compliance prove karke. Both together best case banate hain. Kamboh Associates ACS-friendly FBR documentation bhi prepare karta hai.
Student visa GTE (Genuine Temporary Entrant) kya hai?
GTE assessment mein immigration officer consider karta hai ke student study ke baad Pakistan wapas aane ka genuine intention rakhta hai ya nahi. Strong ties to Pakistan — family, property, FBR filed returns, stable employment — ye sab Pakistan ties prove karte hain. FBR wealth statement Pakistan mein financial interests show karta hai jo GTE mein helpful hai.
Partner bhi apply kar raha hai — dono ki returns chahiye?
Agar partner bhi financial sponsor ya co-applicant hai tou haan — partner ke bhi FBR returns, NTN aur bank statements required honge. Student visa mein typically primary applicant ke parents ki returns maangte hain. Skilled visa mein partner ka employment history bhi additional evidence add karta hai.
