Pending FBR Tax Returns — Abroad Jane Se Pehle Backlog Kaise Clear Karein Pakistan 2026

July 15, 2026Aitsaam Ali10 min readVerified 2026

Pakistan mein ek bahut common situation: abroad jaana hai — Italy, Canada, UK — lekin 3, 5, ya kabhi kabhi 7 saal ki FBR returns pending hain. Embassy documents maangti hai, visa apply karna hai, waqt kam hai. Ye guide specifically un logon ke liye hai. Pending returns kya hain, kaise file hongi, penalty kitni aayegi, aur kab tak complete ho sakti hai — sab step-by-step, verified data ke saath.

Ahem: Italy, Spain, Canada, UK sab ki embassies visa application mein last 2–3 saal ke tax returns maangti hain. Non-filer ka visa reject ya delay ho sakta hai. Pending returns clear karna visa se pehle zaruri hai.

Penalty — Kitna Banta Hai Late Filing Par

Bahut se log penalty ke darr se return file nahi karte. Haqiqat ye hai ke zyada tar ordinary salaried logon ki penalty bahut kam hoti hai:

SituationPenalty RulePractical Amount
Taxable income zero tha (below threshold)Rs 1,000 per year minimumRs 1,000 per year — bohot kam
Tax tha lekin pay ho gaya (employer ne deduct kiya)Rs 1,000/day OR 0.1%/week of tax — whichever higherVary karta hai — lekin capped at 50% of tax
Tax tha aur nahi bharaPenalty + 12% default surcharge on unpaid taxSignificant — ASAP file karo

Example: 5 Saal Ki Salaried Employee Ki Filing

Agar aap ki salary Rs 50,000/month thi (Rs 600,000/year — below taxable threshold in most years), toh:

  • Taxable income: zero (below slab)
  • Tax due: zero
  • Penalty per year: Rs 1,000 (minimum)
  • 5 saal ki total penalty: Rs 5,000
  • Ye amount file karne ka total cost — bahut manageable hai

Penalty maafi ka option: FBR Commissioner ko penalty waive karne ka ikhtiyaar hota hai agar aap genuine reason show karein (nahi pata tha filing zaruri hai, employer ne kaha tha wo karta hai, etc.). Kamboh Associates ye application bhi handle karta hai.

IRIS Par Pending Returns File Karne Ka Process

1

IRIS Login — iris.fbr.gov.pk

CNIC number se IRIS account mein login karo. Agar account nahi hai toh pehle register karo — NTN automatically link ho jaata hai CNIC se.

2

Sabse Purane Saal Ka Return Pehle

IRIS mein: Declaration → Return of Income → Tax Year select karo. Sabse pehle oldest pending year select karo (jaise TY 2022). Sequence mein file karna zaroori hai — naya saal purane ke baad.

3

Income Declare Karo

Salary, business income, rental income — jo bhi tha us saal. Zyada tar salaried employees ke liye sirf employer ka name, salary amount aur salary certificate number chahiye hoti hai.

4

Wealth Statement Complete Karo

Us saal ke end (June 30) tak aap ke paas kya tha: property (FBR value), car (model year), bank balance, investments — aur liabilities (loans). Ye return ke saath ek saath submit hota hai.

5

Submit Karo aur Acknowledgment Save Karo

Return submit hone ke baad IRIS ek acknowledgment number deta hai. Ye save karo — embassy documents mein zaruri hota hai. Iske baad agla saal file karo.

6

ATL Check Karo

2–3 working days mein fbr.gov.pk par ATL mein naam aana chahiye. Text karo: 9966 par ACTIVE space CNIC number — FBR confirm karta hai filer status.

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawalat

Kitne saal ki pending returns file kar sakte hain?
FBR 5 saal pehle tak ki assessment open kar sakta hai. Practically TY 2022 onward (July 2021 ke baad ki income) file karna best practice hai. Embassy ke liye last 3 saal kaafi hote hain — TY 2024, 2025, 2026.
Late filing ki penalty kitni hoti hai?
Agar taxable income zero tha (salary below Rs 600,000 zyada tar saalon mein) toh penalty sirf Rs 1,000 per year hai. 5 saal ka total Rs 5,000. Agar tax banta tha toh Rs 1,000/day ya 0.1% per week — whichever higher — maximum 50% of tax due.
Kya mujhe khud IRIS par jaana hoga?
Nahi. Kamboh Associates aap ki taraf se IRIS par sab file karta hai. Aap sirf documents WhatsApp par bhejein — salary certificates, CNIC, property details — baqi ham karte hain. Abroad se bhi possible hai.
5 saal ki filing mein kitna waqt lagega?
Documents ready hone par 5–8 working days. Emergency mein 3–5 working days. Sab se zaroori: salary certificates ya bank statements har saal ke, aur agar property/car hai toh un ki details. WhatsApp karo: 0328-4675162.
Penalty maaf ho sakti hai?
Haan. FBR Commissioner ko penalty waive karne ka ikhtiyaar hota hai agar genuine reason ho. "Mujhe maloom nahi tha ke salaried employees ko bhi file karna hota hai" ek valid reason hai. Kamboh Associates ye application bhi prepare karta hai.
Filing ke baad ATL mein kab naam aata hai?
Return submit hone ke 2–3 working days mein ATL update hota hai. Check karo: fbr.gov.pk/VerifyPerson ya SMS: 9966 par ACTIVE CNIC# bhejo. Embassy ke liye ATL verification print/screenshot lena hota hai.
Abroad se pending returns file ho sakti hain?
Haan. IRIS portal anywhere accessible hai. Ya Kamboh Associates ko POA (Power of Attorney) de sakte hain — ham Pakistan se aap ki taraf se filing complete karte hain. Documents email ya WhatsApp par share karein.
