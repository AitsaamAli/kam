IT Export Tax Exemption 2029 Tak Extend — Budget 2026-27 Update

July 23, 2026Aitsaam Ali7 min readIT Sector

Pakistan ki IT industry ke liye achhi khabar — Budget 2026-27 mein IT aur software export income par milne wali 0.25% concessionary tax rate ko 2029 tak extend kar diya gaya hai. Freelancers, IT companies, aur BPO firms ab teen saal ki clarity ke sath planning kar sakte hain. Ye guide extension ki details, eligibility, aur PSEB registration process cover karta hai.

TL;DR

IT/software export ki 0.25% final tax rate ab 2029 tak confirmed hai. PSEB registration + active filer status zaroori. Non-filer ko concessionary rate nahi milti. Foreign remittance banking channel se aani zaroori hai.

Kya Extend Hua Hai — Budget 2026-27 Announcement

IT aur software export income par pehle se 0.25% concessionary final tax rate available thi, lekin har saal renewal ki uncertainty rehti thi. Budget 2026-27 ke tehat FBR ne is regime ko Tax Year 2029 tak confirm kar diya hai — is se IT companies aur freelancers ko long-term planning ka faida milta hai.

  • Rate: 0.25% final tax on IT/software export proceeds
  • Validity: Tax Year 2029 tak confirmed (3 saal ki clarity)
  • Applicable to: Software development, IT services, BPO, data processing, digital content export
  • Requirement: PSEB registration + active filer status + banking channel remittance

Kis ko fayda: Freelance developers, IT export companies, digital agencies, aur BPO firms — jo foreign clients se USD/EUR/GBP mein payment receive karte hain — sab is extension se directly benefit hote hain.

PSEB Registration — Step by Step

PSEB Portal Par Account Banayein

pseb.org.pk par jaake free registration form fill karein.

NTN aur Business Details Submit Karein

Apna NTN, business description, aur services offered enter karein.

Verification Ka Intezar Karein

PSEB team documents verify karti hai — usually kuch din lagte hain.

Registration Certificate Milega

Approval ke baad certificate milta hai jo FBR filing mein reference ke liye use hota hai.

IT Export Income Return Mein Kaise Declare Karein

Annual return mein "Export of Services" category mein foreign remittance income declare karein — bank SWIFT records se verify hoke. WHT already deducted (0.25%) ko credit ke taur par show karein. Ye final tax hai — isliye is income par annual return mein additional tax nahi lagta chahe total income kitni bhi ho.

Important: Concessionary rate ka fayda sirf active filers ko milta hai. Agar aap non-filer hain, IT export proceeds par normal rate ya higher slab apply ho sakta hai — filer status maintain karna is exemption ka pehla step hai.

Aksar Puchhe Gaye Sawal — IT Export Tax Exemption 2029

IT export tax exemption kab tak extend hui hai?
Budget 2026-27 ke tehat, IT aur software export income par 0.25% concessionary final tax regime Tax Year 2029 tak extend kar diya gaya hai — pehle ye har saal renewal ke uncertainty mein rehta tha, ab businesses ko 3 saal ki clarity mil gayi hai.
0.25% rate kis ko milti hai?
Ye concessionary rate un IT/software exporters ko milti hai jo PSEB (Pakistan Software Export Board) registered hain, active tax filer hain, aur foreign remittance banking channel se receive karte hain. Freelancers, IT companies, aur BPO — sab eligible hain sahi documentation ke sath.
PSEB registration kaise hoti hai?
pseb.org.pk par online free registration hoti hai — NTN aur business description chahiye hota hai. Individual freelancer se lekar IT company tak — sab register ho sakte hain. Registration ke baad hi 0.25% rate ka benefit milta hai.
Agar filer nahi hain toh IT export par kitna tax lagega?
Concessionary 0.25% rate sirf active filers ko milti hai. Non-filer IT exporters ko normal export proceeds rate ya higher slab apply ho sakta hai — is liye filer status maintain karna is exemption ka fayda uthaane ke liye zaroori hai.
