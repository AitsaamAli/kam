Purane Saal Ka Section 7E Notice Aa Gaya — Abolition Ke Baad Kya Karein

September 2026 Kamboh Associates 10 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Tax Consultant | NTN & Compliance Specialist

Agar aapko abhi 2026 mein Section 7E se related koi notice mila hai, to sab se pehle yeh samajh lein: Section 7E ko Federal Court ne May 2026 mein unconstitutional qarar diya, aur Finance Act 2026 ne Tax Year 2027 (1 July 2026) se ise formally khatam kar diya hai. Lekin agar notice purane tax years — TY2026 ya us se pehle — ke liye hai, to us ka jawab dena abhi bhi zaroori hai, kyunke purane saalon ki legal position abhi mukammal saaf nahi hui. Yeh guide un purane notices ke practical jawab ka tareeqa dekhti hai.

TL;DR

Section 7E ab (Tax Year 2027 se) lagu nahi hota — is par humari alag detailed guide maujood hai. Lekin agar aapko TY2026 ya us se pehle ke kisi saal ka 7E-related notice mila hai, to us ka jawab dena zaroori hai, sirf "yeh to abolish ho gaya hai" kehna kaafi nahi. Sab se pehle apni exemption categories check karein (ek property ki chhoot, Rs. 25 million ka threshold), phir FCC ruling ka reference apni application mein zikr karein, aur documents ke sath jawab file karein.

Pehla Farq Samajhna — Naya Saal Vs Purana Saal

Section 7E ka status abhi kaafi confusion ka baais ban raha hai, is liye clarity zaroori hai. Federal Court ne May 2026 mein Section 7E ko unconstitutional declare kiya, aur Finance Act 2026 (Budget 2026-27) ne isay Tax Year 2027 se (yani 1 July 2026 se) formally khatam kar diya. Agar aap poochh rahe hain ke "kya mujhe abhi 7E dena hai," to jawab hai: Tax Year 2027 aur is se aage ke liye nahi.

Lekin yeh guide specifically un logon ke liye hai jinhe purane tax years (TY2026, yani June 2026 tak khatam hone wala saal, ya us se pehle) ke liye 7E-related notice mila hai. Yeh purani situation abolition se pehle ki hai, aur is ki legal position abhi bhi kuch had tak zair-e-bahas hai.

Poori tafseel ke liye: agar aap Section 7E ki abolition, exemptions, aur is ke asar ki mukammal tafseel samajhna chahte hain, hamari Section 7E Abolition Guide dekhein. Yeh guide sirf "purana notice mila to kya karein" wale practical sawal par focus karti hai.

Yeh farq baar-baar zehan mein rakhna zaroori hai, kyunke aksar log dono baaton ko mila kar confuse ho jate hain — "abolition ho gaya" aur "purani liability khatam ho gayi" do alag baatein hain. Pehli baat qanooni tor par sahih hai, doosri abhi tay nahi hui.

Pehle Exemption Categories Check Karein

Notice ka jawab dene se pehle, yeh check karna zaroori hai ke aap us saal Section 7E ki kisi exemption category mein aate the ya nahi — kyunke bohat se cases mein notice ghalat assessment par bhi ban jata hai. Purani Section 7E structure mein kuch exemptions maujood thi, jaise:

Ek property, us ki qeemat ka koi lehaaz na karte hue, exempt thi.
Agar aapki baqi properties ki combined FBR value ek muqarrar threshold se kam thi, to bhi liability nahi bunti thi.
Business ke liye istemal hone wali property (dukaan, daftar, warehouse) bhi exempt thi.

Agar aap in mein se kisi category mein aate hain, to notice ka jawab yeh exemption document karke di ja sakti hai — us soorat mein FCC ruling ka reference dena bhi zaroori nahi hoga, kyunke aap shuru se hi liable nahi thay.

FCC Ruling Ka Reference Apni Application Mein

Agar aap exemption mein nahi aate lekin phir bhi purani liability ka notice mila hai, to aapki application mein Federal Court ki May 2026 ruling ka hawala dena ek ahem hissa hai. Ruling ka mafhoom yeh hai ke Section 7E shuru se hi unconstitutional thi — is buniyad par yeh dalil banti hai ke purani liability bhi qanooni tor par sahih nahi thi.

Khabardar: yeh ek legally complex maidan hai. FBR har case mein is dalil ko automatically qabool nahi karta, aur adalati fasla ka retroactive asar abhi mukammal tay nahi hua. Is liye sirf "ruling aa gayi hai" keh dena kaafi nahi — professional advice ke sath jawab dena zaroori hai.

Jawab Ke Liye Kaunse Documents Chahiye

Notice ki copy aur us mein diya gaya tax year.
Property ki ownership documents aur FBR valuation ki tafseel.
Agar exemption claim kar rahe hain, us ka sabooti record (jaise business-use ka proof, ya baqi properties ki combined value).
Agar pehle se koi partial payment ki hai, us ka record.
FCC ruling ka reference (agar dalil is par based hai).

Agar Koi Purana Notice Nahi Mila Lekin Fikr Hai

Kuch log yeh sawal bhi karte hain ke unhe koi notice nahi mila, lekin unhe khayal hai ke shayad woh purane kisi saal mein 7E ke tehat liable thay aur unhone kabhi kuch nahi kiya. Aisi soorat mein khud se harkat karna — chahe woh apni properties assess karna ho ya professional se ek baar review karwana ho — behtar hai us se ke notice ka intezar karein. Jitna jaldi apna status clear ho jaye, utna behtar.

IRIS Mein Response Ka Process

Notice ka jawab aam tor par IRIS ke through, ya jahan zaroori ho likhit application ke zariye, muqarrar muddat ke andar dena hota hai. Deadline miss karna khud ek alag masla bana sakta hai — chahe aapki asal dalil mazboot ho, waqt par jawab dena zaroori hai taake case age barh sake.

Agar Pehle Se Tax Ada Kar Diya Ho

Agar aap ne pehle Section 7E tax purane saalon ke liye ada kar diya tha, aur ab aapko lagta hai ke FCC ruling ke tehat aap refund ke liye qabil hain, to yeh ek alag application hai — sirf notice ka jawab nahi. Refund ka process apna waqt le sakta hai aur FBR ki taraf se ek official notification ya guidance abhi comprehensively nahi aayi, is liye is rasta ko realistically, professional advice ke sath, aage barhana chahiye.

Ek Worked Example

Farz karein ek sahab ko TY2025 ke Section 7E ke tehat ek notice mila hai, jis mein unse ek dusri property (jo unke naam ek plot ke tor par hai) par deemed income tax mangi gayi hai. Pehla qadam yeh hoga ke unki baqi properties ki combined FBR value dekhi jaye — agar woh threshold se kam nikalti hai, to woh shuru se hi exempt thay aur wohi dalil notice ka jawab banegi, bina FCC ruling ka sahara liye. Agar woh threshold se zyada hain, tab FCC ruling ka reference application mein shamil kiya jayega, sath mein property ke documents aur us saal ki wealth statement.

Common Ghaltiyan

"7E abolish ho gaya hai" keh kar notice ignore karna: abolition sirf Tax Year 2027 se aage ke liye hai, purane saal ka notice abhi bhi jawab talab karta hai.
Exemption check kiye baghair FCC ruling par bharosa karna: pehle apni exemption category dekh len, shayad aap shuru se hi liable nahi thay.
Deadline miss karna: mazboot dalil hone ke bawajood waqt par jawab na dena masla bana sakta hai.
Refund ki umeed mein kuch bhi jawab na dena: notice ka jawab aur refund application dono alag kaam hain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Section 7E to abolish ho gaya hai, phir notice kyun aaya?
Abolition Tax Year 2027 (1 July 2026) se lagu hai. Agar notice purane saal — TY2026 ya us se pehle — ke liye hai, to woh purani liability ki baat kar raha hai, jis ka jawab dena abhi bhi zaroori hai.
Kya main FCC ruling ki bunyad par notice ignore kar sakta hoon?
Nahi, ignore karna risky hai. Ruling ek mazboot legal dalil deti hai lekin FBR har case mein isay automatically qabool nahi karta. Jawab professional advice ke sath, waqt par dena chahiye.
Mere paas sirf ek ghar hai — kya main pehle se hi 7E se exempt tha?
Bohat mumkin hai. Section 7E ki purani structure mein ek property, uski qeemat ka lehaaz na karte hue, exempt thi. Yeh check karna sab se pehla qadam hona chahiye.
Maine pehle 7E tax ada kar diya tha — kya refund mil sakta hai?
FCC ruling ke tehat refund ka legal basis maujood hai, lekin FBR ki taraf se comprehensive guidance abhi nahi aayi. Yeh ek alag application hai jo professional advice ke sath aage barhani chahiye.
Notice ka jawab kitni der mein dena zaroori hai?
Notice mein hi muqarrar muddat likhi hoti hai. Deadline miss karna alag masla bana sakta hai, chahe aapki dalil kitni bhi mazboot ho — waqt par jawab dena zaroori hai.
Meri baqi properties ki combined value threshold ke qareeb hai — kya karoon?
Yeh borderline case hai jahan exact FBR valuation ki tafseel se dekhna zaroori hai. Property ke documents aur valuation table dono le kar professional se confirm karayein, khud andaza na lagayein.
Kya business-use property par 7E ka notice sahi hai?
Purani Section 7E structure mein business ke liye istemal hone wali property (dukaan, daftar, warehouse) exempt thi. Agar notice aisi property par hai, us ka business-use hone ka sabooth dena notice ka seedha jawab ban sakta hai.
Kya "abolition ho gaya hai" aur "purani liability khatam ho gayi" ek hi baat hai?
Bilkul nahi. Abolition ka matlab hai Tax Year 2027 se aage 7E lagu nahi hoga — yeh qanooni tor par tay hai. Lekin purane saalon ki liability ka masla alag hai aur abhi mukammal saaf nahi hua, is liye purana notice phir bhi jawab talab karta hai.
Notice ka jawab khud submit kar sakte hain ya professional se hi karana zaroori hai?
Technical tor par khud submit karna mumkin hai, lekin yeh legally sensitive maidan hai — exemption assessment aur FCC ruling ki sahih application dono mein ghalti ka khatra hota hai. Professional advice yahan asal faida-mand hai.
Kya sab property owners ko yeh check karna chahiye chahe unhe koi notice na mila ho?
Agar aapko lagta hai aap purane saalon mein 7E ke daire mein aa sakte the aur koi notice bhi nahi mila, ek proactive review karwana behtar hai — is se pehle ke masla khud ba khud khara ho.
