What's Actually Getting Registered in Islamabad

Islamabad's registration activity splits fairly cleanly into three streams: IT and software export startups planning to register with PSEB once incorporated, consultancy and advisory firms often built around securing government or institutional contracts, and branch or subsidiary entities of foreign companies establishing a Pakistani presence through the Board of Investment. Each stream has a slightly different priority at the registration stage — an IT startup cares about getting to PSEB registration quickly, a government-facing consultancy cares about an MOA broad enough to cover future contract types, and a foreign subsidiary cares about getting the BoI and SECP paperwork properly sequenced.

Kamboh Associates handles SECP registration for all three, sequencing the process around whichever comes next for your specific business.

The SECP Registration Process

Registration runs entirely through SECP's eServices portal — no physical office visit is required in Islamabad or any other city.

  1. Name reservation: Proposed company name checked and reserved with SECP
  2. Digital signature setup: Required for directors to sign incorporation documents electronically
  3. Memorandum & Articles of Association: Drafted to match your actual and reasonably anticipated business activity
  4. Incorporation filing: Submitted to SECP for review
  5. Certificate of Incorporation issued: Your company legally exists from this point
  6. Post-incorporation setup: NTN registration, PSEB registration where applicable, and bank account documentation

IT & Software Startups — Registering for PSEB After Incorporation

Once an Islamabad IT company is SECP-registered, most founders' next priority is PSEB (Pakistan Software Export Board) registration, since this unlocks reduced withholding tax on export proceeds and access to PSEB's various support programs.

Common registration considerations: PSEB registration requires the company's MOA to clearly state software/IT export as a core business activity — an MOA drafted too generically can create friction at the PSEB application stage even after SECP has already approved it. Founders need to decide company structure (SMC versus multi-shareholder Pvt Ltd) with an eye on future investment plans, since most institutional investors expect a standard shareholding structure rather than a single-member company. Kamboh Associates sequences SECP incorporation and PSEB registration together so the company doesn't lose weeks between the two.

Consultancies Built Around Government & Institutional Clients

A distinct category of Islamabad-registered companies exists primarily to contract with federal ministries, autonomous bodies, and international development organizations headquartered or represented in the capital.

Common registration considerations: The MOA needs to cover the breadth of consulting, advisory, and project-implementation activity the company might realistically pursue across different government tenders, since amending it later to add a new activity type can delay a bid submission. Companies bidding on procurement often need their registration, NTN, and ATL status all in order well before any specific tender deadline, so Kamboh Associates recommends founders in this category register earlier than they might otherwise think necessary.

Foreign Subsidiaries via Board of Investment

Islamabad is where most foreign companies process initial Board of Investment registration before establishing a branch, liaison, or subsidiary presence in Pakistan.

Common registration considerations: A subsidiary (locally incorporated, foreign-owned) goes through standard SECP registration with foreign-shareholder documentation requirements, while a branch office requires separate BoI permission with a narrower, specifically defined scope of permitted activity. Getting the sequencing right — BoI approval before or alongside SECP filing, depending on structure — avoids delays that catch first-time foreign investors off guard. Kamboh Associates coordinates both processes for foreign-owned Islamabad entities.

Freelancer Collectives Formalizing Into Agencies

A specific Islamabad pattern involves loose groups of independent freelancers — developers, designers, writers — who've been working together informally on shared client projects and eventually decide to formalize as a registered agency, usually once client contract sizes grow beyond what makes sense to handle as unrelated individual freelancers invoicing separately.

Common registration considerations: Deciding who becomes a formal shareholder-director versus who remains a contracted freelancer working for the new company is a decision worth making deliberately rather than defaulting to "everyone who was involved originally," since it has lasting governance and tax implications. The new company's contracts with international clients need to be reissued under the company's name rather than individual freelancers' names, which sometimes requires renegotiating payment terms with existing clients. Kamboh Associates has guided several Islamabad freelancer groups through exactly this transition, including the sensitive equity-split conversations that come with it.

Registered Office Address — What Islamabad Companies Actually Need

Every SECP-registered company needs a documented registered office address in Pakistan, which trips up some Islamabad founders working from co-working spaces, home offices, or shared startup hubs who assume they need a formal standalone office before they can even register.

Common registration considerations: A home address or a co-working space with proper documentation (lease agreement or letter of permission) generally works fine as a registered office for SECP purposes — there's no requirement for dedicated commercial real estate at the registration stage. Companies that later move offices need to formally update their registered address with SECP, a simple filing that's easy to forget amid an office move but creates compliance gaps if left undone. Kamboh Associates advises on what address documentation SECP will actually accept before submission, saving founders from assuming they need more than they do.
