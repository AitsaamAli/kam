Filing Your Income Tax Return in Rawalpindi

Rawalpindi's economy is anchored by Raja Bazaar's wholesale trade, Saddar's retail and services sector, and growing residential investment in Bahria Town and DHA. Each segment faces different tax filing requirements, and we tailor our approach accordingly.

Who Must File in Rawalpindi

  • Salaried employees above the taxable income threshold
  • Wholesale and retail traders in Raja Bazaar, Moti Bazaar, Commercial Market
  • Property owners in Bahria Town and DHA Rawalpindi
  • Service providers and small business owners across Saddar and Satellite Town

Filing Process for Traders

StepDetail
1. Records CollectionSales records, purchase invoices, bank statements
2. Income ComputationDetermine net taxable income after allowable expenses

For Raja Bazaar & Commercial Market traders: Many shopkeepers remain non-filers and pay higher withholding tax on banking transactions without realizing it. Filing brings immediate savings on WHT rates and avoids FBR enforcement action under the Tajir Dost scheme.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who needs to file an income tax return in Rawalpindi?
Any individual with taxable income above the exemption threshold, all registered businesses, traders in Raja Bazaar and Commercial Market, and property owners in Bahria Town or DHA must file annual returns.
Can Raja Bazaar traders file tax returns without visiting an office?
Yes, we handle tax filing for traders and shopkeepers across Raja Bazaar, Moti Bazaar, and Commercial Market entirely online — you just send your sales records via WhatsApp.
What happens if a Rawalpindi trader doesn't file taxes?
Non-filers face higher withholding tax rates on banking transactions, cannot register vehicles or property easily, and risk FBR audit notices along with penalties for non-compliance.
