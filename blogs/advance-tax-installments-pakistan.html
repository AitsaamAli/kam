Advance Tax Installments Pakistan 2026 — Section 147 Payment Guide

June 24, 2026Aitsaam Ali6 min readCompanies & AOPs

Advance tax ka matlab hai apni estimated annual tax liability saal khatam hone ka intezar kiye bagair, quarterly installments mein pehle se pay karna. Section 147 ke tehat companies aur bara turnover wale individuals/AOPs is ke liable hain. Ye guide due dates, calculation method, aur payment process poori tarah cover karta hai.

TL;DR

Companies har quarter (Sep 25, Dec 25, Mar 25, Jun 15) apni tax liability ka 25% advance pay karti hain. Individuals/AOPs bhi threshold cross karne par yehi schedule follow karte hain. Underpayment par 12% annual markup surcharge lagta hai.

Advance Tax Kya Hai — Section 147 Overview

Section 147 ke tehat, ek estimated tax liability wale taxpayer ko poore saal ka tax ek sath year-end par pay karne ke bajaye, quarterly installments mein advance dena hota hai. Ye FBR ke liye cash flow better banata hai aur taxpayer ke liye bhi bara lump-sum payment se bachata hai.

  • Companies: Sab companies advance tax dene ki paband hain
  • Individuals/AOPs: Sirf tab jab pichlay assessment mein tax liability threshold (generally Rs 1,00,000+) cross ki ho
  • Calculation basis: Last assessed tax ya current year ka estimate, jo zyada ho

Aksar Puchhe Gaye Sawal — Advance Tax Installments

Advance tax installments kya hain Section 147 ke tehat?
Section 147 ke tehat companies aur bara turnover wale individuals/AOPs ko apna estimated annual tax quarterly installments mein advance pay karna hota hai — poore saal ka intezar nahi karna padta. Har installment last assessed tax ya current year estimate ka 25% hoti hai, jo zyada ho.
Advance tax installments ki due dates kya hain 2026 mein?
Companies ke liye: 25 September, 25 December, 25 March, aur 15 June. Individuals/AOPs jinka advance tax liability threshold cross karti hai unke liye bhi yehi quarterly schedule follow hoti hai.
Advance tax kaun pay karta hai — sab businesses ya sirf bari companies?
Sab companies advance tax pay karti hain. Individuals aur AOPs sirf tab pay karte hain jab unki tax liability ek certain threshold se zyada ho (generally jab pichlay assessment mein tax payable Rs 1,00,000 se zyada raha ho).
Advance tax kam pay karne par kya penalty hai?
Agar advance tax underpaid ho (actual liability se kam), FBR default surcharge lagata hai — approximately 12% annual markup rate shortfall amount par, jab tak balance clear na ho jaye.
