Medical Housing Society

Housing societies set up for doctors in Lahore have a familiar membership pattern: many members trained at Lahore's medical colleges and now work in the NHS, Ireland, the Gulf or North America. They pay plot instalments from abroad, sometimes build a house for parents, and plan to return one day. Their Pakistani tax position is simple once residency is clear.

Wider area: Tax Consultant in Lahore

Short answer

Where the Medical Housing Society society office records a transfer, it collects 236C from the seller and 236K from the buyer. Medical Housing Society filers see their FBR office in IRIS (Lahore's RTO is on Nabha Road); Medical Housing Society services register with PRA free. Non-resident doctors

A doctor working abroad and spending fewer than 183 days a year in Pakistan is generally non-resident. Foreign salary isn't taxed in Pakistan. What remains:

  • the plot or house in Lahore (an asset to declare if you file);
  • rent, if the house is let;
  • profit on Pakistani bank accounts;
  • any gain if you sell.

Paying instalments from abroad

Instalments paid from foreign earnings through banks are the cleanest source of funds. Keep the society's payment receipts and the bank remittance records. If a parent pays instalments on your behalf from money you send, record it that way.

Building a house for parents

Doctors often build on the plot for parents to live in. The house is your asset; construction cost is added year by year. If parents live rent-free, there's no rental income. See recording construction.

Selling from abroad

Selling through a power of attorney is common. Advance tax under 236C applies at your ATL status. See selling through attorney. Proceeds can generally be repatriated through banks with documents.

Coming back

Doctors returning to Pakistan — to teaching hospitals or private practice — become residents again. Plan the first year back, including foreign savings and pensions you keep abroad. See returning residents.

UK and Irish tax authorities may ask about overseas property income for residents there. Rent from your Lahore house can be relevant in both countries; keep records that serve both.

Membership dues and development charges

Medical and professional housing societies collect development charges, membership fees and periodic dues. Development and possession charges paid towards the plot are part of its cost; annual maintenance dues for an empty plot are a personal expense. Ask the society for an annual statement of what you've paid — doctors abroad often lose track of which payments were instalments and which were dues.

Plots in a spouse's name

Doctor couples working abroad often buy plots in both names, or one in each. Each spouse's share is their asset, funded by their own earnings or a gift from the other. If only one spouse earns abroad, record the other's plot as a gift.

Medical Housing Society — questions we get asked

I work in the NHS. Is my UK salary taxed in Pakistan?
Not if you're non-resident — generally fewer than 183 days in Pakistan in the tax year and not meeting the multi-year test.
Should I file in Pakistan for my Lahore plot?
It's usually worthwhile: lower advance tax as an ATL filer and a clean record of the plot's source.
My parents live in the house I built. Do I declare rent?
If they live rent-free, there's no rental income. The house is still your asset.
Can I sell my plot without coming to Lahore?
Yes, through a power of attorney. Advance tax applies at your ATL status.
