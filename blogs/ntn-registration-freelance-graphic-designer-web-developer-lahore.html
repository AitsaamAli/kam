NTN Registration for Freelance Graphic Designers & Web Developers in Lahore

September 2026 Kamboh Associates 11 min read
By Aitsaam Ali, Tax Consultant | NTN & Compliance Specialist

Lahore's freelance design and development community — working through Upwork, Fiverr, direct international contracts, or a mix — shares much of the general freelancer filing picture, but design and development work specifically often qualifies for IT-export benefits worth understanding clearly rather than missing entirely.

TL;DR

Freelance graphic designers and web developers register NTN as a business individual (Rs. 2,000) and file annual returns (Rs. 5,000), with foreign-currency income correctly declared as business income. Web development, and often design work supporting software/digital products, may qualify for PSEB registration and reduced export withholding tax — worth checking specifically rather than assuming. The Typical Income Pattern for This Profession

Freelance designers and developers in Lahore typically earn through a mix of platform work (Upwork, Fiverr), direct client retainers, and project-based contracts — often with foreign clients paying in USD or other foreign currency via Payoneer, wire transfer, or platform payout. This income needs to be declared as business income, correctly converted and reconciled against what actually lands in a Pakistani bank account.

PSEB Eligibility — Often Overlooked for Design Work Specifically

Web developers providing IT-enabled services clearly qualify for PSEB registration consideration, given reduced withholding tax on export proceeds for eligible IT/software export income. Graphic designers sometimes assume this doesn't apply to them since design isn't "coding" — but design work supporting software products, UI/UX design for digital platforms, and similar IT-adjacent creative work can also qualify depending on the specific service description and client relationship. This is worth a direct check rather than an assumption either way, since the potential tax saving compounds every year the eligibility goes unclaimed.

A Note on Mixed Project Types

Many designers and developers work across a mix of project types in a given year — a web development contract, a logo design project, ongoing platform maintenance work — and this mix doesn't need to be separated into different registration categories; it's all consolidated as business/freelance income under one NTN and return, with PSEB eligibility assessed based on which specific portions qualify.

Equipment and Software Subscription Costs

Designers and developers typically have legitimate business expenses — a capable computer, design/development software subscriptions, cloud hosting or tool costs — that offset gross income when correctly accounted for. Keeping records of these expenses, even informally, supports a more accurate net income calculation than declaring gross platform earnings without any expense offset.

Income From a Personal Portfolio Site or Passive Sources

Some designers and developers also earn smaller amounts from passive sources alongside active freelance work — ad revenue from a portfolio blog, occasional template or asset sales through marketplaces like ThemeForest or Creative Market. This needs to be declared alongside primary freelance income, not treated as separate or too minor to mention, since FBR's assessment is based on total income regardless of source.

A Worked Example: A UI Designer Discovering PSEB Eligibility

A UI/UX designer in Lahore worked exclusively on interface design for software products through direct contracts with two international SaaS companies — genuinely design-focused work, not writing code, which led her to assume PSEB registration only applied to developers. Once her specific service was actually assessed — designing interfaces specifically for software products, delivered digitally to foreign clients — it qualified under the IT-enabled services framework the same way development work does. Registering meant a real reduction in withholding tax on her export proceeds going forward, a saving that would have continued being missed indefinitely had she not specifically checked rather than assumed her work "wasn't really IT" just because it was creative rather than technical.

Documents Needed

  • CNIC
  • Platform earning statements (Upwork, Fiverr, direct client invoices)
  • Payoneer/bank statements showing actual receipts
  • PSEB registration certificate, if already registered
