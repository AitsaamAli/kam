Gulshan Park, Lahore — Freelance Translators, Content Writers and Editors

Updated October 2026

Gulshan Park

Writing and translation are among the most common home-based freelance careers in Lahore: Urdu–English translation for courts, embassies and NGOs, content writing for websites, academic editing, subtitling. Clients range from local law firms to agencies in the UK and US, so income arrives through several channels.

Foreign work

Writing and translation for foreign clients, received through banking channels, may be treated as an export of services, depending on how the work is set up and received. Keep invoices and bank certificates. See freelancers.

Local work and withholding

Local businesses paying for services may withhold tax. Collect certificates.

Certified translation

Translators providing certified translations for embassies and courts may work through an agency or independently. Attestation fees collected and paid to authorities on clients' behalf are pass-through.

Costs

  • Laptop, software (translation tools, grammar checkers).
  • Internet.
  • Dictionaries and subscriptions.
  • Platform fees.

Records

A simple job log — client, words or pages, rate, amount, payment route — is enough to file accurately.

Writers often have dozens of small clients. A monthly summary of receipts by channel — bank, wallet, platform, cash — makes the return simple.

Payments through freelancing platforms

Platforms deduct service fees before payout. Your income is the full amount the client paid; the platform fee is a cost. Download annual platform statements — they match your bank withdrawals to client work.

Rates in foreign currency

Foreign clients pay in dollars or pounds; record the rupee amount actually credited to your bank.

Agencies and subcontracting

Translators often pass overflow work to other translators and keep a margin. Payments to subcontractors are costs; your income is what clients pay you. If you're a prescribed person, withholding may apply to payments to subcontractors.

Academic editing for students

Editing theses and papers for students locally and abroad is common. Keep it on the right side of academic integrity — and record it as professional income like any other work.

Equipment and software

Laptops, translation memory tools and subscriptions are costs or assets. Keep receipts.

Gulshan Park — questions we get asked

Is income from foreign translation clients taxable?
Yes, for residents. It may qualify as an export of services if received through banks.
Local clients deduct tax from my fees. How do I claim it?
Collect their withholding certificates and claim them in your return.
Are attestation fees I collect my income?
No, if you pay them to authorities on clients' behalf; they're pass-through.
What records do I need?
A job log with client, volume, rate, amount and payment route.
