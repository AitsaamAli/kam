Italy Shift Hone Ke Baad — Aapki Pakistan Tax Filing, NTN Aur FBR Status (2026)

28 August 2026Aitsaam Ali11 min read2026 Verified

Italy jaane wale zyada tar Pakistani workers ka irada shuru mein "kuch saal kaam kar ke wapas aana" hota hai. Lekin haqeeqat mein bara hissa wahin reh jata hai — permesso di soggiorno renew hota rehta hai, kaam jam jata hai, aur phir ek din khandaan bhi bula liya jata hai. Yeh guide us tabdeeli ke Pakistani pehlu ke baare mein hai: kya aap ke peeche chhota hai, kya chalta rehta hai, aur kya band ho jata hai.

TL;DR:

  • Italy pohanchne ke 8 din ke andar permesso di soggiorno ki darkhwast deni hoti hai — yeh visa se alag cheez hai.
  • Italy shift hone se aapka NTN khatam nahi hota. Woh ek permanent registration hai, koi subscription nahi.
  • Jis tax year mein aap gaye, us saal ki Pakistani income par return ki zimmedari barqarar rehti hai — deadline 30 September.
  • Aapki Italian salary Pakistan mein chargeable nahi hoti, lekin Pakistan mein maujood kiraya, bank profit aur property gain chargeable rehte hain.
  • Permanently ja rahe hain to Section 8 tax clearance certificate (Rs. 5,000) aapke record par ek saaf exit line kheench deta hai.

Permit se settlement tak ka safar

Italy ka system marhala-war chalta hai, aur har marhale par aapka Pakistan se taluq thora aur badalta hai. Sab se pehla qadam Italy pohanchne ke 8 din ke andar permesso di soggiorno ki darkhwast dena hai. Yeh residence permit visa se alag cheez hai — visa sirf aap ko mulk mein daakhil hone deta hai; qanooni tor par rehne aur kaam karne ke liye permesso chahiye.

Iske baad permit renew hota rehta hai, aur waqt guzarne ke sath long-term residence aur aage ke rastay khulte hain. Pakistani workers ke liye yeh safar aksar chup chaap hota hai — koi ek din aisa nahi aata jab aap keh sakein "aaj main muhajir ban gaya." Bas saal guzarte jate hain.

Tax ke lehaz se yeh chup-chaap tabdeeli hi masla hai. Kyunki koi ek waqia aisa nahi hota jo aapko yaad dilaye ke Pakistan mein aapka record abhi zinda hai. Aur jab teen chaar saal baad zaroorat parti hai, tab tak backlog ban chuka hota hai.

NTN ka kya hota hai

Yeh sab se zyada poocha jane wala sawal hai, aur jawab aam tor par log ki tawaqqo ke ulta hota hai.

NTN Italy shift hone se khatam nahi hota. Yeh ek registration number hai — koi subscription nahi jo expire ho, aur koi cheez nahi jise "band" karna zaroori ho. Bohat se log poochte hain ke Pakistan chhorne se pehle NTN cancel karwa dein. Jawab tagreeban hamesha nahi hai, kyunki Pakistan mein aap jo bhi transaction karenge — property, bank, virasat, kisi bhi qism ka transfer — us mein wohi NTN dobara chahiye hoga.

Aur agar aapka NTN hai hi nahi to? To woh banwa lena behtar hai, chahe aap ja rahe hon. Kamboh Associates par NTN registration Rs. 2,000 hai aur yeh ek dafa ka kaam hai. Jo log yeh soch kar chhor dete hain ke "ab kya faida," unhein aksar chaar paanch saal baad Italy mein baith kar yehi kaam karwana parta hai — aur us waqt zyada mushkil hota hai kyunki documents aur signatures Pakistan mein chahiye hote hain.

Jaane wale saal ki return — sab se zyada miss hone wali cheez

Pakistan ka tax year 1 July se 30 June tak chalta hai. Italy ki joining dates is se koi taluq nahi rakhtin — aap kisi bhi mahine ja sakte hain. Iska matlab yeh hai ke aap tagreeban hamesha tax year ke beech mein ja rahe hote hain.

Farz karein aap ne Pakistan mein July se January tak kaam kiya aur February mein Italy chale gaye. Us tax year mein aapki saat mahine ki Pakistani income maujood thi. Us par return ki zimmedari khatam nahi hoti, aur uski deadline agli 30 September hai — jab aap Italy mein permesso, ghar, aur naye kaam mein masroof honge.

Yehi wo return hai jo sab se zyada chhoot jati hai. Aur uska nuqsan foran nazar nahi aata — woh saalon baad zahir hota hai, jab aap ATL se bahar ho chuke hote hain aur koi property ka kaam karna hota hai.

Ek amali mashwara: Italy jaane se pehle hi kisi ko yeh zimma de dein ke agli September mein aapki return file honi hai. Yeh remotely ho jati hai — na aapko wapas aana parta hai, na kisi attestation ki zaroorat hoti hai. Sirf yaad rehna chahiye.

Kya cheez chargeable rehti hai

Yeh sab se ahem tafseel hai, aur is par saaf hona zaroori hai.

Yani agar aapke naam Gujrat ya Mandi Bahauddin mein koi dukan kiraye par hai, to us ka kiraya Pakistan mein chargeable hai chahe aap Milan mein rehte hon. Aur agar us par return file nahi hoti, to yeh ek aisa masla ban jata hai jo waqt ke sath barhta hai aur aam tor par tab saamne aata hai jab aap woh jaidad bechna chahte hain.

Yeh jaan lena bhi zaroori hai ke aapka non-resident hona kis bunyad par tay hota hai — Pakistan mein yeh faisla 182-day rule par hota hai aur har saal alag se dekha jata hai. Seasonal workers ke liye yeh hisaab khaas tor par pecheeda hota hai; us par humne alag guide likhi hai.

Section 8 tax clearance — kab banwana chahiye

Section 8 tax clearance certificate wo document hai jo yeh record karta hai ke aap Pakistan chhorte waqt tax ke lehaz se clear thay. Italian embassy is ka mutalba nahi karti — yeh Italian requirement hai hi nahi, aur koi bhi jo yeh kehta hai ke "Italy visa ke liye clearance certificate chahiye" theek nahi keh raha.

Phir kis ke liye? Do soorton mein yeh waqai faida deta hai:

Agar aap permanently ja rahe hain. Yeh aapke Pakistani tax file par ek saaf line kheench deta hai — is tareekh tak sab clear tha. Baad mein koi sawal uthe to aapke paas reference point maujood hai.
Agar aapke naam Pakistan mein jaidad hai. Saalon baad us property ko bechte ya transfer karte waqt ek documented exit record kaam aata hai.

Kamboh Associates par Section 8 clearance certificate ki fee Rs. 5,000 hai. Agar aap do saal ka contract le kar ja rahe hain aur wapas aane ka pakka irada hai, to iski itni zaroorat nahi — yeh permanent departure ka document hai.

Peeche chhore huye khandaan aur jaidad

Yahan Italy ke Pakistani migrants ka ek makhsoos pattern hai jo dusre mulkon mein itna aam nahi. Kyunki yeh labour migration hai, tanha aadmi jata hai aur khandaan Pakistan mein rehta hai — kam az kam shuru ke saalon mein. Aur is arse mein aksar jaidad ka intezam ghair-rasmi ho jata hai.

Aam soorat yeh hoti hai: bhai ya walid aapke naam ki zameen dekhte hain, kiraya wasool karte hain, kabhi kabhi aapke naam par kuch khareed bhi lete hain. Sab ghar ka mamla hai, koi likhat parhat nahi.

Masla tab uthta hai jab kuch badalta hai — koi jaidad bikti hai, virasat taqseem hoti hai, ya bhaiyon mein ikhtilaf hota hai. Us waqt yeh sawal ahem ho jata hai ke kagaz par kya likha hai. Jo log Italy mein rehte hue apni filing aur wealth statement chalate rehte hain, unke paas har saal ka ek documented record hota hai ke un ke naam kya tha. Jo nahi chalate, unke paas sirf yaad-dasht hoti hai.

Wealth statement is silsile mein sab se kaam ki cheez hai — Rs. 2,500 mein yeh aapke assets ka saalana snapshot bana deti hai. Yeh sirf FBR ke liye nahi, aapke apne khandani record ke liye bhi qeemti hai.

Family ko bulane ka marhala

Jab aap khandaan ko Italy bulate hain to Pakistan-side ki tasveer dobara badalti hai. Ab aapki biwi aur bachche bhi bahar hain, aur Pakistan mein sirf jaidad reh jati hai.

Is marhale par do cheezein karni chahiyen. Pehla, yeh tay karein ke Pakistan ki jaidad kis ke naam rehni hai aur usay kaun dekh raha hai — aur agar koi doosra shakhs aapki taraf se kaam kar raha hai to uske liye baaqaida authority hona chahiye, zubani intezam nahi. Dusra, apni filing chalate rahein, kyunki ab aapka Pakistan se rabta sirf jaidad ka reh gaya hai aur wohi cheez tax ke lehaz se sab se hassas hai.

Yeh woh nuqta hai jahan zyada tar log filing band kar dete hain — "ab to poora khandaan yahan hai." Aur yehi wo nuqta hai jahan filing chalate rehne ka faida sab se zyada hota hai, kyunki ab aapka Pakistan mein wajood sirf kagaz par hai.

Italy Ke Baad Pakistan Tax — Aam Sawalat

Italy shift hone ke baad kya mera NTN cancel karwana chahiye?
Nahi. NTN ek permanent registration number hai, koi subscription nahi jo expire ho ya jise band karna zaroori ho. Italy shift hone se woh khud khatam nahi hota, aur usay cancel karwana aam tor par ghalat faisla hai — kyunki Pakistan mein koi bhi property, bank ya virasat ka transaction karte waqt wohi NTN dobara darkar hoga.
Italy mein rehte hue kya mujhe Pakistan mein return file karni paregi?
Yeh is par depend karta hai ke Pakistan mein aapki koi chargeable income hai ya nahi. Italy ki salary Pakistan mein chargeable nahi hoti. Lekin agar aapke naam koi kiraye ki jaidad hai, bank account hai jis par profit aata hai, ya kisi business mein hissa hai, to filing ki zimmedari barqarar rehti hai. Return Italy se remotely file ho jati hai.
Kya Italy visa ke liye Section 8 tax clearance certificate chahiye?
Nahi. Italian authorities is ka mutalba nahi kartin — yeh Italian requirement hai hi nahi. Yeh Pakistan-side ka document hai jo permanent departure par mufeed hota hai, kyunki aapke tax record par ek saaf exit line ban jati hai jo baad mein property transactions mein kaam aati hai. Iski fee Rs. 5,000 hai.
Jis saal main Italy gaya, us saal ki return file karni hai?
Ji haan, agar us tax year mein Pakistan mein aapki income thi. Pakistan ka tax year 1 July se 30 June hai aur zyada tar log beech saal mein jate hain, is liye us saal ki Pakistani income par return ki zimmedari barqarar rehti hai. Deadline agli 30 September hai. Yeh wohi return hai jo sab se zyada chhoot jati hai, kyunki us waqt aap Italy mein settle ho rahe hote hain.
Meri jaidad Pakistan mein bhai dekh rahe hain — kya masla hai?
Ghar ka intezam apni jagah, lekin masla tab uthta hai jab kuch badalta hai — jaidad bikti hai, virasat taqseem hoti hai, ya ikhtilaf hota hai. Us waqt yeh dekha jata hai ke kagaz par kya darj hai. Jo log Italy mein rehte hue saalana return aur wealth statement chalate rehte hain, unke paas har saal ka documented record hota hai ke un ke naam kya tha. Kisi doosre shakhs ke aapki taraf se kaam karne ke liye baaqaida authority honi chahiye, zubani intezam kaafi nahi.
