Rental Income Tax Pakistan 2026 — Makan Ka Kiraya Kitna Tax | Section 15 Complete Guide

July 17, 2026Aitsaam AliLandlord Tax ExpertFBR 2026-27

Pakistan mein lakho landlords hain — DHA, Bahria Town, Gulberg, Model Town, Johar Town — har jagah se kiraya aa raha hai. Lekin bahut kam log jaante hain ke rental income par FBR Section 15 tax lagta hai — aur agar return file nahi ki toh FBR Section 111 notice, penalties, aur filer benefits ka loss. Rental Income Deductions — Tax Kaise Bachein

FBR Section 15(3) ke under landlords ko kuch deductions milti hain jo taxable income reduce karti hain:

1. Standard 1/5 (20%) Deduction — Without Proof

Sabse asaan aur popular deduction — gross rent ka 20% automatic deduce ho jaata hai bina kisi receipt ya proof ke. Ye maintenance, repair, depreciation, aur property management expenses ke liye hai:

  • Rs 1 lakh/month rent → Rs 12 lakh annual → 20% = Rs 2.4 lakh deduction → taxable: Rs 9.6 lakh
  • Koi bills ya receipts ki zarurat nahi — automatic hai

2. Actual Expense Deductions (20% ki jagah)

Agar actual expenses 20% se zyada hain — actual prove kar ke ye deductions le sakte ho (20% standard ki jagah not in addition):

  • Insurance premium: Property insurance — actual premium deductible
  • Legal fees: Rent collection ke liye advocate fees
  • Municipal taxes: Property tax paid to local government
  • Ground rent: Agar aap bhi kisi se zameen rent par lete ho

20% ya actual expenses — dono mein se SIRF EK choose karo per property. 20% flat zyada convenient hai aur most landlords ke liye kafi hai. Actual mein zyada bills, documentation, aur audit risk aata hai.

Multiple Properties — Lahore Landlords Guide

Bahut landlords ke paas 2-5+ properties hoti hain. In ke liye:

  • Combined income: Saari properties ka rent ek total mein — aggregate pe slab
  • Har property alag declare: Annual return mein address, tenant naam, rent amount — har property
  • Wealth statement: Saari properties ki value wealth statement mein declare karo
  • Vacancy: Khali property par zero rental income — lekin wealth statement mein property show karo
  • Self-occupied: Apne use ki property — deemed rental income nahi (Pakistan mein)

Example — 3 Properties Lahore: DHA (Rs 80K/month) + Johar Town (Rs 40K/month) + Bahria Town (Rs 50K/month) = Rs 1.7 lakh/month = Rs 20.4 lakh annual. Tax on Rs 20.4L (after 20% deduction = Rs 16.32L): Rs 1,95,000 + 25% of excess = Rs 2,28,000 annual. Monthly equivalent: Rs 19,000.

Rental Income Declare Na Karne Ka Nateeja

FBR ke paas rental income track karne ke kai tarike hain:

  • Tenant business return: Business tenant kiraya declare karta hai — FBR ko automatically pata chal jaata hai
  • Stamp paper data: Rent agreement stamp paper par hoti hai — Nazimabad records available hain
  • Utility bills: LESCO/SNGPL consumer data — property address se landlord ka pata milta hai
  • NADRA: Property owner identification — connected to FBR

Rental income declare nahi ki aur FBR ko pata chal gaya — Section 111 (unexplained income), Section 114 (return na filing notice), aur penalties. Proactively return file karna sabse safe option hai.

FAQs — Rental Income Tax Pakistan

Pakistan mein kiraye ki amdani par kitna tax lagta hai 2026?
Section 15 ke under: Rs 3 lakh annual tak zero. Rs 3–6 lakh: 5% on above Rs 3L. Rs 6–24 lakh: Rs 15,000 + 10%. Rs 24 lakh+: Rs 1,95,000 + 25%. Pehle 20% standard deduction karo — effective tax rate gross rent se kam hota hai. Rs 1 lakh/month rent par actual tax: Rs 51,000 annual (after 20% deduction).
Rental income return file na karein to kya hoga?
FBR Section 114 notice aa sakta hai (return file karo). Agar income high hai aur track ho gaya — Section 111 unexplained income notice. Penalties: late return Rs 1,000 per month + potential 35% income tax + 10% surcharge. Commercial tenant already 15% kaat raha hai — return file na karo toh ye refund bhi miss ho jaata hai. Return file karna zaroori aur faydamand dono hai.
Agar ghar khali hai aur koi kiraya nahi aa raha — tax hoga?
Khali property par zero rental income — rental tax zero. Lekin property wealth statement mein declare zaroori hai agar aap property owner hain. Annual return mein property address aur current status (self-occupied/vacant/rented) show karo. Khali ho toh rental income column zero.
Salary bhi hai aur kiraya bhi — dono ka tax kaise hoga?
Salary aur rental income dono separately calculate hoti hain — alag alag slabs. Salary Section 149 ke under, rental Section 15 ke under. Annual return mein dono declare karte hain — total tax dono ka combine nahi hota balke each has separate treatment. Kamboh Associates multiple income sources wale landlords ka return properly file karta hai.
Commercial shop ka kiraya Rs 2 lakh/month — tenant company kaatta hai — mujhe aur kya karna hai?
Tenant company Rs 2 lakh se 15% = Rs 30,000 monthly kaat rahi hai — annual Rs 3.6 lakh. Annual rental income: Rs 24 lakh. Actual tax on Rs 24 lakh (after 20% = Rs 19.2 lakh): Rs 1,47,000. Tenant ne Rs 3.6 lakh kaata — refund: Rs 2.13 lakh. Return file karo — filer bano — refund claim karo. Non-filer ho toh Rs 3.6 lakh final tax, refund zero, actual liability kuch bhi ho.
