Brandreth Road

Brandreth Road is known for machinery, bearings, motors, pumps, tools and industrial hardware — the parts that keep Lahore's factories running. Unlike most bazaar traders, Brandreth Road businesses sell largely to other businesses: factories, contractors and workshops that pay by bank, ask for proper invoices and deduct tax at source.

CustomerWhat they expect

Factories and companies are usually withholding agents and deduct income tax on payments for supplies, issuing certificates. Many also require you to be registered for sales tax so they can claim input tax. Collect every certificate; over a year, these deductions can cover much of your tax.

Imported machinery and parts

Bearings, motors and many machine parts are imported. Buy from importers with invoices, or import yourself through proper channels. If you import, customs duty, sales tax and advance income tax are paid at the port; keep each shipment's file. See small importers.

Sales tax: input and output

As a registered supplier, you charge sales tax on sales and adjust the sales tax you paid on purchases and imports. Monthly returns reconcile the two. Factories will check that your invoices appear in FBR's system against their purchases — errors on your side affect their input claims.

Stock and slow movers

Bearings and parts come in many specifications. Track stock by part number and keep slow-moving items at cost until sold.

Repairs and rewinding

Some Brandreth Road businesses rewind motors, repair pumps or machine parts. That's a service, possibly within PRA's sales tax on services depending on turnover. Keep service jobs separate from goods sales.

Receivables and payment delays

Factories often pay 30–90 days after delivery. Receivables at 30 June are assets. Disputed or unpaid bills written off are business losses if documented.

Business customers judge suppliers by their paperwork. A trader who issues correct sales tax invoices and supports factories' input claims keeps customers that cash-only traders lose.

Tenders and government supplies

Some Brandreth Road traders supply government departments, WASA, railways or public-sector factories through tenders. Tender supplies need NTN, sales tax registration and often a filer certificate at bidding. Payments come through government accounts with income tax and sometimes sales tax withheld at source. Keep the tender documents, supply orders, delivery challans and payment advices together for each contract — they're also what an auditor will ask to see.

Family firms on old premises

Many Brandreth Road businesses run from shops held by the same family for decades, sometimes on old tenancies. If the shop is owned by one family member and used by the business, record rent between them — or document that no rent is paid — so each return tells the same story.

Brandreth Road — questions we get asked

Factories deduct tax from my payments. Is that my final tax?
It's advance tax credited in your return. Collect every certificate and file to settle your position.
Why do factories insist on my sales tax registration?
They need your invoices to claim input tax. Unregistered suppliers' invoices don't allow that.
I also rewind motors. Is that taxed differently?
Yes. It's a service, possibly within PRA's sales tax on services. Keep it separate from goods sales.
How should I record imported bearings?
With the importer's invoice, or your own shipment file if you import, including customs documents.
