Why "Same Day" Claims Need an Honest Breakdown

Nearly every tax consultant in Lahore advertises fast service, and the word "urgent" appears on enough business profiles that it's stopped meaning much. The actual truth is that some services genuinely complete the same day because they only depend on the consultant's own speed, while others depend partly or entirely on a government body's own processing timeline that no amount of consultant urgency can shorten. Kamboh Associates states this distinction plainly rather than promising universal same-day service and quietly missing it.

This page exists specifically to set accurate expectations — what's genuinely fast, what realistically isn't, and why — for Lahore clients facing a real deadline.

What Genuinely Completes Same-Day

These services depend almost entirely on document availability and consultant processing speed, not on a separate government body's own timeline, which is why same-day delivery is a realistic, consistently achievable promise.

Salaried income tax return: Once your salary certificate and CNIC are shared, filing through FBR IRIS and receiving your acknowledgment receipt genuinely happens the same day for the large majority of straightforward cases. NTN registration: A first-time NTN application, submitted with correct documentation, typically processes within the same day to 24 hours. Wealth statement (straightforward cases): For a client with a small number of clearly documented assets, same-day preparation and filing is realistic.

What Cannot Genuinely Be Same-Day, and Why

Being honest about this category is exactly what separates a realistic consultant from one making a promise they know they can't consistently keep.

SECP company registration: This depends on SECP's own processing timeline for name reservation and incorporation review, which typically runs 7-10 days regardless of how quickly the consultant submits documentation on their end. Anyone promising SECP registration in 24 hours is either misunderstanding the process or setting an expectation they can't reliably meet. Sales tax registration (STRN): FBR's own verification process for STRN typically takes 2-3 days minimum. FBR notice response: While a response can be drafted quickly, the notice itself typically carries a 15-30 day statutory deadline, and rushing a response without properly reviewing the underlying issue can create a worse outcome than a carefully prepared response delivered within a few days.

Common Urgent Scenarios and Realistic Timelines

Understanding the difference above matters most when a specific real-world deadline is driving the urgency, so here's how it plays out for situations that come up regularly.

Bank requiring an NTN this week for account opening: Genuinely achievable — NTN registration completes within 24 hours, comfortably within a week's notice. Visa application requiring proof of active filer status by a specific date: Achievable if there's at least a few days' notice, since it requires an actual return filed and processed onto the Active Taxpayer List, not just a same-day promise. A property transaction requiring active filer status to avoid higher withholding, closing this week: Realistic if the underlying return can be filed same-day and ATL status updates within the following few days — worth starting immediately rather than waiting until the closing date is imminent.

The One Variable That Actually Determines Your Speed

Beyond which service you need, the single biggest factor in whether your case genuinely completes same-day is how quickly you can put your own documents together — a consultant's speed only matters once the underlying paperwork is actually in hand.

What to have ready before reaching out for a salaried return: a clear photo of your CNIC, your latest salary certificate or salary slip, and your bank account details for any refund. For NTN registration: CNIC and, if applicable, a business bank statement or basic business details. For a wealth statement: a simple list of what you own — properties with rough size and location, vehicles, bank balances — doesn't need to be perfectly formatted, just complete. Clients who gather this before their first message routinely see same-day completion; clients who start the conversation without any documents in hand add real delay searching for paperwork mid-process, regardless of how quickly Kamboh Associates itself moves.

Can SECP Registration Be Sped Up at All?

While the full 7-10 day SECP timeline can't be collapsed into 24 hours, there are legitimate ways to avoid the most common delays that push a registration well past even that realistic window.

What actually causes SECP delays beyond the standard timeline: A proposed company name that conflicts with an existing registered name, requiring resubmission and losing several days. Incomplete director or shareholder documentation submitted upfront, triggering a query-and-response cycle with SECP that adds time. A Memorandum of Association drafted too narrowly or with unclear business activity, sometimes flagged for clarification. Kamboh Associates runs a name-availability check and reviews all documentation for completeness before submission specifically to avoid these common delay triggers, which is the realistic way to keep a Lahore company registration within the standard 7-10 day window rather than falling behind it — not by promising to beat SECP's own processing time.
