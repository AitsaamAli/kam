Near Mayo Hospital, Lahore — House Officers, Postgraduate Trainees and Young Doctors

Near Mayo Hospital

Mayo Hospital and King Edward Medical University, by Anarkali, train thousands of young doctors. House officers and postgraduate trainees earn stipends or salaries, pick up locum shifts at private hospitals, and many plan exams for the UK, US, Ireland or the Gulf. Few think about tax — until a visa or licensing application asks for proof of income and filing.

Why filing matters early

  • Visa and foreign licensing applications sometimes ask for evidence of income, tax status or source of funds for exam and visa fees.
  • Being on the Active Taxpayer List lowers withholding on bank profit and vehicles.
  • A filing history established early makes later wealth — a car, a plot — easy to explain.

Locum income

Locum shifts at private hospitals are often paid in cash or by transfer, sometimes with tax withheld. Record each shift's payment. Collect certificates where tax was deducted.

Exam fees and costs

PLAB, USMLE, IELTS/OET and membership exams cost a lot and are paid in foreign currency. These are personal expenses (not deductible against salary), but large foreign payments should be funded from declared income or documented family support. Keep receipts.

Going abroad

When you move for training abroad, your residency changes. Your final resident-year return should show your Pakistani assets, and your foreign salary afterwards generally isn't taxed in Pakistan while you're non-resident. See moving abroad for work and doctors abroad owning Lahore property.

Get your annual salary certificate from the hospital accounts office each July. It's the one document every later application will rely on.

Family support

Parents often fund exam fees, hostel costs or a car. Those are gifts — record them in both wealth statements if you file.

Your first car

Young doctors often buy a car with family help or a bank lease. The car goes in your wealth statement at cost, with a gift or loan as its source — and being on the ATL lowers the advance tax at registration.

Private clinics while training

Some trainees assist at private clinics in the evening for a fixed monthly payment. That's usually salary from the clinic or professional income, depending on the arrangement. Ask for a payment record at year end.

Medical college fees and loans

If you took a loan for medical education — from a bank or family — the outstanding amount is a liability in your wealth statement, and repayments come from your income.

Near Mayo Hospital — questions we get asked

Do house officers need to file tax returns?
If your income is taxable, yes. Even if not, filing helps with visas and keeps you on the Active Taxpayer List.
Is locum income taxable?
Yes. It's professional income, separate from your salary. Collect certificates where tax was deducted.
Can I claim PLAB or USMLE fees?
They're personal expenses, not deductible against salary, but should be funded from declared income or documented support.
My parents pay my exam fees. Is that income?
No. It's a gift from your parents.
