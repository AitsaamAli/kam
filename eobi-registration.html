FAQ

EOBI FAQ

Is EOBI registration mandatory?
Yes. Under the EOBI Act 1976, every employer with 5 or more workers must register with EOBI within 30 days of reaching 5 employees. Non-compliance attracts prosecution and arrears with 10% annual surcharge.
What is the EOBI contribution amount?
Employer contributes 5% of minimum wage (Rs. 37,000 from July 2025) = Rs. 1,850 per employee per month. Employee contributes 1% = Rs. 370 per month. Total Rs. 2,220 per employee deposited with EOBI monthly.
What benefits do employees get from EOBI?
EOBI provides old-age pension, invalidity pension, survivors' pension, and old-age grant. Employees become entitled after 15 years of contribution and reaching 60 years of age (55 for women).
What is the penalty for not registering with EOBI?
Under the Employees' Old-Age Benefits Act 1976, failure to register an establishment carries a fine of up to Rs. 50,000 and/or 6 months imprisonment for the employer. Non-payment of monthly contributions attracts additional penalty surcharge. Labour inspectors can conduct surprise visits and issue show-cause notices to unregistered employers.
Who is an "insured person" under EOBI?
Any employee in an EOBI-covered establishment earning wages up to Rs. 50,000 per month is an insured person. Employees earning above this threshold are still required to contribute but their contribution is capped. Casual workers, contract workers, and part-time employees in covered establishments may also be required to register depending on the nature of their employment.
Can I register EOBI online in Pakistan?
Yes. EOBI registration is done online through the EOBI portal (eobi.gov.pk). Employers must submit a Form E-1 (Employer Registration Form) along with CNIC, NTN, business registration certificate, and a list of employees. EOBI Explained

EOBI Contribution Rates & Monthly Calculation

EOBI contributions are calculated as a percentage of the Minimum Wage, not the employee's actual salary. This keeps contributions predictable regardless of salary increases.

5%
Employer Contribution

Employer pays 5% of minimum wage per employee per month. For 2026, minimum wage is Rs. 37,000 — so contribution is Rs. 1,850 per employee.

1%
Employee Contribution

Employee contributes 1% of minimum wage, deducted from salary. For 2026 minimum wage: Rs. 370 per employee per month.

15th
Monthly Deadline

Total EOBI contribution (employer + employee share) must be deposited by the 15th of the following month via EOBI portal or designated banks.

Who is covered under EOBI?

All private sector establishments with 5 or more employees are required to register with EOBI. This includes factories, shops, commercial establishments, hotels, educational institutions, hospitals, and construction companies. The threshold was reduced from 10 employees to 5 employees — making EOBI applicable to a much wider range of small businesses. If your business has 5 or more employees on payroll, registration is mandatory, not optional.

EOBI vs PESSI vs SESSI — Which Applies to You?

EOBI is a federal scheme covering all provinces. However, Punjab (PESSI), Sindh (SESSI), and KPK (KPWA) also have their own provincial social security schemes which provide medical and related benefits. Employers with operations in Punjab must register with both EOBI (for pension) and PESSI (for medical coverage). Kamboh Associates manages registrations across both EOBI and the relevant provincial social security authority depending on your business location.

Labour Law Context

EOBI in Pakistan Labour Law

EOBI is one of several mandatory labour obligations. Understanding how it fits with other laws helps you build a fully compliant payroll from day one.

EOBI Federal Pension Scheme

Mandatory for all establishments with five or more employees. Monthly contribution is five percent from employer plus one percent from employee, calculated on minimum wage. Covers old-age pension, invalidity pension, survivors pension, and old-age grant for retiring workers.

PESSI and SESSI Provincial Medical Security

Punjab Employees Social Security Institution (PESSI) and Sindh Employees Social Security Institution (SESSI) provide medical and maternity coverage for industrial workers in Punjab and Sindh respectively. Employers with Punjab or Sindh operations must register with both EOBI and the relevant provincial authority. Contribution is typically six percent of wages up to a provincial cap.

Gratuity Rights After Five Years

Employees who complete five years of continuous service are entitled to thirty days wages per year of service as gratuity on termination or retirement. This is a statutory right under the West Pakistan Industrial and Commercial Employment Ordinance 1968, separate from and in addition to any EOBI benefits.
