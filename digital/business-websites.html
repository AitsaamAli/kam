Service — Business Websites

A Business Website That Actually Does Its Job

Most small businesses don’t need a thousand features. They need a site that loads fast, looks credible, explains what you do, and makes it effortless for a customer to get in touch. That’s the foundation we build every time.

WhatsApp 0328-4675162 See Pricing

What’s Actually Included

  • 5–10 pages: home, about, services, a contact page with a working form, and whatever else your business genuinely needs — not padded out with filler pages
  • Mobile-first design — the majority of your visitors will be on a phone, and the site is built for that reality first
  • Fast loading — optimised images, clean code, no bloated page-builder plugins slowing everything down
  • A content management system you can actually use — update your own text and images without calling us for every small change
  • Basic on-page SEO — proper titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, so search engines can read your site correctly from day one
  • A working contact form, click-to-call and click-to-WhatsApp buttons — every path to reaching you is tested before launch

Who This Is For

Trading and import/export businesses that need a credible online presence for partners and buyers to check. Restaurants and cafes that need a clean site with a menu and clear hours. Trades and service businesses — contractors, logistics firms, repair shops — that need to look established and be easy to contact. Professional practices — consultants, agents, small firms — that need a site that builds trust before the first phone call.

Built to a Real Standard, Not Just a Looks Standard

Where it's relevant to your market, we build with accessibility guidelines (WCAG 2.1 AA) and proper privacy/cookie consent structure in mind as standard, not as a paid add-on. We'll tell you plainly if your specific situation doesn't carry a particular legal requirement, rather than charging you for compliance work you don't need.

Our Design Approach

We don't start from a generic template and swap in your logo. We start from what your specific business needs a visitor to understand first — which differs for a trading company, a restaurant, and a consultant — and build the page structure around that.

Realistic Timeline

A standard 5–10 page business website takes 10–14 working days from the point we have your content (text, logo, any images) in hand. Projects that need custom copywriting or photography take longer — we'll give you an honest timeline once we know exactly what's needed.

If You Don’t Have Content Ready

Most small business owners don't walk in with polished website copy ready to hand over. We work from a short set of questions (what do you do, who's it for, what makes you different) and turn your plain-spoken answers into proper site copy, which you review and adjust.

After Launch

We don't disappear once the site is live. You get a short walkthrough of how to update your own content, our direct WhatsApp line for questions, and the option of a simple paid maintenance arrangement if you'd rather not touch it yourself. There's no obligation — the site is yours outright, hosted in your own name.

Speed and Search Basics, Not Just Looks

A beautiful site that loads slowly loses visitors before they ever see the design. We optimise images, avoid bloated plugin stacks, and test real-world load times on mobile connections, not just on a fast office WiFi during development. Basic SEO — correct titles, meta descriptions, a sensible heading structure — is built in from the start, not bolted on as an afterthought once the site is already live.

How Revisions Work

Every project includes a set number of revision rounds, agreed upfront in the scope — typically two full rounds of feedback and changes before final delivery. This isn't meant to be restrictive; most projects don't need more than that once you've seen a working draft and can react to something real rather than a concept. If a project genuinely needs more back-and-forth than usual, we'll say so honestly rather than let scope quietly expand unpriced.

Domain Names and Business Email

If you don't already have a domain name, we'll help you register one in your own name — never in ours, so you're never dependent on us to keep control of your own web address. We can also set up professional business email addresses (you@yourbusiness.com rather than a free Gmail account) as part of the same process, which matters more for credibility than most business owners realise until a client mentions it.

What Makes a Project Go Smoothly

The fastest, smoothest projects are the ones where a client can describe, even roughly, two or three competitor or reference sites they like the feel of — not to copy, but to give us a starting point for conversation. The slowest are the ones where "surprise me" is the entire brief, which usually means more rounds of revision later as preferences surface that weren't stated upfront. A little direction at the start saves real time for both sides.

Pages We Build Most Often

Beyond the core home/about/services/contact structure, the pages that come up most often are a team or "meet the owner" page (surprisingly effective for trust, especially for a service business), an FAQ page that genuinely reduces repetitive customer questions, a simple blog or news section for businesses that want to post updates, and a dedicated page per major service for businesses offering more than one distinct service — each one written specifically, not as a copy-pasted template with a swapped heading.

A Brief, Honest Note on the Technology Underneath

We build on well-established, widely supported platforms rather than anything exotic or proprietary to us — so if you ever want to move your site elsewhere, or hire a different developer down the line, you genuinely can, without being trapped in a system only we understand. That's a deliberate choice, not an accident.

If You Don’t Have Content Ready

Most small business owners don't walk in with polished website copy ready to hand over — that's normal, not a problem. We work from a short set of questions (what do you do, who's it for, what makes you different, what should someone do after reading this page) and turn your plain-spoken answers into proper site copy, which you then review and adjust rather than write from a blank page yourself.

More Than One Language

If your business serves customers in more than one language — common for businesses with a Pakistani or South Asian customer base alongside a local one — we can build a site with a language switcher, provided you can supply or approve the translated content. We don't rely on automatic translation tools for anything client-facing; a machine-translated page reads as careless, and we'd rather do it properly or flag honestly that it needs a human translator.

Comparing Us to Other Options

You should feel free to get a second and third quote before deciding — we'd encourage it. Compare not just the headline price but what's actually included: is mobile testing real or assumed, is basic SEO included or an upsell, is the revision policy clear or vague, is the payment structure a deposit-and-milestones arrangement or full payment upfront. Those details matter more than the first number you see.

One Final Thought on Choosing a Developer

The question worth asking any web developer, including us, isn't just "how much" — it's "what happens if something goes wrong halfway through." Our answer: a written agreement that covers exactly that, agreed before any money changes hands, not figured out after a problem appears.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a business website take?
10–14 working days once we have your content, for a standard 5–10 page site. Projects needing custom copywriting take longer, confirmed upfront.
Do I own the website once it’s built?
Yes. You own the domain, the hosting account (set up in your name) and the website itself. We don’t hold your site hostage behind an ongoing contract.
Can I update the site myself after launch?
Yes — we build on a content management system and walk you through how to edit text and images yourself.
Do you write the content, or do I need to provide it?
Either works. Send us rough text and we’ll refine it, or answer a short set of questions and we’ll write the first draft for your review.
Is hosting included?
We help you set up hosting in your own name and can recommend reliable, affordably priced providers.
