Why This Page Exists

Ireland's own EU Digital Decade reporting shows a real gap: large Irish enterprises have adopted digital tools and AI at roughly three times the rate of small businesses. That's not because Irish SME owners don't see the value — it's because the agency market serving them splits sharply between boutique Dublin studios built for enterprise budgets, and a thinner tier of smaller operators where pricing and quality are harder to judge from outside. We built this page specifically for the business owner caught in that gap.

Being Honest About the Dublin Agency Market

Dublin has genuinely strong digital agencies — some of Ireland's best-reviewed studios build beautiful, award-winning work for hospitals, government bodies and national brands. That's exactly the issue for a small business: those studios are built, priced and staffed for that kind of client, not for a local trades business or a small trading company. We're not claiming to out-design Dublin's enterprise-tier agencies. We're offering something different: the same compliance standard, delivered at a price and a pace that actually fits a small business.

GDPR and Accessibility, Built In — Not Sold as an Upgrade

Ireland's accessibility requirements, flowing from EU standards including the Accessibility Act that took effect in mid-2025, and GDPR's standard EU/EEA requirements, aren't optional extras on an Irish-facing website — they're close to table stakes. We build every Ireland-facing site to WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility guidelines and proper GDPR-compliant cookie consent and privacy policy structure as part of the standard build, not as a paid add-on you have to know to ask for.

If You’re Eligible for Digitalisation Funding, We’ll Tell You

Ireland's European Digital Innovation Hub programme is in a funded second phase through 2029, aimed specifically at scaling SME digitalisation, alongside a national AI and digital literacy campaign for small businesses. If your business might be eligible for support toward a website project, we'll flag it honestly as part of our conversation with you — we're not a funding consultancy, but we'll point you toward the right programme rather than staying silent because it's not directly in our interest to mention.
