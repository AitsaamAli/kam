How to Reach Us

WhatsApp is our main channel, for a simple reason: it's the fastest way for most of our clients to reach us, and the fastest way for us to send back drafts, screenshots and quick questions during a project. Message 0328-4675162 and describe what you need in a few sentences — what kind of site, roughly what it's for, and which market you're based in.

What to Include in Your First Message

  • What you need: a new business website, an online store, a custom tool, or a redesign of something you already have
  • A rough idea of your business and what the site needs to do
  • Which market you're based in (Ireland, Canada, UAE or elsewhere)
  • Any timeline pressure — if you need it by a specific date, tell us up front

You don't need a fully worked-out brief. A few honest sentences is enough for us to ask the right follow-up questions.

What Happens After You Message Us

We reply, usually within one business day, with clarifying questions if we need them, or directly with a fixed quote and timeline if your request is straightforward. There's no obligation at this stage — getting a quote costs you nothing and commits you to nothing. If you want to proceed, we send a short written agreement covering scope, price and payment milestones before anything else happens.

When We’re Available

Our team works Pakistan Standard Time, which overlaps substantially with the working day in each of our core markets — roughly the second half of the working day in Ireland and the UAE's full working day given the one-hour time difference, and the early morning through midday in Canada. We check messages outside these windows too; WhatsApp means you're rarely waiting long for a reply regardless of exact time zone overlap.

A Few Things Worth Knowing Before You Message

We're a new division of an established firm, not a twenty-year-old web agency — we said so plainly on our homepage and we'll say it here too. We publish our prices rather than negotiating blind. What We’re Not the Right Fit For

Large enterprise or government-scale projects with complex procurement requirements — that's genuinely better served by an established enterprise agency with the track record those processes require. Extremely rushed turnarounds measured in days rather than weeks, where corners would have to be cut to meet the date. If either describes your project, we'd rather tell you honestly than take it on and underdeliver.

What “Within a Business Day” Actually Means

If you message us Monday morning Irish time, expect a reply by Tuesday at the latest, often same-day. Messages sent over a weekend are picked up the next business day. We'd rather commit to a realistic response window we consistently meet than promise instant replies we can't actually sustain once we're handling several projects at once.

If You Already Have a Website

Tell us the current site's address when you message us. We'll take a genuine look — what's working, what's actually costing you customers — before recommending whether a full rebuild makes sense or whether targeted fixes would serve you better and cost less. A rebuild isn't always the right answer, and we'd rather say so than sell you one unnecessarily.

Languages We Work In

We communicate in English by default across all three markets, and can work in Urdu where that's more comfortable for a client with ties to Pakistan. For market-specific language needs — French for Quebec-specific projects, for instance — tell us upfront and we'll confirm honestly whether we can deliver that directly or would involve a translator in the process.

An Example of a Good First Message

Something like: "Hi, I run a small accounting practice in Cork with about 200 clients. I need a new website — current one is old and doesn't work well on mobile. Looking for maybe 6 pages, nothing fancy, just professional and easy to update myself." That's genuinely enough for us to come back with real, specific questions and a fixed quote — you don't need more detail than that to start.

What Happens to the Information You Send Us

Whatever you share with us while discussing or building your project — business details, content, sometimes customer data for testing — is used only for your project and not shared or reused elsewhere. This is the same standard Kamboh Associates applies to client financial documents, carried over to this division as a matter of course, not as a special promise made only here.
