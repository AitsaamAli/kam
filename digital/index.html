What Actually Makes a Small-Business Website Work

It's rarely the flashiest design. In practice, three things decide whether a small business website actually earns its keep: it loads fast enough that a mobile visitor doesn't bounce before it finishes rendering, it answers the visitor's first question (what do you do, where are you, how do I reach you) within one scroll, and it's easy enough to update that it doesn't go stale six months after launch because updating it required calling a developer. We build toward those three things first, and treat visual polish as something that should serve them, not compete with them.
