Ecommerce SEO in Pakistan: Getting Category, Product and Collection Pages Found

Published 30 October 2026
Updated 30 October 2026

Ecommerce SEO in Pakistan starts with category pages that match how people search, such as "lawn suits" or "men's kurta", then unique product pages with real details and photos. Control filter URLs so Google does not index thousands of near-duplicates, add product structured data, and write for the 3 ways Pakistanis search: English, Urdu and Roman Urdu.

Key Takeaways

  • Category and collection pages usually win more searches than individual products.
  • Plan categories around real search phrases, not just your internal stock groups.
  • Filters and sorting can create thousands of duplicate URLs; control them with canonicals and sensible indexing.
  • Product structured data must match what is on the page, including price and availability.
  • Many shoppers type Roman Urdu or mixed phrases; reflect those naturally where they fit.

Where Should Ecommerce SEO in Pakistan Start?

Most Pakistani store owners think about SEO product by product. In practice, the pages that bring the most search visitors are usually category and collection pages: "unstitched lawn suits", "kids school shoes", "solar inverters 5kW". Searchers start broad, then narrow down. Ecommerce SEO in Pakistan therefore starts with a sensible category structure, then strong product pages, then technical control so search engines index the right pages and ignore the rest.

How Should You Structure Categories?

Build categories from the phrases customers actually use. Look at the search terms in Google Search Console, the words customers use on WhatsApp, and Google's search suggestions. A clothing store might use: Women, then Unstitched, Stitched, Lawn, Khaddar, Formal; and collections for seasons and occasions such as Eid. Each category page should have:

  • A clear H1 matching the main phrase, such as "Unstitched Lawn Suits".
  • A short, useful introduction: fabric types, price range, sizing or delivery notes.
  • Products that genuinely belong there, and links to sub-categories.
  • Its own title tag and meta description.

How Do Filters Create Duplicate URLs?

Filters for colour, size, price and brand, plus sorting options, can generate a separate URL for every combination. A store with 20 categories and a dozen filters can end up with tens of thousands of URLs showing almost the same products. Google may waste crawling on these and struggle to pick the right page.

SituationUsual handling
Sort order (price low to high)Canonical to the main category; keep out of the index
Single filters with real search demand (e.g. "black abaya")Consider a dedicated, indexable collection page with its own text
Multiple combined filtersCanonical to the parent category; avoid linking them in ways that invite crawling
Product in several categoriesOne product URL, linked from each category
Colour or size variantsOne product page with options, unless variants are searched separately

Google's documentation on consolidating duplicate URLs explains canonical tags in detail. Platforms such as Shopify and WooCommerce handle some of this automatically, but themes and apps can undo it, so check.

What Makes a Product Page Rank?

Unique descriptions with real specifications, several real photos, size charts, delivery and exchange information, and genuine reviews. Avoid copying supplier text that appears on dozens of other stores. Our guide to AI product descriptions covers writing at scale without thin pages.

Should You Add Product Structured Data?

Yes. Product structured data tells search engines the name, image, price, currency, availability and reviews in a format they read reliably, and can make listings eligible for richer results. It must match what customers see: if the page shows Rs 4,500 and in stock, the markup must say the same. Our schema markup guide covers the basics.

How Do Pakistanis Search for Products?

Shoppers search in English ("lawn suit 3 piece"), Urdu script, and Roman Urdu ("lawn ka suit", "bachon ke joote"), often mixed. You do not need separate pages for every spelling. Use the main English phrase in headings, and include common Roman Urdu terms naturally in introductions, FAQs or image text where they read well. If you have a full Urdu audience, a proper Urdu version of key pages is better than scattered phrases.

Which Technical Basics Matter Most?

  • Fast mobile pages, especially image-heavy category pages.
  • Out-of-stock products kept live with a clear status if they will return; redirected if permanently gone.
  • A clean XML sitemap of indexable pages only.
  • No important content hidden behind scripts that search engines cannot render.

What About Customer Data and Reviews?

Reviews help SEO and trust, but show only a first name or initials, never phone numbers or addresses. Ask customers before publishing photos they send of themselves wearing your products.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where should ecommerce SEO in Pakistan start?
Start with category and collection pages built around the phrases customers actually search, such as lawn suits or school shoes. Then improve product pages with unique details and photos, and control filter URLs. Category pages usually bring more search visitors than individual product pages.
Do filters on my store hurt SEO?
They can. Filters and sorting can create thousands of near-duplicate URLs. Use canonical tags pointing to the main category, keep sort and multi-filter pages out of the index, and create dedicated collection pages only for filter combinations people genuinely search for, with their own text.
Should I write product descriptions in Urdu or Roman Urdu?
Use clear English for headings and specifications, and include common Roman Urdu phrases naturally where they read well, such as in introductions or FAQs. If many customers prefer Urdu, a proper Urdu version of key pages works better than scattered keywords. Avoid stuffing spelling variations.
Does product structured data help a Pakistani store?
It helps search engines read the name, price, currency, availability and reviews reliably, and can make listings eligible for richer results. The markup must match what customers see on the page. Mismatched prices or stock status can cause problems in search results.
What should I do with out-of-stock products?
If the product will return, keep the page live with a clear out-of-stock message and perhaps a notify option. If it is permanently discontinued, redirect the URL to the closest category or replacement product, so visitors and search engines are not sent to an empty page.
Is Shopify good for SEO in Pakistan?
Shopify handles many technical basics well, including canonical tags and sitemaps. Results still depend on category structure, unique content, speed and how themes and apps are configured. Check that apps do not add duplicate pages or slow down category pages before you rely on them.
Online store ko Google par upar kaise laayen?
Pehle category pages ko un alfaaz ke mutabiq banayein jo customer search karte hain, jaise lawn suits. Har product ki apni asli tafseel aur tasveerein dein, supplier ka text copy na karein. Filters se banne wale duplicate pages ko control karein, aur website mobile par tez rakhein.
