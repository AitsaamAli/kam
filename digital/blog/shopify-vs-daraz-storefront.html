Shopify vs Daraz for Pakistani Sellers: Running Your Own Store and a Daraz Shop Together

Kamboh Associates Digital team
Updated 30 October 2026
6 min read
Lahore, Pakistan
Short answer

Shopify vs Daraz is rarely either-or. Daraz brings marketplace traffic and handles much of the buying journey; your own Shopify store gives brand control, customer data and repeat sales without marketplace commissions. Many Pakistani sellers run both, with 1 stock count, clear pricing per channel and a daily routine to prevent overselling.

Key Takeaways

  • Daraz brings buyers who are already shopping; your own store builds a brand and repeat customers.
  • Marketplace sellers do not own the customer relationship in the same way as on their own store.
  • One stock count across channels prevents overselling and cancelled orders.
  • Prices can differ by channel, but must stay consistent with each channel's rules.
  • Start with one channel well, then add the second with a clear process.

Shopify vs Daraz: Which Should a Pakistani Seller Use?

For Pakistani online sellers, Daraz is the biggest marketplace and often the first place they sell. A Shopify store, or another own-brand store, is where many then go to build something they control. Asking Shopify vs Daraz as an either-or question misses the point: the two channels do different jobs. The real challenge is running both without overselling, confusing pricing or losing track of customers. How Do You Keep Stock in Sync?

Overselling, selling the same last item on both channels, is the most common problem. Options, from simplest to most automated:

  1. Separate allocations: assign fixed stock to each channel and top up manually. Simple, but stock can sit idle on one side.
  2. One master sheet, updated after every order on either channel. Works at low volume, error-prone at high volume.
  3. Inventory sync tools or integrations that update both channels when an order arrives. Check how quickly they update and what happens if a sync fails.

Whatever you use, count physical stock regularly and compare it with the system.

How Should Prices Differ by Channel?

Many sellers price slightly higher on the marketplace to cover commissions, or offer exclusive bundles on their own store to reward direct buyers. Keep it simple and honest, follow each channel's rules on pricing and promotions, and make sure delivery charges and cash on delivery terms are clear on both.

Who Owns the Customer Data?

On your own store, customers' details and order history sit in your account, and you can contact them with their consent. On a marketplace, the platform controls much of the relationship and its rules limit how you contact buyers. Do not try to move marketplace buyers off the platform against its rules. Instead, build your own customer base through your store, social media and packaging inserts where permitted.

What Daily Routine Prevents Chaos?

  • Check orders on both channels at fixed times each day.
  • Update stock immediately or confirm the sync worked.
  • Pack and dispatch in one queue, labelled by channel.
  • Track cancellations and returns per channel.
  • Review profit per channel monthly, after all fees.

Which Channel Should You Start With?

If you have no audience yet, a marketplace can bring first sales and reviews. If you already have followers on Instagram or WhatsApp, your own store can convert them directly. Either way, master one channel's operations before adding the second.

How Should Customer Data Be Handled Across Channels?

Use marketplace buyer details only for fulfilling those orders, as the platform's rules require, and keep your own store's customer data secure with limited staff access.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I sell on Daraz or my own Shopify store?
Often both. Daraz brings marketplace shoppers and handles much of the buying journey, while your own store gives brand control, direct customer relationships and repeat sales without marketplace commissions. Start with one channel done well, then add the other with a clear process.
How do I avoid overselling on Daraz and Shopify?
Keep one stock count across channels. Options include fixed stock allocations per channel, a master sheet updated after every order, or inventory sync tools that update both channels automatically. Count physical stock regularly and compare it with the system to catch errors early.
Can I charge different prices on Daraz and my own store?
Many sellers do, often pricing slightly higher on the marketplace to cover commissions or offering exclusive bundles on their own store. Keep pricing honest, follow each channel's rules on pricing and promotions, and make delivery and cash on delivery terms clear on both.
Do I own customer data from Daraz orders?
Marketplace platforms control much of the customer relationship, and their rules limit how sellers may use buyer details, usually to fulfilling orders. On your own store, customer data sits in your account and you can market to customers with their consent.
How much does Shopify cost compared with Daraz?
Shopify's pricing page, checked 30 October 2026, lists Basic at $29 a month billed yearly, plus apps and third-party transaction fees in Pakistan. Daraz charges marketplace commissions and fees set in its seller centre, which change, so check its official pages for current figures.
What daily routine helps run two sales channels?
Check orders on both channels at fixed times, update or confirm stock immediately, pack and dispatch from one queue labelled by channel, track cancellations and returns per channel, and review profit per channel every month after all fees and charges.
Daraz aur apna Shopify store dono kaise chalayein?
Daraz se tayyar customers milte hain aur apne store se brand aur dobara khareedne wale customers bante hain. Dono ka stock aik hi jagah se control karein taake aik cheez do baar na bik jaye. Har roz muqarrar waqt par orders dekhein aur har mahine dono ka munafa alag hisaab karein.
