What Is a Landing Page, and How Is It Different From a Website?

A landing page is a single page built around one offer and one action. A website is a set of pages that describes a whole business. Most people arrive on a landing page from an advert, a social media post or a QR code, and the page's only job is to turn that visit into an enquiry.

When Is One Page Enough?

  • You sell one main service, such as AC repair in one city or a single course.
  • You are running an advert and want the visitor to land on a page that matches it exactly.
  • You are launching a product or event with a fixed date.
  • You need something live this week while a full website is built.

One page is not enough if you want to rank in Google for many different services, if customers need to compare options, or if you sell many products. In those cases start with a business website or an online store.

The Structure of a Landing Page That Converts

  1. Headline that matches the advert. If the ad says "Same-day AC repair in Lahore", the page should say the same.
  2. One-line value proposition and the main button above the fold on a phone.
  3. Proof: reviews, photos of real work, numbers you can back up.
  4. What you get and the price or price range, if you can publish it.
  5. How it works in three or four steps.
  6. Answers to objections: delivery areas, guarantees, payment methods.
  7. Repeated call to action after each section, plus a sticky WhatsApp button.

Landing Pages and Google Ads Quality

Google's advertising guidance says landing page experience is one of the factors in ad quality. It looks at whether the page is relevant to the ad, useful and easy to navigate, including on mobile. A page that matches the ad and loads quickly tends to earn better ad positions for the same budget. Google's Core Web Vitals give a practical speed target: the main content should load within about 2.5 seconds.

Tracking: Know What Each Enquiry Costs

Without tracking you cannot tell whether a campaign is working. We set up events for:

  • WhatsApp button clicks, separated by button position.
  • Phone number taps.
  • Form submissions.

With these in place, you can divide your ad spend by enquiries and see your cost per lead. That number decides whether to scale the campaign or change the offer.

Common Landing Page Mistakes

  • Sending ad traffic to the home page instead of a matching landing page.
  • Asking for too many form fields.
  • Large, uncompressed images that make the page slow on mobile data.
  • No phone number or WhatsApp button visible without scrolling.
  • Claims without proof, such as "best in Pakistan".

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a landing page cost in Pakistan?
Our landing page costs Rs 22,500 and includes a mobile-first single page, WhatsApp and call buttons, a form, conversion tracking and speed optimisation. Other providers' prices vary widely, so compare whether copy, tracking and speed work are included.
Is a landing page enough for my business?
A landing page is enough if you sell one main service, run a specific ad campaign or launch one product or event. If you offer many services or want to rank for many searches in Google, a full website is better.
What is the difference between a landing page and a website?
A landing page is one page with one goal, usually an enquiry, and little navigation. A website has several pages that describe the whole business and supports many goals, such as information, trust and different services.
Do I need a landing page for Google Ads?
It is strongly recommended. Google considers landing page experience in ad quality, so a page that matches the ad and works well on mobile usually performs better than sending visitors to a general home page.
How fast should a landing page load?
Aim for the main content to load within about 2.5 seconds on mobile, which is Google's good threshold for Largest Contentful Paint. Compress images, avoid heavy scripts and test on a phone using mobile data.
Can I track WhatsApp leads from a landing page?
Yes. WhatsApp button clicks, phone taps and form submissions can be tracked as events in Google Analytics and imported into Google Ads, so you can see the cost of each enquiry.
Can a landing page be turned into a full website later?
Yes. If it is built on the same platform and design system, its content and layout carry over into a starter or business website, so you do not pay twice for the same work.
