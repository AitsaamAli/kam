Business Email in Pakistan on Your Own Domain: Google Workspace vs Zoho Mail vs Hosting Email

Kamboh Associates Digital team
Updated 30 October 2026
5 min read
Lahore, Pakistan
Short answer

For business email in Pakistan on your own domain, the main choices are Google Workspace, Zoho Mail or the email included with your hosting. Zoho's official page lists a free plan for up to 5 users; Workspace and paid Zoho plans charge per user. Hosting email is cheapest but often weaker on spam filtering and deliverability.

Key Takeaways

  • An address like name@yourbusiness.com looks more trustworthy than a free webmail address.
  • Google Workspace and Zoho Mail are dedicated email services with strong filtering and apps.
  • Hosting email costs nothing extra but ties your email to your web host.
  • Whoever controls the admin account controls your email; keep it in the business's name.
  • Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC whichever service you choose.

Why Use Business Email in Pakistan on Your Own Domain?

Many Pakistani businesses still use a free webmail address for quotes and invoices. It works, but customers and overseas buyers notice, and it is easier for scammers to imitate. A business email on your own domain, such as accounts@yourbusiness.pk, signals a real organisation, stays with the business when staff change, and can be secured properly. The question is which service to use.

Email set-up is one of the practical fixes grouped under our getting-on-Google guide.

Which Should You Choose?

  • Google Workspace suits teams already using Gmail and Google Drive, and those who want shared calendars and video meetings in one place.
  • Zoho Mail suits budget-conscious small teams; the free plan can cover a very small business with limited needs.
  • Hosting email suits a single address with low volume, where cost matters most, provided you set up authentication and back up mail.

Why Does Deliverability Matter?

Quotes and invoices that land in a customer's spam folder are lost business. Dedicated services generally have better sending reputations than shared hosting servers. Whatever you choose, set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC records for your domain; our guide to emails going to spam explains them in plain words.

Who Owns the Email Account?

The admin account controls every mailbox. If a developer or former employee set it up under their own login, you may lose access when they leave. Make sure the admin account uses a business address you control, with two-step verification, and that billing is in the business's name. Our guide to who owns your website covers the same principle for domains and hosting.

How Do You Switch Email Providers?

Create the mailboxes at the new service, migrate old mail using the provider's import tools, then change the MX records at your DNS host and add the new SPF and DKIM records. Keep the old service running for a few days to catch delayed messages. Tell staff to check spam folders during the change.

Which Addresses Should a Small Business Create?

Start with a few role addresses, such as info@, sales@ and accounts@, plus a personal address for each person who deals with customers. Role addresses can be shared or forwarded, so they survive staff changes. Avoid creating dozens of unused mailboxes, especially on per-user plans where each one costs money.

How Should Business Email Be Secured?

Email holds customer details, invoices and passwords. Turn on two-step verification for every user, remove accounts when staff leave instead of sharing passwords, and train staff to spot fake payment-change requests, a common fraud against small businesses.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best business email option in Pakistan?
It depends on your team. Google Workspace suits teams wanting Gmail, Drive, Calendar and Meet together. Zoho Mail suits budget-conscious small teams and has a free plan for up to five users. Hosting email is cheapest but usually weaker on filtering and deliverability.
Is Zoho Mail free for businesses?
Zoho's official pricing page lists a Mail Free plan for up to five users on one domain, with 5 GB storage per user and no IMAP or POP access, available in select data centres. From the sixth user, all users must move to a paid plan.
How much does Google Workspace cost in Pakistan?
Google Workspace charges per user per month, and its pricing page shows prices in local currency, with a saving of 16% for a one-year commitment. The figures did not load when we checked on 30 October 2026, so confirm current prices on Google's official page.
Is hosting email good enough for a business?
For one or two addresses with low volume it can be, if SPF, DKIM and DMARC are set up and mail is backed up. Its drawbacks are weaker spam filtering on some hosts, shared server reputation, and email moving whenever you change web hosting.
Why do my business emails go to spam?
Common causes are missing or wrong SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, sending from a shared server with a poor reputation, or messages that look like spam. Set up authentication records, use a reputable email service and test with a few external addresses.
Who should own the business email admin account?
The business. The admin account controls every mailbox, so it should use an address you control, with two-step verification and billing in the business's name. If a developer or former employee still holds it, ask them to transfer it to the business straight away.
Apne domain par business email kaise banayein?
Google Workspace, Zoho Mail ya hosting wali email mein se chunein. Zoho ka free plan paanch users tak hai. Domain ke DNS mein MX, SPF aur DKIM records lagayen taake email spam mein na jaye. Admin account business ke naam par rakhein, kisi developer ke paas nahin.
