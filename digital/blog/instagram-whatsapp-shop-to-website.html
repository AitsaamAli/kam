From Instagram Shop to Website: A Step-by-Step Plan That Keeps Your WhatsApp Orders

Kamboh Associates Digital team
Updated 29 October 2026
6 min read
Lahore, Pakistan
Short answer

Moving an Instagram shop to a website takes about 4 to 8 weeks: list your products with prices, choose Shopify or WooCommerce, keep a WhatsApp order button on every product, connect your catalogue to Instagram, then guide followers to the site gradually. Customers still message you, but they see prices and stock first.

Key Takeaways

  • A website does not replace Instagram or WhatsApp; it gives them a proper catalogue and checkout to point to.
  • Publish prices on the website; 'DM for price' loses buyers who will not message a stranger.
  • Keep a WhatsApp button on every product, so customers who prefer chatting can still order that way.
  • Sync your product catalogue with Instagram, so posts and the website show the same stock and prices.
  • Move customers gradually with link-in-bio, story links and order follow-ups, not by stopping DM orders overnight.

Why Move From an Instagram Shop to a Website?

Many Pakistani brands start on Instagram and WhatsApp, and for good reason: it is free, fast and where customers already spend time. Growth exposes the limits. Orders arrive as DMs at midnight, the same "price?" question is answered a hundred times, stock is tracked in a notebook, and a lost phone or blocked account can stop the business.

Moving your Instagram shop to a website does not mean leaving Instagram. It means giving your posts and chats a proper home: a catalogue with prices, sizes and stock, a checkout that works when you are asleep, and an address on Google that no platform can take away.

What Is the Step-by-Step Migration Plan?

  1. Week 1: list your products. Make a spreadsheet of every product with name, price, sizes or variants, stock and the best photos. This is the slowest step for most brands.
  2. Week 1: choose a platform. Shopify or WooCommerce; our Shopify vs WooCommerce guide helps.
  3. Weeks 2 to 4: build the store. Add products, delivery charges by city, COD, an online payment option and policy pages.
  4. Week 4: connect Instagram. Set up a product catalogue in Meta's tools so you can tag products in posts, where your account is eligible.
  5. Week 5: test. Place orders on a phone over mobile data, including COD and a WhatsApp order.
  6. Week 5 onward: move followers gradually. Put the website link in your bio, add product links to stories and reply to "price?" DMs with the product link.

How Do You Keep WhatsApp Ordering?

Do not force every customer through a checkout. Many Pakistani buyers like to ask a question before ordering. Add a WhatsApp button to every product page that opens a chat with a message already written, such as "Hi, I want to order the black kurta, size M". WhatsApp's click-to-chat links support this kind of pre-filled message.

Your team then confirms the order in the website's admin, so stock and records stay correct. Over time, more customers will use the checkout for simple orders and WhatsApp for questions.

How Do You Keep the Catalogue in Sync?

The danger of two shops is selling the same last piece twice. Make the website the master list. Shopify and WooCommerce can feed your product catalogue to Meta's commerce tools through official channels or plugins, so Instagram product tags show the same prices and stock. Check current eligibility rules in Meta's business help pages, as they vary by country and account type.

If you also sell on a marketplace, read Daraz vs your own website for managing stock across channels.

A Worked Example: A Home-Baked Brand in Lahore

Illustrative example, not a real client. A cake and dessert brand takes about 40 orders a week through Instagram DMs. Two family members spend hours each day answering "price?" and "available on Friday?".

  • The website lists each cake with price, sizes, flavours and order notice period.
  • A delivery date picker blocks fully booked days.
  • Each product has a WhatsApp button for custom requests.
  • Instagram stories link to products instead of answering prices in DMs.

DMs continue. Most of them now read "I placed order 1043, can you add a message?" instead of starting from zero.

Which Mistakes Should You Avoid?

  • Launching with half the products missing, so followers cannot find what they saw on Instagram.
  • Hiding prices on the website as well.
  • Removing WhatsApp ordering overnight.
  • Using different prices on Instagram and the website.
  • Poor photos; good product pages matter more on a website. See our product page checklist.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a website if I sell on Instagram?
You can start without one. A website helps once orders grow. It shows prices and stock without DMs, takes orders while you sleep, keeps order records in one place and keeps working if your Instagram account is blocked or hacked. You keep Instagram and WhatsApp as sales channels alongside it.
Can customers still order on WhatsApp after I launch a website?
Yes, and they should be able to. Add a WhatsApp button on every product page that opens a chat with the product name already written. Your team then enters or confirms the order in the website's admin, so stock and records stay accurate while customers keep the way of ordering they prefer.
How do I connect my website products to Instagram?
Shopify and WooCommerce can send your product catalogue to Meta's commerce tools through official channels or plugins, so you can tag products in posts and stories. Eligibility depends on your country, account type and Meta's current rules, so check Meta's business help pages before relying on product tags.
Should I show prices on my website?
Yes. Many buyers will not send a message to a seller they do not know just to ask a price, so hidden prices lose sales. Showing prices, delivery charges and sizes on every product page answers the first questions instantly and leaves WhatsApp chats for real questions and custom orders.
How long does it take to move an Instagram shop to a website?
Most brands need four to eight weeks. The slowest part is usually listing products with prices, variants and good photos, not the website build itself. Prepare a product spreadsheet early, and launch once the main products customers see on Instagram are all available on the website.
Instagram page se website par shift kaise karein?
Pehle apne tamam products ki list banayein jismein price, size aur tasveerein hon. Phir Shopify ya WooCommerce par store banwayen, har product par WhatsApp button rakhein, aur Instagram bio aur stories mein website ka link dein. WhatsApp orders band na karein, customers ko aahista aahista website par layein.
