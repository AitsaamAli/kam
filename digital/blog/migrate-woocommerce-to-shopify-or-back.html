WooCommerce to Shopify Migration (or Back): What Moves, What Breaks and What It Costs

Published 30 October 2026
Updated 30 October 2026

A WooCommerce to Shopify migration moves products, images, customers and order history, usually with an import tool or script. Passwords, some plugin data and custom features do not move cleanly. The biggest risk is losing search traffic, so map every old URL to its new one with 301 redirects. Plan for 2 to 6 weeks, depending on store size.

Key Takeaways

  • Migrate for a clear reason: cost, maintenance burden, features or payment options.
  • Products, customers and orders can move; passwords and many plugin settings cannot.
  • URL structures differ between platforms, so redirects are essential to keep rankings.
  • Run the new store in parallel and switch over at a quiet time, never before Eid or a big sale.
  • Compare total monthly costs on both platforms, including apps or plugins, before deciding.

Why Consider a WooCommerce to Shopify Migration?

Pakistani store owners usually consider a WooCommerce to Shopify migration because their WooCommerce site has become slow, keeps breaking after plugin updates or needs a developer for every change. Others move the opposite way, from Shopify to WooCommerce, to escape monthly app bills and transaction fees or to gain more control. Both moves can be right. Both carry real risk if done carelessly. How Do You Keep Search Traffic?

WooCommerce and Shopify use different URL patterns for products and categories. If old URLs simply stop working, Google rankings and links from other sites are lost. Google's guidance on site moves recommends mapping every old URL to its most relevant new URL with permanent (301) redirects. Make a spreadsheet of all old product, category, page and blog URLs, match each to its new address, and load the redirects before switching. Keep titles and descriptions for important pages. Our guide to changing websites without losing traffic explains the process step by step.

How Do You Avoid Downtime?

  1. Build and test the new store on a temporary address while the old one keeps selling.
  2. Import data, then check a sample of products, prices and stock against the old store.
  3. Test checkout with every payment method and courier integration.
  4. Choose a quiet switch date, never just before Eid, 11.11 or a sale.
  5. Do a final import of orders and customers added since the first import.
  6. Point the domain to the new store, activate redirects and watch for errors.
  7. Keep the old store backed up and accessible to staff for a few months.

Will Your Payment Methods Still Work?

Check this before anything else. Your current gateway may have a plugin for WooCommerce but no integration with Shopify, or the other way round. Shopify Payments is not available in Pakistan, and Shopify charges third-party transaction fees of 2%, 1% or 0.6% depending on plan when Shopify Payments is not used, according to its pricing page checked on 30 October 2026. Cash on delivery and courier integrations also need confirming on the new platform.

What Affects the Cost of Migration?

  • Number of products and variants, and how clean the data is.
  • Whether the design is rebuilt from scratch or adapted from a theme.
  • Custom features that must be rebuilt or replaced.
  • Number of URLs to redirect.
  • Payment, courier and other integrations.

How Should Customer Data Be Handled During the Move?

Export files contain customer names, phones, addresses and order histories. Store them securely, share them only with the people doing the migration, and delete working copies once the move is complete. Tell customers if they need to reset passwords.

What Should You Check After Switching?

Watch Search Console for 404 errors and fix missing redirects, compare daily orders with the weeks before, check that emails and order notifications arrive, and ask a few loyal customers to place test orders. See e-commerce development for help.

Frequently Asked Questions

What data moves in a WooCommerce to Shopify migration?
Products, variants, images, prices, stock, customers, order history, blog posts and pages can usually be moved with import tools or scripts. Customer passwords, plugin features, custom code and the theme design do not move and must be replaced or rebuilt on the new platform.
Will I lose Google rankings if I change store platforms?
You can, if old URLs stop working. Map every old product, category, page and blog URL to its new address with permanent 301 redirects before switching, keep titles and descriptions for important pages, and watch Search Console for errors after the move.
How long does a store migration take?
Typically two to six weeks, depending on the number of products, how clean the data is, whether the design is rebuilt, and how many integrations must be replaced. Build and test on a temporary address first, and switch over at a quiet time.
Should I move from Shopify to WooCommerce to save money?
Compare total monthly costs first: Shopify's plan, apps and transaction fees against WooCommerce hosting, paid plugins, maintenance and developer time. WooCommerce can be cheaper to run but needs more upkeep. The right answer depends on your store's size and in-house skills.
Will my payment gateway work after migrating?
Not necessarily. A gateway may support WooCommerce but not Shopify, or the reverse. Confirm integrations for every payment method, cash on delivery handling and courier booking on the new platform before committing to the move. Shopify Payments is not available in Pakistan.
When is the best time to migrate an online store?
During a quiet period, well away from Eid, 11.11, Black Friday or your own sales. Leave at least a few weeks after switching to fix problems before any peak, and keep the old store backed up and accessible to staff for a few months.
Online store ka platform badalna chahiye ya nahin?
Pehle wajah saaf karein: kharch, speed, ya features. Products, customers aur orders muntaqil ho jate hain, lekin passwords aur plugins nahin. Purane links ko naye links par 301 redirect zaroor karein, warna Google ki ranking gir sakti hai. Eid ya sale se pehle platform kabhi na badlein.
