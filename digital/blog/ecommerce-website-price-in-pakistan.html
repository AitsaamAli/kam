Short answer

There is no single best platform. For most Pakistani stores starting with cash on delivery and local wallets, WooCommerce keeps monthly costs lowest. Shopify suits brands that value low maintenance and are comfortable paying a US-dollar subscription. Custom builds make sense when your process does not fit a standard store. MethodReported fee per transactionNotes
JazzCash / Easypaisa (wallet)about 1.5% – 3%Depends on your agreement and volume
PayFast (wallets and bank accounts)about 2.2%A one-time setup charge of about Rs 20,000 and service tax are reported on top
Local debit/credit cardsabout 2.5% – 3%Varies by gateway
International cardsabout 3.5%Higher because of cross-border processing
Cash on deliverycourier COD chargeNo gateway fee, but you carry the risk of refused parcels

Treat these as planning figures and ask each gateway for a written rate. On Shopify, add the platform's own fee for third-party gateways on top.

Cash on Delivery: Plan for It, Then Reduce It

Most Pakistani shoppers still prefer to pay cash on delivery, and a store that refuses COD often loses sales. The cost of COD is not a gateway fee but refused parcels: you pay delivery and return charges on orders that never convert. A well-built store reduces this with:

  • An order confirmation step by WhatsApp or SMS before dispatch.
  • A small advance or delivery charge for high-risk orders or areas.
  • A discount for paying online, which nudges customers toward wallets.
  • Clear delivery times and return rules on every product page.

These features cost little to build but can save far more than their price within months.

Costs People Forget to Budget For

  • Product photography and descriptions. A store with 100 products needs 100 sets of photos and descriptions. If you do not have them, budget for them separately.
  • Paid apps or plugins. Size charts, reviews, abandoned-cart messages and courier apps often carry monthly fees.
  • Courier account and COD settlement. Couriers charge per parcel and take time to settle COD amounts.
  • Exchange-rate exposure. Shopify and many apps bill in US dollars, so your rupee cost moves with the exchange rate.
  • Maintenance. Stores change constantly: new products, sales, plugin updates. Plan for monthly support.

Worked Example: A 60-Product Clothing Store

A Lahore boutique wants 60 products with sizes and colours, COD, Easypaisa and card payments, courier booking and a WhatsApp order button.

  • WooCommerce route: build at our store price of Rs 157,500; hosting about Rs 10,000–15,000 a year; gateway fees per sale; optional maintenance from Rs 7,200 a month.
  • Shopify route: a similar build price; Basic plan at $29 a month billed yearly; a 2% Shopify fee on gateway payments on Basic, on top of the gateway's own fee; COD orders carry no Shopify transaction fee.

Over two years, the WooCommerce route usually costs less in fees, while the Shopify route needs less technical upkeep. The right choice depends on whether you value lower running costs or lower maintenance.

What to Ask an E-commerce Developer

  1. Which platform do you recommend for my products and why?
  2. Which payment methods will be live at launch, and who opens the merchant accounts?
  3. Which courier will be integrated, and does booking happen automatically?
  4. How will fake COD orders be reduced?
  5. Who owns the domain, hosting or Shopify account?
  6. What training will my staff get for adding products and handling orders?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an online store cost in Pakistan?
Building an online store in Pakistan usually costs Rs 120,000 to Rs 600,000 in 2026, depending on the number of products, payment and courier integrations and design. Our e-commerce package is Rs 157,500 for up to 100 products with COD, a payment gateway and courier booking.
Online store banane ki qeemat kitni hai?
Pakistan mein online store aam tor par Rs 120,000 se Rs 600,000 tak mein banta hai. Hamara e-commerce package Rs 157,500 ka hai, jis mein 100 products tak, cash on delivery, JazzCash/Easypaisa ya bank gateway aur courier booking shamil hai.
Is Shopify or WooCommerce cheaper for a Pakistani store?
WooCommerce usually has lower running costs because there is no monthly platform fee and no extra fee on gateway payments. Shopify costs more each month in US dollars but handles hosting and security for you. Compare both over two years, not just the build.
Is Shopify Payments available in Pakistan?
No. Shopify Payments is not available to businesses based in Pakistan, so online payments on a Pakistani Shopify store go through a third-party gateway. On the Basic plan Shopify adds a 2% fee on those payments, on top of the gateway's own charge.
What are payment gateway charges in Pakistan?
Reported 2026 figures are about 1.5–3% for JazzCash and Easypaisa wallets, about 2.2% for PayFast wallet and bank payments plus a setup charge, about 2.5–3% for local cards and about 3.5% for international cards. Rates are negotiated, so get written quotes.
Do I need a payment gateway if I only take cash on delivery?
No, a store can launch with cash on delivery only. Adding a wallet or card option later usually increases sales and lets you offer a prepaid discount that reduces refused parcels. Plan the gateway even if you launch without it.
How long does it take to build an e-commerce website?
A small theme-based store can launch in three to five weeks once products and photos are ready. Larger custom stores take two to three months. Product data entry and photography are usually the slowest parts, not the development.
Can I add products myself after the store is built?
Yes. Shopify and WooCommerce both let you add products, change prices and run discounts from an admin panel. Ask your developer for a short training session and a written guide as part of the handover.
What monthly costs does an online store have?
Expect hosting or a Shopify plan, paid apps or plugins, payment gateway fees on each sale, courier charges and optional maintenance. For a small WooCommerce store the fixed part can stay under Rs 3,000 a month; Shopify starts at $29 a month billed yearly.
