Wholesale Website in Pakistan: Bulk Prices, Dealer Logins and Minimum Order Quantities

Kamboh Associates Digital team
Updated 30 October 2026
6 min read
Lahore, Pakistan
Short answer

A wholesale website in Pakistan lets trade buyers log in, see bulk prices, order above a minimum quantity and pay or use agreed credit. Many wholesalers can run this as an online store with wholesale features, from Rs 157,500. Distributors needing customer-specific prices, credit limits and field order bookers usually need a custom portal.

Key Takeaways

  • Hide wholesale prices behind a login so retail customers and competitors do not see them.
  • Tiered pricing rewards bigger orders: for example one price per piece for 12, a lower price for 60.
  • Minimum order quantities and carton sizes should be enforced by the site, not checked by hand.
  • Credit customers need balance and limit checks before orders are confirmed.
  • A wholesale store can run alongside a retail store from the same stock.

What Is a Wholesale Website in Pakistan?

Wholesalers in Shah Alam Market, Hall Road, Faisalabad's cloth markets and Karachi's Jodia Bazaar have traditionally sold through phone calls, visits and WhatsApp catalogues. A wholesale website in Pakistan moves that online: approved trade buyers log in, see wholesale prices, order by carton or dozen, and track their orders. It reduces order mistakes, saves staff time and lets buyers in other cities order without travelling.

How Do Credit Customers Fit In?

Many wholesale relationships run on credit: a buyer orders now and pays in 15 or 30 days. The website can show each credit buyer their limit, outstanding balance and overdue invoices, and hold orders that would exceed the limit for approval. Simple versions record payments manually; more advanced ones connect to your accounts system.

How Should the Wholesale Catalogue Be Organised?

Trade buyers know what they want and order in bulk, so speed matters more than browsing. Organise products by the codes and names buyers already use, show pack size and carton quantity next to every item, and let buyers search by article number. A downloadable price list for logged-in buyers, updated automatically from the site, saves your team from sending PDFs on WhatsApp every time rates change. Show stock status clearly so buyers do not order items you cannot supply this week.

How Do You Get Trade Buyers Online?

Start with your regular buyers. Create their accounts for them, send each a short WhatsApp message with the login link, and take their next phone order while walking them through the site. Keep phone ordering open at first, but enter those orders on the website so buyers see their history there and gradually switch.

How Do Wholesale Buyers Pay?

Bank transfer is the most common method for larger trade orders, with mobile wallets and cash on delivery for smaller ones. Show bank details only after an order is placed, and ask buyers to send the transfer receipt on WhatsApp with the order number. Payment gateway fees on large orders can be significant, so check the provider's charges before offering card payment.

How Do You Protect Trade Prices and Buyer Data?

Wholesale prices are commercially sensitive. Approve accounts manually, never show trade prices to logged-out visitors, and remove accounts for buyers who stop trading with you. Buyers' shop addresses, CNIC copies and credit history should be visible only to staff who need them.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a wholesale website in Pakistan?
It is an online store or portal where approved trade buyers log in, see wholesale prices, order above minimum quantities and track their orders. It replaces phone and WhatsApp orders, reduces mistakes and lets buyers in other cities order without visiting your market or warehouse.
How much does a wholesale online store cost?
Many wholesalers can run an online store with wholesale features such as trade logins, tiered prices and minimum quantities, from Rs 157,500. Distributors needing customer-specific prices, credit limits and field order bookers usually need a custom portal, which starts from Rs 360,000.
How do I hide wholesale prices from retail customers?
Set prices to show only to approved trade accounts. Visitors who are not logged in see a message such as login for trade prices. Approve each trade account manually, and remove accounts for buyers who stop trading with you, so prices do not leak to competitors.
Can a wholesale store enforce minimum order quantities?
Yes. The store can block orders below a minimum quantity or value, and only allow quantities in pack multiples such as 6, 12 or 24 to match how products are packed. This prevents staff from having to contact buyers about orders that cannot be supplied.
Can I run retail and wholesale from one website?
Yes. Customer groups let retail visitors see normal prices while approved trade buyers see wholesale prices and minimum quantities, all from the same stock. This avoids running two separate stores, two sets of product data and two stock counts that must be kept in sync by hand.
How do wholesale buyers usually pay online?
Bank transfer is most common for larger trade orders, with mobile wallets and cash on delivery for smaller ones. Approved buyers may order on credit and pay later. Check gateway fees before offering card payment on large orders, because the charges can be significant.
Wholesale ka online store kaise banayein?
Trade buyers ke liye login rakhein taake sirf woh wholesale qeemat dekh sakein. Zyada maal par kam qeemat, kam se kam order ki had aur carton ke hisaab se quantity website khud lagaye. Agar har dealer ki qeemat aur udhaar alag hai to custom portal behtar hai.
