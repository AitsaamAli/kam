Dealer Order Portal for Distributors: Cut Phone Orders, Errors and Credit Disputes

Kamboh Associates Digital team
Updated 30 October 2026
5 min read
Lahore, Pakistan
Short answer

A dealer order portal lets your dealers and retailers log in, see their own price list, place orders, check credit and track dispatch, without phone calls or WhatsApp voice notes. Off-the-shelf B2B tools suit simple catalogues. A custom portal, from Rs 360,000, suits distributors with customer-specific prices, schemes and credit limits.

Key Takeaways

  • Phone and WhatsApp orders cause errors in items, quantities and prices.
  • Each dealer should see only their own prices, schemes and balance.
  • Credit limits and outstanding balances must be visible before an order is confirmed.
  • Order bookers in the field can use the same portal on a phone.
  • Integration with your accounts or stock system is usually the most complex part.

Why Do Distributors Need a Dealer Order Portal?

A distributor of paints, electrical goods, pharmaceuticals or FMCG products in Pakistan may take hundreds of orders a week by phone, WhatsApp text, voice notes and visits from order bookers. Someone then types them into the system. Wrong items, wrong quantities and disputes over agreed prices are common, and nobody can easily see whether a dealer is already over their credit limit. A dealer order portal moves ordering online: each dealer logs in, sees their own prices, places the order and tracks it.

How Should Credit Limits Work?

Each dealer has a credit limit and payment terms. When an order would take the balance over the limit, the portal can block it, allow it with a warning, or send it to a manager for approval. Dealers see their balance and overdue invoices when they log in, which often prompts payment without a reminder call. Payments received are recorded against the account, either by staff or automatically through a payment link.

What About Integration With Accounts and Stock?

Most distributors already use accounting or inventory software. The portal is far more useful when approved orders flow into that system automatically, and stock and balances flow back. How easy this is depends entirely on the existing software: some have documented ways to exchange data, others only allow manual exports. We check this before quoting, because it often decides the budget. Any statutory or government reporting from your accounts system is outside our scope; ask your adviser about those requirements.

How Do Order Bookers Use It?

Field staff visiting shops use the same portal on a phone: select the shop, see its prices and balance, enter the order and submit. Orders arrive at the warehouse immediately rather than at the end of the day. Managers can see visits and orders per booker and route.

How Do You Get Dealers to Use It?

Dealers who have ordered by phone for twenty years will not switch overnight. Start with your most active dealers, set up their accounts for them, and show them quick reorder on their own phone during a visit. Keep accepting phone orders at first, but enter them into the portal so dealers see their history there. Some distributors offer a small scheme on portal orders during the first months to encourage the habit.

How Is Dealer Data Kept Separate?

Price lists and balances are commercially sensitive. Each dealer must only see their own information, staff access should follow roles, and every login should use strong passwords. Test this carefully: a portal that shows one dealer another's prices damages trust quickly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a dealer order portal?
It is a private website or web app where your dealers and retailers log in, see their own prices and schemes, place orders, check their credit balance and track dispatch. It replaces phone, WhatsApp and paper orders, reducing errors and saving time for your order desk.
How much does a custom dealer portal cost?
A custom portal with dealer logins, customer-specific prices, ordering and status tracking starts from Rs 360,000. Credit control, field order booker features and integration with your accounts or stock software increase the cost, which we confirm after checking your existing systems.
Can each dealer see different prices?
Yes. A dealer portal can show each customer their own price list, discounts and schemes. This is one of the main reasons distributors move from phone orders, because it ends disputes about agreed rates and bonus offers when invoices arrive.
Can the portal enforce credit limits?
Yes. When an order would take a dealer over their limit, the portal can block it, warn them, or send it to a manager for approval. Dealers also see their balance and overdue invoices when they log in, which often prompts payment without reminder calls.
Will the portal connect to my accounting software?
It depends on your software. Some systems offer documented ways to exchange orders, stock and balances; others only allow manual exports. We check before quoting, because integration is often the most complex part. Statutory reporting from your accounts system stays with your adviser.
Can field order bookers use the portal?
Yes. Order bookers can use the same portal on a phone to select a shop, see its prices and balance, and submit orders on the spot. Orders reach the warehouse immediately, and managers can see visits and orders per booker and route.
Dealers ke liye online order portal kyun banwayen?
Phone aur WhatsApp par orders mein ghaltiyan bohat hoti hain. Portal par har dealer apni qeemat, scheme aur udhaar ki had dekh kar khud order karta hai, aur dispatch ka status bhi dekh sakta hai. Order bookers bhi mobile se isi portal par order daal sakte hain.
