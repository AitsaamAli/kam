Product Page Best Practices for Pakistani Online Stores: A 15-Point Checklist With Examples

Kamboh Associates Digital team
Updated 29 October 2026
6 min read
Lahore, Pakistan
Short answer

Product page best practices for a Pakistani store come down to 15 checks: several real photos, a clear price, sizes or specifications, stock per variant, delivery charge and time, COD availability, returns terms, genuine reviews and a WhatsApp button. Customers should never need to message you to learn the basics.

Key Takeaways

  • Show several real photos per product, including close-ups and the product in use.
  • Put the full price, delivery charge, delivery time and COD availability on the product page itself.
  • Give a size chart or specification table, and show stock for each size or variant.
  • Write short, specific descriptions in plain English, with a Roman Urdu line where your buyers expect it.
  • Show only genuine reviews, and make it easy to ask a question on WhatsApp.

Why Do Product Page Best Practices Matter So Much?

The product page is where a visitor decides to buy or leave. In a shop, a customer can touch the fabric and ask the shopkeeper. Online, the page must answer those questions. When it does not, the customer messages you, waits, then often buys elsewhere.

The Nielsen Norman Group, a respected usability research firm, notes in its product page guidelines that one product view is rarely enough to answer shoppers' questions, and that descriptions should lead with key facts because people skim. The Baymard Institute's checkout research found unexpected extra costs, such as shipping, to be the top checkout-related reason shoppers abandon, cited by 40%. Both point the same way: show more, hide less.

How Should You Photograph Products?

Use real photos, not only supplier images that appear on dozens of other stores. Shoot in daylight or with a simple light box, on a plain background, from the same angles for every product. For clothing, show the item worn, the fabric close up and the embroidery or print detail. For food, show the portion size next to a familiar object. Compress images before upload, so pages load quickly on mobile data.

How Do You Write Descriptions That Sell?

Lead with facts, then add feeling. Here are original examples you can adapt.

English, clothing: "Unstitched 3-piece lawn suit. Printed front, back and sleeves, chiffon dupatta, cotton trouser. Shirt fabric 3 metres. Colour may vary slightly from screen."

Roman Urdu line for the same product: "3 piece unstitched suit, shirt ka kapra 3 meter, chiffon dupatta. Rang screen se thora mukhtalif ho sakta hai."

English, skincare: "50 ml vitamin C serum for daily use. Apply 2 to 3 drops after cleansing. Patch test first. Store away from sunlight."

Keep each product's description unique. Copying the same text across many products, or from a supplier, weakens your pages in Google search and does not answer buyers' questions.

Why Show Delivery and COD Details on the Product Page?

Pakistani shoppers want to know three things before adding to cart: what delivery costs, how long it takes, and whether they can pay cash on delivery. Showing these on the product page, not only at checkout, prevents the surprise that makes shoppers abandon. If you charge a partial advance on some orders, say so here as well. Our guide to cutting COD fake orders explains when that makes sense.

How Should You Handle Reviews?

Show only genuine reviews from real buyers. Fake reviews are easy to spot, damage trust when found out, and break most platforms' rules. Ask buyers for a review with a WhatsApp message a few days after delivery, and invite a photo. Show negative reviews too, with a polite reply; a page with only perfect reviews looks less believable.

What Are the Most Common Product Page Mistakes?

  • "DM for price" or prices only in images.
  • One blurry photo, or only supplier photos.
  • No size chart, leading to wrong sizes and returns.
  • Showing a size as available when it is sold out.
  • Delivery charges revealed only at the last step.
  • Large, uncompressed images that load slowly on mobile.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should a product page include?
A good product page includes several real photos, a clear price, variant buttons with stock per size, a size chart or specifications, a short factual description, material and care details, delivery charge and time, cash on delivery availability, a returns summary, genuine reviews and a WhatsApp button for questions.
How many photos should a product have?
At least four for most products: front, back, a close-up of material or detail, and the product worn or in use. Usability research from the Nielsen Norman Group notes that one product view is rarely enough. Use real photos, consistent angles and compressed files so pages load quickly.
Should I write product descriptions in Urdu?
Write in plain English for clarity and search, and add a short Roman Urdu or Urdu line where your buyers expect it, for example fabric length or care notes. Keep both short and factual. If most customers ask questions in Roman Urdu on WhatsApp, a Roman Urdu line on the page helps.
Should I show delivery charges on the product page?
Yes. Unexpected extra costs such as delivery are a leading reason shoppers abandon checkout, according to Baymard Institute research. Show the delivery charge or a free-delivery threshold, the delivery time and whether cash on delivery is available on the product page, before the customer reaches checkout.
Can I use supplier photos and descriptions?
You can start with them if you have permission, but replace them with your own as soon as possible. The same images and text appear on many other stores, which makes your products harder to trust and to find on Google. Your own photos and unique descriptions answer buyers' questions better.
Product page par kya kya likhna zaroori hai?
Product page par saaf tasveerein, poori qeemat, size chart, har size ka stock, delivery charges aur waqt, COD ki sahulat, return policy aur asli reviews zaroor dein. Aik WhatsApp button bhi rakhein jismein product ka naam pehle se likha ho, taake customer aasani se sawal pooch sake.
