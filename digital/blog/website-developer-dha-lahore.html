What Should a Website Developer for DHA Lahore Understand?

The Defence Housing Authority areas of Lahore stretch across many phases, each with its own commercial zones. Y-Block in Phase 3 is a long-established shopping street, and the commercial areas known as CCA in several phases hold restaurants, boutiques, clinics and offices. Large destinations such as Dolmen Mall in Phase 6 draw visitors from across the city, and LUMS university is also in DHA.

Businesses here serve customers with high expectations, and a large share of property owners and clients live overseas. A developer working with DHA businesses needs to design for both.

What Do DHA Real Estate Agencies Need?

Property is a large part of DHA's economy, and many buyers search from the Gulf, the UK or North America. An estate agency website here needs listings with phase, sector, plot size, price and status, real photos, and a WhatsApp button per agent. Above all, listings must be current. Sold plots left online damage trust quickly, especially with overseas buyers who cannot drive past to check. Google's Business Profile rules do not allow listings for individual properties for sale, so keep the profile for your office and the listings on your website. Our real estate website guide covers listing structure in detail.

What About Retail, Cafés and Clinics?

Y-Block and CCA retailers often sell through Instagram already. A website adds a full catalogue, prices and an online checkout for customers who do not want to message. Cafés need menus as text, reservations and accurate weekend hours. Clinics and dental practices need doctor profiles with PMDC registration numbers, fees and booking. In every case, show your exact phase and commercial area, because DHA addresses confuse first-time visitors. Add a short line on parking, since it is a common worry for customers driving in from other parts of the city at busy times.

How Should a DHA Website Serve Overseas Clients?

  • state your working hours with Pakistan time, and reply to overseas enquiries within one business day
  • offer email as well as WhatsApp for formal enquiries
  • explain your process in steps for clients who cannot visit
  • keep prices in rupees, with a note that clients convert at the day's rate when they pay
