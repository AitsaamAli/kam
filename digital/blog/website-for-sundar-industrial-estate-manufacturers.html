What Should a Manufacturer Website in Lahore Prove?

Lahore has several planned industrial areas. Sundar Industrial Estate, off Raiwind Road, hosts factories in sectors such as food processing, textiles, packaging, engineering and pharmaceuticals. Quaid-e-Azam Industrial Estate at Kot Lakhpat is one of the city's older industrial zones, with steel, engineering, chemicals, textile and packaging units. Many smaller workshops and factories also operate along the city's main industrial roads.

The buyers these factories serve, such as distributors, brands, contractors and importers, judge a manufacturer on whether it can make the product to specification, in the volume needed, on time. A manufacturer website in Lahore should prove that, quickly, to someone who has never visited the factory.

How Do Local and Export Buyers Differ?

Local distributors and contractors want model numbers, prices or price lists, stock and delivery times within Pakistan, and they often prefer WhatsApp. Export buyers want specifications in international units, minimum order quantities, compliance documents for their market, Incoterms and replies in their working hours. One website can serve both with separate enquiry routes. Our guides to exporter websites and B2B catalogue websites cover each side in depth.

How Should You Show Certifications?

List only the certifications and registrations your factory genuinely holds, with the certificate number, issuing body and expiry date, and link to the issuer's verification page where one exists. If you supply public sector engineering projects, show your Pakistan Engineering Council registration if your firm holds one. Never display a logo you are not entitled to; buyers and procurement teams do check.

Why Do Factory Photos Matter So Much?

Many factory websites use stock images of foreign plants. Buyers notice, and it undermines trust. Real photos of your production line, quality checks, packing area and loading bay, even if not glamorous, show you are real and capable. Shoot during a normal working day with safety equipment in use, and avoid showing anything confidential to your clients.

Should Catalogues and Spec Sheets Be Downloadable?

Yes. Procurement teams often need a PDF to attach to an internal request. Offer a spec sheet per product and a full catalogue per range, with the version and date in the file name, and replace old files rather than adding new ones beside them. Keep files small enough to open on a phone over mobile data.

How Should Enquiries Be Handled?

  1. Separate routes for local orders (WhatsApp and phone) and export enquiries (RFQ form and email).
  2. Each enquiry goes to the responsible sales person, with a copy to a manager.
  3. An automatic acknowledgement promises a reply within one business day.
  4. A simple log tracks enquiries, quotes sent and orders won.
