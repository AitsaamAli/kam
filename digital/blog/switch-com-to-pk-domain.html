Change Domain Extension From .com to .pk, or Back: SEO Risks, Email and Redirects

Kamboh Associates Digital team
Updated 3 November 2026
6 min read
Lahore, Pakistan
Short answer

A change of domain extension from .com to .pk, or the reverse, is a full site move. It can make sense if your market changed, but it carries short-term ranking risk and email set-up work. If you switch, keep both domains, redirect every page with a 301 and use Search Console's Change of Address tool. Most moves settle within weeks.

Key Takeaways

  • Switch only for a real reason, such as a mainly Pakistani or mainly international audience, not for a small gain.
  • A .pk domain signals Pakistan to visitors and Google; a .com is neutral and familiar worldwide.
  • Treat the switch as a site move: page-by-page 301 redirects and the Change of Address tool.
  • Plan email carefully; business mailboxes, forwarding and DNS records all change with the domain.
  • Keep renewing the old domain, and buy the other extension anyway to protect your name.

Why Do Businesses Consider a Change of Domain Extension?

Usually because the business has changed. A shop that started with a .com now sells only in Pakistan and wants a local look. An exporter on .pk now sells mainly to buyers in Europe and the Gulf and wants a neutral international address. Sometimes the preferred name was taken on one extension and has since become available. Each of these is a valid reason, but a domain change is never free in effort, so it should solve a real problem.

Google uses country-code domains like .pk as a signal that a site targets that country, which can help local results but may make international ranking harder. A .com can rank well in Pakistan with local content, a Pakistani address and a Google Business Profile. What Are the SEO Risks of Switching?

Any domain move can cause a temporary drop in traffic while Google processes the redirects and transfers signals to the new address. The risk grows if redirects are missing, sent to the homepage only, or removed too early. Links from other websites still point to the old domain, so the old domain must keep redirecting for a long time. Google's site move documentation is clear that well-implemented moves usually recover, but some fluctuation is normal for a few weeks.

Do Not Forget Email

Business email addresses change with the domain, such as info@business.com becoming info@business.pk. Plan the email move separately: set up mailboxes on the new domain, configure SPF, DKIM and DMARC records so mail is not marked as spam, forward the old addresses to the new ones for a long period and update email signatures, invoices and printed material. Our guide to business email on your own domain explains these records.

Steps for a Safe Switch

  1. Register the new domain in your own name, and keep the old one renewed.
  2. Copy the site to the new domain and update internal links and canonical tags.
  3. Map every old URL to its new URL and set up 301 redirects.
  4. Verify both domains in Search Console and use the Change of Address tool.
  5. Submit the new sitemap and monitor the Pages and Performance reports.
  6. Move email with forwarding, and update your Google Business Profile, social profiles and directories.

Choosing the Right Time to Switch

Avoid moving during your busiest season, such as before Eid for a clothing store or during admissions for a school, because even a short dip in traffic hurts most then. Plan the move for a quieter month, launch early in the week so problems are caught on working days, and watch Search Console daily for the first two weeks.

Is There an Alternative to Switching?

Often, yes. If you mainly want to protect your name, register the other extension and redirect it to your main site. If you want a Pakistan section on a .com site, a well-structured local section with Pakistani prices, contact details and a Google Business Profile may be enough. A switch is worth it when the extension itself is holding the business back.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is .pk better than .com for a Pakistani business?
A .pk domain clearly signals a Pakistani business to visitors and Google, which suits companies serving mainly Pakistan. A .com is neutral and familiar worldwide, which suits exporters and mixed audiences. Either can rank well in Pakistan with local content, a Pakistani address and a Business Profile.
Will changing from .com to .pk hurt my SEO?
There is usually a temporary fluctuation while Google processes the move. With page-by-page 301 redirects, the Change of Address tool in Search Console, an updated sitemap and redirects kept in place long-term, most sites recover. Missing or homepage-only redirects cause lasting losses.
What happens to my email if I change domain extension?
Your addresses change with the domain. Set up mailboxes on the new domain, configure SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, forward old addresses to new ones for a long period, and update signatures, invoices and printed material so customers and suppliers can still reach you.
Should I buy both .com and .pk domains?
Often yes, to protect your name. Use one as the main site and redirect the other to it. This stops competitors or squatters from taking the other extension and gives you the option to switch later without losing the name you have built.
How do I register a .pk domain?
A .pk or .com.pk domain is registered through PKNIC, Pakistan's registry, directly or through an authorised registrar. Register it in your own name with your own email as the contact, and check PKNIC's pages for current payment and renewal details.
How much does it cost to move a website to a new domain extension?
Apart from registering the new domain, the cost depends on the number of pages and email accounts to move. Small sites need modest work on redirects, Search Console and email; large stores take longer. We quote after reviewing the site, and post-move monitoring is part of maintenance from Rs 7,200 a month.
Kya .com se .pk par shift hona chahiye?
Sirf tab jab koi asal wajah ho, jaise aap ke customer ab sirf Pakistan mein hain. Yeh poori website ki muntaqili hai, is liye har page ka 301 redirect, Search Console ka Change of Address tool aur email ki tayyari zaroori hai. Purana domain renew karte rahein.
