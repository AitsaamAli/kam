Google Ads in Pakistan: Is Your Landing Page Ready Before You Spend a Rupee?

Published 30 October 2026
Updated 30 October 2026

A Google Ads landing page must match the ad's promise, load quickly on phones, state the offer and price signal clearly, and make calling or messaging easy. Set up conversion tracking first, so you know which clicks become enquiries. Google rates landing page experience as 1 of 3 Quality Score parts. Our landing pages start from Rs 22,500.

Key Takeaways

  • Sending ad clicks to a generic homepage wastes budget; match each ad to a relevant page.
  • Google describes landing page experience as how relevant and useful the page is to people who click.
  • Set up conversion tracking before spending, or you cannot tell what works.
  • Phone calls and WhatsApp clicks are conversions too; track them.
  • Start with a small daily budget and a few tightly focused keywords.

Why Check Your Google Ads Landing Page First?

Many Pakistani small businesses try Google Ads, spend a few thousand rupees, get clicks but no customers, and decide ads do not work. Often the ads were fine; the page people landed on was not. A Google Ads landing page has one job: turn a paid click into an enquiry or sale. If it is slow, confusing or unrelated to the ad, the money is wasted regardless of how good the keywords were.

This guide is part of our planning series, which begins with getting your website on Google. Our landing page cost guide covers building one.

What Does Google Say About Landing Pages?

Google Ads Help describes Quality Score as having three components: expected clickthrough rate, ad relevance and landing page experience, which it defines as how relevant and useful your landing page is to people who click your ad. Each is rated above average, average or below average compared with other advertisers. Google notes that Quality Score is a diagnostic tool and is not itself an auction input. Even so, a below-average rating points to something worth improving.

How Should Conversion Tracking Be Set Up?

Google Ads conversion tracking can measure website actions such as form submissions, calls from ads, calls to a number on your site, taps on a phone number on mobile, and offline conversions you upload. Google Analytics key events can also be imported. For Pakistani businesses, add WhatsApp button clicks as a website action. Test every conversion before launching ads, because untracked campaigns are guesswork. Our WhatsApp button guide explains click tracking.

Should Each Ad Group Have Its Own Page?

Ideally, yes, for your main services. Someone searching "AC repair Johar Town" should land on an AC repair page mentioning the area, not a general homepage listing ten services. Our service page guide shows the structure.

How Much Should You Spend at First?

Start with a small daily budget you are comfortable losing while you learn, a few tightly focused keywords, and location targeting that matches where you actually serve. Review search terms weekly, add negative keywords for irrelevant searches, and increase budget only for keywords that bring tracked enquiries. Ad costs are set by Google's auction and vary by industry and city.

What Are Common Mistakes?

  • Sending all ads to the homepage.
  • No call or WhatsApp button on mobile.
  • Targeting all of Pakistan when you serve one city.
  • Broad keywords that attract job seekers or students.
  • Judging success by clicks instead of enquiries.

What Happens After the Click Matters Too

An enquiry from an ad is only valuable if someone answers it quickly. Make sure calls are picked up during ad hours, WhatsApp messages get replies within minutes, and form enquiries are followed up the same day. Schedule ads to run when your team is available, rather than overnight when nobody can respond.

What Privacy Points Apply?

Conversion tracking uses cookies and tags. Mention them in your privacy policy, never send personal details such as phone numbers to ad platforms as plain data, and follow consent rules if you target overseas audiences.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a good Google Ads landing page?
It matches the ad's promise, loads quickly on phones, explains the offer with a price signal, shows call and WhatsApp buttons without scrolling, includes trust signals such as reviews and an address, focuses on one goal, and has conversion tracking set up and tested.
What is landing page experience in Google Ads?
Google Ads Help describes it as how relevant and useful your landing page is to people who click your ad. It is one of three Quality Score components, alongside expected clickthrough rate and ad relevance, each rated against other advertisers showing for the same searches.
Do I need conversion tracking before running Google Ads?
Yes. Without it you cannot tell which keywords and ads bring enquiries. Google Ads can track website actions, calls from ads and your site, phone taps on mobile and offline conversions. For Pakistani businesses, track WhatsApp clicks too, and test everything before launching.
Why are my Google Ads getting clicks but no enquiries?
Common causes are sending traffic to a generic homepage, slow mobile pages, no visible call or WhatsApp button, targeting areas you do not serve, broad keywords attracting the wrong people, and unclear offers or prices. Fix the landing page before increasing spend.
How much should a small business spend on Google Ads in Pakistan?
Start with a small daily budget you are comfortable losing while learning, use a few focused keywords and accurate location targeting, then increase spend only for keywords that bring tracked enquiries. Click costs are set by Google's auction and vary by industry and city.
Should each Google Ads campaign have its own landing page?
For your main services, ideally yes. A searcher looking for a specific service in a specific area should land on a page about exactly that, which improves relevance and conversions. A general homepage listing many services usually performs worse for paid traffic.
Google Ads chalane se pehle website mein kya check karein?
Landing page ad ke wade ke mutabiq ho, mobile par tez khule, qeemat ka andaza de, aur call aur WhatsApp ke button upar hi nazar aayen. Ads se pehle conversion tracking zaroor lagayen. Shuru mein kam budget, chand khaas keywords aur sirf apne shehar ko target karein.
