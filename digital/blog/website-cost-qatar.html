Website Design Cost in Qatar: Local Price Signals and Working With a Lahore Team

Published 30 October 2026
Updated 30 October 2026

Reliable published figures for website design cost in Qatar are scarce. A Qatar Living services page says most small business brochure sites fall around QAR 3,000 to 6,000, while agency listings show starting prices from about €500 to several thousand euros. A Lahore team quotes in rupees, with business sites from Rs 85,500.

Key Takeaways

  • Few dated, independent price sources exist for Qatar; treat published ranges as signals only.
  • Compare written quotes against one brief rather than relying on averages.
  • Bilingual Arabic and English sites need right-to-left design, not only translation.
  • Doha is two hours behind Lahore, giving a near-full overlap of working hours.
  • Keep the domain, hosting and code in your own company's name.

What Does a Bilingual Qatari Site Need?

Many Qatari businesses serve Arabic-speaking citizens and a large English-speaking expatriate population, so bilingual sites are common. Done properly, this means a mirrored right-to-left layout for Arabic, fonts that render clearly on phones, separate URLs per language with correct language tags, and a language switch that keeps visitors on the same page. Our Arabic RTL guide explains the details.

How Does the Time Difference Work?

Qatar runs on UTC+3 and Pakistan on UTC+5, with no daylight saving in either, so Lahore is two hours ahead of Doha. When it is 9:00 AM in Doha, it is 11:00 AM in Lahore. Our office hours, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Monday to Saturday Pakistan time (Friday until 12:30 PM), cover most of a Qatari working day. Note that the Qatari weekend and Pakistani office days differ; agree meeting days at the start.

How Should You Brief Providers?

  1. List pages, languages and must-have features.
  2. Say who will write and translate content.
  3. Name integrations: booking, payments, WhatsApp, CRM.
  4. State your deadline and any launch event.
  5. Ask for a fixed price, milestones, exclusions and ownership terms.

What Should You Check Before Hiring Offshore?

  • Live examples of bilingual or Arabic sites.
  • Who your day-to-day contact is, and their response time.
  • Milestone payments rather than full payment upfront; we work with a 30 to 40 percent deposit.
  • Domain and hosting registered to your company.
  • Post-launch support terms.

Which Features Push a Qatari Quote Up?

The jump from a simple brochure figure to a real quote usually comes from a handful of items: a custom design instead of a template, a second language with a mirrored layout, online booking or payments, links with a CRM or WhatsApp, and content writing or translation by the provider. Each of these is reasonable to pay for, but ask for them as separate lines in the quote, so you can decide which to keep and compare providers fairly. Content is often the hidden delay: if you are writing in two languages, prepare the text early, because translation and review take longer than most owners expect.

What About Customer Data?

Qatar has its own personal data protection law. Ask your adviser what it requires for your website forms, analytics and hosting location, and share that with your developer early.
