How Much Does a .pk Domain Cost?

PKNIC is the registry for .pk domains. Its own website states the current fees:

RegistrantFeeBilling
Based in PakistanRs 2,100 per domain per yearCharged every two years; can be prepaid up to ten years
Based outside Pakistan$15.99 per domain per yearUnchanged in the August 2026 revision

The Pakistan fee rose by Rs 300, from Rs 1,800, on 1 August 2026. Because billing is biennial, registering a .pk domain directly means paying for two years at a time, which is Rs 4,200 at the current rate.

Many hosting companies also sell .pk domains as resellers. Their prices are higher because they include their margin and handle the paperwork for you. One reseller listed Rs 3,899 a year in late 2025, and some reseller pages still show older figures. Either route is legitimate; just make sure the domain is registered to your name and email.

Local or International Hosting?

If most of your visitors are in Pakistan, server location affects speed less than you might expect, because most good hosts use content delivery networks and caching. What matters more is:

  • Server quality: modern hardware, LiteSpeed or Nginx servers, and solid-state storage.
  • Support: can you reach someone quickly, in a language you are comfortable with?
  • Payment: local hosts accept bank transfers and wallets; international hosts usually need a card that works for foreign payments.
  • Backups: does the host keep daily backups, and can you restore them yourself?

Renewal Traps to Avoid

  • Cheap first year, expensive renewal. Ask for the renewal price in writing.
  • Free domain with hosting. Often free only for the first year, and sometimes registered to the host, not you.
  • Auto-renew turned off. An expired domain takes your website and email offline and can be bought by someone else.
  • Accounts in the developer's name. If your developer leaves, you may lose access to your own domain.

How to Register a .pk Domain Step by Step

  1. Check availability on the PKNIC website or through a reseller.
  2. Create an account in your own name, with a business email you control.
  3. Submit the registration and pay the fee for at least the two-year billing period.
  4. Point the domain's name servers to your hosting company.
  5. Set up business email (for example info@yourcompany.pk) and turn on auto-renew or set calendar reminders.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a .pk domain cost in 2026?
PKNIC charges Pakistani registrants Rs 2,100 per domain per year since 1 August 2026, billed every two years, so Rs 4,200 per billing period. Foreign registrants pay $15.99 a year. Resellers charge more for handling the registration.
.pk domain kaise le?
PKNIC ki website ya kisi reseller se naam check karein, apne naam aur email par account banayein, kam az kam do saal ki fees (Rs 4,200, yani Rs 2,100 saalana) ada karein, phir domain ko apni hosting se connect karein aur auto-renew on rakhein.
Is .pk or .com better for a Pakistani business?
Use .pk for a business that serves Pakistan and .com if you sell internationally. Many businesses register both. Google ranks either normally; content, speed and relevance matter far more than the extension.
How much is web hosting in Pakistan per year?
Shared hosting suitable for most business websites costs about Rs 5,500 to Rs 15,000 a year in Pakistan. Busy online stores and web applications may need VPS or cloud hosting, which typically costs Rs 25,000 or more a year.
Should my developer register the domain for me?
They can do the work, but the domain must be registered in your name and email. If it is registered to the developer, you may lose your website and email address if the relationship ends.
Is hosting included in a website price?
Usually not. Most professional developers recommend you buy hosting in your own name and pay the host directly, so you stay in control. Some offer hosting as part of a maintenance plan; check whose name the account is in.
What happens if my domain expires?
Your website and email stop working, and after a grace period the domain can be registered by someone else. Turn on auto-renew or set reminders, and keep your registrar contact email up to date.
Do I need to pay for an SSL certificate?
Usually not. Most hosting companies include a free SSL certificate, which gives your site the padlock and https address. Paid certificates are only needed in special cases.
