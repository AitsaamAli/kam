WordPress Is Free. Your Website Is Not. Here Is Why.

WordPress software costs nothing to download, but a working business website needs more than the software:

  • A theme controls the design. Good premium themes carry a one-time licence fee in US dollars and include updates and support for a period.
  • Plugins add features: contact forms, SEO tools, caching, backups, security and WooCommerce extensions. Many have free versions, but the features businesses need often sit in paid plans billed yearly.
  • Hosting runs the site. Shared plans in Pakistan typically cost Rs 5,500 to Rs 15,000 a year.
  • A domain gives the site its address. A .pk domain costs Rs 2,100 a year at the PKNIC registry, billed in two-year blocks.
  • Skilled time to configure, design, add content, test and secure the site. This is the main part of what you pay a developer for.

The Nulled Theme Problem

Very cheap WordPress quotes are sometimes cheap because they use "nulled" themes and plugins: paid products with the licence check removed and shared for free online. They look identical on day one, but:

  • They can contain hidden code that injects spam links or redirects visitors.
  • They never receive security updates, so known vulnerabilities stay open.
  • You have no right to use them, and no support if they break.

Ask every developer for the names of the theme and paid plugins, and for the licence keys to be registered to your email. A professional developer will not hesitate.

Adding WooCommerce: What Changes in the Price?

WooCommerce turns WordPress into an online store. It has no monthly platform fee and no extra fee on payments, which is why it suits many Pakistani stores that rely on cash on delivery and local wallets. Building a store takes more work than a brochure site because each product needs data and photos, and payments, couriers and order emails need setting up and testing. See our e-commerce price guide for a full comparison with Shopify.

Keeping a WordPress Site Fast

A WordPress site can be quick or painfully slow depending on how it is built. Heavy page-builder plugins, unoptimised images and dozens of plugins slow pages down, especially for visitors on mobile data. A well-built site uses a lightweight theme, compressed modern image formats, caching and only the plugins it needs. Ask your developer to show you a mobile speed test before final payment.

Checklist Before You Hire a WordPress Developer

  1. Which theme and paid plugins will be used, and will the licences be in my name?
  2. Will I get an administrator account, and who else will have access?
  3. How will backups be taken, and where will they be stored?
  4. Who updates WordPress, the theme and plugins after launch?
  5. How fast does the site load on a mobile phone?
  6. Are the domain and hosting registered to me?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a WordPress website cost in Pakistan?
In 2026 a WordPress business website in Pakistan typically costs Rs 25,000 to Rs 150,000, with simple theme-based sites around Rs 35,000 to Rs 80,000 and custom designs higher. Our WordPress starter site is Rs 40,500 and our business site Rs 85,500.
Is WordPress really free?
The WordPress software is free, but a business website also needs a theme, often some paid plugins, hosting, a domain and someone to build and maintain it. These make up the real cost of a WordPress site.
WordPress website banwane ki qeemat kya hai?
Pakistan mein WordPress website aam tor par Rs 25,000 se Rs 150,000 tak mein banti hai. Hamari starter WordPress website Rs 40,500 aur business website Rs 85,500 ki hai. Hosting aur domain ka saalana kharcha alag hai.
What is a nulled WordPress theme and why avoid it?
A nulled theme is a paid theme with its licence check removed and shared illegally. It can contain malicious code, never gets security updates and gives you no support. Always ask for licensed themes and plugins registered to your email.
How much is WordPress hosting in Pakistan?
Shared hosting plans suitable for most WordPress business sites typically cost about Rs 5,500 to Rs 15,000 a year in Pakistan. Busy WooCommerce stores may need VPS or cloud hosting, which costs more.
Do WordPress websites need maintenance?
Yes. WordPress core, themes and plugins release updates regularly, often for security. Sites left un-updated for months are the most common hacking targets. Update monthly yourself or use a maintenance plan.
Can I edit my WordPress website myself?
Yes. That is one of WordPress's main strengths. A good developer sets up editable blocks and gives you a short training session so you can change text, images and posts without technical help.
Is WordPress good for an online store in Pakistan?
Yes, through WooCommerce. It has no monthly platform fee or extra transaction fee, works with local payment and courier plugins, and suits stores that rely on cash on delivery. It does need regular updates and good hosting.
