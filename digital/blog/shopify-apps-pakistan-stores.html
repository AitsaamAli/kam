Shopify Apps for Pakistan: Essential Apps for Pakistani Stores, and Which to Skip

Kamboh Associates Digital team
Updated 30 October 2026
6 min read
Lahore, Pakistan
Short answer

The useful Shopify apps for Pakistan stores fall into 5 groups: cash on delivery and order confirmation, courier booking, WhatsApp chat, reviews, and image or SEO help. Choose by need, local support, monthly dollar cost and speed impact. Most stores need fewer apps than they install; every app adds cost and can slow pages.

Key Takeaways

  • Install apps for a specific problem, not because a list said they were best.
  • Many apps charge monthly in US dollars, which adds up alongside Shopify's own plan fee.
  • Each app can add scripts that slow your store; test speed before and after.
  • Local courier and COD apps should be checked for support quality and how they handle your data.
  • Uninstalling an app does not always remove its code from your theme; check afterwards.

How Should You Choose Shopify Apps for Pakistan?

Shopify's App Store has thousands of apps, and every "best apps" list recommends different ones. For a Pakistani store, the right approach is the opposite of installing everything: list the problems you actually have, install one app per problem, and remove anything that is not earning its keep. We do not rank named apps here, because quality, prices and support change often; instead this guide sets out the categories that matter and the criteria for judging any app.

For the bigger picture of launching, read how to start an online store in Pakistan. If you are still choosing a platform, see Shopify vs WooCommerce.

What Criteria Should You Use?

  1. Need: what specific problem does it fix, and could the theme or a small code change fix it instead?
  2. Cost: the monthly charge in US dollars, any usage fees, and whether the free plan's limits fit your volume.
  3. Speed: how many scripts it adds; test page speed before and after installing.
  4. Local fit: support for Pakistani couriers, phone formats, Urdu text and cash on delivery.
  5. Support: recent reviews mentioning response times, and support hours relative to Pakistan time.
  6. Data: what customer data it accesses and stores, and whether that is necessary.

How Do App Costs Add Up?

Shopify's own plans, according to its pricing page checked on 30 October 2026, start at $29 a month for Basic when billed yearly ($39 monthly), $79 for Grow and $299 for Advanced, with third-party transaction fees of 2%, 1% and 0.6% when Shopify Payments is not used. Shopify Payments is not available in Pakistan, so those fees apply to Pakistani stores using other gateways. Apps add their own monthly charges on top, listed on each app's App Store page. Five apps at modest monthly prices can cost more than the plan itself, so total them before installing.

How Do Apps Affect Store Speed?

Many apps load extra JavaScript and styles on every page, even pages where the app does nothing. On mid-range Android phones over mobile data, this adds up quickly. Run a speed test on a product page, install the app, and test again. If the difference is large, ask the developer whether the script can load only where needed, or look for a lighter option. Our mobile speed guide explains what to measure.

Which Apps Can Most Stores Skip?

  • Multiple pop-up apps doing the same job.
  • Fake urgency tools such as invented "15 people are viewing this" counters, which damage trust.
  • Apps that duplicate features your theme already has.
  • Currency converters, if you only sell in Pakistan.

What About Customer Data?

When you install an app, Shopify shows what data it can access. COD confirmation and courier apps need names, phones and addresses; a countdown timer should not. Decline apps asking for more than they need, and remove apps you no longer use so they stop receiving data.

What Happens When You Uninstall an App?

Some apps leave code snippets in your theme after removal, which can keep slowing pages or cause errors. After uninstalling, check the theme files or ask your developer to clean up. Our Shopify stores, from Rs 157,500, start with only the apps a store needs. See e-commerce development.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Shopify apps do Pakistani stores really need?
Most need a cash on delivery confirmation tool, a courier booking app if volumes are high, a WhatsApp chat button, and a reviews app once orders are regular. Image optimisation and SEO helpers are sometimes useful. Install only for a specific problem and remove apps that are not used.
How much do Shopify apps cost?
Each app sets its own monthly price in US dollars, shown on its App Store listing, and some have free plans with limits. These charges come on top of Shopify's plan, which starts at $29 a month on Basic billed yearly. Total all app charges before installing.
Do Shopify apps slow down my store?
Many do, because they add scripts to every page. Test a product page's speed before and after installing each app. If the slowdown is large, ask the developer whether the script can load only where needed, or choose a lighter alternative.
Does Shopify charge transaction fees in Pakistan?
Shopify Payments is not available in Pakistan, so stores using other gateways pay Shopify's third-party transaction fees: 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow and 0.6% on Advanced, according to Shopify's pricing page checked 30 October 2026, in addition to the gateway's own charges.
Do I need an app for a WhatsApp button on Shopify?
No. A WhatsApp click-to-chat button can be added to the theme with a few lines of code, without an app or monthly fee. An app may still help if you want extra features such as different numbers for different pages or a chat widget with opening hours.
What should I do after uninstalling a Shopify app?
Check whether the app left code in your theme files, because leftover snippets can slow pages or cause errors. Remove them or ask your developer to do it, and confirm the app no longer has access to your store's customer data.
Shopify store ke liye kaun si apps zaroori hain?
Aam taur par COD confirm karne wali app, courier booking, WhatsApp button aur reviews kaafi hain. Har app ka mahana kharch dollar mein hota hai aur kuch apps website ko slow karti hain, is liye install se pehle aur baad speed check karein. Jo app kaam na aaye usay hata dein.
