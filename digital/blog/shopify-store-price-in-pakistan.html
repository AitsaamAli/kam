Shopify Store Price in Pakistan: Setup, Monthly Plan and Hidden Costs

Kamboh Associates Digital team
Updated 28 October 2026
7 min read
Short answer

A Shopify store in Pakistan has three costs: the plan (Basic $29 a month billed yearly or $39 monthly, Grow $79 or $105, Advanced $299 or $399), setup by a developer, and fees. Shopify Payments is not available in Pakistan, so card and wallet payments use a third-party gateway and Basic adds a 2% Shopify fee on them. Cash on delivery carries no Shopify fee.

Key Takeaways

  • Shopify bills Pakistani stores in US dollars; your rupee cost moves with the exchange rate and your bank's card charges.
  • Yearly billing is about 25% cheaper than monthly billing on every standard plan.
  • Because Shopify Payments is unavailable in Pakistan, online payments go through third-party gateways and attract a Shopify fee of 2%, 1% or 0.6% depending on the plan.
  • Cash on delivery orders do not attract Shopify's transaction fee.
  • Paid apps for reviews, courier booking or size charts are where Shopify budgets usually overrun.

Most new Pakistani stores start on Basic. Moving to Grow starts to pay for itself only when the saving on the transaction fee (1% instead of 2%) exceeds the higher subscription, which for many stores means online-paid sales well above a few hundred thousand rupees a month.

Why Payment Fees Work Differently in Pakistan

In countries where Shopify Payments is available, merchants who use it pay no extra Shopify transaction fee. Shopify Payments is not available to businesses based in Pakistan. A Pakistani store therefore collects online payments through a third-party provider, and Shopify adds its own fee on those payments: 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow and 0.6% on Advanced. That is on top of the gateway's own charge, which for wallets and cards in Pakistan is commonly reported at around 1.5% to 3.5%.

Cash on delivery is treated as a manual payment method, and Shopify does not charge its transaction fee on it. This is one reason many Pakistani Shopify stores launch with COD and add online payments once volumes justify the fees.

How Much Does Shopify Setup Cost in Pakistan?

The subscription gives you the platform; someone still has to build the store. Published 2026 guides from Pakistani agencies put a Shopify store build at roughly Rs 60,000 to Rs 250,000 or more, depending on design and features. At the very bottom of the market, marketplace listings advertise "Shopify setup" for a few thousand rupees. Those offers usually mean installing a free theme and nothing more.

The Hidden Cost: Shopify Apps

Shopify's app store is its strength and its budget risk. Features that Pakistani stores commonly add through apps include:

  • Courier booking and tracking for local couriers.
  • COD order confirmation by WhatsApp or SMS.
  • Product reviews with photos.
  • Size charts and variant swatches.
  • Abandoned-cart reminders.
  • Urdu or multi-language support.

Many apps charge $5 to $30 a month each. Five apps can add more to your monthly bill than the Basic plan itself. A good developer chooses apps carefully and builds simple features into the theme instead of adding an app for each one.

Is Shopify the Right Choice for a Pakistani Store?

Choose Shopify if: you want low technical maintenance, plan to sell abroad as well as in Pakistan, and are comfortable with a US-dollar subscription.

Consider WooCommerce if: most sales will be COD and local wallets, you want to avoid platform transaction fees, and you have a developer or maintenance plan for updates. Our e-commerce price guide compares both platforms side by side.

What to Check Before Hiring a Shopify Developer

  • The Shopify account and store must be created under your email, with the developer added as staff or as a collaborator.
  • Ask which apps they will install and their monthly cost.
  • Confirm which payment methods and courier will be live at launch.
  • Ask for training on adding products and processing orders.
  • Get the scope, price and milestones in writing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Shopify cost per month in Pakistan?
Shopify's Basic plan costs $29 a month billed yearly or $39 billed monthly for Pakistani stores, according to a pricing check dated 9 October 2026. Grow is $79 or $105 and Advanced $299 or $399. Prices are in US dollars and can change, so confirm at checkout.
Is Shopify Payments available in Pakistan?
No. Shopify Payments is not available to businesses based in Pakistan. Online payments go through a third-party gateway, and Shopify adds a transaction fee of 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow or 0.6% on Advanced on those payments.
Does Shopify charge a fee on cash on delivery orders?
No. Cash on delivery is a manual payment method, and Shopify does not charge its third-party transaction fee on it. You still pay the courier's delivery and COD charges.
How much does a Shopify developer charge in Pakistan?
Published 2026 guides put a professional Shopify store build at roughly Rs 80,000 to Rs 200,000, with custom builds higher and bare theme installs much lower. Our e-commerce package is Rs 157,500 and can be built on Shopify or WooCommerce.
Shopify store banane ka kharcha kitna hai?
Shopify ke teen kharche hain: plan (Basic $29 mahana saalana billing par), developer se store banwana (aam tor par Rs 80,000 se Rs 200,000), aur fees (online payment par Basic plan mein 2% Shopify fee + gateway fee). COD par Shopify fee nahi lagti.
Can I pay for Shopify from Pakistan?
Yes, usually with a debit or credit card enabled for international transactions. Shopify bills in US dollars, so your bank converts the amount and may add its own charges and taxes. Check your bank's terms before choosing yearly billing.
Is Shopify or WooCommerce better for Pakistan?
Shopify is better if you want low maintenance and plan to sell internationally. WooCommerce is usually cheaper to run for stores relying on cash on delivery and local wallets, because there is no platform subscription or extra transaction fee.
Which Shopify plan should a new Pakistani store choose?
Most new stores should start on Basic billed yearly. Move to Grow only when the 1% saving on gateway payments exceeds the higher subscription, which usually needs substantial monthly online-paid sales.
