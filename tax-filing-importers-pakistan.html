Tax Filing for Importers in Pakistan
Complete 2026 Guide

📅 May 31, 2026 ⏱ 9 min read ✍️ Kamboh Associates

Importers in Pakistan deal with one of the most complex tax environments — multiple taxes are collected at the import stage (customs duty, sales tax, advance income tax), and then again on domestic transactions. Understanding which taxes are final and which are adjustable is critical for accurate annual tax filing and avoiding overpayment.

Key principle: Advance income tax collected at import under Section 148 is generally adjustable for industrial importers and acts as minimum tax for commercial importers. In both cases, you still need to file an annual income tax return to reconcile everything.

Section 148: Advance Tax at Import Stage

When you clear goods through Pakistani customs, the customs department collects advance income tax on your behalf:

Importer TypeTax RateTax Treatment
Industrial Importer (filer)5.5%Adjustable against annual liability
Commercial Importer (filer)6%Minimum tax (can be adjusted up)
Industrial Importer (non-filer)8%Adjustable
Commercial Importer (non-filer)9%Minimum tax

Industrial vs Commercial Importer

  • Industrial importer: Imports raw materials, components, or machinery for use in your own manufacturing/production business. The Section 148 tax is adjustable — credited against your total income tax when you file your return.
  • Commercial importer: Imports finished goods for resale in the Pakistani market. The Section 148 tax acts as the minimum tax — you pay at least this amount. If your actual income tax liability (computed on net profit) is higher, you pay the difference.

Other Taxes at Import Stage

Beyond Section 148 income tax, importers pay multiple taxes when clearing goods:

  • Customs Duty: Based on HS code and applicable rates (0%–100%)
  • Sales Tax on Import: Standard 17% (or reduced rates for certain goods)
  • Additional Customs Duty (ACD): If applicable to product category
  • Regulatory Duty (RD): On specified goods
  • Withholding Tax (Section 148): Advance income tax as discussed above

Sales tax paid at import is generally input tax that you claim as credit against your output sales tax, reducing your monthly GST liability.

Deductible Expenses for Importers

  • Clearing agent fees (customs broker charges)
  • Freight and shipping costs
  • Port handling and demurrage charges
  • Bank charges (Letter of Credit, T/T fees)
  • Warehouse rent and storage costs
  • Import documentation costs
  • Staff salaries (buying, clearing, accounts teams)
  • Office rent and overheads

Annual Tax Filing for Importers

  1. Compile all Goods Declaration (GD) documents for the year
  2. Get customs WHT certificates (Section 148 tax paid) from your bank or customs
  3. Calculate total import turnover and gross profit on trading operations
  4. Deduct all documented business expenses to arrive at net profit
  5. Log in to FBR IRIS → File Form IT-2
  6. Declare import income under "Business Income" head
  7. Enter Section 148 tax paid as adjustable/minimum tax credit
  8. Pay balance tax if actual liability exceeds credits
  9. Submit return before September 30 deadline

Refund opportunity: If your Section 148 advance tax paid exceeds your actual income tax liability (common for importers with thin margins), you are entitled to a tax refund. File your return accurately and apply for the refund through IRIS.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is advance income tax at import stage in Pakistan?
Under Section 148, customs collects advance income tax when goods are cleared at the port. For industrial importers this is 5.5% and for commercial importers it is 6% of the import value — this is adjustable against the annual income tax return.
Is the tax paid at import stage final or adjustable?
For industrial importers (importing for own use/manufacturing), the tax is adjustable. For commercial importers (importing for resale), the tax is generally the minimum tax, but can be adjusted if the normal tax liability is higher.
Do importers need to file an annual income tax return?
Yes. All importers must file an annual income tax return with FBR to maintain ATL status and declare all income sources, even if the import-stage tax is the minimum/final tax.
