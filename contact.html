Pakistan Tax Calendar 2026-27 — Key Dates Every Taxpayer Must Know

Missing FBR deadlines triggers automatic penalties and interest. Save these dates to avoid unnecessary costs.

DeadlineWhat Is DueWho It Applies To
September 30, 2026Income Tax Return TY 2026Salaried individuals, AOP, sole proprietors

Penalties for Missing FBR Deadlines

  • Late return filing — Rs.1,000 per month for individuals; Rs.10,000 per month for companies
  • Late tax payment — 12% per annum markup on unpaid tax
  • Late WHT statement — 0.1% per day of outstanding WHT amount
  • Late sales tax return — Rs.10,000 per late return
  • Failure to register for NTN — Up to Rs.50,000 penalty under Section 181
