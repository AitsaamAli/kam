Essential Tax Deductions for 2026

May 19, 2026 Kamboh Associates 5 min read

As we progress through 2026, understanding the tax deductions available to your business is crucial. This guide outlines essential deductions that Pakistani businesses should leverage.

1. Business Operational Expenses

All ordinary and necessary expenses incurred in running your business are deductible:

  • Salaries and wages for employees
  • Rent for office or commercial space
  • Utilities (electricity, water, internet)
  • Office supplies and equipment
  • Professional services (accounting, legal)

2. Depreciation and Capital Assets

Depreciation allows you to deduct the cost of business assets over their useful life. Keep detailed records of machinery, equipment, vehicles, and computer systems.

3. Business Interest Expenses

Interest paid on loans used for business purposes is generally deductible, including loan interest, credit card interest, and overdraft charges.

4. Insurance Premiums

Business insurance is deductible: general liability, professional indemnity, workers' compensation, and property insurance.

5. Travel and Transportation

Business-related travel expenses are deductible when properly documented: airfare, accommodations, meals (50% deductible), and vehicle mileage.

Key Recommendations

  • Maintain meticulous records of all business expenses
  • Separate personal from business expenses
  • Consult a tax professional for compliance
  • Review quarterly for missed deductions
  • Stay updated on regulatory changes
