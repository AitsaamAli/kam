Common Questions

When should I file my return?
File between July 1 and September 30 each year. Filing after deadline incurs penalties. It's best to file by September 15 to avoid rush.
What if my employer already deducted tax?
You must still file your personal return. You'll likely get a refund if tax was over-deducted. You remain on Active Taxpayer List only after personal filing.
Can I file for multiple years at once?
Yes. Back returns can be filed, though penalties apply for late filing. Contact us for multi-year filing packages.
What documents do I need?
Salary slips, NIC, investment receipts (NSCs, SBs), rental income proofs, bank statements showing major transactions.
