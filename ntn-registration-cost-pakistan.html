NTN Registration Cost in Pakistan 2026 — FBR Portal is FREE, But Here's What Consultants Charge

May 29, 2026
Kamboh Associates
5 min read
Lahore, Pakistan

Bahut logon ke zarehen mein yeh sawaal hota hai — "NTN registration ka kitna kharcha hai?" Short answer yeh hai ke FBR IRIS portal par NTN registration bilkul free hai — koi government fee nahi. Lekin agar aap kisi consultant ke zariye karwa rahe hain, to unki fee alag hoti hai. Is guide mein dono scenarios cover karenge — DIY aur professional.

FBR Par NTN Registration — Zero Government Fee

FBR ne NTN registration deliberately free rakkha hai. Maqsad yeh hai ke zyada se zyada people tax system mein aayein. iris.fbr.gov.pk par ja kar, "New Registration" select kar ke, aap apna CNIC number use kar ke register kar sakte hain — bilkul free.

Individual ke liye NTN asal mein usi ka CNIC number hota hai. Business NTN alag generate hota hai — lekin woh bhi FBR ki taraf se free hai. Koi stamp duty, koi processing fee, kuch bhi nahi.

FBR Official Policy: NTN registration sirf CNIC se hoti hai — Individual, AOP, Company sab ke liye FBR ka apna portal free hai. Agar koi aapko "FBR registration fee" bata raha hai to woh misleading kar raha hai.

Consultant Charge Kyun Karta Hai?

Yeh ek fair sawaal hai. FBR free hai — to consultant fee kyun?

Consultant ki value in cheezon mein hai: sahi form fill karna, documents check karna, jurisdiction code select karna, business address verify karna, aur agar application reject ho to resubmit karna. Aksar log pehli baar akele try karte hain, galti hoti hai, NTN mein wrong address ya wrong category enter ho jata hai — baad mein correction karna aur mushkil ho jata hai.

Aik real example: Humara ek client tha jo khud ne business NTN register kiya — business type galat select kiya. Months baad FBR ne notice bheja. Correction ki process mein time aur kharcha dono zyada lage. Had they come to us first, it would've been done correctly the first time.

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal

Kya NTN registration ke liye FBR koi fee leta hai?
Bilkul nahi. FBR IRIS portal par NTN registration completely free hai. Salaried, business, freelancer — kisi ke liye bhi government fee nahi. Jo amount consultant ko dete hain woh unki service charge hai.
Individual NTN number kya hota hai?
Pakistan mein individual ka NTN asal mein unka CNIC number hi hota hai. Jab aap IRIS par register karte hain, to system aapka CNIC ko NTN ke tor par assign kar deta hai. Business NTN alag 7-digit number hota hai.
Company NTN registration fee kitni hai?
Company ka NTN SECP incorporation ke saath automatically generate hota hai — FBR side free. Agar aap SECP ke zariye company register karwa rahe hain, to SECP ki apni fees hoti hain (Rs. 1,500–3,000 + consultant charges). Standalone NTN for existing company: Rs. 4,000–8,000 consultant fee.
NTN register karne ke baad kya karna hota hai?
NTN ke baad FBR mein enrollment hoti hai (agar pehle se nahi), phir income tax return file karni hoti hai har saal September 30 tak. Return file karne se ATL (Active Taxpayer List) mein naam aata hai aur filer status milta hai.
NTN registration mein kitna time lagta hai?
Online registration same day ho jati hai — zyada se zyada 24 ghante. Manual ya complex business registration mein 3–7 working days lag sakte hain. Detailed NTN timeline guide padhein.
