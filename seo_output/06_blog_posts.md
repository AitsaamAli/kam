## Wealth Statement Pakistan 2025 — How to File It Correctly with FBR

Filing your income tax return in Pakistan without a wealth statement is incomplete — and FBR will reject it. Yet most Pakistanis either skip it, fill it incorrectly, or don't know what to include. This guide tells you everything.

### What is a Wealth Statement in Pakistan?

A Wealth Statement (officially called Statement of Assets and Liabilities) is a mandatory part of every income tax return filed on FBR Iris. It is a declaration of everything you own and everything you owe.

Every person filing an income tax return in Pakistan must submit a wealth statement — salaried employees, freelancers, business owners, and even housewives who file.

The purpose is simple: FBR wants to make sure your assets are consistent with your declared income. If you suddenly own a house worth PKR 2 crore but only declared PKR 5 lakh income, FBR will question the source.

### What Must Be Included in Your Wealth Statement?

**Assets (What You Own):**
- Immovable property (house, plot, commercial property) — at FBR value
- Vehicles (cars, motorcycles) — at current market value
- Bank accounts — closing balance on June 30
- Cash in hand
- Gold and jewelry — at market value
- Business capital / investments
- Shares in companies (SECP registered)
- Agricultural land
- Foreign assets and bank accounts (if any)

**Liabilities (What You Owe):**
- Bank loans (home loan, car loan, personal loan)
- Informal loans from family/friends
- Credit card outstanding balance
- Business liabilities

**Reconciliation:**
The key formula FBR checks:
```
Opening Net Worth + Total Income − Total Expenditure = Closing Net Worth
```
If the math doesn't add up, FBR sends a notice.

### Common Wealth Statement Mistakes Pakistanis Make

**Mistake 1: Not Declaring Spouse's Assets**
If you own a house jointly with your spouse, or if assets are registered in your spouse's name but purchased with your money, they must be declared in your wealth statement as "assets in the name of family members."

**Mistake 2: Wrong Property Valuation**
Many people declare property at the old DC rate. Since 2023, FBR accepts property at either the DC value OR FBR's own notified value — whichever is higher. Use the FBR property valuation table for your area.

**Mistake 3: Forgetting Cash in Hand**
Informal cash savings at home must be declared. If you later invest this cash, you need a trail — otherwise FBR treats it as undisclosed income.

**Mistake 4: Inconsistency Between Years**
If your 2024 wealth statement showed PKR 10 lakh in bank balance and your 2025 statement shows PKR 50 lakh — FBR will ask where the extra PKR 40 lakh came from. Be consistent and explainable.

**Mistake 5: Forgetting Foreign Income / Remittances**
Overseas Pakistanis sending remittances home must declare them. Family members receiving remittances should declare them as "gifts received" in their income section.

### How to File Wealth Statement on FBR Iris

1. Log in to iris.fbr.gov.pk with your NTN and password
2. Go to Declaration → Income Tax Return
3. Under the return form, find the "Wealth Statement" tab
4. Fill Assets tab: property, vehicles, bank accounts, cash, jewelry, investments
5. Fill Liabilities tab: loans, credit cards
6. System auto-calculates net worth
7. Check reconciliation — it must balance
8. Submit along with your income tax return

### Wealth Statement for Salaried Employees

Many salaried employees think they only need to declare their salary. Wrong. The wealth statement covers ALL assets, not just employment income.

Example:
- Annual salary: PKR 24 lakh
- Family expenditure: PKR 18 lakh
- Savings: PKR 6 lakh
- Opening bank balance: PKR 10 lakh
- Closing bank balance should be: PKR 16 lakh

If your actual closing balance is PKR 16 lakh — perfect, it reconciles. If it's more, explain the source.

### Penalties for Incorrect or Missing Wealth Statement

Under FBR's penalty schedule:
- Non-filing of wealth statement: PKR 50,000 per year
- Concealment of assets: 25% of the tax on concealed amount PLUS 100% penalty
- Unexplained assets: Taxed as income at the highest rate

These penalties are serious. A house worth PKR 50 lakh that you "forgot" to declare can result in PKR 12-15 lakh in taxes and penalties.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can a housewife file a wealth statement?**
A: Yes, and she should. Any Pakistani who files a tax return must include a wealth statement. For housewives with assets gifted by husband or received as inheritance, these must be declared.

**Q: Do I need to declare inherited property?**
A: Yes. Property received as inheritance must be declared in the year of inheritance at its FBR notified value.

**Q: What if I have foreign bank accounts?**
A: Yes, foreign bank accounts and assets must be declared. Pakistan is a signatory to the Common Reporting Standard (CRS), so foreign banks now report Pakistani account holders to FBR automatically.

**Q: My assets increased a lot this year — will FBR question me?**
A: If your asset increase is explained by your declared income, business profits, or documented gifts/inheritance — you're fine. FBR questions unexplained increases.
