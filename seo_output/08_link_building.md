## F) COMPETITOR BACKLINK GAP ANALYSIS (Free Tools)

### Step-by-Step Guide

**Step 1: Find Competitor Backlinks (Ubersuggest Free)**
1. Go to app.neilpatel.com/en/ubersuggest
2. Enter competitor domain (e.g., a competing tax consultant site)
3. Go to "Backlink Opportunity" tab
4. See which sites link to them but NOT to you → those are your targets

**Step 2: Ahrefs Free Version**
1. ahrefs.com/free-tools/backlink-checker
2. Enter competitor URL
3. See top 100 backlinks (free)
4. Identify patterns: guest posts, directory listings, news mentions

**Step 3: Moz Link Explorer (Free)**
1. moz.com/link-explorer
2. Enter your URL → see DA score
3. Enter competitor → compare their DA vs yours
4. Find sites linking to multiple competitors → priority targets

**Step 4: Google Search (Free)**
- Search: `site:propakistani.pk "tax consultant"` → see who's been covered
- Search: `"tax filing Pakistan" -site:fbr.gov.pk "contact us"` → find blogs with tax content
