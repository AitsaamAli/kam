Which Businesses Must Comply

FBR supervises three groups of Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions under the Anti-Money Laundering Act, 2010 and FBR's AML/CFT Regulations.

Real Estate Agents, Builders and Developers

Agents, and builders or developers when they deal in property for clients. See our real estate guide.

FAQ

Common Questions

Who has to register as a DNFBP with FBR?
FBR supervises real estate agents (including builders and developers when they deal in property), dealers in precious metals and stones who take cash of Rs 2 million or more in a transaction, and accountants not regulated by ICAP or ICMAP when they carry out the listed activities.
What does DNFBP compliance involve after registration?
Appointing a compliance officer, a written risk assessment and AML/CFT policy, customer due diligence, sanctions screening, reporting suspicious and large cash transactions to the FMU on goAML, and keeping records for the required period.
What is the penalty for not complying?
The AML/CFT Sanctions Rules 2020 (S.R.O. 950(I)/2020) allow penalties of up to Rs 100 million per violation, and significant sanctions are published with the business name.
Is goAML registration separate from FBR registration?
Yes. DNFBP registration is done with FBR on IRIS; goAML is the Financial Monitoring Unit's reporting portal, which needs its own registration.
How much does DNFBP compliance cost with Kamboh Associates?
A fixed fee, confirmed on WhatsApp before any work starts, based on your category and the size of your business.
Which FBR office handles DNFBPs in my city?
S.R.O. 1497(I)/2026 (2 September 2026) sets the jurisdiction of DNFBP authorities. Our city guides explain which directorate covers Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Peshawar and Quetta.
