Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

Answers to questions we get asked every day.

What documents do I need to provide?
For individuals: CNIC, salary certificate / pay slips, bank statement. For business: CNIC, business name, income/expense summary. We guide you through exactly what's needed after first contact.
Do you work for clients outside Lahore?
Yes — 100% online service. We serve clients across all of Pakistan (Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Quetta) and overseas Pakistanis (UK, UAE, USA, Canada, Saudi Arabia) via WhatsApp.
How long does tax return filing take?
Individual returns are typically filed within 2–4 hours of receiving your documents. Corporate returns with financial statements take 2–5 business days. Same-day filing available for urgent cases.
What payment methods do you accept?
Bank transfer (all major banks), Easypaisa, JazzCash, and Mezan Bank. No advance payment — we only collect after your return is filed and you have the confirmation.
Do your fees include previous year returns?
Each tax year is priced separately. If you need to file multiple years (e.g., 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 all at once), we offer a bulk discount. WhatsApp us for a combined quote.
What if I receive an FBR notice after filing?
For returns filed by us, we handle any subsequent FBR notices at no extra charge for the first year. We stand behind our work. For notices on returns filed elsewhere, our rate starts at Rs. 5,000.
