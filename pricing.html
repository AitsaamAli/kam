Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

Questions we get asked every day.

What documents do I need to provide?
For salaried: CNIC + salary certificate or payslips + bank statement. For business: CNIC + income/expense summary. For freelancers: CNIC + Payoneer/Wise/bank statements showing remittances. We guide you on exact requirements after first contact.
Do you serve clients outside Lahore?
Yes — 100% online service. We serve all of Pakistan (Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Faisalabad, Quetta) and overseas Pakistanis in UK, UAE, USA, Canada, Saudi Arabia, Australia via WhatsApp.
How long does filing take?
Individual returns: 2–4 hours after receiving documents. Corporate returns with financial statements: 2–5 business days. Same-day filing available for urgent cases — just mention it on WhatsApp.
What payment methods do you accept?
Bank transfer (all major banks), Easypaisa, JazzCash, and Mezan Bank. No advance payment required — we collect only after your return is filed and you have the confirmation.
Can you file returns for multiple previous years?
Yes. We file returns for all previous years including 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025. If you need multiple years filed together, we offer a bulk discount. WhatsApp us for a combined quote.
What if I receive an FBR notice after filing?
For returns filed by us, we handle any FBR notices at no extra charge within the first year. We stand fully behind our work. For notices on returns filed elsewhere, our fee starts at Rs. 5,000.
