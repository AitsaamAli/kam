# Client brief

- **Business:** Kamboh Associates — an FBR-registered/certified tax consultancy in Lahore, Pakistan, operating since 2008. Serves individuals, salaried employees, freelancers, SMEs, corporates, and overseas Pakistanis 100% remotely over WhatsApp (0328-4675162). ~5,000+ clients claimed, 347 Google reviews (verified real figure, normalized sitewide 2026-09 after an earlier inconsistency), 4.9★ average.
- **Website / repo:** https://kambohassociates.com — GitHub `AitsaamAli/kam`, branch `main`, auto-deploys to Vercel on push.
- **What it actually sells:** Income tax return filing (Rs. 3,500 salaried / Rs. 5,000 business-freelancer), NTN registration (Rs. 2,000), SECP private limited company registration (Rs. 15,000+ service fee), sales tax/STRN registration (Rs. 3,000 one-time + Rs. 3,000/mo filing), wealth statement filing (Rs. 2,000), FBR notice response/defense (Rs. 5,000+), corporate tax return (Rs. 18,000+), EOBI registration (Rs. 8,000 + Rs. 2,000/mo), withholding tax compliance, AOP registration/return (Rs. 8,000), import/export registration (Rs. 10,000+), tax exemption certificates, plus free tools (income tax calculator, salary calculator, GST calculator, income estimator).
- **Target markets:** Pakistan nationally (explicit city coverage: Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, Sialkot, Gujranwala, plus many smaller cities), and overseas Pakistanis specifically in Dubai/UAE, UK, Saudi Arabia, and USA. Content is English + Roman Urdu mixed register; no Urdu-script (اردو) content currently exists as a deliberate choice, unconfirmed whether tested against real query data.
- **Audience:** Price-sensitive Pakistani salaried individuals and small business owners, freelancers/creators (YouTubers, IT freelancers, gig-economy workers), SME owners across many professions (doctors, lawyers, IT professionals, retailers, restaurant owners, manufacturers, exporters, real estate agents), and overseas Pakistanis maintaining property/NTN/filing obligations back home. Buyer state when searching ranges from anxious/urgent (FBR notice received, deadline approaching) to comparison-shopping (pricing, "best consultant in X") to first-time/confused (never filed before, turning 18, first job).
- **Competitors:** Befiler, Pak Tax Services, JZARR Filers, TaxIt.pk, TaxFlow.com.pk, Difbr.pk, Oscar.pk, Switcher Techno, Tenco Consulting (identified via live competitor research during a prior content-gap project, 2026-08-13 — re-verify current positioning with a fresh live check before any competitor-facing deliverable, don't treat this list as current truth).
- **Goal:** Explicitly stated by the operator (2026-09): traffic has grown substantially (1,567 total pages, 1,403 of them blog posts) but too much of it is informational-only — the operator wants more commercial-intent, contact-driving content and conversion, not just pageviews. No specific numeric target given yet; ask if a number is wanted for the roadmap.
- **Constraints:** Near-zero budget — no paid SEO tools used to date (no Ahrefs/Semrush/paid rank tracker observed in this project's workflow), free-tier Vercel hosting, single-operator team (the user + Claude as the entire "team"). Per OPERATOR-CONTEXT.md's cross-project default, assume ~$5/month ceiling for anything paid unless told otherwise.
- **Stack / CMS:** Static hand-written HTML/CSS/JS — no framework, no `package.json`, no CMS. Every page is its own `.html` file (mostly a shared MINIFIED-family template: nav, topbar promo, breadcrumb, article body, sidebar, WhatsApp float button, footer). `blogs.html` holds a client-side `blogPosts` JS array (1,279 entries as of 2026-09-08) plus a static SEO-crawlable link list, both manually maintained. `sitemap.xml` (1,560 URLs) and `robots.txt` (fully open to Googlebot, Bingbot, and every major AI crawler — GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, etc., with only aggressive scrapers like MJ12bot/DotBot blocked) are both manually maintained flat files. `llms.txt` exists at the root with services, tools, key guides, city coverage, and contact info.
