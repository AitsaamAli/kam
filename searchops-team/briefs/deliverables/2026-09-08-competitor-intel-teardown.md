## The one finding worth acting on immediately

**Weekend/off-hours availability is now a verified, not assumed, competitive gap.** PakFiler — a real, currently active, well-positioned competitor targeting the same overseas-Pakistani/freelancer segment this project targets — states capped Mon-Sat 9am-6pm support. This project's Section K pages on weekend and 24-hour availability are therefore not a speculative content angle; they're aimed at a confirmed real gap. Worth making sure those two pages are genuinely strong and prominently internally linked, since the differentiator they claim is now evidence-backed.
