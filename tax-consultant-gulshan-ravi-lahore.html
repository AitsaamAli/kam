Frequently Asked Questions

I pay property tax to Excise. Do I still need to file with FBR?

Yes. Excise property tax is a provincial tax on the property; FBR income tax is a separate federal tax on income and transactions.

Do embassies ask for Pakistani tax returns?

Many visa applications ask for evidence of the sponsor's income and source of funds, and tax returns with wealth statements are strong evidence.

Is income tax collected on my truck's token?

Yes. Advance tax on goods transport vehicles is collected with motor vehicle tax, generally based on laden weight or capacity.

Is my salary from the mosque taxable?

It's salary income. If it's below the taxable threshold, no tax is due, but it's still income.
