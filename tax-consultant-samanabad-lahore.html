Filing status for Tax Year 2026

The Tax Year 2026 return deadline for individuals and AOPs was extended to 31 October 2026 and has now passed. A late return can still be filed on IRIS. Under Section 182 the late-filing penalty is 0.1% of the tax payable per day, up to 50%, with a minimum of Rs 40,000 (Rs 5,000 where at least 75% of income is salary below Rs 5 million). Joining the Active Taxpayers List after the due date also needs the Section 182A surcharge: Rs 25,000 for individuals, Rs 50,000 for AOPs and Rs 100,000 for companies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my homeopathic clinic's income taxable?

Yes. Consultation fees and medicines sold are business income, after the cost of medicines and other expenses.

Is an advance for stitching my income when I receive it?

It's a customer deposit until the clothes are delivered. Record advances separately and recognise the sale on delivery.

Is my EOBI pension taxable?

EOBI old-age pensions are generally exempt or favourably treated. They should still be declared if you file a return.

My cousin is selling our house under a power of attorney. Who pays the tax?

The owners. Advance tax under 236C is collected against each owner's CNIC or NICOP, and the gain is each owner's.
