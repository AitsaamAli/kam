Amazon Seller Tax Filing Checklist Pakistan 2026 — Complete FBR Compliance Guide

May 30, 2026
Kamboh Associates
9 min read
Lahore, Pakistan

Pakistan se Amazon selling ek growing industry hai — private label, FBA, dropshipping, wholesale. Thousands of Pakistani sellers globally sell kar rahe hain. Lekin tax compliance? Woh aksar unke radar par nahi hoti. FBR 2025-26 mein ecommerce income par specially focused hai — bank remittances ka data already share ho raha hai. Is guide mein Amazon sellers ke liye complete tax checklist cover karte hain.

Amazon Pakistan Sellers — Types Aur Tax Treatment

🚚

Inventory nahi — margin income, foreign remittance declare

Required Registrations — Amazon Sellers Pakistan

Sab se pehle in registrations complete karni hain:

Zaroori Registrations
NTN Registration — FBR IRIS par Individual ya business NTN zaroori hai
Income Tax Return Filing — Har saal September 30 tak return file karna zaroori
ATL Status Maintain — Active filer hona — bank WHT rate half ho jata hai
Sales Tax Registration (if applicable) — Annual turnover Rs. 10M+ par SRB/FBR sales tax zaroori
Business Bank Account — Dedicated account for Amazon payments recommended

Amazon Income Pakistan Mein Kaise Declare Hoti Hai?

Amazon payments aksar Payoneer, Hyperwallet, ya direct bank transfer se aati hain. Jo bhi route ho — Pakistani bank mein entry hoti hai. Yahi income declarable hai:

  • Gross Amazon payouts (minus Amazon fees) = Net income to declare
  • Tax Year July 2025 – June 2026 ka total calculate karein
  • PKR mein convert karein (bank credit statement rates use karein)
  • IRIS mein "Business Income" mein declare karein
  • IT export income qualify hoti hai agar digital services involved — confirm karein

Amazon Sellers Ki Deductible Expenses

Amazon sellers ke paas bahut sari legitimate deductible expenses hoti hain jo taxable income significantly reduce karti hain:

Deductible Expenses Checklist
Amazon referral fees (8-15% of sales)
FBA fulfillment fees (storage + packing)
Cost of goods sold (inventory purchase)
Product photography / listing optimization costs
Amazon PPC advertising spend
Payoneer / bank transfer fees
Software tools (Helium 10, Jungle Scout)
Freight / shipping to Amazon warehouse
Returns and refunds
Internet and laptop (proportional)

Sales Tax — Amazon Pakistan Mein Kab?

Sales tax confusion ka sabse zyada source hai Amazon sellers mein. Seedha jawab:

  • Pakistan se exports (selling to USA, UK, Europe): Pakistan mein no sales tax on exports — zero-rated
  • Pakistan mein local selling (amazon.pk jab aaye): Sales tax applicable hoga threshold ke baad
  • Imported inventory (for FBA abroad): Import duties at Pakistan customs if goods physically pass through Pakistan — relevant for some dropshippers

Good news for most Amazon sellers: Agar aap Pakistan se internationally sell kar rahe hain (Amazon.com, Amazon.uk, etc.), Pakistan mein sales tax registration most cases mein zaroori nahi — sirf income tax return filing zaroori hai. Specific guidance ke liye consultant se confirm karein.

Amazon Sellers Ki Common Tax Mistakes

  • Income declare hi nahi karna: "Amazon's in the US — FBR ko kaise pata chalega?" — bank remittance data share hota hai
  • COGS miss karna: Cost of goods sold aksar declare nahi karta — taxable income unnecessarily high ho jata hai
  • Amazon fees deduct nahi karna: Amazon ka referral fee aur FBA fees significant deductions hain
  • Wealth statement ignore karna: Income declare ki, assets nahi — FBR notice
  • Exchange rate issue: Dollar income ko PKR mein convert karna zaroori hai — aksar log yeh step miss karte hain

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal

Kya Amazon income Pakistan mein taxable hai?
Haan. Amazon se jo income Pakistani bank account mein aati hai woh taxable hai. FBR bank remittance data track karta hai. Income declare karna zaroori aur faydemand dono hai.
Amazon seller ko Pakistan mein sales tax registration chahiye?
Zyada-tar cases mein nahi — agar internationally sell kar rahe hain. Pakistan mein local selling ka threshold cross ho tab sales tax zaroori. Specific situation ke liye consultant se confirm karein.
Amazon FBA income kaise declare karein?
Gross Amazon payouts minus Amazon fees = net income. Pakistani bank account mein jo amount credit hoti hai woh declare karein. IRIS mein "Business Income" section use karein.
Amazon PPC advertising expense deductible hai?
Haan. Amazon Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands — sab advertising spend directly deductible business expense hai. Amazon Seller Central billing statements se document karein.
Helium 10 ya Jungle Scout subscription deductible hai?
Haan. Amazon research tools, listing optimization software, virtual assistant costs — sab deductible business expenses hain when used for Amazon business.
Amazon seller filer hone ka kya faida hai?
Payoneer/bank transfer par 1% vs 2% WHT — real savings. Property aur vehicle purchases par reduced rates. FBR notice ka risk kam. Bank loans easier. Visa applications ke liye income proof. Kul mila ke — significant financial benefits.
