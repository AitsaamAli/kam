FBR Filing Bilkul FREE Hai — Pehle Yeh Samajh Lo

Bahut clients hamare paas aate hain aur poochhte hain: "bhai FBR ki fee kitni hai?" Jawab simple hai — koi fee nahi. FBR ne deliberately IRIS portal free rakkha hai taake compliance بڑھے اور zyada log register ho sakein.

Aap khud bhi iris.fbr.gov.pk par ja kar, CNIC se login kar ke, income tax return submit kar sakte hain. Zero cost. Lekin — aur yeh ek bada "lekin" hai — pehli baar karna mushkil hota hai. Wealth statement, foreign assets declaration, employer deduction reconciliation — yeh sab naye logon ke liye confusing hoti hai. Isili consultant hire karte hain log.

Key point: Jab aap consultant ko pay karte hain, aap unke time, expertise, aur FBR correspondence handling ke liye pay kar rahe hain. FBR ko directly koi fee nahi jati.

Ek Consultant Kya Kya Karta Hai Aapke Liye?

Sirf IRIS par return submit karna ek consultant ka kaam nahi hota. Sahi consultant bahut kuch handle karta hai jo aap akele miss kar sakte hain:

  • IRIS mein correct return type select karna — salaried, business, AOP, corporate — galat type select karna ek common mistake hai
  • Wealth statement balance karna — assets aur liabilities ka aapas mein match karna zaroori hota hai, warna FBR notice aata hai
  • Employer withholding tax reconcile karna — Section 50 certificate se match karna
  • Eligible deductions claim karna — education fees, medical, Zakat, profit on debt — jo aksar log miss karte hain
  • ATL status verify karna — return submit ke baad active taxpayer list check karna
  • FBR notice ka jawab dena — agar baad mein koi inquiry aaye

Fee Zyada Kab Hoti Hai?

Consultant fee simple cases mein low hoti hai, lekin kuch situations mein fee naturally badh jati hai:

  • Multiple income sources: Salary + rental income + business + investments — har source alag declare hoti hai
  • Pichle 5 saal unfiled returns: Ek saal ka haazir karna separate hota hai, 5 saal ka ek saath karna zyada time leta hai
  • FBR notice reply: Agar pehle se notice aaya hua hai, to uska jawab dena alag charge hota hai
  • Complex wealth statement: Multiple properties, vehicles, investments — sab ka proper documentation zaroori hai
  • Accounts ka kaam: Agar aapke pas business accounts nahi hain aur consultant ko accounts bhi banana padte hain, to fee zyada hogi
  • Late filing penalty: September 30 ke baad filing mein FBR ka penalty bhi lagta hai — consultant charges alag

Rs. 500 Wali "Sasti" Service — Kya Yeh Kaam Karta Hai?

Hum honestly bata dete hain — kuch online platforms par "Rs. 500 mein tax return" ki offers milti hain. Inse hoshiyar rahein.

Common trap: Sasti service wale aksar sirf "nil return" ya incomplete return submit karte hain. Wealth statement skip kar dete hain. Ya galat income amount declare karte hain. FBR ka system smart ho raha hai — discrepancies identify karti hai. Baad mein jo penalty aati hai woh Rs. 5,000 se Rs. 20,000+ tak ho sakti hai — Rs. 500 ki savings kaafi mehnga pad jaati hai.

Ek reliable consultant hamare office mein aapse sawal poochta hai — income source kahan se, bank mein kitna hai, property hai ya nahi, vehicle hai ya nahi. Agar koi consultant sirf CNIC number le ke 10 minute mein "ho gaya" kahe — to yeh sahi kaam nahi hua.

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal

Kya FBR income tax return file karne ki koi fee leta hai?
Bilkul nahi. FBR ka IRIS portal par registration aur return filing completely free hai. Koi government fee nahi lagti. Jo amount aap consultant ko dete hain woh service charge hai, government fee nahi.
Salaried person ke liye tax consultant fee kitni honi chahiye?
Simple salaried case (single employer, no investment income) ke liye Rs. 2,000–5,000 reasonable hai. Agar investments hain, multiple sources hain, ya first time filing hai, to Rs. 5,000–8,000 tak ho sakti hai. Rs. 500–1,000 mein offer karne walon se bachein.
Kya hum khud income tax return file kar sakte hain?
Haan, bilkul. FBR IRIS portal par khud file karna possible hai. Agar simple salaried hain, ek employer hai, investments nahi hain, aur pehli baar seriously seekhna chahte hain — to khud try karein. FBR ki website par guides bhi hain. Lekin pehli baar ke liye ek baar consultant se guidance lena better hota hai.
Late filing karne par kya extra charges lagte hain?
Haan. FBR ki taraf se late filing penalty hoti hai — minimum Rs. 1,000 per quarter. Aur consultant alag se apna extra charge le sakta hai zyada kaam ki wajah se. September 30 deadline miss mat karein.
Company ka tax return kitne mein hota hai?
Private Limited Company ke liye Rs. 20,000 se Rs. 50,000+ tak hoti hai — depends on size, transactions, whether audit is required, and whether accounts preparation is included. Large companies ka Rs. 1 lakh+ bhi ho sakta hai.
Online filing service aur in-person filing mein kya fark hai fee mein?
Usually same fee hai. Online se sirf documents share karne ka process easy ho jata hai — WhatsApp ya email par bhejo, filing ho jati hai. Kuch consultants online ke liye slight discount bhi dete hain kyunki travel time nahi lagta. Kamboh Associates both options offer karta hai.
