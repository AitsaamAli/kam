Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to start importing from China?

An NTN, customs registration on WeBOC/PSW, usually sales tax registration, and a licensed clearing agent.

Do distributors need sales tax registration?

Distributors of many goods are required to register, and principal companies usually insist on it.

Is my exam duty payment from the board taxable?

Yes. It's income, and boards often withhold tax. Ask for a statement of payments and tax deducted.

Is there sales tax on books?

Printed books are often exempt or specially treated, while many stationery items are standard taxable goods. Keep them separate in your sales.
