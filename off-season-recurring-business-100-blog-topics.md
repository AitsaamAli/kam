# Off-Season / Recurring-Business Blog Topics (100)

**Goal:** individual return filing season (deadline 30 September) is a one-time annual spike. This list is deliberately built around services that generate client engagement and revenue **year-round, especially October–June** — the months when return-filing demand drops but a well-run firm should still have a steady client pipeline. Every section below targets either (a) a *recurring* compliance obligation (monthly/quarterly, not annual) that keeps a client coming back all year, or (b) an *off-season-specific* trigger (post-deadline notices, new-year planning, new business formation) that naturally peaks exactly when filing demand falls.

**Pre-write audit run 2026-09-11** against the live site (1,443 blog files). Existing coverage checked and found thin/absent for: monthly sales tax return filing, monthly/bi-annual withholding statement filing, SECP compliance beyond the single existing annual-return page, provincial sales-tax-on-services registration (PRA/SRB/BRA/KPRA), EOBI/labour recurring compliance, and retainer-package positioning beyond the two existing generic pages. This list goes deliberately into that gap rather than re-covering advance tax or FBR notices broadly (both already have existing pages — `advance-tax-pakistan-2026.html`, `advance-tax-installments-pakistan.html`, `advance-tax-adjustment-guide-lahore.html`, `fbr-notice-*` series, `secp-annual-return-filing-pakistan-2026.html`, `bookkeeping-accounting-charges-pakistan.html`, `payroll-tax-compliance-calendar-pakistan.html`, `retainer-vs-one-time-tax-consultant-pakistan.html`, `tax-consultant-monthly-retainer-cost-pakistan.html`).

No fabricated rates or slabs — every post hedges to the firm's real, published fee schedule (NTN Rs. 2,000; individual return Rs. 3,500-5,000; wealth statement Rs. 2,500; STRN Rs. 3,000) or to "Contact for Quote" where no clean flat rate exists, and sends the reader to FBR's/SECP's own current rate card for anything that changes every Finance Act. Never name a competitor.
