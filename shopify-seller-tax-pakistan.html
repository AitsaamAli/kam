Shopify Seller Tax Guide Pakistan 2026 — Complete Compliance Handbook

May 30, 2026
Kamboh Associates
8 min read
Lahore, Pakistan

Shopify Pakistan mein rapidly growing ecommerce platform hai — dropshipping, print-on-demand, digital products, physical goods. International clients, dollar payments, aur growing business. Lekin Shopify income ka Pakistan mein tax treatment kya hai? Payment gateways, income declaration, deductions — is guide mein sab honestly cover karte hain.

Shopify Income — Taxable Hai Pakistan Mein?

Haan. Shopify se jo bhi income Pakistani bank account mein aati hai — woh taxable hai per Income Tax Ordinance. Platform ka location (Canada mein Shopify HQ hai) koi fark nahi dalta — aap Pakistan mein resident hain, income declare karni hogi.

Good news: agar aap digital services ya IT-related products internationally sell kar rahe hain, IT export income exemption applicable ho sakti hai — jab designated banking channel se payment aaye.

Payment Gateway — Tax Treatment Per Gateway

Shopify sellers Pakistan mein different payment gateways use karte hain — har ek ka tax treatment thoda different hai:

💳
Payoneer
Bank transfer par 1%/2% WHT
✓ Pakistani sellers widely use
🏧
2Checkout / Verifone
Payoneer ya wire transfer — same WHT
✓ Popular for digital products
💰
Stripe (via Atlas)
Wire transfer — same bank WHT
⚠ Requires international entity

Stripe Pakistan: Stripe direct Pakistani accounts support nahi karta (as of 2026). Jo log Stripe use karte hain woh Stripe Atlas (US LLC) ya doosre gateways ke zariye karte hain. Income tax treatment same hai — jo Pakistani bank mein aata hai woh declare hota hai.

Shopify Income IRIS Mein Declare Karna

Process Amazon jaise hi hai — IRIS mein Business Income section:

  • Gross Shopify revenue (minus platform fees, refunds) = Net income
  • PKR conversion: bank credit statements ka use karein
  • July 2025 – June 2026 = Tax Year 2026
  • IT export exemption: digital products (templates, software, SaaS) qualify ho sakti hain
  • Physical dropshipping: standard business income treatment

Common Mistakes Shopify Sellers Pakistan Mein Karte Hain

  • Shopify subscription fee deduct nahi karna: Monthly $29-$299 fee significant deduction hai — miss mat karein
  • Ad spend ignore karna: Facebook aur Google ads ka spend direct deductible hai — without documentation kaafi log miss karte hain
  • Dropshipping supplier payments deduct nahi karna: AliExpress ya supplier payments Cost of Goods Sold hai — direct deductible
  • Refunds account nahi karna: Refunds gross income se minus karo — nahi kiya to zyada tax pay hoga
  • Multiple gateways ki income alag track nahi karna: Payoneer + 2Checkout — dono combine karein annual total mein

2026 update: FBR ecommerce businesses ko increasingly focus kar raha hai. State Bank of Pakistan ke through bank data aur international transaction data FBR ke paas accessible hai. Voluntary compliance — isi mein faida hai. Late notice se sahi waqt par filing better hai every time.

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal

Kya Shopify income Pakistan mein taxable hai?
Haan. Shopify se Pakistani bank mein aane wali income taxable hai. Digital products income par IT export exemption possible hai. Physical dropshipping income normal business income hai.
Shopify subscription fee deductible hai?
Haan. Shopify monthly subscription fee ($29-$299) directly deductible business expense hai. Shopify billing page se PDF invoice download karein as documentation.
Dropshipping income aur product sale income — koi fark?
Tax treatment mein same — dono "Business Income" declare hoti hain. Fark documentation mein hai: dropshipping mein COGS = supplier payment, product selling mein COGS = inventory purchase.
Shopify se Pakistan mein sales tax registration zaroori hai?
Agar internationally sell kar rahe hain — generally nahi. Pakistan mein local ecommerce threshold Rs. 10M cross karne par applicable. Specific situation ke liye consultant se confirm karein.
Shopify app subscriptions (Klaviyo, Oberlo) deductible hain?
Haan. All Shopify apps used for business operations — email marketing (Klaviyo), dropshipping (Oberlo/DSers), review apps — sab deductible business expenses hain.
Facebook ads ka spend deductible hai?
Haan. Facebook, Instagram, Google, TikTok — sab advertising platforms ka spend directly deductible business expense hai. Facebook billing statements ya bank credit card statements se document karein.
