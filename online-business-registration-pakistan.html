Online NTN Registration on FBR IRIS Portal

The FBR IRIS portal (iris.fbr.gov.pk) is your one-stop destination for all income tax registrations and filings in Pakistan. Registering for your National Tax Number (NTN) is the first step for any new business.

1

Visit iris.fbr.gov.pk

Navigate to the FBR IRIS portal. Click "Registration for Unregistered Person" to begin NTN registration.

2

Enter Your CNIC and Mobile Number

FBR verifies your identity through NADRA. Your CNIC must match NADRA records exactly. A one-time PIN (OTP) is sent to your registered mobile number.

3

Fill Registration Form

Provide your business address, nature of business, bank account details (optional), and income sources. For sole proprietors, your CNIC number becomes your NTN.

4

Submit and Receive NTN

After submission, FBR processes your application within 1–2 business days. You receive your NTN certificate by email and can download it from IRIS.

Online SECP Company Registration

SECP's eServices portal allows you to register a Private Limited Company, OPC, or Partnership online — from anywhere in Pakistan or abroad. The process is fully digital and paperless.

SECP Online Registration Steps

  1. Visit eservices.secp.gov.pk and create a user account
  2. Search for company name availability and reserve your preferred name
  3. Complete the online incorporation form with directors' CNIC details, business address, and share structure
  4. Upload scanned Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Articles of Association (AoA)
  5. Pay SECP government fees online via credit/debit card or bank transfer
  6. SECP reviews and issues Certificate of Incorporation within 5–15 working days
  7. Download your Certificate of Incorporation from the SECP portal

SECP One-Stop Shop: SECP's One-Stop Shop allows simultaneous NTN registration with FBR when you complete company registration. This reduces the overall registration timeline significantly.

Online STRN Sales Tax Registration

If your business requires Sales Tax Registration, you can complete STRN registration online through the FBR portal. STRN is separate from NTN — it is your Sales Tax identification number.

STRN registration is done through the Sales Tax Automated Registration (STARR) system on the FBR portal. You must already have an active NTN before applying for STRN.

  • Log into IRIS portal with your NTN and password
  • Navigate to Registration > Sales Tax Registration
  • Provide business details, bank account for sales tax, and business activity
  • Upload required documents (lease agreement, CNIC, NTN certificate)
  • Submit application — STRN is typically issued within 3–7 days

For full eligibility criteria, see our Sales Tax Registration Guide.

How to Check Your ATL Status Online

After registering and filing your first tax return, you should verify your status on FBR's Active Taxpayer List (ATL). Being on the ATL entitles you to reduced withholding tax rates across all transactions.

  • Visit atl.fbr.gov.pk or check through IRIS portal
  • Enter your CNIC or NTN to check your status
  • ATL is updated every Monday — new filers appear within 1 week of filing
  • Download your ATL verification certificate as proof

For a complete guide, see: How to Check ATL Status in Pakistan 2026.

Filing Income Tax Return Online — IRIS Portal

Once registered, you must file your annual income tax return online. The IRIS portal handles all return filings for individuals, sole proprietors, AOPs, and companies.

How to File Tax Return Online

  1. Log into IRIS at iris.fbr.gov.pk with your NTN/CNIC and password
  2. Click "Declaration" in the top menu, then select "Income Tax Return"
  3. Choose the relevant tax year (July 1 – June 30)
  4. Fill in income details, deductions, tax paid (withholding), and wealth statement
  5. Verify details and submit — save the acknowledgment receipt

Common Mistakes in Online Business Registration

  • CNIC mismatch with NADRA: Your name and father's name must exactly match NADRA records. Even minor spelling differences cause rejection.
  • Wrong mobile number: FBR sends OTP to the mobile number linked to your CNIC in NADRA. If your number has changed and NADRA isn't updated, registration fails.
  • Incomplete SECP MoA: A poorly drafted Memorandum of Association is the most common cause of SECP rejection. Use a qualified consultant to draft it.
  • Selecting wrong business activity: Wrong NTN or STRN business category affects your withholding tax treatment and sales tax applicability.
  • Not filing after registration: Registering for NTN without filing a tax return doesn't put you on the ATL. File your first return (even nil) immediately after registration.

Important: An NTN alone does not make you an Active Filer. You must file your annual income tax return AND appear on FBR's Active Taxpayer List to benefit from reduced withholding tax rates on bank transactions, property deals, and vehicle purchases.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I register NTN online in Pakistan?
Yes. FBR NTN registration is done completely online through the IRIS portal at iris.fbr.gov.pk. You need your CNIC, mobile number, email address, and basic income information. The process takes 1–2 business days after submission.
How do I register a company online with SECP?
Visit the SECP eServices portal (eservices.secp.gov.pk), create an account, search for your company name availability, prepare your Memorandum and Articles of Association, pay fees online, and submit digitally. The system sends your Certificate of Incorporation online within 5–15 working days.
What is the IRIS portal and how do I use it?
IRIS (Integrated Revenue Information System) is FBR's online portal for all tax-related registrations and filings. At iris.fbr.gov.pk you can register for NTN, file income tax returns, check ATL status, respond to FBR notices, and manage all your tax compliance online.
Can I complete SECP registration from outside Pakistan?
Yes. SECP's online portal allows company registration from anywhere in the world. You need a Pakistani CNIC or NICOP, and documents can be submitted digitally. Kamboh Associates handles SECP registrations for overseas Pakistanis.
What if my online NTN registration is rejected?
Common rejection reasons include CNIC mismatch with NADRA records, incorrect mobile number, or incomplete information. Check the rejection notice on IRIS, correct the issue, and resubmit.
