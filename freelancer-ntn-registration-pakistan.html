Step-by-Step IRIS NTN Registration

1
Go to iris.fbr.gov.pk
Open the FBR IRIS portal. On the login page, click "Registration for unregistered person" — this is the link for first-time registrants.
2
Enter Your CNIC
Enter your 13-digit CNIC number (without dashes). The system will check if an NTN already exists for your CNIC.
3
OTP Verification
Enter the mobile number linked to your CNIC. FBR sends a One-Time Password (OTP) via SMS. Enter it to proceed. If your number is not linked, update your NADRA record first.
4
Fill Registration Form
Enter your name (as on CNIC), date of birth, residential address, email, and occupation. Select "Freelancer" or "IT Services" or "Individual — Business" as your occupation type.
5
Submit & Activate
Submit the form. You receive an activation link at your email address. Click it to activate your IRIS account. Your NTN (= your CNIC) is now active.
6
File Your First Return
Log in to IRIS with your new credentials. File an income tax return (even if income is zero or fully exempt). This places you on the Active Taxpayer List (ATL).
7
Verify ATL Status
SMS your CNIC (13 digits, no dashes) to 9966. You receive a confirmation of your ATL status. Share this with your bank to get the 1% WHT rate applied going forward.

NTN vs ATL — What's the Difference?

NTN vs ATL — Key Distinction

NTN = National Tax Number. Your tax identity, registered in FBR's system. For individuals = your CNIC. Obtained by registering on IRIS.

ATL = Active Taxpayer List. A published list of taxpayers who filed their return on time last year. Banks check ATL to determine WHT rate (1% filer vs 2% non-filer).

Timeline: Register NTN on Day 1 → File first return → Appear on next ATL update → Bank applies 1% rate. ATL updates on March 1 each year. New filers who file before this date appear in the next update.

Documents Required

  • CNIC — original 13-digit number (mandatory)
  • CNIC-linked mobile number — for OTP verification
  • Email address — for IRIS account activation
  • Residential address — as per CNIC or current address

That's it. No bank statement, no office address, no business registration, no photos. NTN registration for an individual freelancer requires only the above four items.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NTN registration mandatory for freelancers in Pakistan?
NTN registration is mandatory for any Pakistani earning regular income above the taxable threshold (Rs. 600,000/year). Most active freelancers exceed this. Even below the threshold, registering is strongly recommended — it reduces your bank WHT from 2% to 1%.
Is NTN registration free for freelancers in Pakistan?
Yes. NTN registration on iris.fbr.gov.pk is completely free. There are no government charges. A tax consultant can help with the full setup (NTN + first return) for a professional fee, but registration itself costs nothing.
What is the difference between NTN and ATL?
NTN is your tax identity number (= your CNIC for individuals). ATL is the Active Taxpayer List — taxpayers who filed their prior year return on time. Banks check ATL to apply 1% (filer) vs 2% (non-filer) WHT rate. You need both NTN and ATL status.
What documents do I need for freelancer NTN registration?
CNIC number, CNIC-linked mobile number for OTP, email address, and residential address. No business registration, bank statement, or office address is required for individual freelancer NTN.
How long does NTN registration take?
15–30 minutes online. You receive your IRIS activation email within minutes. ATL status activates after you file your first tax return — ATL updates are published quarterly by FBR, with the main update on March 1 each year.
What happens if I don't register NTN as a freelancer?
Without NTN/ATL: your bank deducts 2% WHT on every Payoneer withdrawal. You cannot formally claim IT export exemption. FBR can issue notices for undeclared income. The cost of non-compliance far exceeds the 30-minute registration effort.
How do I verify my ATL status?
SMS your CNIC number (13 digits, no dashes) to 9966 from any Pakistani mobile number. You receive a reply confirming ATL status. You can also check at fbr.gov.pk → ATL Verification.
Do I need to renew my NTN every year?
NTN itself does not expire. However, to remain on ATL (1% WHT rate), you must file your annual income tax return every year by September 30. Failure to file removes you from ATL in the next update cycle.

IT Export Exemption — How to Get 0.25% Tax Rate

Under SRO 1006(I)/2024, Pakistani freelancers providing IT and IT-enabled services can pay just 0.25% tax on foreign remittances received through banking channels. To qualify:

  1. Register with Pakistan Software Export Board (PSEB)
  2. Receive payment through banking channels (not cryptocurrency)
  3. File annual income tax return declaring IT export income
  4. Claim the reduced rate in your FBR return

Wealth Statement — Declaring Your Foreign Earnings

All foreign remittances received by Pakistani freelancers must be declared in the wealth statement under the "Cash and Bank Balance" section. Foreign remittances themselves are not taxable — but you must show them as a source explaining your bank balance growth. Without declaration, FBR can issue a Section 111 notice for unexplained bank deposits.

5 Tax Mistakes Pakistani Freelancers Make

  • Not registering NTN — pays double WHT on all bank transactions
  • Not filing annual return — loses ATL status and all filer benefits
  • Not opening a separate bank account for freelance income — makes accounting harder
  • Receiving payment via crypto instead of banking — loses IT export exemption eligibility
  • Not claiming business expenses — internet, laptop, software licenses are all deductible

As a freelancer or self-employed professional in Pakistan, you can deduct: laptop and computer expenses, internet and mobile bills (business portion), software subscriptions, home office rent (proportionate), electricity bills (business portion), bank charges, and professional development courses.
