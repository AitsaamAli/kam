NTN Status Checker
FBR IRIS Portal — Verify NTN & Active Filer

Verify your NTN (National Tax Number) and Active Filer status directly on the FBR IRIS portal. Real-time status check with your CNIC or NTN number.

  • 1
    Visit the FBR IRIS portal using the button below
  • 2
    Click "Online NTN/STRN Inquiry" or use "ATL Filer Verification"
  • 3
    Enter your CNIC number (13 digits without dashes)
  • 4
    Your NTN number and active filer status will display
  • 5
    For assistance, contact KA directly via WhatsApp
