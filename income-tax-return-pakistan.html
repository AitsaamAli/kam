Who Should File Income Tax Return in Pakistan? | Complete Guide for Lahore Taxpayers 2026

Filing an income tax return in Pakistan is no longer optional for many individuals and businesses. The Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) has expanded tax compliance requirements, making it essential for salaried individuals, business owners, freelancers, property investors, and digital entrepreneurs to understand whether they are legally required to file taxes.

If you are living in Lahore or operating a business anywhere in Pakistan, understanding tax filing obligations can help you avoid penalties, Active Taxpayer List (ATL) removal, higher withholding taxes, and legal notices from the FBR.

Understanding Income Tax Return in Pakistan

An income tax return is a declaration submitted to the official FBR portal showing your annual income, assets, expenses, and taxes paid.

Official FBR Portal: https://iris.fbr.gov.pk/

Who Should File Income Tax Return in Pakistan?

Salaried Individuals

If you are a salaried employee earning above the taxable income threshold defined by the FBR, you are required to file an income tax return.

  • Government employees
  • Private company employees
  • Bank employees
  • Teachers and professors
  • IT professionals
  • Engineers and consultants

Business Owners and Traders

All business owners operating in Pakistan should file income tax returns regardless of whether the business is small or large.

  • Sole proprietors
  • Partnership firms
  • E-commerce sellers
  • Import/export businesses
  • Retail and wholesale traders

Freelancers and Remote Workers

Freelancers earning through Fiverr, Upwork, Amazon, Shopify, and remote jobs should also file tax returns in Pakistan.

  • Banking compliance
  • Visa applications
  • Loan approvals
  • Avoiding tax complications

Tax Filing Requirements for Property Owners

You may need to file an income tax return if you:

  • Own property above a certain value
  • Buy or sell plots
  • Receive rental income
  • Invest in commercial real estate

Vehicle Owners Required to File Tax Returns

  • Luxury vehicle owners
  • Commercial vehicle owners
  • Multiple vehicle owners

Step-by-Step Process to File Income Tax Return in Pakistan

Step 1: NTN Registration

Before filing taxes, you need an NTN.

Register through: FBR IRIS Registration Portal

Step 2: Gather Financial Information

  • Income records
  • Expense details
  • Banking information
  • Asset details

Step 3: Login to IRIS Portal

  • Login using CNIC
  • Open income tax return form
  • Enter required details

Step 4: Submit Wealth Statement

  • Assets
  • Liabilities
  • Expenses
  • Source of income

Step 5: Submit Return and Become Active Filer

  • Your return gets processed
  • ATL status updates
  • You become an active filer

FAQs – Income Tax Return in Pakistan

1. Who is required to file income tax return in Pakistan?

Anyone earning taxable income, owning assets, running a business, or freelancing may need to file an income tax return.

2. How can I file income tax return online in Pakistan?

You can file online through the official FBR IRIS Portal.

3. What documents are required for tax filing in Pakistan?

CNIC, bank statements, salary slips, business records, and property documents are commonly required.

4. What is the cost of income tax filing in Lahore?

The cost depends on the complexity of your income and business structure.

5. How long does the tax filing process take?

Simple salaried cases may take a few hours while corporate filings may take several days.

6. What happens if I do not file my tax return?

Non-filers may face penalties, higher taxes, and FBR notices.
