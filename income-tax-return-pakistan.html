Who Should File Income Tax Return in Pakistan? Complete Guide for Lahore Taxpayers 2026

May 20, 2026
Kamboh Associates
6 min read
Lahore, Pakistan

Filing an income tax return in Pakistan is no longer optional for many individuals and businesses. The Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) has expanded tax compliance requirements, making it essential for salaried individuals, business owners, freelancers, property investors, and digital entrepreneurs to understand whether they are legally required to file taxes.

TL;DR

Income tax return is mandatory for all Pakistanis earning above the taxable threshold. File on FBR IRIS before September 30, 2026 to avoid penalties and remain an active filer.

Understanding Income Tax Return in Pakistan

An income tax return is a declaration submitted to the official FBR IRIS portal showing your annual income, assets, expenses, and taxes paid. Filing regularly keeps you on the Active Taxpayer List (ATL) — which gives you significant benefits on banking, property, and vehicle transactions.

Official FBR Portal: iris.fbr.gov.pk

Who Should File Income Tax Return?

The following categories of people are required or strongly recommended to file income tax returns in Pakistan:

Salaried Individuals
  • Government employees
  • Private company employees
  • Bank employees
  • Teachers and professors
  • IT professionals
  • Engineers & consultants
Business Owners & Traders
  • Sole proprietors
  • Partnership firms
  • E-commerce sellers
  • Import/export businesses
  • Retail & wholesale traders
Freelancers & Remote Workers
  • Fiverr & Upwork earners
  • Amazon & Shopify sellers
  • Remote job holders
  • Digital marketers
  • Software developers
Property Owners & Investors
  • Those owning property above threshold
  • Plot buyers and sellers
  • Rental income earners
  • Commercial real estate investors

Benefits of Becoming a Tax Filer in Lahore

  • Lower withholding taxes on banking transactions
  • Better banking profile and credibility
  • Visa and foreign travel support
  • Easier loan and mortgage approvals
  • Protection from FBR notices and penalties
  • Lower taxes on vehicle registration and transfers

Documents Required for Income Tax Return

Keep the following documents ready before filing your income tax return in Pakistan:

CNIC (front & back)
CNIC-registered SIM
Salary slips / payslips
Bank statements (6–12 months)
Business registration (if any)
Property ownership documents
Utility bills (address proof)
Foreign remittance proof (freelancers)

Step-by-Step Filing Process

1

Register for NTN

Get your National Tax Number through the official FBR IRIS Portal. NTN registration is free.

2

Gather Financial Information

Collect all income records, bank statements, expense details, and asset information for the tax year.

3

Login to IRIS Portal

Login using your CNIC at iris.fbr.gov.pk, open the income tax return form, and enter all required details.

4

Submit and Verify ATL Status

Submit your return. Your name will appear on the Active Taxpayer List (ATL) within 24–72 hours. Verify via SMS: send your 13-digit CNIC to 9966.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is required to file income tax return in Pakistan?
Anyone earning taxable income, owning assets above the threshold, running a business, or doing freelance work may be required to file an income tax return. It is highly recommended even for those below the threshold to enjoy filer benefits.
How can I file income tax return online in Pakistan?
You can file online through the official FBR IRIS Portal at iris.fbr.gov.pk. You will need your CNIC-registered phone number for OTP verification and your NTN to login.
What is the filing deadline for income tax return 2026?
The income tax return for FY 2024–25 is due by 30 June 2026 for individuals and AOPs. Filing before the deadline ensures your ATL status is maintained for the following year.
What happens if I do not file my tax return?
Non-filers face higher withholding taxes on banking, property, and vehicle transactions, along with potential FBR notices and penalties of PKR 1,000 per month for late filing.
