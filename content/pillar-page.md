## 1. Who Must File an Income Tax Return in Pakistan?

Under Pakistan's Income Tax Ordinance 2001 (as amended), you **must** file an annual income tax return if you fall into any of the following categories:

- **Annual income above PKR 600,000** (the basic exemption threshold)
- **NTN holders** — once you have an NTN, filing is mandatory every year
- **All company directors** and business owners
- **Freelancers earning foreign remittance** (required to maintain filer status for tax exemptions)
- **Property owners** who buy, sell, or rent property
- **Vehicle owners** with vehicles above 1000cc
- **Members of associations of persons (AOPs)**
- **Anyone receiving foreign income** including YouTube, Fiverr, Upwork, Amazon earnings

Even if your income is below the taxable threshold, filing a tax return makes you an "active filer" on the FBR ATL (Active Taxpayer List) — which saves you money on dozens of withholding tax categories.

## 3. How to File Income Tax Return Online in Pakistan (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Register for NTN
Visit the [FBR IRIS portal](https://iris.fbr.gov.pk) and register for your National Tax Number (NTN). You need your CNIC, mobile number, and email.

### Step 2: Log In to IRIS
Go to iris.fbr.gov.pk and log in with your NTN/CNIC and password set during registration.

### Step 3: Select Tax Year
Click on "Income Tax Return" and select the relevant tax year (July 1 to June 30).

### Step 4: Fill Your Income Details
Enter all sources of income:
- Salary income (from employer certificates)
- Business income (turnover and expenses)
- Foreign remittance income (bank statements)
- Property rental income
- Capital gains

### Step 5: Enter Tax Already Paid
Add all withholding taxes deducted by banks, employers, and government agencies.

### Step 6: Calculate and Submit
IRIS auto-calculates your tax liability. Pay any remaining tax via bank challan, then submit the return.

### Step 7: Download Acknowledgment
Download your 125(Acknowledgment) receipt — proof that your return has been filed.

## 7. Common Income Tax Return Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Filing with wrong NTN or CNIC** — double-check before submission
2. **Not declaring all bank accounts** — FBR cross-checks with NADRA and banks
3. **Skipping foreign remittance** — Payoneer/Wise credits must be declared (even if exempt)
4. **Missing the deadline** — late filing removes you from ATL and attracts penalties
5. **Not declaring property/vehicle** — FBR's real estate and vehicle data is comprehensive
6. **Claiming wrong deductions** — Only allowable deductions under ITO 2001 are valid
7. **Filing blank or incomplete returns** — partial filing is as bad as no filing

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to file a tax return if my employer already deducts tax?**
A: Yes. Employer withholding (tax deducted from salary) is not the same as filing a return. You must still file an annual income tax return with FBR even if tax has already been deducted.

**Q: Can I file a tax return if I have no income?**
A: Yes — a "nil return" can be filed, which keeps you active on the ATL list and preserves filer status.

**Q: What happens if I don't file my tax return?**
A: You become a non-filer, losing half your withholding tax advantage. FBR can also issue notices and impose penalties up to PKR 50,000 for willful non-filers.
