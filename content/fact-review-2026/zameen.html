Section 236C aur 236K — Kya Hai Ye Tax?

Section 236C (seller): jo shakhs property ka transfer register, record ya attest karta hai, woh seller ya transferor se advance tax collect karta hai. Rate First Schedule Part IV Division X mein hai: gross consideration ka 2.75%.

Section 236K (buyer): wohi shakhs buyer ya transferee se advance tax collect karta hai. Rate Division XVIII mein hai: fair market value ka 1.25%.

Dono sections ki Explanation ke mutabiq collect karne walon mein registrar of properties, housing authorities, housing societies, co-operative societies aur registered public/private real estate projects shamil hain. Is liye online platform se mili deal bhi registration ya society transfer ke waqt isi qanoon ke tehat aati hai.

ATL se bahar logon par Tenth Schedule ke rates lagte hain: 236C par 11.5%, aur 236K par value ke hisaab se 10.5%, 14.5% ya 18.5%. Ye figures Finance Act 2025 ne tay ki theen aur ITO ke 30 June 2026 tak amended text mein yahi hain.

236C seller ki sale price (gross consideration) par lagta hai, jab ke 236K fair market value par. Agar FBR ki value aur aap ki sale price mukhtalif hon, to dono taxes alag base par banenge.

Section 114C — Bari Property Deals Par Pabandi Ka Qanoon

Finance Act 2025 ne section 114C aur Fifteenth Schedule shamil kiye. "Ineligible person" ki aisi property transfer register nahi ki jayegi jis ki fair market value Rs 100 million (Rs 10 crore) se zyada ho. Ye pabandi us tareekh se lagu hogi jo Federal Government Gazette notification se tay kare (section 114C(5)).

  • Eligible kaun: jis ne pichle tax year ki return file ki ho aur wealth statement mein deal ke liye kaafi resources (deal value ka 130%) dikhaye hon, ya jis ne FBR portal par sources of investment and expenditure statement file ki ho.
  • Family: individual ke liye parents, spouse aur dependent children bhi eligible person mein shamil hain.
  • Exempt: non-resident persons aur public companies (section 114C(2)).
  • Section 7E khatam: property par deemed income ka tax (section 7E) Finance Act 2026 ne omit kar diya hai.

236C/236K Adjustable Hai — Kab Wapas Milta Hai

Annual return mein ye dono taxes aap ki tax liability ke against adjust hote hain:

  • 236K: adjustable hai (section 236K(2)). Return mein jo liability banti hai, us mein se ye minus hota hai.
  • 236C: adjustable hai (section 236C(2)), lekin agar property usi tax year mein khareedi aur bechi gayi ho to ye minimum tax hai aur wapas nahi hota.
  • Overseas (FCVA/NRVA): non-resident individual ne property FCVA ya NRVA account se khareedi ho, to 236K final discharge hai. Bechne par 236C capital gains ki jagah final tax hai.
  • Return zaroori: adjustment ya refund sirf return file karne se hi mumkin hai.

FAQs — Zameen.com Property Tax

Mujhe property deal kal hai — kya aaj filer ban sakta hoon?
NTN aur return hum same day file kar dete hain. Lekin registrar deal ke din ka ATL status dekhta hai, aur ATL mein naam FBR ki list update hone par aata hai. Deadline ke baad file hui return ke liye section 182A ka surcharge bhi dena hota hai. Is liye deal se kuch din pehle rabta karein.
Registrar ne 236C collect nahi kiya — ab kya hoga?
Tax collect karna registering/attesting authority ki zimmedari hai. Agar collect nahi hua, to section 162 ke tehat FBR ye tax us shakhs se recover kar sakta hai jis se collect hona tha, yani seller se. Is liye sale ke documents aur tax challan sambhal kar rakhein.
Property gift ya wirasat mein mili hai — kya tax lagega?
236C aur 236K "sale or transfer" ke registration par collect hote hain. Gift aur wirasat ke transfer ki surat mein faisla documents aur case par depend karta hai, is liye registration se pehle registrar aur apne tax adviser se confirm karein. Wealth statement mein aisi property dikhana har haal mein zaroori hai.
Builder ya developer se direct khareedi par bhi 236K lagta hai?
Haan. Section 236K ki Explanation mein registered public aur private real estate projects bhi collecting persons mein shamil hain. Qiston par khareed mein tax har qist ke saath liya ja sakta hai (section 236K(3)). Property par deemed income ka section 7E Finance Act 2026 ne khatam kar diya hai.
Overseas Pakistani property khareed raha hai — kya special rules hain?
Non-resident individual (POC, NICOP ya CNIC) agar FCVA ya NRVA account se khareede, to 236K final discharge hai (section 236K(2) proviso). Section 114C ki pabandiyan non-residents par lagu nahi. Resident ya non-resident status section 82 ke 183 din wale rule se tay hota hai.
