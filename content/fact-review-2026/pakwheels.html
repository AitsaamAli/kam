Section 114C — Mehngi Gaari Par Pabandi Ka Qanoon

FAQs — PakWheels Car Tax

Car registration ke din ATL mein nahi hoon — kya ATL wala rate milega?
Nahi. Registration ke waqt jo status ho, wohi rate lagta hai. ATL se bahar hon to 231B teen guna lagega. Is liye NTN, return aur ATL ka kaam registration se pehle mukammal karein. Hum NTN aur return same day file kar dete hain, lekin ATL mein naam FBR ki list update hone par aata hai.
Husband ATL par hai — wife ke naam gaari register ho to kaun sa rate?
Rate us shakhs ke status par lagta hai jis ke naam registration ho rahi hai. Wife ATL par nahi to ATL se bahar wala rate lagega. Section 114C (mehngi gaari ki pabandi) mein "eligible person" ki tareef mein immediate family shamil hai, lekin 231B ka rate registration wale ke apne ATL status se tay hota hai.
Purani gaari bechne par seller ko tax dena hoga?
Transfer par 231B(2) ka tax buyer ki registration ke saath lagta hai, seller par nahi. Personal use ki gaari bechne ka gain capital gain nahi, kyun ke personal use ki movable property section 37 ke capital asset se bahar hai. Dealer ka munafa business income hai.
Electric vehicle (EV) par 231B kitna hai?
Division VII ke proviso ke mutabiq jahan engine capacity lagu nahi aur gaari ki value Rs 5 million ya zyada ho, wahan nayi gaari par 3% of value aur transfer par Rs 20,000 lagta hai. ATL se bahar hon to ye bhi teen guna.
Car dealer ka tax kaise handle karein?
Business NTN aur saalana return se ATL mein rahein, taake har transfer par 231B ATL wale rate par lage. Khareed-farokht ka hisaab aur kata hua 231B return mein dikhayein. Registration se pehle beche gaye vehicle par 231B(2A) ka tax alag hai.
