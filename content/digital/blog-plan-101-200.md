### 2.3 Search behaviour has changed: Google AI Mode and AI Overviews

- **Simple informational questions lose clicks.** Answer engines now answer them on the page. Reported click losses vary widely and many figures come from vendors. The direction is consistent: purely informational pages lose most.
- **Commercial, local and transactional searches still send visitors.** People still click through to compare, call or buy.
- **Google's position:** it says it is "misleading to make generalizations" about traffic declines.
- **Our response:**
  - weight the next 100 towards commercial, local and decision content
  - include original tools and data that answer engines must cite rather than replace
- **Sources:**
  - [WordStream 2026 SEO trends](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2026-seo-trends)
  - [Technology Magazine on AI Mode](https://technologymagazine.com/articles/how-googles-new-ai-mode-could-devastate-web-traffic-seo)

## Part 3: Strategy for #101–#200 (ten principles)

1. **Own Lahore around our office.** 12 genuine neighbourhood guides form cluster K. Each has a different business angle and honest directions to the Awan Town office.
2. **Lead with buying intent.** 64 of the 100 are commercial, local or transactional; the informational 36 each feed a commercial page.
3. **Price the software people search for.** Cluster M covers school, clinic, POS, booking, LMS, CRM, society and portal systems, each as a web app with build-vs-buy honesty.
4. **Cover AI, without hype.** Chatbots, the WhatsApp API, AI Mode, AI builders and automation (cluster L), each with real costs, risks and privacy notes.
5. **Help stores grow after launch.** Cluster N covers e-commerce operations: SEO, carts, sale seasons, policies and tracking.
6. **Produce content that shortens our own projects.** Cluster P covers what to write on each page.
7. **Answer owners' everyday technical problems.** Cluster Q covers domains, hosting, email and security. These posts build trust and attract maintenance clients.
8. **Give every post an original asset** that AI search must cite rather than replace:
   - a calculator-style table
   - a template
   - a checklist
   - a decision matrix
   - a dated price table
9. **Update the first 100 as we go.** Each new post links to 2–4 older posts. The pillars get a "related guides" refresh after each wave, and `dateModified` changes only when content really changes.
10. **One truth everywhere.** The same prices (P), the same entity description and the same facts across the site, `llms.txt`, Google Business Profile and directory listings.

## Part 4: Quality standard: the same brief, plus these upgrades

All rules in `content/digital/KAD-BLOG-WRITER-BRIEF-23-100.md` apply unchanged: format, SEO, AEO, GEO, separation, sources, auditor and clustering audit. A version 2 brief and kit will add the following.

**Neighbourhood pages (cluster K):**
- State the office address and that it is in Awan Town.
- Give directions only with real landmarks.
- Include at least 3 area-specific business facts.
- Never claim to be the "best" in an area. The phrase "best website developer in X" goes in the cluster terms, and the post answers it with criteria.
- No two K posts may share a paragraph. The auditor's 12-word overlap check runs across K.

**AI topics (cluster L):**
- No claims about AI outcomes.
- Every price is either an official pricing page with a date or our own P price.
- Every L post includes a privacy section.

**Software price guides (cluster M):**
- Always include a build-vs-buy table.
- Name off-the-shelf options only from official sites.
- Our price comes from P['app'] or P['app_range'].
- Never cover tax or statutory integrations; send readers to "your adviser".

**Market price ranges (cluster O):** quote only dated sources, and say plainly when sources disagree.

**New categories** to add to the auditor: "AI and Automation", "Software and Web Apps", "Content and Conversion".

**Pillars of the new clusters:** #101 (K), #113 (L), #122 (M), #151 (P). Spokes put their pillar in `related`.

**Length:** the same as before: spokes 600–1,300 body words, pillars 1,000–2,200. FAQs: 6–8 per spoke, 8–10 per pillar, including one Roman Urdu FAQ for every Pakistan-audience post.

**Upgrade #1–#22 to the new standard as well**, in a small separate batch:
- a link to a service page in the body
