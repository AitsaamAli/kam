Late Filing Penalty and ATL Surcharge Calculator Pakistan 2026

Sections 182 and 182A Rules checked 19 November 2026

Quick answer

Under Section 182 of the Income Tax Ordinance (text amended up to 30 June 2026), a late return costs the higher of 0.1% of tax payable or Rs 1,000 for each day late. The minimum is Rs 10,000 if at least 75% of your income is salary, and Rs 50,000 for everyone else. The maximum is 200% of tax payable. The penalty is reduced by 75%, 50% or 25% if you file within one, two or three months after the due date. Separately, an individual pays a Rs 25,000 ATL surcharge or furnishes Form ATL-U to rejoin the Active Taxpayers List.

The Tax Year 2026 deadline for individuals and AOPs was extended to 31 October 2026. File by 30 November 2026 for the 75% reduction. Rules are taken from the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001 (FBR, amended up to 30.06.2026), Section 182 table entry 1 and Section 182A. This is an estimate, not an order.

Worked Example

Short answer: a salaried person with Rs 60,000 tax who files on 20 November 2026 owes about Rs 5,000 penalty and no surcharge with Form ATL-U.

Filed 20 days late. 0.1% of Rs 60,000 for 20 days is Rs 1,200; Rs 1,000 a day for 20 days is Rs 20,000, the higher figure. That is above the Rs 10,000 salaried minimum and below the 200% cap of Rs 120,000. Filing within one month of 31 October cuts it by 75%, to Rs 5,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the late filing penalty calculated in Pakistan in 2026?

Under Section 182 (text amended up to 30 June 2026), the penalty is the higher of 0.1% of tax payable or Rs 1,000 for each day of default, with a minimum of Rs 10,000 for an individual whose income is at least 75% salary and Rs 50,000 for others, and a maximum of 200% of tax payable.

Is the late filing penalty reduced if I file soon after the deadline?

Yes. The amount of penalty is reduced by 75% if the return is filed within one month after the due date or extended due date, by 50% within two months and by 25% within three months. For Tax Year 2026, one month after 31 October 2026 is 30 November 2026.

What does tax payable mean in the penalty formula?

The Explanation to the entry defines it as the higher of the tax chargeable on the assessed taxable income for the year, or the tax payable for the preceding tax year for which a return was duly filed.

What is the ATL surcharge for late filers in 2026?

Under Section 182A, a late filer is placed on the Active Taxpayers List only after paying Rs 25,000 (individual), Rs 50,000 (AOP) or Rs 100,000 (company). An individual can instead furnish Form ATL-U under Section 182A(3).

Does Form ATL-U remove the late filing penalty?

No. Form ATL-U replaces only the ATL surcharge. The Section 182 penalty is separate.

Is the ATL surcharge the same as the late filing penalty?

No. The surcharge is paid to rejoin the Active Taxpayers List after a late return. The Section 182 penalty is for the delay itself and is imposed by an order of the tax officer.

Does the calculator give the final penalty FBR will charge?

No. It applies the statutory formula to the figures you enter. The penalty is imposed by an order, a genuine reason for delay can be explained, and the order can be appealed.
