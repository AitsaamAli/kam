Advance Tax Instalment Calculator: Section 147 Quarterly Tax for Individuals

Section 147 Rules checked 19 November 2026

Quick answer

An individual whose latest assessed taxable income, excluding salary and final-tax income, is Rs 1 million or more pays quarterly advance tax under Section 147(4B): one quarter of last year's assessed tax, minus tax already paid in that quarter that qualifies for credit. Instalments are due on 15 September, 15 December, 15 March and 15 June.

AOPs and companies use a turnover-based formula under Section 147(4), not this one. Rules from the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001 (FBR, amended up to 30.06.2026). This is an estimate, not advice for your case.

Who Must Pay Advance Tax

Short answer: individuals with business, professional, rental or other non-final income of Rs 1 million or more last year.

Salary taxed under Section 149 and income under final or separate regimes are excluded from the threshold and the formula. Most salaried people therefore never pay Section 147 instalments.

Worked Example

Short answer: last year's tax Rs 400,000 and Rs 20,000 already paid this quarter: the instalment is Rs 80,000.

A/4 = Rs 100,000; minus B of Rs 20,000 = Rs 80,000 due by the quarter's due date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who has to pay advance tax under Section 147?

Individuals whose latest assessed taxable income, excluding salary and final-tax income, is Rs 1 million or more.

How is the instalment calculated?

(A/4) - B: a quarter of last year's assessed tax minus creditable tax already paid in the quarter.

When are instalments due?

15 September, 15 December, 15 March and 15 June.

Do salaried people pay advance tax?

Not on salary. Section 149 deduction covers it, and salary is excluded from the Section 147 threshold.
