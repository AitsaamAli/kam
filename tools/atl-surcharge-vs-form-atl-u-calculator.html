ATL Surcharge vs Form ATL-U Calculator: Cheapest Way Back Onto the Active Taxpayers List

Section 182A and 182A(3) Rules checked 19 November 2026

Quick answer

A late filer rejoins the ATL by paying the Section 182A surcharge (Rs 25,000 individual, Rs 50,000 AOP, Rs 100,000 company) or, as an individual, by furnishing Form ATL-U and obtaining no property for six months. Form ATL-U costs nothing at FBR, so it saves Rs 25,000 whenever you can keep the promise.

Form ATL-U was notified by SRO 2097(I)/2026 on 10 November 2026 under Section 182A(3). Rules from the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001 (FBR, amended up to 30.06.2026). This is an estimate, not advice for your case.

Which ATL Route Costs Less?

Pick who is filing, what kind of return it is, and whether any property is coming to you in six months.

Off the ATL, the 20% deduction on bank profit is increased by 100%.

When Form ATL-U Is the Right Choice

Short answer: when you are an individual and nothing in your household plans brings property to you for six months.

Salaried people, pensioners, freelancers and people who are only selling property usually fit. The undertaking covers buying, acquiring, ownership and beneficial interest, so a plot bought in a relative's name with your money still counts. Our guide on what you cannot buy for six months goes through gifts, inheritance and vehicles.

When to Pay the Surcharge

Short answer: when you are buying or booking property soon, when you are not sure, or when an AOP or company filed late.

The surcharge is certain: once paid, nothing else is required. Pay it before the property deal, so you are on the ATL when the buyer's tax under Section 236K is collected. See our property transfer tax calculator.

Worked Example

Short answer: a salaried late filer with Rs 300,000 bank profit and no property plans pays Rs 5,500 with Form ATL-U instead of Rs 28,500.

Surcharge route: Rs 25,000 to FBR + Rs 3,500 return fee = Rs 28,500. Form ATL-U route: Rs 3,500 + Rs 2,000 = Rs 5,500. Meanwhile, each month off the ATL the bank deducts about Rs 5,000 more on Rs 300,000 of annual profit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Form ATL-U free?

FBR charges nothing for the undertaking. Our fee for preparing and furnishing it is Rs 2,000.

Can an AOP or company use Form ATL-U?

No. Section 182A(3) applies only to individuals.

Does either route remove the late filing penalty?

No. The Section 182 penalty is separate from the ATL route.

What if I breach Form ATL-U?

As notified, the benefit is withdrawn from the date of breach after a hearing, and the surcharge rule applies.

Can I sell property after Form ATL-U?

Yes. The undertaking restricts acquiring property, not selling it.
