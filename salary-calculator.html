Key Salary Deductions in Pakistan

  • Income Tax: Based on annual salary × FBR slab rate ÷ 12
  • EOBI: Employee's Old-Age Benefits Institution — PKR 370/month employee contribution
  • Provident Fund: Voluntary retirement savings — typically 8.33% of basic salary
  • SESSI/PESSI: Social security contribution — varies by province

File Your Tax Return
