Documents Required for Income Tax Return Filing in Pakistan 2026 — Complete Checklist

May 29, 2026
Kamboh Associates
7 min read
Lahore, Pakistan

Pakistan mein income tax return file karne se pehle sahi documents ready hona filing ko fast, accurate, aur stress-free banata hai. Galat ya incomplete documents ki wajah se return delayed hoti hai ya errors aate hain. Yeh complete checklist 4 main categories cover karti hai: salaried employees, business owners, freelancers, aur property/investors. Apni category check karein aur documents prepare karein.

Important note: Har category ke documents alag hain. Sabse pehle yeh decide karein ke aapki income ka primary source kya hai. Phir us category ka checklist follow karein. Kamboh Associates free consultation mein aapko guide karta hai ke aapke liye exactly kaunse documents chahiye hain.

Zaroor Documents — Har Kisi Ke Liye

Yeh documents sab ko chahiye — chahe salaried hon, business owner hon, ya freelancer:

Basic Identity & Account Documents
Sab ke liye compulsory
Original CNIC (13-digit)
CNIC-registered SIM (OTP ke liye)
Active email address
NTN number (agar pehle liya ho)
Bank account number (refund ke liye)
IRIS portal login credentials

Salaried Employees Ke Documents

Pakistan mein salaried persons ke liye yeh sabse common return type hai. In documents ki zaroorat hogi:

Salaried Person — Document Checklist
Government ya private sector employee
Employer tax certificate (Section 50)
Salary slips (12 months)
Bank statements (12 months, all accounts)
Provident fund / pension statement
Property documents (agar property ho)
Vehicle registration copy (agar gaari ho)
Investment statements (shares, mutual funds)
Zakat / sadaqah receipts (deduction ke liye)

Pro tip for salaried persons: "Section 50 Employer Certificate" ya "Annual Salary Statement" — yeh sabse important document hai. Is mein aapki full salary, allowances, aur deducted tax sab likha hota hai. HR department se October-November mein yeh maangein.

Business Owners Ke Documents

Business owners ke liye return zyada complex hoti hai — zyada sources, zyada documents. Yeh checklist follow karein:

Business Owner / Self-Employed — Document Checklist
Shop, factory, agency, ya koi bhi business
Business income records / cashbook
Purchase invoices (saal bhar ki)
Sales invoices / receipts
Business bank statements (12 months)
Sales tax / GST returns (agar registered ho)
Business registration documents (SECP/NTN)
Loan statements (agar business loan ho)
Stock / inventory value (year end)
Rental receipts (agar office rent pay ki ho)
Staff salary records (agar employees hain)

Freelancers & Online Workers Ke Documents

Pakistani freelancers jo Upwork, Fiverr, YouTube, ya kisi bhi platform se income karte hain — unke liye yeh documents:

Freelancer / Digital Worker — Document Checklist
Online income, foreign remittance wale
Platform earnings statement (Upwork, Fiverr)
Bank remittance certificates
Bank statements (12 months)
Payment proof / client invoices
Equipment purchase receipts (deduction)
Internet bills (deductible business expense)

Freelancers ke liye important: Foreign remittance aane par bank automatically WHT certificate banata hai. Is certificate ko carefully save karein — return mein claim karna hota hai. Bank se "Remittance Certificate" ya "WHT on Foreign Remittance" statement maangein.

Property Owners & Investors Ke Documents

Property Owners & Investors — Document Checklist
Rental income, property transactions, investments
Property deed / title document copy
Property tax payment receipts
Rental agreement copy (agar kiraya ho)
Rental income receipts / bank transfers
Share/mutual fund portfolio statement
Dividend income certificates
Vehicle registration + purchase receipt
SECP company shareholding documents

Wealth Statement Ke Liye Documents

Wealth statement return ka critical part hai. Is mein aapki tamam assets aur liabilities declare karni hoti hain. Inki value ke liye yeh documents ready rakhein:

All bank account balance statements
Property valuation / market rate
Vehicle market value
Jewelry / gold approximate value
Stocks / mutual funds portfolio value
Outstanding loans / liabilities list

Wealth statement mein incomplete ya undervalued assets FBR audit ka sabab banti hain. Aapki tamam tangible assets — property, gaari, cash, gold, investments — accurate declare karein. Kamboh Associates wealth statement ko professionally aur safely handle karta hai.

Employer certificate nahi mil rahi — kya return file ho sakti hai?
Haan, salary slips se bhi kaam chal sakta hai. Lekin employer certificate mein exact deducted tax amount hoti hai — jo return mein claim karna hota hai. Bina is ke aap apna deducted tax refund nahi pa sakte. HR department se demand karein — yeh aapka legal right hai.
Bank statement kitne months ki chahiye?
Tax year ke mutabiq July se June tak ki full 12-month statement chahiye. Jo main account use karte hain us ki detailed statement aur sab accounts ki ending balance statement zaroori hai wealth statement ke liye.
