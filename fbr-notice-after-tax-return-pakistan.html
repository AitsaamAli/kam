FBR Notice After Filing Tax Return 2026

June 4, 2026
Kamboh Associates
9 min read
Lahore, Pakistan

Received an FBR notice after filing your tax return? This doesn't automatically mean you've done something wrong. FBR sends notices to clarify discrepancies, verify income sources, or request additional documentation. The key is responding professionally and on time. This guide explains what FBR notices mean, why they're sent, and how to respond effectively.

What is an FBR Notice?

An FBR notice is an official inquiry sent when FBR detects discrepancies or needs clarification on reported information. Common reasons include:

  • Unreported income discrepancies
  • Unexplained increases in assets
  • Inconsistent expense claims
  • Withholding tax mismatches
  • Wealth statement-income gap

Response Deadline

Typically 14-30 days from notice date. Non-response can trigger:

  • Automatic assessment of full income
  • Penalty @50-100% of tax
  • Asset freezing
  • Recovery through courts

How to Respond Professionally

  1. Don't ignore: Respond within deadline, even if partially
  2. Gather documents: Receipts, bank statements, invoices
  3. Clarify position: Explain income/expense discrepancies
  4. Request extension: If 30 days insufficient, request 15 days more
  5. File amendment: Correct errors in original return
  6. Seek professional help: Consult tax advisor

Critical: Never ignore FBR notice. Professional response significantly improves outcome and reduces penalties.

Amending Your Return

You can file amended return within 120 days of notice to:

  • Correct calculation errors
  • Add previously unreported income
  • Claim additional deductions
  • Update wealth statement

Strategic advantage: Amendment shows good faith and often eliminates penalties. FBR appreciates voluntary corrections.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does FBR notice mean?
FBR notice is an inquiry notification when FBR detects discrepancies or needs clarification. Not necessarily an adverse action — just a request for documentation.
What's the deadline to respond?
Typically 14-30 days from notice date. Non-response triggers automatic assessment and penalties.
Can I amend after FBR notice?
Yes. Amendment within 120 days often eliminates penalties and demonstrates voluntary compliance.
