How to Become a Filer in Pakistan 2026: Step-by-Step FBR ATL Guide

Published on: May 20, 2026 | Category: Tax Compliance

In Pakistan's current economic landscape, staying compliant with the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) is no longer optional—it is a financial necessity.

Why You Must Become a Filer Today

  • Reduced Tax on Banking Transactions
  • Property Transaction Benefits
  • Vehicle Registration Savings
  • Business Compliance Advantages

Step 1: Online NTN Registration via FBR Iris Portal

  1. Go to the official FBR Iris Portal.
  2. Complete registration process.
  3. Verify CNIC and mobile number.
  4. Submit required information.

Step 2: Filing Your Annual Income Tax Return

Filing annual income tax return is mandatory for ATL status.

Step 3: Checking Your ATL Status Online

  • SMS your CNIC to 9966
  • Check through FBR website
