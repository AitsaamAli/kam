Step 1: Online NTN Registration via FBR Iris Portal

Before filing taxes, you must obtain a National Tax Number (NTN).

  1. Visit the official FBR IRIS portal.
  2. Click on Registration for Unregistered Person.
  3. Enter your CNIC, mobile number, and email.
  4. Verify OTP codes.
  5. Complete the 181 registration form.

Official Portal:

https://iris.fbr.gov.pk/

Step 2: Filing Your Annual Income Tax Return

After obtaining NTN, you must file your annual income tax return to become part of the Active Taxpayer List (ATL).

Proper filing includes declaration of:

  • Income
  • Assets
  • Expenses
  • Tax deductions
  • Banking information

Step 3: Check Your ATL Status

  • Send your CNIC to 9966
  • Verify online through FBR ATL portal

Documents Required for Tax Filing

  • CNIC Copy
  • Bank Statements
  • Salary Slips
  • Business Income Records
