Becoming a tax filer in Pakistan is now essential for individuals, freelancers, business owners, and salaried persons. Active taxpayers enjoy lower withholding taxes, better banking benefits, and smoother property and vehicle transactions.

Why You Should Become a Filer in Pakistan

  • Lower banking transaction taxes
  • Reduced property purchase taxes
  • Lower vehicle registration taxes
  • Better visa and loan approval chances
  • Legal compliance with FBR

Step 1: Register for NTN Online

Before filing taxes, you must obtain an NTN through the official FBR IRIS portal.

  1. Visit the FBR IRIS portal
  2. Choose registration for unregistered person
  3. Enter CNIC, phone number, and email
  4. Verify OTP
  5. Complete the registration form

Official Portal:
https://iris.fbr.gov.pk/

Step 2: File Income Tax Return

After NTN registration, you must file your annual income tax return and wealth statement to become part of the Active Taxpayer List (ATL).

  • Declare annual income
  • Provide expense details
  • Submit asset information
  • Declare banking details

Step 3: Check ATL Status

  • Send CNIC to 9966
  • Check online through FBR ATL portal

Documents Required

  • CNIC Copy
  • Bank Statements
  • Salary Slips
  • Business Records
  • Utility Bills
