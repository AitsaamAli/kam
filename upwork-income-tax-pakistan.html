Tax on Upwork Income in Pakistan 2026 — Everything a Pakistani Freelancer Needs to Know

May 30, 2026
Kamboh Associates
8 min read
Lahore, Pakistan

Upwork Pakistan ka sabse bada professional freelance platform hai — hourly aur fixed-price contracts, long-term clients, aur $10 se lekar $100+ hourly rates. Lekin jab rupees account mein aate hain, ek sawaal zaroor aata hai: Upwork income par Pakistan mein kitna tax lagta hai? Jawab is guide mein hai — clearly aur practically.

Upwork Income — Kaise Classify Hoti Hai?

Upwork se jo income aati hai woh Pakistan mein "foreign source income" ya "business income" classify hoti hai depending on how it's received. Practically, zyada-tar freelancers ise business income declare karte hain IRIS mein.

Upwork payments aksar Payoneer ke zariye aati hain, jo phir Pakistani bank mein transfer hoti hai. Yeh remittance channel zaroori hai — kyunki IT export tax exemption ka faida sirf designated banking channel se aane wali income par milta hai.

Upwork vs Fiverr — Tax treatment same hai: Dono platforms ki income ka Pakistan mein ek jaisa treatment hai — IT export income classify hoti hai, same WHT rates apply hoti hain, aur same deductions available hain. Fiverr tax guide bhi dekhen →

WHT Rates — Upwork Bank Transfer Par

Jab Payoneer se Pakistani bank mein transfer karte hain ya Upwork direct bank transfer use karte hain:

Transaction TypeActive Filer (ATL)Non-Filer
Foreign remittance (bank deposit)1% WHT2% WHT
Cash withdrawal (over Rs. 50,000)0.6%1.2%
Banking transactions0.3%0.6%

Yeh WHT deductible hai — matlab return file kar ke credit milta hai. Agar total tax liability WHT se kam hai, to refund bhi possible hai.

Upwork Income IRIS Mein Declare Karna

1
Saara Saal Ki Income Calculate Karein
Upwork payment history + Payoneer transactions + bank credit entries — July 2025 to June 2026 ka total income Pakistani rupees mein convert karein. SBP exchange rate use karein (ya bank credit amounts).
2
Business Expenses Separate Karein
Upwork service fee (5-20% tiered), internet, equipment, software — inhe subtract karein net income calculate karne ke liye.
Upwork 5-20% fee directly deductible hai
3
IRIS Mein Income Tax Return File Karein
Business Income section mein net income enter karein. WHT credits enter karein. IT export exemption claim karein agar eligible.
4
Wealth Statement Bharein
Bank balance, cash, aur koi bhi assets declare karein. Return validate nahi hoti without completed wealth statement.
Wealth statement accuracy zaroori hai — FBR mismatch notice bhejta hai

Upwork Long-Term Contract vs Project-Based — Koi Fark?

Tax treatment mein koi fark nahi — dono ka income same tarike se declare hota hai. Lekin documentation mein fark hai:

  • Long-term hourly contracts: Upwork time logs + Payoneer statements = documentation
  • Fixed-price projects: Upwork milestone payments + bank credit = documentation
  • Enterprise/direct contracts: Invoice + bank transfer = documentation

Upwork Connects khareedna: Agar aap Pakistan se Upwork Connects khareedne ke liye credit card use karte hain — yeh amount bhi deductible business expense hai. Screenshot rakhein.

IT Export Exemption — Upwork Par Kaise Apply Hoti Hai?

SRO 1125(I)/2023 ke under IT/software export income par tax relief available hai. Upwork se jo income aati hai — web development, software, design, writing — yeh mostly IT export qualify karti hai.

Condition: income designated Pakistani bank (HBL, UBL, Meezan, etc.) mein proper remittance channel se aani chahiye. Payoneer → Bank Transfer yeh route sahi hai. Payoneer card se ATM cash nahi chahiye.

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal

Kya Upwork income taxable hai Pakistan mein?
Haan. Lekin IT export income par significant exemptions available hain. Properly file karne par effective tax rate bahut low ho sakti hai.
Upwork Payoneer withdrawal par WHT kitna hai?
Active filer: 1% WHT. Non-filer: 2% WHT on foreign remittance to Pakistani bank. Yeh adjustable hai — return mein credit milta hai.
Upwork income FBR mein kahan declare karein?
IRIS mein "Business Income" ya "Income from Other Sources" section mein. Net income (gross minus Upwork fees and expenses) declare karein.
Kya Upwork ki service fee deduct hoti hai?
Haan. Upwork ka 5-20% tiered service fee directly deductible business expense hai. Upwork statement ya payment history se document karein.
Kya dollar income ko rupees mein convert karein?
Haan. FBR return mein PKR mein declare karna hota hai. Bank credit amount (jo already PKR mein hota hai) use karna sab se easy hai — bank ne jo rate apply kiya.
Upwork Plus membership fee deductible hai?
Haan. Upwork Plus membership aur Connects khareedna — dono deductible business expenses hain. Screenshot ya payment receipts rakhein.
