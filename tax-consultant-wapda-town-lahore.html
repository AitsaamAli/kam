Filing status for Tax Year 2026

The Tax Year 2026 return deadline for individuals and AOPs was extended to 31 October 2026 and has now passed. A late return can still be filed on IRIS. Under Section 182 the late-filing penalty is 0.1% of the tax payable per day, up to 50%, with a minimum of Rs 40,000 (Rs 5,000 where at least 75% of income is salary below Rs 5 million). Joining the Active Taxpayers List after the due date also needs the Section 182A surcharge: Rs 25,000 for individuals, Rs 50,000 for AOPs and Rs 100,000 for companies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is free electricity from WAPDA taxable?

Employer-provided benefits can be taxable perquisites. How the electricity concession is treated depends on your employer's practice and the current rules; we follow your salary certificate.

Is buying in Wapda Town different from buying in DHA?

Wapda Town is a cooperative society, so the transfer involves membership approval and NOCs from the society, alongside advance tax and provincial charges.

Is my Airbnb income rent or business income?

If you provide services like cleaning, linen and check-in for short stays, it's often business income. A monthly let to one tenant is usually rent.

Does audit selection mean FBR thinks I've done something wrong?

No. Returns are selected by risk-based balloting, random selection or on information. It's a review of what you filed.
