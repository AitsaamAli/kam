How to Become a Filer in Pakistan 2026: Step-by-Step FBR ATL Guide

Published on: May 20, 2026 | Category: Tax Compliance

In Pakistan's current economic landscape, staying compliant with the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) is no longer optional—it is a financial necessity. Whether you are buying a car, purchasing immovable property, or running a business, being a Non-Filer subjects you to heavily penalized withholding tax rates. Becoming an active filer saves you money and establishes your financial credibility.

At Kamboh Associates, we simplify tax matters for you. Here is the definitive, expert-backed guide on how to register for an NTN and successfully enter the FBR Active Taxpayer List (ATL).

Why You Must Become a Filer Today

The Government of Pakistan has significantly increased the tax differential between filers and non-filers. By appearing on the ATL, you enjoy numerous legal and financial benefits, including:

  • Reduced Tax on Banking Transactions: Lower withholding taxes on cash withdrawals and banking instruments.
  • Property Transactions: Massive savings on the purchase, sale, and transfer of immovable real estate property.
  • Vehicle Registration: Substantially lower token tax and registration fees for cars (Suzuki, Honda, Toyota, etc.).
  • Import/Export Clearence: Essential compliance advantages for commercial setups and corporate enterprises.

Step 1: Online NTN Registration via FBR Iris Portal

Before you can become a filer, you must have a National Tax Number (NTN). If you do not have one, follow these steps:

  1. Go to the official FBR Iris Portal.
  2. Click on 'Registration for Unregistered Person'.
  3. Enter your CNIC, mobile number (registered in your own name), and email address.
  4. Submit the system-generated OTP verification codes sent to your mobile phone and email.
  5. Log in using the credentials sent to you by the FBR system and complete the 181 Form for business or salary registration.

Step 2: Filing Your Annual Income Tax Return

Simply having an NTN does not automatically make you a filer. To enter the Active Taxpayer List, you must file your formal Annual Income Tax Return (Wealth Statement and Declaration Form 114).

You must accurately declare your annual income, expenses, domestic assets, and tax deductions. Any discrepancy or misdeclaration can result in formal FBR legal notices or audit complications under tax laws.

Step 3: Checking Your ATL Status Online

Once your returns are successfully submitted, you can verify your active status through the following channels:

  • SMS Service: Type "ATL [space] 13-Digit CNIC" and send it to 9966.
  • FBR Website: Check the online portal under the 'Active Taxpayer List (Income Tax)' tab.
