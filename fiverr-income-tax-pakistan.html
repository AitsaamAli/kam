Tax on Fiverr Income in Pakistan 2026 — Complete Guide for Pakistani Freelancers

May 29, 2026
Kamboh Associates
8 min read
Lahore, Pakistan

Fiverr se paise kamaana mushkil kaam hai — dollar mein income aana, clients satisfy karna, aur platform ka 20% commission. Phir bhi, bahut Pakistani freelancers regular $500–$5,000 monthly earn kar rahe hain Fiverr se. Sawaal yeh hai: Pakistan mein Fiverr income par kitna tax lagta hai, aur FBR ko declare karna zaroori hai ya nahi? Is guide mein honestly aur clearly sab kuch batate hain.

Kya Fiverr Income Taxable Hai?

Seedha jawab: Haan — Fiverr income Pakistan mein taxable hai. Yeh chahe local income ho ya foreign, Pakistani resident ki har income par income tax apply hoti hai per Income Tax Ordinance 2001.

Lekin — aur yeh ek important lekin hai — IT export income par significant tax exemptions available hain. Matlab yeh ke properly declared Fiverr income par effective tax rate bahut kam ho sakti hai — kuch cases mein almost zero for IT export qualified income.

Good news: SRO 1125(I)/2023 ke under, IT/digital services export income (jo Fiverr type work hai) par significant tax relief available hai — jab income designated Pakistani bank channel se aaye. Iska matlab properly filing karne par aapki effective tax rate dramatically lower ho sakti hai.

Bank Transfer Par WHT — Filer vs Non-Filer

Fiverr Payoneer withdraw karo ya seedha bank mein — Pakistani bank aapke account par withholding tax (WHT) deduct karta hai:

TransactionFiler (ATL)Non-Filer
Foreign remittance (bank transfer)1%2%
Cash withdrawal over Rs. 50,0000.6%1.2%
Banking transaction (cheque/transfer)0.3%0.6%

Ek example: Agar aap monthly $2,000 (approx Rs. 5,56,000) Payoneer se bank mein transfer karte hain, to filer hone par Rs. 5,560 WHT, non-filer par Rs. 11,120. Saal bhar mein Rs. 66,720 ka fark sirf filer hone se. Aur yeh tax return mein adjustable bhi hota hai.

Fiverr Income FBR Mein Kaise Declare Karein?

1
IRIS Portal Par Login Karein
iris.fbr.gov.pk — Declaration → Income Tax Return. Tax Year 2026 (July 2025 – June 2026) select karein.
2
Business Income Section Bharein
"Income from Business" ya "Income from Other Sources" section mein total Fiverr earnings (PKR mein convert) enter karein. Bank statements se total calculate karein.
Fiverr payment history + bank statement = your income proof
3
Expenses Deduct Karein
Legitimate business expenses deduct karein — internet, equipment, Fiverr commission (20%), software subscriptions, etc.
4
IT Export Exemption Claim
Agar designated bank se remittance receive hua hai, IT export exemption/SRO benefit claim karein. Consultant se help lein is step mein.
Yeh step maximum savings dilata hai
5
WHT Adjustments Enter Karein
Bank ne jo WHT deduct ki hai — woh "Tax already paid" section mein enter karein. Zyada katne par refund mil sakta hai.

Fiverr Freelancers Ki Common Tax Mistakes

  • Non-banking channels use karna: Crypto, friends transfer, ya cash channels se income lena — documentation nahi milti aur IT exemption bhi nahi milti
  • Income declare hi nahi karna: "FBR ko kaise pata chalega" — yeh soch 2026 mein risky hai. Bank SBP ko report karta hai, SBP FBR ke saath data share karta hai
  • Sirf bank WHT dekhna: "Bank ne tax kata — ab kya karein" — WHT adjustable hai, return file karein aur balance karein
  • IT exemption miss karna: Largest potential saving freelancers aksar miss karte hain

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal

Kya Fiverr income Pakistan mein taxable hai?
Haan. Pakistan mein resident ki sari income taxable hai including Fiverr. Lekin IT export income par significant exemptions available hain jab designated bank channel se remittance aaye.
Fiverr income FBR mein kaise declare hoti hai?
IRIS mein "Business Income" ya "Income from Other Sources" section mein total Fiverr earnings declare karo. Bank statements aur Fiverr payment history documentation ke tor par rakhein.
Kya Fiverr Payoneer withdrawal par tax lagta hai?
Payoneer se Pakistani bank transfer par filer: 1% WHT, non-filer: 2% WHT. Yeh tax adjustable hai — return mein credit milta hai.
Kya Fiverr commission expense deduct hoti hai?
Haan. Fiverr ka 20% service fee directly business expense hai — deduct ho sakti hai taxable income se. Sab expenses properly document karein.
IT export exemption kya hoti hai freelancers ke liye?
SRO 1125(I)/2023 ke under qualified IT/digital services export income par tax relief available hai. Condition: income designated Pakistani bank se receive honi chahiye. Consultant se exact eligibility aur current SRO status verify karwayein.
Fiverr income declare na karne par kya hoga?
Bank transactions SBP ko report hoti hain, SBP FBR ke saath data share karta hai. Unexplained income notices 2025-26 mein common ho rahe hain. FBR notice + penalty zyada pareshaan karta hai — voluntarily file karna better hai.
