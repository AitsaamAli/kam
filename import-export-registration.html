FAQ

Common Questions

What is WeBOC and why do I need it?
WeBOC (Web Based One Customs) is Pakistan Customs' online system for processing import and export declarations. Your NTN must be linked to WeBOC before any customs clearance can proceed. Without WeBOC enrollment, you cannot legally import or export goods in Pakistan.
Do I need a Sales Tax number for importing?
Yes. All importers are required to have a Sales Tax Registration Number (STRN) as import GST is collected at the import stage. Without STRN, you cannot claim input tax adjustment on your imports.
How long does the complete registration take?
NTN and STRN are typically issued within 3-5 working days. WeBOC linkage takes an additional 2-3 days. TDAP registration takes 7-10 working days. Our full package averages 10-15 working days end-to-end.
Can I export without registering with TDAP?
Yes, TDAP registration is not mandatory for all exporters. However, TDAP-registered exporters get access to export rebates, duty drawback schemes, and government export facilitation programs that can significantly reduce your costs.
