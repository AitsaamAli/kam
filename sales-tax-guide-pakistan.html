Sales Tax & STRN Pakistan
The Complete Guide — Registration, Monthly Filing & Business Structures

KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • STRN (Sales Tax Registration Number) is mandatory once annual taxable turnover exceeds Rs. 10 million
  • Monthly sales tax return + payment is due by the 18th of the following month
  • Businesses also face withholding tax obligations and, depending on turnover, minimum tax under Section 113
  • The right business structure (sole proprietor, AOP, or private limited company) changes your tax rate and compliance burden

Sales Tax Registration — Who Must Register

Manufacturers, importers, and Tier-1 retailers must register regardless of some thresholds; other businesses must register once annual taxable turnover crosses Rs. 10 million. Exporters typically register voluntarily to claim input tax refunds on zero-rated supplies. Registration is completed on IRIS and requires a bank account certificate, business premises verification (including GPS-tagged photos in many cases), and CNIC copies of owners or directors — a mismatch between the address on the utility bill, rent agreement, and premises photo is the single most common cause of a rejected application.

Sales Tax Registration (STRN) — Complete Guide covers documents needed, the IRIS application process, and common rejection reasons.

Monthly Sales Tax Return Filing

Once registered, a return is due every month by the 18th — including a Nil return in months with no sales, since non-filing (even of a Nil return) carries the same penalty as a late substantive return. The return itself is built from annexures that must reconcile with each other: a purchase/input register, a sales/output register, and for manufacturers, a stock statement — a large unexplained gap between reported sales and stock movement is one of the most common audit triggers.

Sales Tax Return Filing — Complete FBR IRIS Guide covers the annexures in detail, withholding by government buyers, refunds for exporters via the FASTER system, and de-registration.

Withholding Tax Obligations

Businesses that qualify as withholding agents — government departments, public sector organizations, and specified large companies — must deduct tax at source on payments to contractors, suppliers, and service providers, and file a monthly WHT statement separate from the sales tax return. Rates differ sharply between filers and non-filers on the paying end, which is one more reason maintaining active filer status matters even for a business that doesn't itself sell to consumers.

Minimum Tax — Section 113

If a company's normal computed tax liability is less than a set percentage of gross turnover, minimum tax applies instead — even in a loss-making year. This catches businesses with thin margins, heavy deductions, or a deliberately low declared profit, and is a common surprise for first-time filers who assume a loss year means zero tax due.

Choosing a Business Structure

Sole proprietorship, AOP/partnership, and private limited company each carry different registration steps, tax rates, and compliance obligations. A sole proprietorship has the simplest setup but no liability separation from the owner; an AOP suits a partnership between a small number of people but files its own return in addition to each partner declaring their share individually; a private limited company offers liability protection and easier fundraising but comes with SECP compliance on top of FBR filing. The right choice depends on the number of owners, liability protection needs, and growth plans — get this decision reviewed before registering, since converting structures later is far more work than choosing correctly the first time.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is sales tax registration (STRN) mandatory in Pakistan?
Once annual taxable turnover exceeds Rs. 10 million, or immediately for manufacturers, importers, and Tier-1 retailers regardless of turnover.
When is the monthly sales tax return due?
By the 18th of the month following the tax period, including a Nil return if there were no sales that month.
What is minimum tax under Section 113?
A tax on gross turnover that applies when a company's normal computed tax liability would otherwise be lower — it applies even to loss-making businesses above the applicable turnover threshold.
Which business structure has the lowest compliance burden?
Sole proprietorship has the simplest registration and filing, but offers no liability protection. AOPs and private limited companies have more compliance steps but different tax and liability trade-offs — see the linked guides for a full comparison.
