Income Tax Filing Pakistan
The Complete Guide — Filing, Deadlines, Filer Status & Refunds

Who Must File an Income Tax Return in Pakistan

Filing is mandatory if any of the following apply to you: annual income exceeds Rs. 600,000; you own immovable property of 250 sq. yards or more, or any commercial property; you own a motor vehicle; you hold a National Tax Number (NTN) for any reason; you're a resident who has foreign income or assets; or you're a company, AOP, or registered business regardless of profit. Even if your employer already deducts tax at source, filing your own return is still legally required and is what actually gets your name onto the Active Taxpayer List (ATL).

Income Tax Return Filing Process — Step by Step

Returns are filed through FBR's IRIS portal (iris.fbr.gov.pk). The exact screens differ slightly by income type, but the process is broadly: log in with your NTN/CNIC credentials, select the relevant tax year, declare your income under the correct head (salary, business, property, capital gains, other sources), declare assets and liabilities in the wealth statement, reconcile any withholding tax already deducted, and submit.

Filing Deadlines and Penalties for Late Filing

The standard deadline for individuals and AOPs is September 30; companies generally file by December 31 (varies by accounting year-end). Missing the deadline doesn't stop you from filing late, but it does trigger real costs.

Filer vs Non-Filer Status

Being on FBR's Active Taxpayer List is what actually delivers the financial benefit of filing — non-filers pay substantially higher withholding tax on property purchases, vehicle registration, banking transactions, and dozens of other transactions specified across the Income Tax Ordinance's withholding provisions.

Wealth Statement — A Mandatory Attachment

Every individual return must be accompanied by a wealth statement (Section 116) declaring your assets, liabilities, and net worth reconciliation year-over-year. A wealth statement that doesn't reconcile with declared income is one of the most common triggers for an FBR notice.

Getting a Refund

If withholding tax deducted through the year exceeds your final assessed liability, the difference is refundable. FBR is legally required to process a refund application within 60 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is legally required to file an income tax return in Pakistan?
Anyone earning above Rs. 600,000 annually, anyone owning specified property or a vehicle, anyone with an NTN, and all registered businesses and companies regardless of profit, must file an annual return by the deadline.
What is the deadline for filing income tax return in Pakistan 2026?
September 30 for individuals and AOPs; companies typically file by December 31, depending on accounting year-end. FBR occasionally announces short extensions.
What happens if I file my income tax return late?
You face a penalty of Rs. 1,000 per month of delay (individuals) plus a 0.1% daily surcharge on tax payable, and you drop off the Active Taxpayer List until the surcharge is cleared and ATL inclusion is processed.
Do I need to file a wealth statement along with my return?
Yes, individuals must file a wealth statement (Section 116) declaring assets, liabilities, and net worth reconciliation alongside the income tax return every year.
How long does an income tax refund take in Pakistan?
FBR is legally required to process a refund application within 60 days of filing, though complex cases involving verification can take longer.
