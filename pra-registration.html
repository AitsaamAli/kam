Who Needs It

Who Needs PRA Registration

Section 25(1): anyone who provides a taxable service from an office or place of business in Punjab, or is otherwise required by the Act, the rules or a PRA notification. Section 26 allows voluntary registration.

Sole Proprietors

One-person service businesses register as individuals, under the owner’s CNIC and NTN, with the trade name on each business place. See PRA for sole proprietors.

Firms and Companies

AOPs and companies register with their registration or incorporation number and their partners or directors. Several centrally billed branches can share one registration (rule 2(2)).

Businesses From Other Provinces

Services rendered in Punjab need a separate Punjab registration, even if you are registered in another province (rule 2(4)).

Fee: Rs 5,000 fixed for PRA registration; Rs 2,000 more only if you need an FBR NTN. PRA itself charges nothing (rule 15). Both fees are on our pricing page.

  • A person required to register who does not apply can be registered compulsorily by PRA after notice and a hearing (section 27).

FAQ

Common Questions

Who must register with PRA?
Section 25(1) of the Punjab Sales Tax on Services Act 2012: anyone who provides a taxable service from an office or place of business in Punjab, or is otherwise required by the Act, rules or a PRA notification. Others may register voluntarily under section 26.
Does PRA charge a registration fee?
No. Rule 15 of the Punjab Sales Tax on Services (Registration and De-registration) Rules 2012 makes registration and de-registration free of any fee or service charge.
How much do you charge for PRA registration?
Rs 5,000 fixed, as published on our pricing page. If you need an FBR NTN first, that is Rs 2,000 more.
What documents are needed?
CNIC, FBR NTN, the address of each business place with how it is held (and the landlord's CNIC or NTN if rented), its electricity reference number, each business bank account, and the services you provide (Form PST-01).
How long does PRA registration take?
Rule 3 gives a provisional certificate immediately on applying and the final Form PST-03 certificate after verification, preferably within thirty days.
I am already registered with FBR. Do I need this?
Rule 10 registered existing FBR taxpayers with PRA automatically, with P+NTN. We check the Taxpayer Profile Inquiry first and apply only if you are not found.
I received a PRA notice. Can you help?
Yes. Section 27 requires advance notice and a hearing before compulsory registration. We read the notice, advise whether registration applies, and file on the notice route or prepare your reply.
