Filing status for Tax Year 2026

The Tax Year 2026 return deadline for individuals and AOPs was extended to 31 October 2026 and has now passed. A late return can still be filed on IRIS. Under Section 182 the late-filing penalty is 0.1% of the tax payable per day, up to 50%, with a minimum of Rs 40,000 (Rs 5,000 where at least 75% of income is salary below Rs 5 million). Joining the Active Taxpayers List after the due date also needs the Section 182A surcharge: Rs 25,000 for individuals, Rs 50,000 for AOPs and Rs 100,000 for companies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Developers deduct tax from my design fees. How do I claim it?

Collect each withholding certificate and claim the deductions in your return.

Can self-employed people get a tax credit for pension savings?

Yes. Contributions to approved voluntary pension funds can qualify for a tax credit, subject to limits and conditions.

Is a wedding booking advance income when I receive it?

A non-refundable advance is generally income when received or when the event happens, depending on your consistent policy. A refundable deposit is a liability until the event.

Is printing a supply of goods or a service?

Printed products sold are usually goods; design, installation and printing on customer-supplied material are often services. Many print shops do both.
