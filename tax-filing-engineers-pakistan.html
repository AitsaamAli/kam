Tax Filing for Engineers in Pakistan
Complete 2026 Guide

📅 May 31, 2026 ⏱ 8 min read ✍️ Kamboh Associates

Engineers in Pakistan span multiple disciplines — civil, mechanical, electrical, chemical, software, and more. Whether you work for a multinational firm, a government department, or you freelance internationally, your tax obligations depend on your income type and employment structure.

Software engineers take note: If you earn in foreign currency through platforms like Upwork or direct client payments, you may qualify for the 0.25% fixed income tax on foreign remittances regime under SRO 1487(I)/2022 — a significant tax saving.

Income Types for Engineers

  • Salary income: Monthly pay from a registered employer
  • Consultancy fees: Project-based income from companies
  • Freelance contracts: Direct client payments via bank or digital transfer
  • Foreign remittances: Overseas client payments to Pakistani bank accounts
  • Construction projects: Civil engineers running their own contracting firm
  • Teaching/training: Engineering courses and workshops

Deductible Expenses for Engineers

If you operate as a freelancer or have project-based income, these costs reduce your tax:

  • Laptop and workstation: 30% annual depreciation on IT equipment
  • Engineering software: AutoCAD, MATLAB, SolidWorks, VS Code paid plans
  • Professional tools and instruments: Site-survey equipment, meters, etc.
  • Home office: Proportionate share of rent and utilities
  • PEC registration fee: Pakistan Engineering Council annual fee
  • Professional development: Certifications, courses, engineering seminars
  • Vehicle/travel: Site visits and client meetings (maintain log)
  • Internet and phone: Business-use portion

The 0.25% Foreign Income Tax Benefit

Under current FBR rules, engineers (especially software engineers) who receive foreign currency payments via official banking channels can opt for 0.25% income tax as a final tax on those remittances. This is far lower than the normal slab rate. Requirements:

  • Payment must be received through a Pakistani bank account
  • Payment must be from an overseas client (foreign currency)
  • You must be registered with FBR and have an active NTN
  • The service must be exported (rendered to a foreign entity)

Filing Your Tax Return

  1. Register on FBR IRIS (iris.fbr.gov.pk) — get your NTN
  2. Collect salary certificates, client invoices, and bank statements
  3. Gather withholding tax certificates from all sources
  4. Open IRIS → Declaration → Income Tax Return (Form IT-2)
  5. Enter salary income and/or business/freelance income
  6. Claim deductible expenses under the business income section
  7. Apply all withholding tax credits
  8. Complete wealth statement if your net assets exceed the threshold
  9. Pay balance tax via PSID and submit before September 30

Frequently Asked Questions

Do engineers need to file income tax in Pakistan?
Yes. All engineers earning above Rs 600,000 annually must file an income tax return with FBR. Salaried engineers get a salary certificate from their employer; freelance engineers file under business income.
What is the tax treatment for freelance engineers in Pakistan?
Freelance engineers earning in foreign currency can benefit from the 0.25% fixed income tax on foreign remittances credited to a Pakistani bank account through official channels, or file under normal business income slabs.
Can engineers deduct work tools and equipment from taxes?
Yes. Engineers can deduct depreciation on laptops, AutoCAD licenses, engineering software, surveying equipment, and other tools used directly for professional work at 30% per year for IT equipment.
