Tax on Payoneer Withdrawals in Pakistan 2026 — Complete WHT & Income Tax Guide

May 30, 2026
Kamboh Associates
7 min read
Lahore, Pakistan

Payoneer Pakistan mein freelancers ka sab se zyada use hone wala payment platform hai — Fiverr, Upwork, Amazon, Toptal — sab Payoneer support karte hain. Lekin jab Pakistani bank mein transfer karte hain, bank automatically withholding tax (WHT) deduct karta hai. Kitna? Filer ke liye 1%, non-filer ke liye 2%. Is complete guide mein Payoneer ka Pakistan mein poora tax picture samjhate hain.

Payoneer Bank Transfer Par WHT — Direct Comparison

Jab aap Payoneer se apne Pakistani bank account mein paise transfer karte hain, bank Section 231AA ke under WHT deduct karta hai:

Active Filer (ATL)
1%
Per foreign remittance transfer to Pakistani bank account
Half the rate of non-filer
Non-Filer
2%
Per foreign remittance transfer — double the filer rate
Double the cost annually

Real calculation: Agar aap monthly Rs. 3 lakh Payoneer se bank mein transfer karte hain, to saal mein:
— Filer: Rs. 36,000 WHT deducted (adjustable in return)
— Non-filer: Rs. 72,000 WHT deducted (not adjustable)
Fark: Rs. 36,000 annually — sirf filer hone se

WHT Adjustable Hai — Return Mein Credit Milta Hai

Yeh samajhna zaroori hai: filer ke liye Payoneer par kata hua 1% WHT adjustable tax hai. Matlab income tax return mein yeh amount credit ho jati hai. Agar aapki total income tax liability WHT se kam hai — refund possible hai.

Non-filers ke liye 2% WHT final tax ki tarah treat hoti hai — refund nahi milta. Toh sirf filer hone se double saving: rate kam bhi, refundable bhi.

Example: Aapne saal mein Rs. 5 lakh Payoneer se bank mein transfer kiya. 1% WHT kata = Rs. 5,000. Aapki actual income tax liability calculations ke baad Rs. 3,000 nikli. Return file karne par Rs. 2,000 refund milega aapko — kyunki WHT adjustable hai.

Payoneer Income FBR Mein Declare Karna

WHT alag hai — income tax return alag hai. Dono zaroori hain:

  • WHT bank automatically deduct karta hai — aapko kuch nahi karna
  • Income tax return aapko khud file karni hoti hai — har saal September 30 tak
  • Return mein total Payoneer income declare karein (gross amount jo bank mein aai)
  • Expenses deduct karein (platform fees, internet, equipment)
  • WHT credited tax mein show karein — refund ya balance payable calculate hoga

Dollar Account (FCVA) — Payoneer Users Ke Liye

Bahut Pakistani banks Foreign Currency Value Account (FCVA) offer karte hain — dollars mein rakhne ki facility. HBL, UBL, Meezan mein yeh account available hai. Iske fayde:

  • Dollars mein rakh sako — jab chahein PKR mein convert karo
  • Dollar appreciation ka faida uthao
  • Remittance documentation intact rehti hai
  • IT export exemption ke liye better documentation

Important: Payoneer dollar card se Pakistani ATM se cash nikalte time jo WHT kata hai — woh bhi adjustable hai. Lekin proper banking channel se transfer karna better practice hai documentation ke liye.

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal

Payoneer se bank transfer par kitna WHT lagta hai?
Active filer: 1% WHT. Non-filer: 2% WHT. Yeh Section 231AA ke under deduct hota hai automatically by the bank.
Kya Payoneer WHT adjustable hai ya final tax?
Active filer ke liye adjustable — income tax return mein credit milta hai, refund possible. Non-filer ke liye it's treated as near-final — no adjustment.
Payoneer card se ATM withdrawal par tax lagta hai?
Haan. Pakistani ATM se Rs. 50,000 se zyada cash withdrawal par WHT lagti hai — filer: 0.6%, non-filer: 1.2%.
Kya Payoneer income return mein declare karna zaroori hai?
Haan. WHT alag cheez hai — income declare karna alag zaroori hai. Bank transfers SBP ko report hoti hain, SBP FBR ke saath share karta hai. Voluntarily declare karna safe aur faydemand hai.
Payoneer annual fee deductible hai?
Haan. Payoneer annual fee, transaction fees, currency conversion charges — sab deductible business expenses hain. Payoneer dashboard se transaction history download karein as proof.
Filer banne se Payoneer par kitni savings hoti hain?
1% vs 2% ka fark aur adjustability — ek freelancer jo Rs. 5 lakh monthly earn karta hai, saal mein Rs. 1.2 lakh+ savings sirf filer hone se. Plus property, vehicle, banking transactions par alag savings.
