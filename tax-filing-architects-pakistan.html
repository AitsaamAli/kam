Tax Filing for Architects in Pakistan
Complete 2026 Guide

📅 May 31, 2026 ⏱ 7 min read ✍️ Kamboh Associates

Architects in Pakistan earn through design fees, project supervision charges, client retainers, and increasingly — international design projects. Under FBR rules, architectural income is classified as "income from business/profession," and architects must file an annual income tax return just like any other professional.

Good news: Architects running their own studios have access to extensive deductible expenses — studio rent, design software, equipment, and more. Properly claiming these can reduce your taxable income by 30–50%.

Sources of Taxable Income

  • Architectural design fees (residential, commercial, industrial)
  • Project supervision and site management charges
  • Interior design consultancy fees
  • Urban planning and master planning projects
  • International project income (remote design work)
  • Teaching at architecture schools and universities

Deductible Expenses for Architects

Architects with a studio or independent practice can deduct these costs:

  • Studio rent: Monthly rent for your design office
  • Staff costs: Junior architects, draftsmen, interior designer salaries
  • Design software: AutoCAD, Revit, SketchUp Pro, Lumion, Photoshop subscriptions
  • Printing and plotting: Drawing prints, site plans, presentation materials
  • Model making: Materials used for architectural scale models
  • PCATP fee: Annual Pakistan Council of Architects and Town Planners membership
  • Site travel: Vehicle fuel, parking, and transport to project sites
  • Utilities: Electricity, internet for the studio
  • Professional development: Architecture conferences, workshops, and CPD programs

Project cost reimbursements: If clients reimburse you for project-specific costs (printing, travel, site surveys), these reimbursements are NOT your income — they are pass-through costs. Do not declare them as revenue, and do not deduct them as expenses either.

How to Declare Architecture Income in IRIS

  1. Login to IRIS at iris.fbr.gov.pk with your NTN credentials
  2. Go to Declaration → Income Tax Return → Form IT-2
  3. Under "Business Income" → enter total professional receipts for the year
  4. Fill out the "Expenses" section with all documented studio costs
  5. Net income = Receipts minus Expenses (this is your taxable income)
  6. Enter all withholding tax certificates from corporate clients as credits
  7. Complete wealth statement showing all personal and business assets
  8. Pay any balance tax via PSID payment link
  9. Submit before September 30 deadline

Frequently Asked Questions

How are architects taxed in Pakistan?
Architects are taxed as professionals under business income. Design fees, supervision charges, and project management income are all taxable at normal slab rates (0%–35%). Corporate clients deduct 7.5% withholding tax from payments.
What expenses can architects deduct from taxable income?
Architects can deduct studio rent, staff salaries, design software (AutoCAD, Revit, SketchUp), printing/plotting costs, PCATP membership fees, vehicle travel to project sites, and professional development expenses.
Do architects need to register a company or can they practice individually?
Architects can practice individually (sole proprietor) under their personal NTN, or register a private company. Most individual practitioners use sole proprietorship, which is simpler for tax filing.
