Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us via WhatsApp, email, or our contact form, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, city, and a description of your tax service requirements. We do not collect CNIC numbers, NTN numbers, or financial documents unless you voluntarily share them for the purpose of tax filing services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the tax consultation, filing, or registration service you requested.
  • To contact you regarding your service status via WhatsApp or email.
  • To send service-related notifications (e.g., FBR deadline reminders).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. FBR / Tax Document Handling

Any tax documents or financial information shared with us for the purpose of income tax return filing or registration services are used solely to complete the requested service. Documents are not stored longer than necessary and are deleted after service completion upon request.
