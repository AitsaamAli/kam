Frequently Asked Questions

Do I file a separate return for each rented property?

No. All rent goes into one return under income from property, but keep a property-by-property record behind it.

Panel companies deduct tax from our lab's invoices. How do we claim it?

Collect each payer's withholding certificate and claim the deductions in your return.

The developer deducts tax from my contract payments. Is that all my tax?

It's credited against your tax, and in some cases may be treated as minimum or final tax. Collect every certificate and file a return to settle your position.

Can I get a tax credit on my life insurance premium?

Resident individuals have been allowed a tax credit on qualifying premiums, subject to conditions and limits. We check your policy and the current rules.
