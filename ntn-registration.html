What Is NTN and Why Do You Need It?

NTN (National Tax Number) is a unique 7-digit identifier issued by the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) to every registered taxpayer in Pakistan. Under the Income Tax Ordinance 2001, any person earning taxable income, owning a business, holding significant bank balances, or purchasing property is legally required to have an NTN and file an annual income tax return.

Frequently Asked Questions — NTN Registration

What is NTN and who needs it in Pakistan?
NTN (National Tax Number) is a unique identifier issued by FBR to every taxpayer. Every Pakistani earning income above the taxable threshold, running a business, owning property, or holding significant bank deposits legally needs an NTN. Salaried individuals, freelancers, business owners, companies, and AOPs all require NTN registration under the Income Tax Ordinance 2001.
What documents are required for NTN registration?
For individuals: CNIC copy, recent utility bill (electricity or gas), mobile number registered on CNIC, email address. For business/AOP: CNIC of all owners/partners, partnership deed, utility bill of business premises, bank account number. For companies: SECP registration certificate + director CNICs.
How long does NTN registration take?
With Kamboh Associates, you receive a temporary NTN on the same working day. The permanent printed NTN certificate from FBR takes 7–10 working days. The entire process is 100% online — no FBR office visit required.
Can a student or housewife get NTN?
Yes. Any Pakistani citizen aged 18+ with a valid CNIC can register for NTN — including students, housewives, and retired persons. Having NTN does not mean you owe tax. Many people get NTN specifically to become ATL filers and save on withholding taxes.
What happens if I don't register for NTN?
Without NTN, you are classified as a non-filer and face higher withholding tax rates on banking transactions, property purchases, and vehicle registration. FBR can also issue notices under Section 114 requiring you to file returns if your income or assets indicate taxable activity.
Is NTN registration free? What is the actual cost?
FBR itself charges no government fee for NTN registration. Kamboh Associates charges Rs. 1,500 for individual NTN and Rs. 2,500 for business/AOP NTN as a professional service fee for handling the IRIS portal registration, document preparation, and certificate follow-up.
