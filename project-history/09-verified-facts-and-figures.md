## Italy visa figures (verified 2026-08-28, used across the Italy cluster)
- **Italy's Decreto Flussi work-visa route has no proof-of-funds requirement at all** — it is employer-sponsored (the employer applies for a *nulla osta*); do not imply FBR documents are needed for this specific route
- Decreto Flussi 2026 total quota: **164,850 workers** (Decree-Law 146/2025, converted into Law 179/2025)
- Pakistan's reserved allocation: **10,500 slots over 2026–2028 (~3,500/year)** — this figure comes from Pakistani government/media sources (Ministry of Overseas Pakistanis), NOT from the Italian decree itself (which doesn't publish country-by-country breakdowns) — always hedge this as Pakistani-sourced, not settled Italian law
- 2024 uptake was only **7.8%**: ~9,331 permits actually issued out of 119,890 available — useful for countering agent overpromising
- Process: employer files nulla osta on click day → Questura + National Labour Inspectorate verification → employer confirms within 7 days (ALI portal/PEC) → worker has **180 days** to apply for the visa (Art. 22(6) T.U. Immigrazione) → permesso di soggiorno within 8 days of arrival
- Pakistan–Italy DTA: **exists** (confirmed via a US Dept of Commerce country guide and PwC's count of Pakistan's 68 treaty partners), but the **exact signature/entry-into-force date could not be confirmed** — do not state a date without verifying against the FBR DTA page
- Do not state: any Italian income tax rate, Italian social security figure, or PKR conversion of a EUR figure — all unverified/out of scope

## USA visa figures (verified 2026-08-28, used across the USA cluster — first USA coverage on the site)
- **The US visa process has no dollar proof-of-funds threshold at all** for F-1 or B1/B2 — a consular officer forms a judgement (INA 214(b) presumption of immigrant intent for B1/B2, which the applicant must personally rebut)
- F-1: SEVIS I-901 fee **$350**; visa application (MRV) fee **$185** — two separate, non-refundable fees. On 17 July 2026 DHS published a final rule establishing a fixed period of admission for F/J visa holders, replacing "duration of status" — recent, practical effects still emerging
- H-1B: cap 65,000 + 20,000 US master's; registration fee **$215**/beneficiary; FY2027 registration ran 4–19 March 2026. DHS final rule replaced the random lottery with **wage-weighted selection**
- H-1B $100,000 fee (Presidential Proclamation 10973, signed 19 Sept 2025): applies to new petitions for beneficiaries outside the US requiring consular processing (i.e. Pakistani applicants) — **status has flipped repeatedly in litigation** (vacated by a Massachusetts court 8 June 2026, opposite ruling from a DC court Dec 2025, appeals ongoing through July 2026, proclamation itself due to expire Sept 2026 absent renewal). **Do not state a current status** — tell readers to confirm with a US immigration attorney
- EB-5: **$800,000** in a TEA (rural/high-unemployment) or qualifying infrastructure project; **$1,050,000** elsewhere (EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act 2022, stable through 2026, next inflation adjustment due 1 Jan 2027). 10 full-time US jobs required. Source-of-funds standard (8 CFR 204.6(j)): lawful source *and* documented path to escrow, preponderance-of-the-evidence standard — for a gift, USCIS traces the donor's source of funds too
- Pakistan–US tax treaty: signed in Washington **1 July 1957**, in force since **1959** — the oldest treaty in Pakistan's network of 60+ agreements (this one IS safely dated — verified via an IRS-published treaty document)
- Do not state: current status of the $100k H-1B fee (see above), any US domestic tax rule (worldwide-income taxation, exit tax, FBAR/FATCA — out of scope), any US visa approval rate for Pakistan, any PKR conversion of a USD figure, EB-5 legal/filing fee amounts, or any proof-of-funds figure for F-1/B1-B2 (none exists)

## Tax law / regulatory facts established during the 100-topic content pushes (verified via WebSearch/WebFetch, cite-checked)
- FBR digital invoicing/e-invoicing legal basis: **SRO 1852(I)/2025** (issued Sept 24, 2025) + Sales Tax General Order No. 01 of 2026 — an earlier internal draft cited a wrong SRO number, corrected before publishing.
- Significant Economic Presence (SEP) tax threshold: changed from a 5-transaction test to a **Rs. 1M volume** test via Finance Act 2025.
- Digital Presence Proceeds Tax Act 2025: suspended via **SRO 1366(I)/2025** the same day it took effect.
- Section 6A e-commerce withholding: **1% digital / 2% COD**.
- Section 152 royalty/FTS withholding: **15%**.
- Section 59B group relief: **75%/55%** ownership tiers (2025 special-regime restriction applies).
- Section 97 amalgamation relief + 2026 SECP filing overhaul: **SRO 669/2026** (beneficial-ownership verification).
- REIT: **15%** dividend tax; SPV tiers **0-35-70%**; **6-year** capital gains exemption.
- Sukuk/TFC: Section 151 profit-on-debt **15% filer / 35-40% non-filer**; TFC capital gains withholding raised **15%→20%** in 2026.
- EOBI: **5% employer + 1% employee** contribution, on the minimum-wage base.
- PESSI (Punjab): **~6%** employer-only. SESSI (Sindh): **~7%** employer-only.
- Section 156 prize/lottery withholding: **15%/30%** (prize bonds & crossword), **20%/40%** (lottery/raffle/quiz/promotional) — final tax, non-refundable.
- Individual/AOP tax filing deadline: **September 30**. Company deadline: **December 31**. Sales tax: **15th of month**.
- FBR helpline: **051-111-772-772**, `helpline@fbr.gov.pk`. Complaint portal: `iris.fbr.gov.pk/complaint`. Federal Tax Ombudsman: `fto.gov.pk`.
- ATL/filer SMS check: **9966**, format `ATL <13-digit CNIC>` (works across Jazz/Zong/Telenor/Ufone).
- No Pakistani inheritance tax — legal heirs are not taxed on inheritance itself; liability is limited to estate value.
